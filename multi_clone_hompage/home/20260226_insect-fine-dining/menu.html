The Philosophy of the Menu

Twelve Acts in
the Theater of Insects

The ENTOMO menu is a narrative. Each course introduces a new species, explores a different facet of its flavor profile, and ties it to a broader understanding of how insects can sustain both human health and planetary ecosystems.

We change approximately 40% of the menu every season, as new harvests, new fermentation experiments, and new collaborations with our farm partners yield new possibilities. The menu shown here reflects the current winter edition.

Winter Menu 2026

All Twelve Courses

Menu subject to seasonal availability and farm harvest cycles. Species substitutions are noted for that evening's service.

01
Ants on Ice
Formica rufa · Red Wood Ant
Formica rufa Vegan
A single translucent sphere of frozen cucumber water, its surface dotted with live red wood ants sourced from certified organic forest plots in Helsingør. The ants impart a sharp, citric formic acid burst — nature's most elegant amuse-bouche. Served as a palate primer and conceptual declaration: we eat the whole world here.
Pairing: Danish sparkling elderflower water, lightly acidified with yuzu.
Amuse-Bouche
Formica rufa
Served at -2°C
02
Cricket Garum
Acheta domesticus · House Cricket
Acheta domesticus
House crickets fermented for 14 months in salt at Insekta ApS. The resulting garum — a clear, bronze-tinted liquid of extraordinary depth — is drizzled over a disc of raw Limfjord oyster, crème fraîche, and dehydrated cricket chitin. More complex than any fish sauce. More ancient, too.
Pairing: Blanc de blancs Champagne, 2019 vintage, briny and mineral.
Cold Starter
Acheta domesticus
Served at 4°C
03
Waxworm Butter
Galleria mellonella · Greater Wax Moth Larva
Galleria mellonella Allergen: Dairy
Rendered waxworm fat — with its naturally occurring notes of banana, vanilla, and beeswax — is churned with cultured cream into a pale gold compound butter. Served with grilled black rye bread and a dusting of smoked cricket salt. The richest butter you have never imagined.
Pairing: Unfiltered orange wine, Skin Contact, skin-fermented Muscat Blanc.
Bread Course
Galleria mellonella
Served at 16°C
04
Cricket Powder Pasta
Acheta domesticus · House Cricket
Acheta domesticus Allergen: Gluten, Egg
Hand-rolled pappardelle incorporating 40% cricket flour milled in-house, creating a nutty, amber-hued pasta of unusual protein density. Dressed with aged Parmigiano Reggiano, cured egg yolk snow, preserved lemon, and a constellation of foraged sorrel. A course that asks you to reconsider every pasta you've eaten.
Pairing: Trebbiano d'Abruzzo Riserva, oxidative and nutty.
Pasta
Acheta domesticus
Served at 68°C
05
Silkworm Broth
Bombyx mori · Domestic Silkworm
Bombyx mori Dairy-Free
Silkworm pupae simmered for 18 hours produce a consommé of remarkable clarity and delicate sweetness, somewhere between dashi and bone broth. Served in a black porcelain vessel with one silkworm pupa confit, three drops of cold-pressed black sesame, and a micro-cilantro crown. Silence at the table is common during this course.
Pairing: Sparkling Sake, lightly effervescent, umami-forward.
Soup
Bombyx mori
Served at 82°C
06
Grasshopper Tempura
Locusta migratoria · Migratory Locust
Locusta migratoria Allergen: Gluten
Three migratory locusts, legs intact, flash-fried in ice-cold dashima batter for twelve seconds. Served with yuzu kosho aioli, pickled daikon batons, and a brushstroke of grasshopper-infused chili oil. Crunchy, savory, faintly grassy. The course most guests nervously begin and then immediately request seconds of.
Pairing: Grüner Veltliner, Smaragd, with bright green pepper notes.
Fried
Locusta migratoria
Served at 75°C
07
Silkworm Risotto
Bombyx mori · Domestic Silkworm
Bombyx mori Allergen: Dairy
Carnaroli rice cooked to wave-like consistency in silkworm broth, finished tableside with three silkworm pupae confit and a canopy of fresh black truffle shavings from Périgord. Eighteen months of recipe development distilled into five minutes of eating. The single most requested dish in ENTOMO's history.
Pairing: White Burgundy, Premier Cru Meursault, creamy and mineral.
Main — Rice
Bombyx mori
Served at 72°C
08
Black Soldier Fly Ceviche
Hermetia illucens · Black Soldier Fly Larva
Hermetia illucens Dairy-Free
BSFL raised on organic citrus pulp, their flesh subtly perfumed with orange and lime. Cured in tiger's milk with habanero and ginger, served with coconut leche de tigre granita and compressed watermelon. A palate-resetting intermezzo with genuine tropical vivacity.
Pairing: Riesling Auslese, just off-dry, apricot and lime blossom.
Intermezzo
Hermetia illucens
Served at 6°C
09
Scorpion-Smoked Duck
Androctonus australis · Saharan Fat-tailed Scorpion
Androctonus australis Allergen: Shellfish
The body of a farm-raised Saharan fat-tailed scorpion — its venom sacs removed — is slow-smoked over applewood and used to scent a Peking duck breast. The exoskeleton becomes a garnish. The smoke carries a profound, almost incense-like depth that no wood chip can replicate. Served with beet gel and scorched green onion.
Pairing: Barolo Riserva, 2015, iron and rose petals.
Main — Meat
Androctonus australis
Served at 58°C
10
Mealworm Miso
Tenebrio molitor · Yellow Mealworm
Tenebrio molitor Vegan
ENTOMO's flagship fermentation: yellow mealworms inoculated with Aspergillus oryzae koji and aged for 18 months in cedar barrels. Prepared as a warm consommé with koji-cured celeriac batons, pine needle oil, and a scattering of dehydrated mealworm crumbles. Transcendent umami. The course that convinced Michelin.
Pairing: Aged Jura Vin Jaune, walnut and curry leaf.
Soup · Signature
Tenebrio molitor
Served at 80°C
11
Honeybee Pollen Sorbet
Apis mellifera · Western Honeybee
Apis mellifera Honey + Sorbet
A pre-dessert: sorbet churned from a base of raw honeybee pollen dissolved in cold-pressed apple juice, set with a ribbon of raw honey from our rooftop hive, and finished with bee pollen dust. The bright, floral sweetness prepares the palate with the gentleness of a closing chord's anticipation.
Pairing: Still Tokaji, 5 Puttonyos, saffron and dried apricot.
Pre-Dessert
Apis mellifera
Served at -8°C
12
Beetle Bark Chocolate
Agrilus planipennis · Jewel Beetle
Agrilus planipennis Allergen: Dairy
The menu's final word: a chocolate bar cast from 72% Madagascan dark chocolate, tempered with ground jewel beetle — its shimmering iridescent wing casings pulverized into the couverture, creating flickers of emerald in the dark surface. A petit four of cricket praline, ant caramel, and bee pollen madeleine complete the final course.
Pairing: Pedro Ximénez Sherry, aged 20 years, raisin and fig.
Dessert
Agrilus planipennis
Served at 18°C

The Wine Programme

Pairing the
Unprecedented

Our sommelier Brigitte Møller has designed three pairing journeys: classic wine, natural wine, and non-alcoholic. Each course's pairing is chosen not just for flavor harmony, but for the way it changes the guest's perception of the insect ingredient itself.

Classic Wine Pairing
DKK 950 · 12 pours
European fine wine from Burgundy, Champagne, Barolo, and Tokaj. Each pour amplifies a specific flavor compound in its paired course.
Natural Wine Journey
DKK 1,050 · 12 pours
Biodynamic and natural producers, many from emerging Nordic and Georgian winemakers. Wild fermentation meets wild protein.
Non-Alcoholic Pairing
DKK 650 · 12 pours
House-made fermented beverages: insect kombucha, wildflower shrubs, cricket tea cold brew, and aged vinegar tonics. Chef Yamamoto's personal project.

Dietary Requirements

Accommodating
Every Guest

Insect Allergies
Guests with crustacean or shellfish allergies may react to insects due to shared chitin proteins. We strongly recommend consulting your allergist before dining at ENTOMO.
Please inform us at least 72 hours before your reservation.
Vegan Adaptation
A fully vegan version of the menu is available upon request, featuring insect-derived umami elements without animal-derived dairy or egg. Several courses are already vegan by design.
Available with 48-hour advance notice.
Gluten-Free
Our kitchen maintains strict separation protocols. A gluten-free adaptation of all 12 courses is available — the cricket pasta is replaced with a cassava-cricket gnocchi of equal complexity.
Please notify us at the time of reservation.
