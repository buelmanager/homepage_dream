Questions

Everything You
Need to Know

If you have a question not answered here, please reach out to us at table@entomo.dk or call during service hours.

We recommend booking 6–10 weeks in advance. Peak season (May–August and December) books up to 4 months ahead. Our waitlist is always open for shorter notice visits.
Our reservations team reviews all requests within 24 hours. If your date is available, you'll receive a payment link for the DKK 500 per person deposit. Your reservation is only confirmed upon receipt of payment.
The deposit is fully refundable if cancelled more than 72 hours before the reservation date. Cancellations within 72 hours of service are non-refundable. We can transfer your deposit to a new date with 5+ days notice.
Please contact us directly at allergies@entomo.dk before booking. Guests with crustacean or shellfish allergies should consult their allergist — insects share chitin proteins with shellfish and cross-reactivity is documented. We take allergies extremely seriously and will work with you individually.
Smart casual. We ask guests to avoid shorts and athletic wear, but we have no formal dress requirement. Our space is warm, intimate, and relaxed — we want you to be comfortable for four hours of dining.
We welcome guests aged 16 and above. Younger guests are welcome for private buyout events with prior arrangement. The length and sensory complexity of the experience is most suitable for adult palates.
We are located at Kronprinsessegade 46, 1306 Copenhagen K. A 4-minute walk from Kongens Nytorv Metro station. Valet parking is not available, but public parking is available nearby at Magasin P-hus.

Private Events

Exclusive Buyouts
& Private Dining

The entire ENTOMO space — 20 covers, private kitchen access, and the full attention of both chefs — is available for exclusive buyout events. We create bespoke menus for corporate dinners, launches, milestone celebrations, and cultural events.

◆
Full Buyout: 12–20 guests, Wednesday–Sunday, bespoke menu development included.
◆
Chef's Table: 4–6 guests in the kitchen, interacting with both chefs throughout the evening.
◆
Brand Partnerships: ENTOMO collaborates with sustainability-aligned brands on experiential events and content creation.
◆
Insect Education Seminars: Pre-dinner talks by Chef Yamamoto on insect science, available for corporate groups.
