Find Us

In the Heart
of Copenhagen

Address
Kronprinsessegade 46
1306 Copenhagen K
Denmark
Located in the historic district, between Kongens Have and the Design Museum Denmark. The entrance is on the ground floor; look for the illuminated ENTOMO sign.
Opening Hours
Wednesday — Saturday
Service begins: 19:00
Kitchen closes: approx. 23:00
We are closed Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday. Private event bookings on off days are available by arrangement.
Getting Here
Metro: Kongens Nytorv (M1, M2)
Bus: Lines 14, 26, 42
Taxi: 4 min from Nørreport
Bicycle parking available in front of the building. Limited street parking on Kronprinsessegade. Nearest car park: Magasin P-hus (5 min walk).
Accessibility
Fully Wheelchair Accessible
Step-free entrance, accessible restrooms, and seating adaptations available on request. Please indicate any accessibility requirements when booking.

Partnerships

Build the Future
of Food With Us

ENTOMO is more than a restaurant. We are a research kitchen, a public platform for sustainable protein, and an active collaborator with the scientists, farmers, and institutions building tomorrow's food systems.

◆
Insect Farm Sourcing
We are always seeking certified organic insect farms within 500km of Copenhagen. We prioritise partners who share our commitment to insect welfare, closed-loop systems, and documented feed protocols. Multi-year sourcing agreements available.
Enquire about sourcing
◇
Academic Research
ENTOMO collaborates with university food science and entomology departments on flavor compound analysis, fermentation research, and consumer behavior studies. Dr. Yamamoto is available as a guest lecturer and co-investigator.
Research collaboration
◈
Brand Partnerships
We partner with brands whose values align with sustainability, precision, and forward thinking. Past collaborators include Nordic biotech firms, premium culinary equipment brands, and sustainable luxury consumer products.
