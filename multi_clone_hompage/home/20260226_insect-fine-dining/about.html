The Beginning

From Noma's Kitchen
to the Forest Floor

In 2017, Chef Astrid Lundqvist took a leave from her position at Noma and disappeared into the street markets of Bangkok, the jungle kitchens of Oaxaca, and the mountain villages of Yunnan Province. She wasn't looking for inspiration. She was looking for proof.

Two billion people worldwide eat insects daily. They have for millennia. What Astrid found was not primitive subsistence food — it was the most sophisticated protein system on the planet, refined over thousands of years, yielding flavors that no conventional kitchen could replicate.

She returned to Copenhagen in 2019 with notebooks full of recipes, a biochemist named Keiichi Yamamoto as her co-conspirator, and a conviction that fine dining had been looking in the wrong direction for decades.

Our Philosophy

Four Pillars of
Entomo's Vision

Every decision at ENTOMO — from sourcing to plating to the temperature of the dining room — flows from four core beliefs about what food can and should be.

◈
Radical Honesty
Insects are named, sourced, and described. No obfuscation. The guest understands exactly what they're eating and why.
◆
Scientific Rigor
Every dish is developed through documented research, flavor compound analysis, and iterative testing before it reaches the tasting menu.
◇
Zero Waste
100% of each insect is used. Shells become powders. Frass becomes garden compost. Every lifecycle stage has culinary value.
◉
Radical Joy
Sustainability should not taste like sacrifice. ENTOMO exists to prove that eating responsibly is the most delicious act possible.

Farm Partnerships

Every Insect Has
a Known Address

We maintain direct relationships with four certified organic micro-farms within the Nordic region. Each farm adheres to our Insect Welfare Protocols — the first such standards written for culinary applications in Europe.

BioKraft Farms
Malmö, Sweden
Scandinavia's largest organic cricket farm operating on closed-loop waste management. All feed is sourced from Malmö's organic vegetable processors.
Acheta domesticus · Gryllus bimaculatus
Nordic Protein Lab
Aarhus, Denmark
Research-farm hybrid that breeds silkworm strains specifically developed for culinary flavor profiles. Collaborates with Aarhus University's food science department.
Bombyx mori · Galleria mellonella
Insekta ApS
Copenhagen, Denmark
Urban farm operating in a converted warehouse 8km from ENTOMO. Specialises in mealworm cultivation and insect fermentation research — birthplace of our signature mealworm miso.
Tenebrio molitor · Zophobas morio
Grøn Larve
Odense, Denmark
Specializes in orthoptera — crickets, grasshoppers, and locusts — raised on organic grain, chamomile, and fennel, which impart distinctive aromatic notes to the insects' flavor.
Locusta migratoria · Acheta domesticus
