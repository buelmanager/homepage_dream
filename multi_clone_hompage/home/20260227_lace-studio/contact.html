Caring for Your Lace

A Guide to Preserving Handmade Lace

Handmade lace is remarkably durable when properly stored and cleaned. Pieces made in the sixteenth century survive today; yours should outlast you by centuries.

☂
Washing

Hand wash only in cool distilled water with a small amount of pH-neutral soap. Do not agitate or wring. Rinse thoroughly and pin to a blocking board to dry flat.

☇
Storage

Store rolled (never folded) in acid-free tissue, inside an archival box. Never use plastic bags or rubber bands. Keep away from direct light and humidity above 55%.

★
Display

Mount on acid-free board or silk-covered frame using conservation-grade pins. Avoid direct sunlight. UV-filtering glass recommended for framed pieces.

△
Restoration

We offer restoration consultations for damaged pieces, whether Dentelle work or historic lace from other sources. Contact us with photographs before any treatment is attempted.
