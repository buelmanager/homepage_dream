Our Story

Born in Darkness,
Built for Light

OBSCURA began in the back of a Toyota Land Cruiser somewhere between Joshua Tree and the Milky Way. Twelve years later, we train the world's finest night photographers.

The Origin

From Obsession to Academy

In 2013, Dr. Elena Varek and Marcus Obi drove into the Mojave Desert with two Canon EOS 5D bodies and one borrowed tripod. They had both left careers — astrophysics and commercial photography respectively — chasing the same impossible image: the Milky Way reflected in a perfectly still desert lake.

"We didn't plan an academy. We planned a photograph. Everything else followed the light."
— Dr. Elena Varek, Founder

What started as two obsessives sharing camera settings on Reddit morphed into informal workshops, then sold-out retreats, then an application-only academy with a 14-week curriculum refined over a decade of teaching. Today, OBSCURA graduates hold positions at National Geographic, NASA's JPL imaging division, and every major photography publication.

We don't teach photography. We teach you to see. The night sky is the canvas — long exposure, light discipline, and post-processing mastery are your brushes. We hand you those brushes and take you to the darkest places on Earth to use them.

History

Twelve Years of Dark Skies

From a desert road trip to the world's leading night photography institution.

2013
Founded in Joshua Tree

Elena Varek and Marcus Obi lead the first informal night photography retreat with 6 photographers. All sold out within 48 hours of posting online.

2015
First International Expedition

OBSCURA leads its inaugural Iceland Aurora Hunt. The resulting graduate portfolios are published in Digital Camera World, launching the academy's international reputation.

2017
Hana Sato Joins as Master

Tokyo-based light painting pioneer Hana Sato joins OBSCURA faculty, adding the Light Painting and Urban Night specializations. Enrollment triples within one year.

2019
47th International Photography Award

OBSCURA graduates sweep the night photography category at IPA 2019, placing 1st, 2nd, and 3rd. The academy is named "World's Best Photography Education Program" by PetaPixel.

2022
Atacama Observatory Partnership

Partnership established with the Atacama Cosmology Telescope facility, giving OBSCURA exclusive access to observatory grounds during dark moon windows. The darkest classroom on Earth.

2026
3,000th Graduate

OBSCURA celebrates its 3,000th graduate — a retired teacher from Osaka who entered the Sony World Photography Awards and took second place in her first year of shooting.
