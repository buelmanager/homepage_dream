Any mirrorless or DSLR camera with manual exposure controls and a wide-angle lens with a maximum aperture of f/2.8 or wider is sufficient. Full-frame sensors perform better in low light, but APS-C cameras are absolutely acceptable for all workshop levels. A sturdy tripod is essential — do not bring a lightweight travel tripod for overnight outdoor sessions. OBSCURA can arrange camera rental for expedition participants who need a backup body.
Cancellations made more than 60 days before the program start date receive a full refund. Between 30 and 60 days, we offer a credit toward any future OBSCURA program. Within 30 days, the fee is non-refundable but transferable to another person. OBSCURA reserves the right to cancel any program due to extreme weather, in which case participants receive a full refund or complimentary rescheduling.
It depends on the expedition. Iceland and Sahara expeditions require only moderate fitness — you'll walk on uneven terrain for 1–2 hours per night. The Himalayan Starfields expedition is classified as "demanding" and requires prior trekking experience at altitude. The Atacama expedition involves elevation (3,500m) — acclimatization days are built into the itinerary. We recommend consulting your physician before any high-altitude expedition.
The Light Painting Mastery and Urban Night Program welcome all levels including beginners. The Milky Way Masterclass requires intermediate skills (comfortable with manual mode, basic Lightroom). The Astrophotography Intensive is advanced only and requires prior night photography experience. If you're unsure, email us and we'll help you find the right program.
All OBSCURA expeditions include: accommodation (luxury lodges, glamping, or high-altitude tents depending on location), all meals from arrival to departure, ground transportation, local guides, shooting permits, OBSCURA instructor fees, gear support, and post-expedition portfolio editing. International flights to the destination country are not included. Travel insurance is strongly recommended and available through OBSCURA at enrollment.
OBSCURA limits class sizes rigorously to ensure instructor attention. Workshops range from 6–12 students depending on the program. Expeditions never exceed 12 participants. The Astrophotography Intensive and Himalayan expedition are capped at 6 participants for maximum personal instruction and to minimize our environmental footprint in sensitive dark sky areas.
