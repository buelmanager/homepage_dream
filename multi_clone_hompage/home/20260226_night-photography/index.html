Masterclass Curriculum

OBSCURA's curriculum is built by award-winning photographers who have shot in the world's darkest locations. Twelve weeks of intensive training that transform passionate amateurs into published professionals.

  • 01
    Night Exposure Fundamentals
    ISO, aperture, long exposure mastery in zero-light conditions
  • 02
    Milky Way Composition
    Sky arc framing, foreground integration, stacking techniques
  • 03
    Light Painting Mastery
    Orb creation, steel wool, kinetic sculpture with light
  • 04
    Astrophotography & Stacking
    Deep-sky objects, planetary imaging, image stacking in Sequator
  • 05
    Post-Processing: Night Edition
    Lightroom Milky Way workflow, gradient masking, star reduction

Meet the Masters

Three award-winning photographers with combined decades of experience shooting in the world's darkest skies.

01 / 03
Dr. Elena Varek
Director · Astrophotography

Former ESA imaging specialist turned photographic educator. Elena has captured nebulae from six continents and led over 80 international dark-sky expeditions. National Geographic contributing photographer.

02 / 03
Marcus Obi
Lead Instructor · Milky Way

Three-time Sony World Photography Award winner specializing in Milky Way composition and galactic center imaging. His course on foreground–sky integration has 40,000+ online enrollments.

03 / 03
Hana Sato
Master · Light Painting & Urban Night

Tokyo-based light painter and urban night specialist. Hana pioneered the "Invisible Architecture" technique and exhibits globally. Holds the Guinness record for largest indoor light orb.
