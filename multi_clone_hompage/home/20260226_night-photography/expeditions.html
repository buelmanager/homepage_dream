The Experience

What Every Expedition Includes

01
Nightly Shooting Sessions

3–5 hours of guided shooting every night on a new moon window. Your instructor works beside you — adjusting your settings, framing your shot, reviewing your histogram in real time.

02
Morning Critique & Edit

Every morning after shooting, the group reviews the night's captures together. Your instructor identifies key images for post-processing and walks through the edit live using your RAW files.

03
Luxury Accommodation

From desert camp glamping to arctic lodges, OBSCURA never compromises on accommodation. You sleep well so you can shoot well. All meals included — including a final celebration dinner.

04
Location Scouting

Pre-expedition, your instructor sends a detailed PDF of all shooting locations, sky maps for the moon window, and recommended camera settings for each spot based on current star positions.

05
Post-Expedition Portfolio

Every participant receives a curated 20-image portfolio selection from the expedition, post-processed to gallery standard, as part of their enrollment.

06
Zero-Impact Protocol

OBSCURA expeditions operate under International Dark-Sky Association approved zero-impact protocols. We leave no trace and contribute 3% of expedition fees to dark sky conservation.

Gear Guide

What to Bring

OBSCURA provides a full pre-expedition gear list. Here is the essential kit for any dark sky expedition.

Camera Essentials
  • Full-frame mirrorless or DSLR (Sony A7IV, Nikon Z6III, Canon R6 II or equivalent)
  • Wide-angle lens f/2.8 or faster (14–24mm range)
  • Sturdy carbon-fiber tripod with ball head
  • Remote shutter release or intervalometer
  • Minimum 128GB fast UHS-II SD cards (×3)
  • Extra batteries × 4 (cold weather depletes rapidly)
  • Dew heater strips for front element
OBSCURA Provides
  • Red-light headlamps for all participants
  • Sky Quality Meter readings at each location
  • Printed star atlas for the expedition dates
  • Portable power banks (for intervalometers)
  • Emergency backup body (Canon R5 available)
  • Weather station and cloud cover monitoring app
  • Satellite phone for remote locations
