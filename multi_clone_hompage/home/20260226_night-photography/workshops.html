The OBSCURA Standard

★
Expert-Led Instruction

All sessions led personally by OBSCURA master photographers — never assistants or substitute instructors.

◎
Small Class Sizes

Maximum 12 students per cohort. Most programs limited to 6–8 for individualized feedback on every image.

▣
Field Shooting Sessions

Two overnight field sessions minimum per program. You learn by doing, not just watching.

◈
Post-Processing Workshop

Full Lightroom and Photoshop workflow sessions. Night-specific presets and Milky Way stacking techniques included.

◐
Portfolio Review

Individual portfolio critique session with your instructor on the final day of every program.

◑
Alumni Community Access

Lifetime access to the OBSCURA Alumni Network, monthly Q&A calls, and graduate portfolio showcases.

◊
Certificate of Completion

Signed certificate from your OBSCURA instructor, recognized by leading photography agencies and publications.

○
Weather Guarantee

If poor weather ruins overnight sessions, OBSCURA offers a complimentary re-enrollment in any future cohort of the same program.
