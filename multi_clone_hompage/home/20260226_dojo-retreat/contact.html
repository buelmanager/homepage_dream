How It Works

The Application Process

We do not take bookings. We receive applications. The distinction matters. A booking is transactional; an application is a conversation. We read every submission personally and respond to each one.

We are not looking for a particular background, profession, or level of practice. We are looking for seriousness — a genuine readiness to be in an unfamiliar and demanding situation without requiring it to be easier than it is.

The process from first submission to confirmed residency typically takes two to three weeks.

01
Submit the Inquiry Form

Complete the form on this page. Tell us which program you are considering, your availability, and your practice background. Write as much or as little as feels honest. There are no wrong answers.

02
Brief Conversation

If your inquiry looks like a good fit, one of our team will schedule a 20-minute call — not an interview, but a genuine conversation. We want to understand what brings you and answer your questions fully.

03
Program Offer

If we think a specific program and date is right for you, we will send a formal offer with full details: confirmed dates, accommodation assignment, payment schedule, and arrival logistics.

04
Confirmation & Preparation

A 50% deposit confirms your place. We then send a detailed preparation guide: what to bring, what not to bring, how to prepare your body and mind, and what to expect on arrival day.
