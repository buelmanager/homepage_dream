# Clone Plan — TIDALUX Tidal Energy Station

## Concept
Premium tidal energy company. Hero G scroll-driven: "TIDAL" and "POWER" words separate as user scrolls through a 200vh sticky hero, revealing ocean depth and astronomical predictability messaging.

## Design Decisions
- P2 Cool Obsidian palette: deep navy-black bg with electric cyan accent evokes ocean depth and bioluminescence
- Fraunces (serif, editorial) + Inter (clean, technical) — authoritative yet approachable
- Hero G: scroll-driven word separation emphasizes that tidal energy is fundamentally about rhythm and predictability
- A3 Precise animation: x:-20px reveals suit the precision-engineering brand voice

## Section Structure
1. Preloader — TIDALUX wordmark
2. Nav — fixed, blur-backdrop
3. Hero G — 200vh sticky, TIDAL/POWER word split
4. Stats — 18 stations, 240MW, 11yr, 99% predictability
5. Philosophy — 3-column grid (Ocean Rhythm, Precision Engineering, Zero Emissions)
6. Projects — 4-card grid (Pentland Firth, Swansea Bay, Alderney Race, Minas Passage)
7. Atelier — full-width image + text overlay
8. Process — 5-step numbered timeline
9. Heritage — 12-entry timeline 2014–2025
10. Press — horizontal scroll carousel
11. Commission — CTA form (name, organization, site coordinates, capacity target)
12. Footer — links + social
