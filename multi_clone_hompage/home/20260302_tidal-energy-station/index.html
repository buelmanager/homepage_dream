Our Science

Precision Energy
from the Tides

Tidal Resource Mastery

Unlike wind or solar, tidal energy is perfectly predictable 100 years into the future. Our hydrodynamic modelling translates tidal data into bankable generation profiles with extraordinary accuracy.

Turbine Excellence

Tidalux horizontal-axis turbines are engineered from duplex stainless steel and composite blades — designed for 25-year immersed operation in the harshest estuarial environments.

Environmental Leadership

Our turbines operate at blade tip speeds below the avoidance threshold of marine mammals and fish, making Tidalux stations among the most ecologically benign energy installations on Earth.

The Station

Engineering the
Inevitable Tide

Tidalux was founded in 2014 by tidal hydrodynamicists and marine engineers who recognised that the world's tidal energy resource — 1,000 terawatt-hours per year — was almost entirely untapped.

Our stations are more than energy installations: they are precision instruments calibrated to the rhythms of the ocean, delivering power as reliably as the tides themselves.

Development

From Tide Table
to Power Grid

01
Tidal Hydrodynamic Survey

ADCP current profiling, bathymetric survey, and hydrodynamic modelling to characterise tidal velocities and energy flux at your site.

02
Array Layout Optimisation

CFD modelling of turbine array configurations to maximise energy yield while minimising wake interference and bathymetric impact.

03
Marine Consenting

Environmental impact assessment, benthic survey, and consultation with maritime regulators and stakeholders through to marine licence approval.

04
Installation & Grid Connection

Turbine seabed foundation installation, underwater cable laying, and onshore grid connection managed by our specialist marine contractors.

05
Remote Operations

Satellite-connected SCADA with real-time generation data, automated fault detection, and scheduled diver inspection programmes.

Our History

A Decade of
Tidal Excellence

2014

Tidalux Founded

Tidal hydrodynamicists establish Tidalux with backing from a UK infrastructure fund, deploying the first prototype turbine in the Pentland Firth.

2017

First Commercial Station

The Pentland Firth 16MW station delivers first power to the Scottish grid — the world's first commercially licensed tidal stream installation.

2020

100MW Milestone

Tidalux passes 100MW of commissioned tidal capacity across four stations — a global milestone for the tidal energy industry.

2023

International Expansion

The Bay of Fundy, with the world's highest tidal range, becomes Tidalux's first North American station — a 80MW landmark project.

2025

240MW Global Portfolio

18 tidal stations delivering 240MW of clean, predictable energy across the UK, Europe, and North America.
