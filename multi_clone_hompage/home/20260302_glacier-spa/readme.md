# KVORETH — Glacial Sanctuary

## Concept
Glacier cryotherapy and cold wellness spa in Norwegian fjords at 1,200 meters. Brand fictional: KVORETH. The sanctuary offers glacial immersion therapy with a strict 24-hour silence protocol. Five-page luxury template.

## Color Palette — P5 Arctic Slate
- Background: #141820 (deep blue-black)
- Surface: #1C2030
- Accent: #A0C4D8 (glacial blue)
- Ivory: #DCE8F0

## Typography — F5 Fraunces + Inter
- Fraunces: italic serif for elegance and warmth
- Inter: clean sans-serif body text
- Heavy use of italic styling for emotional resonance

## Hero — Type D Portraits + Stats
Split layout: left content panel with animated stat counters, right portrait grid with 3 images. Minimalist and calm.

## Animation — A2 Whisper
Slow, gentle animations: duration 1.4–1.6s, y: 14px, stagger 0.06, ease power1.out. Matches the sanctuary's silence philosophy.

## Pages
- index.html: Full landing page with all 13 sections
- about.html: Founder story, manifesto, values, heritage
- collection.html: Six therapy protocols with filter bar
- process.html: 5-stage protocol methodology
- contact.html: Reservation form, contact details

## Performance Notes
- Fraunces italic for emotional display copy
- Stats counters animate on scroll entry
- Swiper for press quotes carousel
- Max 8 guests → smallness is the luxury
