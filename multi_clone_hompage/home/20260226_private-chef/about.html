Lead Chef

Raphael Moreau

Executive Chef & Founder — Three Michelin Stars

Born in Lyon to a family of restaurateurs, Raphael Moreau understood from childhood that a meal is never just sustenance — it is an act of love and artistry. After graduating top of his class at the Institut Paul Bocuse, he spent fifteen years training under legendary kitchens in Paris, Tokyo and New York.

Raphael's philosophy is rooted in rigorous technique married to poetic intuition. He has cooked for heads of state, royalty and cultural figures on six continents, yet approaches each private dinner with the same singular dedication: the guest's experience above all else.

In 2014 he founded Table Privée with the conviction that the world's finest cuisine should not be confined to restaurant walls — it should arrive at your door.

2006 Graduated Institut Paul Bocuse, Lyon — Valedictorian
2009 Sous Chef, Le Bernardin, New York — First Michelin Star
2012 Head Chef, Taillevent, Paris — Second & Third Stars
2014 Founded Table Privée — Global Private Chef Service
2021 James Beard Award — Outstanding Chef, International

How We Work

Our Kitchen Philosophy

Every engagement begins with a detailed consultation — understanding your palate, dietary requirements, occasion and ambitions. We design not just a menu but an experience architecture that unfolds course by course.

Zero-Compromise Sourcing

We reject shortcuts. All proteins are heritage-breed; all produce is seasonal, local or specialist-imported at peak quality.

Technique Meets Intuition

Rigorous classical training provides our foundation. Creative intuition elevates each dish beyond recipe into expression.

Seamless Invisible Service

We arrive early, work quietly and depart leaving no trace — except the memory of an extraordinary meal.
