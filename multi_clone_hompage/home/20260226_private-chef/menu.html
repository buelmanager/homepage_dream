Inclusive Excellence

Dietary Accommodations

Every dietary requirement is met with the same level of creativity and craft as our signature menus. We do not offer a reduced experience — we design a complete one.

V
Vegetarian

Full tasting menu crafted entirely without meat or fish, celebrating the finest seasonal produce.

Vg
Vegan

Plant-forward menus of extraordinary complexity and depth, using fermentation, smoking and innovative techniques.

Gf
Gluten-Free

All courses adapted with no compromise to flavour, texture or presentation. Separate preparation always observed.

Al
Allergies

All 14 major allergens tracked and eliminated upon request. Full ingredient transparency provided on request.
