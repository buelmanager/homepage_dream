Cuisine at Sea

Yacht Catering

From £3,500 per day · Up to 30 guests

We bring the galley to life, transforming your vessel into a floating fine dining establishment. Whether a day charter in the Mediterranean or a week-long Atlantic crossing, our team provisions, prepares and serves with full professional rigour.

  • Pre-voyage menu planning and provisioning
  • Daily breakfast, lunch, sunset canapés and dinner
  • Wines and spirits curated by our Master Sommelier

  • Specialist seafood and local catch integration
