How It Works

Every Detail, Considered

We arrive hours before your guests. You sit down to a flawlessly orchestrated experience. We depart leaving no trace but the memory.

Step One

The Consultation

Every engagement begins with a personal conversation — typically by telephone or video call. We listen far more than we speak. Our goal is to understand not just what you want to eat, but who you are and what this evening means to you.

Occasion & Guest Profile We discuss the occasion, number of guests, ages, cultural sensitivities and the atmosphere you wish to create.
Dietary & Allergen Review Every dietary requirement and allergen is documented. Nothing is approximated or assumed.
Kitchen Assessment We assess your kitchen facilities remotely or via a brief site visit to plan logistics without surprises.
Cuisine Preferences We map your culinary tastes, favourite ingredients and any dishes you have loved — or wish to avoid.

Step Two

Menu Planning

Within 48 hours of your consultation, you receive a personalised menu proposal. This is not a selection from a catalogue — it is a composition created specifically for you and your guests.

Day 1
Chef Composition

Your lead chef reviews the consultation notes and composes a full tasting menu narrative, with wine pairing suggestions.

Day 2
Menu Delivery

You receive a beautifully presented menu document — each course described in full, with ingredient origins and allergen annotations.

Day 3–5
Refinement

We refine together through conversation. Any course can be adjusted, substituted or redesigned until the menu feels definitively yours.

Final
Menu Confirmed

The finalised menu is locked. Sourcing begins immediately to secure the finest ingredients at peak quality.

Step Three

Ingredient Sourcing

Every ingredient is sourced from our curated network of artisan producers across Europe and beyond. We accept no substitutions. If an ingredient is unavailable at its finest, we design around it.

Step Four

The Evening

On the day of your dinner, the Table Privée team orchestrates every detail. You need only look forward to it.

15:00
Arrival
Team Arrival

Our chef and front-of-house team arrive. Equipment and ingredients are brought in discreetly. Your kitchen is organised for an evening of precision.

17:30
Mise en Place
Mise en Place

Every component is prepared: stocks simmering, garnishes arranged, table dressed with our linen, glassware and service elements.

19:30
Guests Arrive
The Welcome

Champagne and canapés greet your guests. The chef introduces the evening's journey. The performance begins.

20:00
Dinner
The Tasting Menu

Each course arrives with perfect pacing. Wine is poured with description. Your guests dine without a single interruption to comfort.

23:30
Service Close
Mignardises & Departure

Final petit fours are served. As your guests linger, our team cleans silently, restoring your home to its pristine state before we depart.

Next Day
Follow-Up
Personal Note

The following morning you receive a personal note from your chef along with the full menu documentation — a keepsake of the evening.

Our Promise

White-Glove Standards

Every Table Privée engagement comes with our unconditional commitment to your comfort, privacy and satisfaction.

Absolute Discretion

All team members sign comprehensive NDAs. Your guest list, preferences and home are never discussed or documented externally.

Zero-Trace Cleaning

We leave your home in better condition than we found it. Every surface is cleaned, every item restored to its original position.

Menu Archive

We maintain a private archive of every menu we have prepared for you, ensuring no two evenings are ever the same.

Standing Arrangements

Regular clients may establish a standing arrangement — monthly dinners, weekly cooking, or on-call availability for spontaneous evenings.
