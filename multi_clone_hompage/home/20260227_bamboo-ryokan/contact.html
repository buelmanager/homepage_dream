Getting Here

Access & Directions

Kuretake is located in the hills above Arashiyama, approximately fifteen minutes on foot from the main bamboo grove. The final path to the ryokan gate is unpaved — beautifully so. We recommend comfortable shoes for arrival.

From Kyoto Station — By Taxi

35 minutes. Direct taxi from the station forecourt. We recommend requesting a Kyoto Green Cab, whose drivers know the property gate. Show them: 呉竹旅館 · 嵐山竹田谷.

From Arashiyama Station — On Foot

12 minutes along the hillside path. Exit the station, cross the Togetsu-kyo bridge, turn north at the bamboo souvenir street, and follow the signed forest path uphill. Look for the black bamboo gate.

From Kansai International Airport

Airport limousine bus to Kyoto Station (75 min), then taxi. Or direct private car transfer: arranged by our team at time of reservation.

Private Car Arrangement

We coordinate private car service from Osaka, Kobe, Nara, or any Kyoto location. Please indicate in your reservation notes and we will provide a quote.
