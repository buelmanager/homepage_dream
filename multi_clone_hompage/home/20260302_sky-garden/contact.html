Above the City

Gardens in the Sky

ELYSIAN occupies the 38th and 39th floors of a contemporary tower, housing 3,000 square metres of living garden with over 1,400 plant species, three dining experiences, and a programme of botanical events year-round.

Cloud Forest Room

Tropical ferns, mosses, and highland species thriving in our precisely controlled cloud forest environment with automated misting.

Orchid Pavilion

Over 200 rare orchid species curated by our botanist. Private viewing by appointment for Canopy and Pinnacle members.

Sky Terrace

Open-air seasonal planting with 360-degree city views. The preferred setting for our evening dining experiences and sunset programmes.
