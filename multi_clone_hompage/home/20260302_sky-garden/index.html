The Experience

Above the City, Within the Garden

ELYSIAN occupies the 38th and 39th floors of a contemporary tower in the heart of the city. Three thousand square metres of living garden — rare plants, private dining pavilions, and seasonal programming — suspended above the urban landscape.

✦
Living Collections

Over 1,400 species cultivated across our sky terraces — from rare cloud forest ferns to endangered highland orchids. Our head botanist, trained at Kew Gardens, curates each seasonal rotation.

◈
Garden Dining

Three dining experiences — from intimate private pavilions to the open-air Sky Table — all menu elements sourced from our growing terraces and partner micro-farms within 60 miles.

〜
Botanical Programming

Seasonal events, horticultural masterclasses, private tours with our botanists, and bespoke cultivation consultancy for our most dedicated members.

Our Vision

A Garden Should Challenge the Sky

ELYSIAN was conceived by landscape architect Seren Hartley and botanist Dr. James Adeyemi, who shared a conviction that luxury horticulture could exist at altitude — that the constraints of height would not diminish a garden but transform it into something entirely new.

Opening in 2019, ELYSIAN now hosts 4,200 members and welcomes day visitors by advance reservation. The garden has been documented in 14 international publications and awarded the Royal Horticultural Society's Garden of the Year for 2024.
