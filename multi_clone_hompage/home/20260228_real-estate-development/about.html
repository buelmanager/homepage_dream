Our Mission

Architecture
That Endures

Paragon Luxury Development Group was established in New York in 1972 by Edmund Paragon with a radical premise: that the finest residential buildings should be conceived, built, and managed with the same rigour applied to great civic monuments.

Over five decades, that conviction has produced $12 billion in developed value across 48 buildings in 12 cities — a portfolio that includes some of the most recognised residential addresses in the world.

Today, Paragon operates from headquarters in New York with regional offices in London, Dubai, and Singapore. Every project is directed personally by a member of the senior leadership team — ensuring that the standards established in 1972 are maintained in every building we create.

Company History

Milestones

1972
The Beginning
Edmund Paragon founds Paragon Development Corporation in Midtown Manhattan, completing the group's inaugural project — a twelve-unit townhouse conversion on Carnegie Hill — to critical acclaim.

1984
First Tower
Completion of 740 Fifth Avenue — Paragon's first skyscraper residential project. The 38-storey building wins the AIA Award for Excellence in Architecture and establishes Paragon as a top-tier developer.
1993
International Expansion
Opening of Paragon London — the group's first international office — with the commission of One Hyde Park Place, a 28-residence building in Knightsbridge that redefines London luxury living.

2008
Middle East Debut
Despite the global financial crisis, Paragon completes Paragon Palm — a 62-storey tower on Palm Jumeirah, Dubai — on schedule and budget. The project sells out three months before completion.
2019
$10B Milestone
Paragon crosses $10 billion in total developed value with the completion of Paragon Singapore — a 44-storey branded residence tower that becomes Singapore's highest-priced residential building per square foot.
