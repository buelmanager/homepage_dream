## Creative Concept

Paragon Development Group is conceived as the gold standard of ultra-luxury residential real estate — a company whose buildings are cultural monuments as much as residential properties. The brand references real-world precedents like Extell, Related Companies, and Vornado but sits above them in aspiration, combining New York institutional authority with European architectural refinement.

The design language draws from:
- The gravitas of major investment banks (Goldman Sachs, Lazard) — restrained typography, Onyx stone palette, no decoration
- The precision of Pritzker Prize-winning architecture firms — grid-based layouts, proportional harmony
- The discretion of private banks — understated materials, no price displays, personal relationship emphasis

---

## Hero Layout Choice — TYPE D

TYPE D (Portrait + Stats Grid) was chosen because:
1. It places architectural photography in dominant position (1.3fr), immediately establishing visual authority
2. The stats panel (0.7fr) delivers core brand credentials instantly: $12B / 48 Buildings / 12 Cities / Since 1972
3. Large Libre Baskerville numbers create an "institutional report" aesthetic appropriate for a finance-adjacent luxury brand
4. Unlike TYPE A (cinematic full-bleed), TYPE D allows information density without sacrificing visual impact

## Differentiation from Similar Templates

- Uses Libre Baskerville (editorial serif) rather than the more common Cormorant Garamond — harder, more institutional
- P8 Onyx Stone palette (--bg: #181818) is warmer and less digital than pure black
- Stats in hero use large serif numbers — feels like an annual report, not a marketing deck
- No gold — accent is --accent: #B0B0C0 (cool steel/silver) — positions above "luxury gold" cliché
- Process page has 7 detailed phases rather than generic "3 steps" — signals genuine expertise
