Our Commitment
to Discretion
Paragon has maintained a strict culture of confidentiality since 1972. The identities of our residents, the details of their acquisitions, and the contents of any communications with our team are never disclosed — under any circumstances — to any third party including the media, market analysts, or competing developers. When you contact Paragon, you are dealing directly and exclusively with Paragon. We operate no referral networks, pay no introducer commissions, and have no relationship with any data broker or marketing aggregator. Your inquiry will be seen only by the Director to whom it is assigned.
