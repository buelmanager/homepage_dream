01
Phase One

Site Curation

Paragon does not acquire sites — it curates them. Our acquisitions team evaluates over 200 potential sites annually, applying a rigorous scorecard that assesses urban significance, air rights potential, architectural opportunity, and long-term value trajectory. We advance fewer than 3% of sites reviewed.

  • 200+ sites reviewed annually worldwide
  • Urban significance and irreplaceability assessment
  • Air rights and FAR analysis
  • Long-term value trajectory modelling
  • Community and regulatory pre-consultation
02
Phase Two

Architecture & Design

Every Paragon project begins with an open competition among three to five world-ranked architecture practices. The selected team is then embedded with Paragon's design leadership for the duration — a collaborative process that typically spans 18 to 30 months before construction commences.

  • Competitive design brief among 3–5 global practices
  • Embedded architect-developer collaboration model
  • Interior design commission from separate specialist
  • Landscape architecture integrated from concept stage
  • Planning and heritage consultation process
03
Phase Three

Pre-Sales &
Private Placement

Paragon conducts all sales privately, through a dedicated private client team. There are no public sales events. Prospective residents are introduced through our global network of private banks, family offices, and discretionary investment managers — ensuring that every acquisition is made by a resident, not a speculator.

  • Private placement through global private bank network
  • No public listings or open-market sales
  • Resident vetting and community curation
  • Bespoke residence customisation programme
  • Escrow and trust structure for international buyers
04
Phase Four

Precision Construction

Paragon manages construction directly rather than through arm's-length contracts. Our in-house construction management team embeds quality control officers on every floor during fit-out — the only way to guarantee that the executed building matches the architect's vision to the millimetre.

  • In-house construction management team
  • Quality control officers embedded floor-by-floor
  • Materials sourced directly from approved quarries and mills
  • Weekly architect and client progress reviews
  • Independent building control inspection programme
05
Phase Five

Resident
Orientation

Six months before completion, each resident receives a dedicated Paragon concierge who guides them through the final customisation, completion, and move-in process. This includes furniture and art advisory, technology orientation, and introductions to the building's hospitality and lifestyle teams.

  • Dedicated pre-completion concierge assigned
  • Final room-by-room customisation review
  • Furniture, art, and object placement advisory
  • Building technology and systems orientation
  • Lifestyle and service team introductions
06
Phase Six

Building Activation

The opening of a Paragon building is a curated cultural event, not a sales launch. We commission a site-specific artwork for each lobby, host a private inauguration for residents and their guests, and engage a cultural programme director to animate the building's shared spaces throughout the year.

  • Lobby art commission from major international artist
  • Private inauguration for residents and invited guests
  • Cultural programme director engagement
  • Amenity floor and Sky Club opening
  • Annual resident cultural calendar established
07
Phase Seven

Legacy Stewardship

Paragon manages every completed building for life. Our building management teams are Paragon employees — not third-party contractors — ensuring that physical standards, resident experience, and asset values are maintained with the same vigilance applied during construction.

  • Paragon-employed building management team for life
  • Annual physical audit against original specification
  • Five-year planned maintenance capital programme
  • Resident community management and event programme
  • Asset value reporting and long-term advisory
Non-Negotiables

Our Absolute
Standards

Every Paragon building must meet these standards unconditionally. They are not aspirations — they are minimum requirements that cannot be waived by budget, timeline, or market conditions.

I
Structural Permanence

Every Paragon building is designed to stand for a minimum of 200 years. We commission independent structural engineers to validate every design before ground-breaking — and never compromise structural specifications for cost savings.

II
Material Provenance

Every primary material in a Paragon building — stone, timber, metal, glass — must come with verified provenance documentation. We work directly with quarries, forests, and foundries rather than through intermediary suppliers.

III
Acoustic Privacy

Every Paragon residence achieves a minimum 65dB sound transmission class between units — significantly above industry standard. Acoustic testing is conducted independently on completion of each floor before the next proceeds.

IV
Service Ratio

Every Paragon building maintains a minimum ratio of one dedicated staff member per four residences. This enables the hotel-standard response times and personalised service that distinguish Paragon buildings from all market alternatives.
