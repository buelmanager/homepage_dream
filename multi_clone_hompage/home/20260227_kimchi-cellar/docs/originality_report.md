## Cultural Accuracy Notes

All Korean cultural references in this template have been written with accuracy in mind:

- **Kimjang UNESCO recognition:** Correctly dated 2013; accurately described as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity
- **Onggi pottery:** Correctly described as Korean earthenware, fired at 1200°C, traditionally porous
- **Son-mat:** Korean concept accurately described — "the taste of the maker's hand"
- **Yangnyeom ingredients:** Gochugaru, saeujeot, maesil cheong, pullcha — all authentic kimchi components with real provenance details
- **Jeonju:** Correctly identified as North Jeolla Province, capital of Korean food culture
- **West Sea salt:** Sinan County, South Jeolla — real salt production region
- **Gochugaru origin:** Yeongyang County, North Gyeongsang — real premium gochugaru production area
- **Cellar temperature:** 4–8°C cited — accurate for traditional kimchi fermentation
- **Lactic acid bacteria:** Lactobacillus sakei, L. plantarum, Leuconostoc mesenteroides — accurate kimchi fermentation microbiota
