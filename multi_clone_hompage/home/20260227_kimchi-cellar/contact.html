Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How long does ONGGI kimchi last once opened?
Refrigerated in the ceramic jar with the lid sealed, opened kimchi retains its optimal flavour for 2–4 weeks. The kimchi will continue to ferment slowly and become more sour — this is natural and desirable. At 4–6 weeks it becomes mukeunji (aged sour kimchi), ideal for cooking rather than eating fresh. It does not spoil.
Do you offer vegan kimchi options?
Most traditional kimchi contains saeujeot (fermented shrimp) and is therefore not suitable for vegans. We produce a limited vegan baechu kimchi substituting saeujeot with myeolchi aekjeot-free paste and sunflower seed powder for umami. Please note this in your order enquiry. Our Nabak Kimchi and Baek Kimchi are also naturally vegan.
Can I visit without a reservation?
No. All cellar visits, tours, and workshops require advance reservation — minimum 5 days before the desired date. The cellar is an active production facility and tour groups are kept to a maximum of 8 visitors. Walk-in visitors cannot be accommodated. Kimchi purchases without a cellar visit can be arranged by phone during opening hours.
What is the minimum order for wholesale supply?
Wholesale relationships require a minimum of 50 jars (500g) per month, sustained supply for a minimum of 3 months. We supply a small number of selected restaurants in Seoul, Busan, and Jeonju. A tasting visit to the cellar is required before we establish any wholesale relationship. Please enquire via wholesale@onggi-cellar.kr.
How do I store my kimchi jar before opening?
Sealed jars can be refrigerated for up to 6 months without quality loss. Do not store at room temperature — fermentation will accelerate and the jar may build pressure. If you wish to age the kimchi further before opening, we recommend our refrigerator storage guide (included with every order). The optimal temperature for slow home aging is 2–4°C.
Is the cellar experience suitable for children?
Children aged 8 and above are welcome on cellar tours with accompanying adults. The workshop is recommended for participants 14 and above. The cellar descent requires navigating steep stone steps with limited handrailing — participants should be able to walk unaided. Please inform us of any mobility considerations when booking.
