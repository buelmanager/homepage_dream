Our Story

Born from a military pilot's dream and shaped by 22 years of flawless skies — ZEPHYR is the world's most trusted luxury balloon company.

1998

The Beginning

From the Cockpit
to the Sky

In 1998, after twelve years navigating military airspace, Captain James Hartley traded his flight suit for a burner and a wicker basket. His mission was unchanged: to take people safely to extraordinary places. His theater shifted from conflict to wonder.

Starting with a single balloon over Cappadocia's fairy chimneys, James built ZEPHYR on three uncompromising principles: absolute safety, unmatched comfort, and experiences that transcend time.

Today, ZEPHYR operates a fleet of three custom-designed balloons across six continents, carrying discerning guests who understand that true luxury is not about speed — it is about stillness, perspective, and the profound beauty that exists above the clouds.

Every morning we lift off, we do so with the same reverence for the sky that James carried on that first Cappadocia dawn. Some things should never change.
