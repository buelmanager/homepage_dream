Getting Started

Reserving Your Flight

Three simple steps separate you from a ZEPHYR experience. Our reservations team handles everything else.

1
Submit Your Enquiry

Complete our reservation form or call our team directly. Tell us your preferred destination, experience type, group size, and ideal dates. No payment is required at this stage.

2
We Confirm & Plan

Within 24 hours, your personal reservations coordinator contacts you to confirm availability, discuss customisation options, and provide a full itinerary. Your experience is tailored to you.

3
Secure Your Date

A 25% deposit secures your flight date. Full payment is due 30 days before your experience. We hold all funds in escrow until 24 hours before departure — your investment is fully protected.

Be Prepared

What to Bring

Very little is needed. ZEPHYR provides almost everything. Here is what we recommend.

◎
Layered Clothing

Pre-dawn temperatures can be 5–10°C cooler than midday. Bring a light jacket — it can always be removed. We provide lightweight blankets on request.

◎
Comfortable Footwear

Closed-toe shoes with flat soles are required for boarding. The basket rail requires you to step over approximately 60cm — flexibility is appreciated.

◎
Camera or Phone

The views are extraordinary. We encourage photography at every stage. A wide-angle lens is ideal for capturing the full panorama at altitude.

◎
Photo ID

Required for all aviation operations per international regulations. A valid passport or government-issued ID must be presented at the briefing.

◎
Open Mind

The weather shapes every flight. No two ZEPHYR journeys are identical. Some of the most extraordinary moments arrive unplanned — trust the sky.

◎
Nothing Else

Champagne, food, transport, certificates, and crew — all provided. ZEPHYR is designed to remove every burden. Arrive with curiosity alone.

Common Questions

What Guests Ask

Is hot air ballooning safe?
ZEPHYR has operated 12,000+ flights over 22 years with zero safety incidents. All pilots are FAA and EASA dual-certified. We conduct rigorous pre-flight weather checks and never fly in conditions that do not meet our strict safety criteria — which exceed all regulatory minimums.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Your safety is always our priority. If conditions are unsuitable on your flight day, we contact you before dawn to reschedule or offer a full refund. There is never a penalty for weather cancellations. Our rebooking guarantee allows you to rebook within 12 months at no additional cost.
Are there weight or height restrictions?
For safety, maximum individual weight is 130kg (290 lbs). The basket railing stands at approximately 1.2m — guests of all heights are accommodated comfortably. Please notify us of any mobility considerations at booking so we can make appropriate arrangements.
Can I bring children?
Children aged 6 and above are welcome on Sunrise Champagne and Group flights. The minimum age for Private Charter flights is 8. Children must be able to stand unassisted for the duration of the flight. Non-alcoholic champagne alternatives are always provided.
How far in advance should I book?
Peak season flights (spring and autumn) book out 8–12 weeks in advance. We recommend booking 4–6 weeks ahead minimum. Private Charters in prime destinations (Cappadocia, Provence) can require 3 months advance booking. Contact us as early as possible to secure preferred dates.
Can I propose during the flight?
Absolutely. Private Charter flights are perfectly suited for proposals. We coordinate discreetly with you in advance — flowers, ring hiding, photographer placement, and post-flight arrangements. Contact our concierge team when booking to begin the planning.
