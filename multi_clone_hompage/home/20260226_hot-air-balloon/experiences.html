Sunrise Champagne

The Classic ZEPHYR Experience

The most celebrated of all our experiences. Board at pre-dawn, ascend through twilight, and witness the sun's first light paint the landscape amber. Every moment is paired with Billecart-Salmon Blanc de Blancs, served precisely at 8°C.
