Your Journey

What to Expect

From the moment you arrive to the final champagne toast, every detail is attended to.

01
Pre-Dawn Arrival

Your private driver collects you from your accommodation before dawn. Coffee and warm pastries are served as you travel to the launch site. No rushing — ZEPHYR's timing is calibrated to the sunrise, not a timetable.

5:00 – 5:30 AM
02
Safety Briefing & Inflation

Your pilot conducts a detailed, unhurried safety briefing covering all procedures. Watch as the balloon inflates dramatically against the pre-dawn sky — one of the most spectacular sights before the flight even begins.

5:30 – 6:00 AM
03
Ascent & Flight

You lift off silently into the dawn sky. Your pilot guides the balloon with expert precision, ascending and descending to give you the best views and wildlife encounters. Champagne is uncorked at altitude as the landscape awakens below.

6:00 – 9:00 AM
04
Gentle Landing

Your pilot chooses the optimal landing site based on wind conditions. The ground crew follows from below and assists with your return to earth. A traditional ballooning ceremony marks the occasion with your flight certificate.

9:00 – 9:30 AM
05
Champagne Celebration

Return to the launch estate for a full post-flight breakfast or champagne brunch. Receive your personalised flight certificate engraved with altitude, coordinates, and date. Your private transfer returns you to your accommodation at leisure.

9:30 – 11:00 AM

Our Commitment

Weather & Cancellation Policy

We never fly in unsafe conditions. Our weather policy is built on complete transparency and zero compromise.

⛅
Three Daily Weather Checks

Our meteorological team conducts three weather assessments per flight day: 48 hours prior, 24 hours prior, and a final determination at 5am on flight morning by the Chief Pilot.

✦
Full Refund Guarantee

If ZEPHYR cancels your flight for any weather-related reason, you receive a 100% refund or complimentary rebooking within 12 months. No administration fees. No questions asked.

★
Flight Conditions Required

We require wind speeds below 25 km/h, visibility exceeding 5 km, no precipitation, and clear thunderstorm margins. These are FAA and EASA minimum requirements — we exceed all of them.
