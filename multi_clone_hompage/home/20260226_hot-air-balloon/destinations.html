Cappadocia
Göreme, Turkey
About This Destination

The world's most iconic balloon landscape. Rising over Cappadocia's fairy chimneys, cave valleys, and ancient volcanic formations at dawn is an experience that redefines what it means to witness beauty. ZEPHYR has operated here since 1998 — our original home.

Best Season

April – June and September – November. Cool, stable mornings with exceptional visibility across the volcanic plateau.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
3–15 km/h ideal
Altitude
Up to 4,000 ft
Visibility
Typically 50+ km
Temperature
8–18°C at altitude
Local Highlights
Göreme Open Air Museum — Byzantine rock churches
Underground cities of Derinkuyu and Kaymaklı
Cave hotel stays in Ürgüp and Uçhisar
Turkish pottery and ceramic workshops in Avanos
Book Cappadocia
Provence
Luberon, France
About This Destination

Endless lavender fields, medieval hilltop villages, and vineyard-stitched valleys — Provence from above is a painting come to life. Our Chief Pilot Élodie Beaumont is both a licensed pilot and certified sommelier, weaving terroir stories into every ascent.

Best Season

June – July for lavender bloom peak. May and September for exceptional flying conditions and golden agricultural landscapes.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
5–18 km/h ideal
Altitude
Up to 5,500 ft
Visibility
Typically 40+ km
Temperature
12–22°C at altitude
Local Highlights
Gordes and Les Baux-de-Provence — perched medieval villages
Abbaye de Sénanque surrounded by lavender
Luberon wine estates and Châteauneuf-du-Pape
Open-air markets in Apt and Roussillon
Book Provence
Napa Valley
California, USA
About This Destination

The American West's most celebrated wine country. Drifting over Napa's vine rows and rolling hills at golden hour, with a glass of local Cabernet Sauvignon in hand, is the definitive California luxury experience.

Best Season

March – October. Summer and early fall provide the most stable morning thermals. Harvest season (September) offers extraordinary aerial views of the vintage.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
5–20 km/h ideal
Altitude
Up to 3,500 ft
Visibility
Typically 60+ km
Temperature
10–25°C at altitude
Local Highlights
Private wine tastings at Opus One and Dominus
The French Laundry — three Michelin star dining in Yountville
Castello di Amorosa medieval castle winery
Artisan food tours through St. Helena
Book Napa Valley
Serengeti
Tanzania
About This Destination

From above, the Great Migration is unlike anything else on Earth — a golden river of wildebeest flowing across an infinite plain as the African sun rises. ZEPHYR's thermal specialist Captain Tariq Al-Rashid guides you precisely where the herds move.

Best Season

June – October for the Great Migration through the northern Serengeti. January – March for calving season in the southern plains.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
8–22 km/h ideal
Altitude
300–1,200 ft low drift
Visibility
Typically 80+ km
Temperature
20–32°C at launch
Local Highlights
Great Migration — over 1.5 million wildebeest
Big Five sightings from above — lion, elephant, leopard
Maasai village cultural immersion
Ngorongoro Crater private safari extension
Book Serengeti
Masai Mara
Kenya
About This Destination

Kenya's crown jewel of wildlife reserves. The Mara's open grasslands afford extraordinary low-altitude balloon drift alongside elephants, cheetahs, and the dramatic wildebeest river crossings at the Mara River. One of Earth's last great wildlife spectacles.

Best Season

July – October for wildebeest crossings. December – March for dry season clarity and exceptional game density. Year-round for predator activity.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
5–18 km/h ideal
Altitude
150–800 ft low drift
Visibility
Typically 70+ km
Temperature
18–28°C at launch
Local Highlights
Mara River wildebeest crossings — one of nature's great dramas
Cheetah and lion prides on open Mara plains
Maasai warrior cultural experiences
Luxury tented camp stays at Angama Mara
Book Masai Mara
Loire Valley
Pays de la Loire, France
About This Destination

The Garden of France, as seen from above. The Loire Valley's UNESCO-listed châteaux, ancient forests, and river meanders create an aerial tapestry of exceptional elegance. Champagne de Loire is served as you drift over Château de Chambord.

Best Season

May – June and September for the finest château visibility and stable morning air. July for sunflower fields in full bloom beneath the basket.

Flight Conditions
Wind Speed
5–15 km/h ideal
Altitude
Up to 5,000 ft
Visibility
Typically 45+ km
Temperature
10–20°C at altitude
Local Highlights
Château de Chambord — world's most ornate French Renaissance castle
Château de Villandry's extraordinary formal gardens
Cave-aged Sancerre and Vouvray wine cellars
Royal troglodyte cave dwellings and mushroom farms
Book Loire Valley
