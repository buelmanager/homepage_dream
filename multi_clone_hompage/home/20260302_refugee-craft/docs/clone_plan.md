# Clone Plan — Artisan Bridge

## Design Direction
A rich, warm terracotta luxury page evoking traditional craft, aged wood, and fire-baked ceramics. The deep brown-amber palette (#1C1008 bg, #D4612A accent) references both the organic materials of craft and the heat of the kiln. Cinzel's classical lines evoke permanence and cultural weight; Crimson Pro provides warmth and readability for body content.

## Hero Approach
Type C diagonal split. The left panel holds the brand identity and call-to-action against the dark background. The right panel shows a high-quality craft/textile photograph with a gentle brightness reduction. The diagonal clip-path creates dynamic tension between text and image.

## Content Strategy
- Stats center on human impact and cultural breadth (340 artisans, 28 traditions, 86%, 14 countries)
- Philosophy positions artisans as masters, not beneficiaries
- Craft disciplines presented with cultural specificity and respect
- Process framed as a pathway to independence, not dependency
- Heritage timeline from 2016 church hall to 2025 three-city program
- Press quotes from migration studies, craft council, and design press
