The Disciplines

All Craft Traditions

Textile
Flatweave Loom Textiles
Traditional flatweave techniques from Anatolian, Kurdish, and Central Asian traditions, including kilim, soumak, and weft-wrapped patterning.
Embroidery
Heritage Needlework
Cross-stitch, goldwork, and silk threadwork drawing on South Asian, North African, and Eastern European embroidery traditions.
Ceramics
Handbuilt Earthenware
Terracotta and glazed vessels in Moroccan, Syrian, and West African ceramic traditions. Wood-fired kiln shared across the collective.
Woodcraft
Carved & Inlaid Woodwork
Geometric carving, marquetry, and latticework in moucharabieh and mashrabiya traditions. Also contemporary furniture from traditional techniques.
Textile
Natural Dye Practice
Plant-based dye techniques using both local and homeland botanicals. Integrates color theory with ecological and cultural knowledge of dye plants.
Mixed Media
Contemporary Craft Fusion
An advanced track for graduates who wish to explore how traditional techniques can be adapted to contemporary design contexts and markets.
