Our Philosophy

Wine as Living Knowledge

Three pillars that define the Clos d'Or approach to wine education.

🍷

Tasting First

Wine is experienced before it is explained. Every programme at Clos d'Or places the glass before the textbook. Sensory training — learning to identify aromas, structure, and quality — is the foundation of everything we teach.

🗺

Terroir & Place

Wine is inseparable from its origin. We teach through geography, geology, and viticulture — ensuring students understand why Burgundy smells different from Barossa and what makes Champagne impossible to replicate elsewhere.

📜

Examination Excellence

Clos d'Or boasts the highest pass rate of any approved wine education provider globally. Our structured approach to examination preparation has produced more Master Sommeliers and Masters of Wine than any comparable institution.

Our Cave

A Cave for
Serious Study

Our Bordeaux headquarters — a nineteenth-century cave à vin in the Saint-Julien appellation — houses over 14,000 bottles and a purpose-built tasting room designed for systematic blind tasting practice.

"To understand wine is to understand time, climate, soil, and the quiet genius of the winemaker."

The cave serves as both classroom and cellar. Students taste at temperature, alongside the library vintages that allow vertical comparisons across three decades. No other wine school offers this depth of tasting access.

Getting Started

Begin Your Wine Journey

01

Level Assessment

Complete our online level assessment — a 20-question sensory and theoretical quiz that places you accurately within our programme pathway. No preparation required; honest answers give the best result.

02

Programme Consultation

A 30-minute call with one of our Masters of Wine to discuss your goals — whether professional certification, personal enrichment, or examination preparation — and confirm the right starting point.

03

Introductory Tasting

Join a complimentary one-hour introductory tasting session at your nearest campus. Taste six wines guided by a sommelier and experience the Clos d'Or method before committing to a full programme.

04

Enrolment & Materials

Complete enrolment online. Your welcome kit — including your tasting notebook, Riedel tasting glass, and study materials — is dispatched before your first session.

05

First Tasting Class

Every programme begins with a two-hour structured tasting session. Six wines, the Clos d'Or tasting method, and the foundation vocabulary of serious wine appreciation. The glass is always in your hand before the lecture begins.

Our History

Forty-Two Years of Wine Scholarship

1984

Founded in Bordeaux

Master of Wine Sylvie Leconte establishes Clos d'Or in Saint-Julien to train winemakers' children and château staff in the formal language of wine appreciation and service.

1997

WSET Accreditation

Clos d'Or receives its WSET Approved Programme Provider status, becoming one of the first French institutions to offer internationally recognised wine qualifications.

2009

Hong Kong Campus

A third campus opens in Hong Kong's Central district, serving the rapidly growing Asia-Pacific market and establishing the Academy's Cantonese and Mandarin study programmes.

2021

MW Pass Record

Clos d'Or students achieve a 94% pass rate across all WSET levels — the highest recorded pass rate in the institution's history and among the highest of any WSET provider globally.
