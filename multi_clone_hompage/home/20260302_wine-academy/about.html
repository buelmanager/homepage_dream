Clos d'Or Wine Academy

The Language of Wine

Clos d'Or Wine Academy has trained wine professionals and passionate enthusiasts since 1984 from our historic cave in the Saint-Julien appellation of Bordeaux. Our eight Masters of Wine bring decades of combined expertise to every programme.

We offer WSET certified courses from Foundation through Diploma, Court of Master Sommeliers examination preparation, and our celebrated Bordeaux and Burgundy estate immersion programmes.
