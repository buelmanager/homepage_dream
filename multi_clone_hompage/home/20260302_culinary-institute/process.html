Admissions

How to Join
the Institute

We look for people with a genuine relationship to food — not necessarily professional experience. What we cannot teach is curiosity, appetite, and seriousness of purpose. Everything else is learnable.

The Process

Five Steps to
the Kitchen

01

Application Form

Complete the online application with your personal details, program preference, and a culinary background statement. Tell us your relationship to food: where you grew up eating, who taught you, what dishes you are most proud of, and what you find hardest.

02

Application Review

The admissions team reviews all applications. We are looking for genuine engagement with food, not credentials. We typically select 50–60% of applicants for the tasting interview.

Decision within three weeks
03

Tasting Interview

A half-day at the Institut. You will cook one dish of your choice — anything you can make well and want to talk about. The faculty tastes and discusses the dish with you. We want to understand how you think about what you made.

In person at the Institut, Meursault
04

Kitchen Skills Assessment

For the Grand Diplôme programmes, a practical session follows the tasting: basic knife skills, mise en place speed, and a simple sauce. We are not looking for perfection — we are looking for hand-eye coordination, focus, and the capacity to receive instruction.

05

Offer & Enrolment

Successful candidates receive a formal offer within one week. A deposit of €2,500 secures your place. We will send a full pre-arrival guide including equipment list, accommodation options near the Institut, and reading recommendations.
