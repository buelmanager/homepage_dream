Cairngorm Wilderness — The Ancient Forest

Covering 1.2 million acres of boreal forest, peat bog, and mountain plateau, Cairngorm Wilderness is the largest protected wild area in the country. Ancient Caledonian pines, golden eagles, and wild reindeer make this one of Europe's most important wildlife landscapes.

Open year-round. Advance booking required for overnight trails. Ranger-led tours available daily in season.
