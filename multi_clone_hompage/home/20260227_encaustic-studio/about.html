ABOUT
The Artist

Rooted in
Ancient Fire

CEREUS is the practice of a single artist — a lifelong student of the Fayum tradition who works with the same materials and the same intentions as the painters of Roman Egypt. Every work is made by hand, one layer at a time.

Studio, 2025

Cereus

Artist Statement

Working With
Living Material

Beeswax is not a passive material. It holds temperature, changes with atmosphere, and responds to the human hand in ways that oil and acrylic cannot. When I first encountered the Fayum portraits in the British Museum, I felt an immediate recognition — not of style, but of intention. Here was a medium that had been used to make the ephemeral permanent, to hold the face of a loved one against the erosion of time.

I spent years studying the technical literature: how the ancient painters blended wax with damar resin, how they used metal tools heated over coals, how each layer was fused before the next was applied. Then I began to practice — slowly, obsessively, and with a growing conviction that this medium was still alive, still capable of new expression.

"Encaustic forces patience. You cannot rush the fusing. You cannot correct a mistake by painting over it without understanding the wax below. It teaches you to work with time, not against it."

The works I make now belong to two worlds simultaneously: the deep past of the Fayum tradition, and the present moment of light and sensation in the studio. I do not try to resolve that tension. I think it is where the work lives.

Materials

The Same Three
Things, Always

Encaustic painting uses exactly three materials. Their interaction, under heat, produces infinite variation. Nothing synthetic is introduced. Nothing changes between a CEREUS panel made today and one made in Roman Egypt.

🐝

Beeswax

Cold-pressed beeswax from organic apiaries, unbleached and unprocessed. The wax carries the natural honey and pollen tones of the hive, contributing warmth to every palette. It is the primary binding and layering medium.

🌲

Damar Resin

Crystalline resin from the damar tree, dissolved into the wax to raise its hardness and melting point. The ratio of damar to wax determines the working properties of each batch — a knowledge passed down through centuries of practice.

🪖

Mineral Pigments

Forty dry mineral and earth pigments — ochre, cobalt, verdigris, burnt sienna, lapis lazuli, malachite. No synthetic dyes. Each pigment has a distinct texture, weight, and behavior in the wax, requiring individual calibration.
