Commissions

How a Commission Works

01

Initial Conversation

We begin with an email or studio visit to understand what you are looking for — subject, scale, intended space, and any specific reference points. There is no commitment required at this stage.

02

Proposal and Agreement

The studio prepares a written proposal including scale, estimated timeline, and fee. A 50% deposit secures the commission. The remaining balance is due upon completion, before delivery.

03

Studio Development

The artist works on the panel, sharing progress photographs at key stages. You are welcome to provide feedback during the process. The medium requires patience — complex encaustic works take six to fourteen weeks.

04

Delivery and Installation

Completed works are professionally packed in custom-built crates. We can arrange delivery anywhere in the world, and offer installation guidance for all works. A certificate of authenticity and care instructions are included.
