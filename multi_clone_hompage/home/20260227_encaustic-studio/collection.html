Learn the Method

Workshops

Intensive studio workshops taught directly by the CEREUS artist. Small groups only — maximum six participants — ensuring individual instruction in the ancient technique.

Foundation

Introduction to Encaustic

A one-day immersion in the medium. Learn to prepare wax, suspend pigment, and fuse your first layers. All materials provided. No prior experience required.

Duration: 1 day, 10am–5pm Group Size: Max 6 participants Materials: All included
Reserve a Place
Intermediate

Fayum Portrait Method

A three-day intensive focused on the Fayum portrait tradition. Study original sources, learn gold-ground technique, and complete a panel in the ancient manner.

Duration: 3 days Group Size: Max 4 participants Materials: All included
Reserve a Place
Advanced

Personal Practice Residency

A five-day one-to-one studio residency. Work on your own project under close mentorship, using the full CEREUS studio and pigment library.

Duration: 5 days Group Size: 1 participant Materials: All included
