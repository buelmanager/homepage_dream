# Clone Plan — VOLDUR Mountain Forge

## Industry Research
Alpine blacksmithing / blade craft. Reference: Swiss knife and blade tradition, Japanese tamahagane steel, Norwegian/Scandinavian iron-age forging heritage.

## Unique Concept Differentiators
1. Altitude as a manufacturing advantage (not just setting)
2. Glacial quench as proprietary technique
3. Single-maker philosophy (no division of labor)
4. Documented ore provenance
5. Lifetime maintenance guarantee

## Structure Map
- Hero: Type B Parallax — dual-layer mountain photography
- Stats: elevation, years, blades, nations
- Philosophy: 3-card grid (Ore, Quench, Maker)
- Collection: 4-item 2x2 grid with hover overlay
- Atelier: alternating image+text with parallax
- Process: vertical numbered timeline 5 steps
- Heritage: 4-col year milestones
- Press: Swiper 3-card quotes
- Commission: 2-col form + info
- Footer: 4-col standard

## Design Decisions
- Onyx palette chosen for industrial-luxury forge aesthetic
- Bebas Neue chosen for its industrial condensed impact
- Hero overlay gradient at 60% opacity to preserve image atmosphere
- Float badge for award recognition — bottom right hero
