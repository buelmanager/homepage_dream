About the Museum

160 years on the waterfront — collecting, conserving, and celebrating the history of seafaring and the sea.

Founded 1865

A Heritage Built on the Tide

Admiralty Maritime Museum was founded in 1865 by a coalition of naval officers, merchant captains, and civic leaders who believed that the nation's maritime heritage was too important to be left to chance. The founding collection — 2,400 artefacts gifted by the Admiralty — represented the golden age of British naval power and the age of steam.

Over 160 years, we have grown from a single warehouse on the harbour wharf into the world's leading maritime heritage institution. Our 24 historic vessels, 80,000 artefacts, and 1 million archive documents tell the story of how the sea shaped the nation — commercially, culturally, and strategically.

Today we welcome 850,000 visitors annually, support 300 researchers, and serve a global online audience of millions through our digital collection.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

01

Preservation

We hold and maintain the nation's maritime heritage in trust for all future generations, applying world-leading conservation expertise to 80,000 objects and 24 historic vessels.

02

Scholarship

Original research drives everything we do — our curators publish 40+ scholarly works annually, and we host international symposia that advance the field of maritime history.

03

Education

We inspire the next generation of maritime historians and conservationists through school programmes, apprenticeships, and our internationally recognised Vessels conservation training course.

04

Access

Children under 16 enter free. Subsidised school visits. Our complete digital collection is freely available online — maritime heritage belongs to everyone.
