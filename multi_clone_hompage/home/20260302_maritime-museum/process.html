Heritage & Practice

Our Story

How we collect, conserve, research, and interpret five centuries of seafaring heritage for every visitor and future generation.

How We Work

The Maritime Heritage Process

From a ship's logbook to a full-scale warship, every artefact in our care follows the same journey — from acquisition and conservation to research, interpretation, and display. Our approach is driven by scholarship and shaped by the stories of real people who lived at sea.

◈

Acquisition & Conservation

We acquire artefacts through purchase, donation, and international collaboration. Every object is assessed for historical significance, conserved in our specialist studios, and registered in our collection database.

◉

Research & Documentation

Our curators conduct original research on every object — establishing its provenance, historical context, and the human stories it represents. This scholarship underpins every gallery and digital interpretation.

◌

Display & Engagement

Objects are installed in purpose-designed gallery environments or aboard their historic vessels, with interpretation designed to bring the stories of seafaring to life for every visitor.

Vessel Conservation

Keeping History Afloat

Conserving 24 historic vessels — the oldest built in 1623 — is the most demanding work we undertake. Our shipwright and conservation teams carry out constant maintenance, using traditional techniques where appropriate and modern materials science where necessary.

Our vessel conservation training programme is internationally recognised, attracting students from maritime heritage institutions worldwide and training the next generation of wooden vessel specialists.

Education

Inspiring Maritime Futures

School Programmes

Curriculum-linked visits for primary and secondary schools, including hands-on navigation workshops, living history sessions, and below-decks vessel tours.

Academic Research

We host 300+ researchers annually, providing access to the archive, collection study rooms, and our specialist maritime library of 40,000 volumes and 1 million documents.

Public Lectures

A year-round programme of free and ticketed lectures by leading maritime historians, naval architects, oceanographers, and conservators.

Digital Learning

Our online education platform provides free access to maritime history resources for teachers, students, and self-learners worldwide.
