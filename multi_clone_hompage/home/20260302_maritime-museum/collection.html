Historic Vessels

24 Ships — Living History

Our fleet of 24 historic vessels — from a 17th-century pinnace to a 20th-century destroyer — are moored in the Great Dock and harbour basin for visitor exploration. Board HMS Resolute, a 74-gun ship of the line, and descend below decks to experience life in the Georgian navy first-hand.

Guided vessel tours available daily. Last entry 30 minutes before closing.
