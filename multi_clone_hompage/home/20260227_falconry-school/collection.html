Four Disciplines

Each program is built on the same three-thousand-year lineage. The bird, the terrain, and the pace change. The discipline does not.

Foundation — Course 01
Beginner
Course

An immersive three-day introduction to the ancient art. You will learn the foundational vocabulary of the glove — manning, weighing, feeding, equipment fitting, and your first lure flights with a Harris Hawk under master supervision. No prior experience required or assumed.

Duration 3 Days
Group Size 2–4 Students
Prerequisite None
What's Included
  • Daily morning and afternoon sessions with allocated bird
  • Full equipment provision — glove, lure, jesses, hood
  • One-to-one ratio of student to supervising falconer for lure work
  • TALON Introductory Certificate upon completion
  • Lunch at the estate house daily

Advanced — Course 02
Advanced
Hawking

For those who have completed a foundation course elsewhere or with us. A week of intensive field work across moorland and forest, flying Peregrines and Red-tailed Hawks. Hood training, telemetry operation, and free flight at altitude above 400 metres. Demanding, precise, exceptional.

Duration 7 Days
Group Size 1–2 Students
Prerequisite Prior Experience
What's Included
  • Full week residential on the Aldric Estate (lodging not included)
  • Access to our Peregrine and Red-tail flight roster
  • Telemetry equipment training and field use
  • Hood training and equipment maintenance sessions
  • TALON Advanced Certificate upon completion

Experience — Course 03
Medieval
Hunt

A full-day excursion into the tradition of medieval hawking. Period dress is provided. A mounted escort leads the party across the estate while Golden Eagles and Red Kites are flown in sequence. The hunt as it was conducted in the courts of Henry II, enacted with complete authenticity. Ideal for corporate retreats, private celebrations, or the discerning individual.

Duration Full Day
Group Size Up to 12
Prerequisite None
What's Included
  • Period costume fitting on arrival (authentic reproductions)
  • Mounted escort and estate carriage transport
  • Golden Eagle and Red Kite flight demonstrations
  • Medieval banquet lunch in the great hall
  • Commemorative manuscript and wax seal certificate

The Raptors

Birds of the Mews

Our 34 birds span four primary training categories. Each species demands a distinct approach, a distinct relationship, a distinct art.

◈
Harris Hawk
Parabuteo unicinctus

The ideal first bird. Social, trainable, and forgiving of beginner error without sacrificing the full dignity of the art. Ideal for foundation courses and the early phases of private training.

◈
Peregrine
Falco peregrinus

The fastest animal on earth. A Peregrine in stoop is falconry in its most distilled form — precision, power, and speed in a body weighing less than a kilogram. Reserved for advanced students.

◈
Golden Eagle
Aquila chrysaetos

The bird of kings and emperors. Our three Golden Eagles are flown exclusively in the Medieval Hunt Experience. They require a specialist handler and are not subject to student training programmes.

◈
Gyrfalcon
Falco rusticolus

The highest bird in medieval rank — reserved by law for kings. Our two Gyrfalcons are available only through private sessions and represent the summit of the falconer's ambition in long-wing discipline.
