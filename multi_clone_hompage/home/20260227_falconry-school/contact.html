Before You Enroll

Common Questions

The Beginner Course, Medieval Hunt Experience, and Private Sessions are all open to complete beginners. The Advanced Hawking programme requires prior falconry experience — either through our Beginner Course or demonstrable experience elsewhere. We assess this at the enquiry stage and will advise if a foundation course is recommended before advancing.
Participants must be 14 years of age or older for all training programmes. For the Medieval Hunt Experience, children aged 8 and above may attend as observers. Participants under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times. All participants must complete our health declaration prior to the session.
Falconry is conducted in all conditions — hawks fly in rain and cold with no adverse effect and some species perform better in overcast conditions. Only extreme wind, lightning, or temperatures below -10°C will cause a session cancellation. In such cases, we reschedule at no additional charge. Our estate buildings provide covered working areas for equipment sessions and hood training regardless of weather.
Falconry is accessible to most levels of physical fitness. Carrying a bird on the fist requires arm strength and stability — this develops naturally during manning sessions. Field work involves walking over uneven terrain. Participants with mobility concerns should indicate this on their enquiry form and we will design a programme suited to their capabilities. We have hosted students with a range of physical conditions and adapt accordingly.
TALON does not sell birds directly. However, we maintain relationships with licensed breeders and can make introductions to reputable sources for Harris Hawks, Red-tailed Hawks, and certain falcon species. Private Session students who complete our ownership preparation pathway receive a letter of recommendation and ongoing advisory support in their first year of ownership.
The estate is located in Northumberland, approximately 45 minutes north of Newcastle upon Tyne. Full directions are provided with your booking confirmation. The nearest train station is Alnmouth (National Rail). We can arrange private transfer from Alnmouth or Newcastle Airport on request. A small car park is available on site for self-drive arrivals.
