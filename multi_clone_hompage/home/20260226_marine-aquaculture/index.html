Ocean Restoration

Our kelp and seaweed farms create underwater nurseries that support juvenile fish populations across regional ecosystems.

Carbon Sequestration

Bivalves and macroalgae naturally absorb atmospheric carbon — our farms function as living carbon sinks at scale.

Zero-Waste Processing

Fish byproducts become fertilizer, shells are ground into calcium supplements, and processing water is triple-filtered before return.
