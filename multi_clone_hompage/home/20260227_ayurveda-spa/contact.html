Common Questions

Answers for
New Guests

Is the first consultation really complimentary?

Yes. We believe that the relationship between vaidya and patient is the most important factor in healing. We offer a 30-minute complimentary pulse consultation to all new guests so that you can experience our approach before making any commitment.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable, loose clothing. Avoid heavy meals for two hours before treatment and avoid strong perfumes or essential oils. We provide everything needed for your session, including organic cotton wraps and robes.

Can I come if I am pregnant?

Some Ayurvedic treatments are specifically designed for pregnancy and are profoundly supportive. Others are contraindicated. Please inform us during booking and your vaidya will design an appropriate, safe program for your stage of pregnancy.

How far in advance should I book?

For single treatments, 3-7 days is usually sufficient. For residential programs, we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance as places are limited. For Panchakarma programs, please allow 6-8 weeks to allow for preparatory guidance.

Do you offer international consultations?

We offer online pulse consultations (via video) for international guests who wish to begin their Ayurvedic journey before visiting in person. We can prescribe herbal formulations for international shipping through our partner apothecary.

What makes Vaidya different from a typical spa?

Every treatment at Vaidya is medically prescribed by a qualified Ayurvedic physician, not simply chosen from a menu. Our therapists operate under direct vaidya supervision. All formulations are prepared in-house or sourced from classical Keralan pharmacies.
