The Six Steps

01
Foundation
Nadi Pariksha — Pulse Consultation

Your journey begins where Ayurveda begins — with pulse. Nadi Pariksha is the ancient art of reading the three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) through three fingers on the radial pulse. A skilled vaidya can detect imbalances years before they manifest as symptoms, reading not only the doshas but also the state of the seven dhatus (tissues), the condition of Agni (digestive fire), and the presence of Ama (accumulated toxins).

This initial session lasts forty-five minutes to one hour. Your vaidya will ask detailed questions about your sleep, digestion, emotional life, physical symptoms, and history. Nothing is too small or irrelevant — in Ayurveda, the whole person is always the patient.

Duration45 – 60 min
CostComplimentary
IncludesPulse + Tongue + Facial Diagnosis
02
Understanding
Prakriti and Vikriti Assessment

Ayurveda makes a critical distinction between Prakriti — your inherent constitutional nature, fixed at conception — and Vikriti, your current state of imbalance. Understanding both is essential for designing effective treatment. You might have a Pitta Prakriti but a current Vata Vikriti, meaning treatments must address the present disturbance without aggravating your underlying nature.

This comprehensive assessment includes evaluation of your body type, skin, hair, digestion, sleep patterns, emotional tendencies, stress responses, and seasonal sensitivities. The resulting clarity guides every aspect of your treatment, diet, and lifestyle recommendations.

ToolConstitutional Questionnaire
MethodPhysical Examination
OutputWritten Report
03
Design
Personalized Treatment Protocol

Based on your assessment, your vaidya designs a comprehensive healing protocol. This is not a menu selection — it is a clinical prescription, written with the same precision as any medical document, specifying not only which treatments but in which sequence, at what intensity, with which herbal preparations, and for how long.

The protocol encompasses treatments, customized herbal formulations, Ayurvedic diet guidelines, daily routine (Dinacharya) recommendations, and seasonal practices. You receive a written copy along with clear explanations of why each element has been chosen for your unique situation.

DeliveryWritten Protocol
IncludesHerbs + Diet + Routine
RevisedAfter Each Session
04
Preparation
Purvakarma — Opening the Pathways

Before any cleansing or deep treatment can occur, the body must be prepared. Purvakarma — preparatory procedures — uses two primary tools: Snehana (internal and external oleation with medicated ghees and oils) and Svedana (herbalized steam therapy). Together, these procedures soften accumulated toxins, lubricate the channels, and mobilize ama from its storage in the deep tissues toward the digestive tract for elimination.

This phase typically lasts three to seven days for intensive programs, or one to two sessions for single-treatment visits. Guests often notice increased softness in skin and joints, improved sleep, and a subtle but distinct sense of the body "opening" during this phase.

Duration1 – 7 Days
MethodsOil + Steam
PurposeMobilize Toxins
05
The Heart
Primary Therapies — Pradhan Karma

This is where the classical Ayurvedic therapies are administered in their fullness: Abhyanga, Shirodhara, Pinda Sweda, Pizhichil, and for intensive programs, the five classical Panchakarma procedures — Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, and Raktamokshana — each applied in the precisely prescribed sequence.

Sessions are administered by our vaidyas themselves, not merely trained therapists. Every touch carries intention and clinical awareness. Your vaidya monitors your responses between sessions, adjusting the protocol fluidly based on what your body reveals. Healing is not linear — it is responsive, relational, and deeply individual.

SessionsDaily Treatments

06
Integration
Paschatkarma — Sustaining Transformation

The final step is often overlooked by impatient seekers — and it is arguably the most important. Paschatkarma encompasses post-treatment dietary protocols (Samsarjana Krama), herbal formulas for home practice, Dinacharya (daily routine), Ritucharya (seasonal routine), and yoga practices calibrated to your constitution and healing goals.

Every guest leaves Vaidya with a written Paschatkarma plan. We schedule follow-up pulse readings at 30 and 90 days to assess integration and refine your ongoing protocol. Healing does not end when you leave our doors — it continues wherever your life unfolds.

The Three Energies

Understanding
Your Dosha

Every person is a unique combination of the three doshas. Your dominant dosha shapes your physical nature, emotional tendencies, and vulnerability to specific imbalances.

∧
Vata
Air + Ether

Vata governs movement, nervous system function, and communication. Vata types tend toward creativity, quick thinking, and enthusiasm — but when excess accumulates, the same qualities become anxiety, restlessness, scattered attention, and dry, depleted tissue.

Signs of Vata Excess

  • Anxiety and mental restlessness
  • Dry skin, hair, and joints
  • Irregular digestion
  • Sleep disruption
  • Cold extremities
△
Pitta
Fire + Water

Pitta governs digestion, transformation, and intelligence. Pitta types are sharp, focused, driven, and decisive — but excess Pitta creates inflammation, irritability, perfectionism, skin reactions, and the burning quality of a fire that has lost its boundaries.

Signs of Pitta Excess

  • Anger and irritability
  • Skin redness, rashes, inflammation
  • Burning sensations in digestion
  • Perfectionism and criticism
  • Heat sensitivity
○
Kapha
Earth + Water

Kapha governs structure, stability, immunity, and love. Kapha types are grounded, loyal, compassionate, and steady — but when stagnant, these same qualities become lethargy, weight gain, attachment, congestion, and a heaviness that makes movement feel impossible.

Signs of Kapha Excess

  • Lethargy and sluggishness
  • Weight gain and fluid retention
  • Congestion and mucus
  • Emotional clinging
  • Slow metabolism
Practical Information

What to Expect
at Vaidya

◎
Before Your Visit

Avoid heavy meals for two hours before treatments. Come without strong perfumes or essential oils. Wear comfortable, loose clothing. Inform us of any medications, recent surgeries, or health conditions during booking.

❖
During Treatment

Treatments involve undressing to your comfort level with full draping throughout. Warm medicated oils are a central part of most treatments. Please communicate any discomfort immediately — your vaidya will adjust accordingly.

∿
After Treatment

Allow time to rest after treatments — ideally 30 minutes minimum. Avoid cold drinks, strenuous exercise, and excessive screen time. The oil on your skin is medicinal — allow it to absorb for several hours before bathing.

✦
What We Don't Do

We do not offer treatments during active fever, acute illness, menstruation (for some procedures), or pregnancy without specialized assessment. Our vaidyas will always recommend the most appropriate approach for your current state.

⊕
Dietary Recommendations

You will receive specific dietary guidance based on your dosha assessment. Generally, warm, easily digestible foods are recommended during any treatment course. Specific foods to avoid and favor will be outlined in your protocol.

◇
The Healing Response

As toxins are released, some guests experience temporary fatigue, emotional shifts, or changes in sleep patterns. These are signs of genuine healing and typically resolve within 24-48 hours. Your vaidya will guide you through any such responses.
