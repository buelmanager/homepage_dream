Shirodhara — The River of Stillness

In Sanskrit, "Shiro" means head and "dhara" means continuous stream. For sixty uninterrupted minutes, a warm, medicated oil flows in an unwavering stream across the Ajna chakra — the third eye point — inducing states of deep neurological quietude that elude even experienced meditators.

The oil is specially prepared with Brahmi, Bhringaraj, and Ashwagandha based on your dosha assessment. The experience bypasses the rational mind entirely, accessing the nervous system through the skin's receptors and the subtle body's energy channels.
