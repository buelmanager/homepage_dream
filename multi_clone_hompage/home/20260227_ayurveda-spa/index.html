Our Foundation

The Three Pillars
of Ayurvedic Healing

Rooted in five thousand years of Vedic science, our practice honors the sacred relationship between body, mind, and the living universe.

01
Dosha Harmony

Every person is a unique constellation of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha energies. Our vaidyas read your constitution through pulse diagnosis, crafting treatments that restore your inherent balance and vitality.

02
Sacred Herbs

We source over 200 medicinal herbs directly from Himalayan foothills and Kerala spice gardens. Each formulation follows classical Charaka Samhita texts, prepared fresh under the guidance of our resident Ayurvedic pharmacist.

03
Seasonal Alignment

Ayurveda teaches that health flows with nature's rhythms. Our treatment protocols shift with the seasons — cleansing in spring, cooling in summer, nourishing in autumn, and grounding through winter's quiet depth.

Meet Our Vaidyas
Your Path to Balance

The Six Steps of
the Healing Journey

01
Pulse Consultation

Your journey begins with Nadi Pariksha — the ancient art of pulse diagnosis. Our vaidya reads the rhythms of your three doshas through three fingers, revealing imbalances invisible to modern tests.

02
Dosha Assessment

A comprehensive evaluation of your Prakriti (inherent constitution) and Vikriti (current imbalance) through tongue examination, facial diagnosis, and lifestyle inquiry.

03
Treatment Design

We design a personalized sequence of therapies, herbs, diet, and daily rhythms tailored precisely to your unique constitution and healing intentions.

04
Purvakarma

Preparatory therapies that soften, lubricate, and open pathways — Snehana (oleation) and Svedana (herbalized steam) loosen accumulated toxins from deep tissues.

05
Primary Therapies

The heart of your healing: classical treatments administered with reverence, skill, and deep intention. Each session builds upon the last in a carefully sequenced progression.

06
Paschatkarma

Post-treatment integration with dietary guidance, herbal formulas, daily routine recommendations, and seasonal practices to sustain your transformed state of health.

Our Lineage

Seventeen Years
of Living Tradition

Vaidya was founded by Dr. Priya Nair, a fifth-generation Ayurvedic physician trained in the classical gurukul tradition of Kerala. What began as a small healing practice has grown into a sanctuary that draws seekers from across the world — yet retains the intimacy and depth of a family lineage.

2008
Founded in Bangalore Dr. Nair opens Vaidya's first healing room with four treatment beds and a herb garden.
2013
Kerala Retreat Expansion Addition of residential Panchakarma programs at our backwater retreat in Alleppey.
2018
International Recognition Named among Asia's top ten Ayurvedic centers by Condé Nast Traveller Spa Awards.
2025
Living Herb Apothecary Launch of our in-house Ayurvedic apothecary offering classical formulations for home practice.
