Our Story

The Atelier Behind the Thread

Forty years of mastery, eight master weavers, and one unwavering belief: that the finest silk is not merely made, it is remembered.

Origin

Born from Devotion

FILAMENT was founded in 1982 by Elara Voss, a master weaver trained in the ateliers of Lyon and later in the silk villages of the Rhône Valley. Armed with two restored hand looms and an archive of pattern drafts inherited from her mentor, she established the studio with a single purpose: to prove that the ancient language of silk was not a relic but a living vocabulary.


What began as a modest workshop has grown into one of Europe's most sought-after textile ateliers — without once compromising its founding principles. Every centimeter of cloth that leaves FILAMENT has been woven by hand, inspected by eye, and finished with the same patience that Elara brought to her first commission.


Today, the atelier operates under the guidance of a second generation of master weavers who trained under Elara herself, carrying forward a practice that belongs equally to art, science, and philosophy.

Loom Heritage

Instruments of Living Craft

Our twelve looms range from an oak-framed floor loom dating to the 1940s to precision-tensioned Jacquard looms capable of producing cloth with up to 600 warp ends per centimeter. Each is maintained to exacting mechanical standards and recalibrated by hand before every new commission.


The FILAMENT Pattern Archive houses over 800 original drafts — hand-drawn point papers and punched Jacquard cards — spanning four generations of design. This archive is our intellectual inheritance, the accumulated visual language of the atelier, and the foundation from which all new commissions depart.


We do not use digital Jacquard systems. Every pattern we weave is first translated by a human hand from draft to loom, preserving the interpretive intelligence that distinguishes a woven cloth from a reproduced one.

What We Stand For

Principles in Practice

Radical Slowness

We take the time that each commission requires — never less. The finest silk cannot be hurried without being diminished. Our lead times reflect our respect for the craft.

Ecological Conscience

From cocoon to cloth, we trace the provenance of every material. Our dye studio uses exclusively botanical and mineral colorants. We maintain zero waste in yarn processing.

Knowledge Transfer

We operate an active apprenticeship program, training two new weavers each year in the classical methods. Preserving this knowledge is inseparable from our mission.
