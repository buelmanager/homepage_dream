Signature Series

Dupioni Silk

Raw Texture, Structured Luminosity

Dupioni is woven from two silkworm cocoons that have naturally interlocked, producing the distinctive slubbed texture that gives this cloth its remarkable character. The irregular cross-threads create a fabric of immense visual depth — simultaneously rustic and refined, matte and luminous.

At FILAMENT, our Dupioni is woven on heritage looms using single-weft techniques that preserve the natural slub without mechanical intervention. The result is a cloth of extraordinary individuality: no two lengths are ever identical.

Fiber Content100% Pure Bombyx Silk
Weave StructurePlain weave, slub weft
Weight90–130 gsm
Width110 cm
ApplicationsInteriors, Couture, Upholstery

Charmeuse Silk

Fluid Drape, Satin Brilliance

Charmeuse is a satin-weave silk prized for its extraordinary fluidity and reflective surface. The front face carries a brilliant sheen while the reverse presents a matte, crepe-like finish — a duality that makes this cloth uniquely versatile for both fashion and interior applications.

Our Charmeuse is woven at a thread count that imparts exceptional softness without sacrificing the structural integrity necessary for tailoring. Dyed in our botanical studio in colorways spanning deep indigo through pale celadon.

Fiber Content100% Pure Bombyx Silk
Weave Structure5-shaft satin
Weight55–75 gsm
Width114 cm
ApplicationsFashion, Lingerie, Linings, Drapes
Commission Charmeuse

Jacquard Silk

Complex Pattern, Woven Relief

Jacquard weaving is the pinnacle of the loom's capacity for expression. By controlling each warp thread independently — through a system of cards and cords that predates digital computing by two centuries — the Jacquard loom can render patterns of extraordinary intricacy directly into the cloth structure.

FILAMENT's Jacquard silks are developed from original point-paper drafts by our in-house designers. Each pattern is translated to the loom by hand, preserving the interpretive decisions that distinguish a woven cloth from a printed one. Available in single and brocaded multi-color variants.

Fiber Content100% Pure Bombyx Silk
Weave StructureJacquard, varied float
Weight150–280 gsm
Width90 cm
ApplicationsCeremonial, Interior Panels, Couture
Commission Jacquard

Organza Silk

Woven to a thread count that produces near-total transparency, our Organza captures light in the manner of stained glass — simultaneously present and absent. Available in natural, bleached, and botanical-dyed variants. Exceptionally suited to couture, window treatments, and sculptural textile installations.
