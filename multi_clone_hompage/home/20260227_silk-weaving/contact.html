Frequently Asked
What is the minimum commission quantity? +
For standard catalogue silks: 5 running meters. For fully bespoke commissions (custom weave structure, exclusive pattern): 15 running meters minimum.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes. We deliver to 12 countries currently and work with specialist art freight handlers for international commissions. Delivery times and costs vary; please enquire at the time of order.
Can I visit the atelier before commissioning? +
Yes — and we encourage it. All studio visits are by appointment. During a visit, you may handle our cloth sample archive and speak directly with the weaver who would oversee your commission.
Do you provide sample cloths? +
For bespoke commissions, we produce a woven sample (typically 30 × 30 cm) in your specified colorway and weave structure before proceeding to the full cloth. Sample production is included in the commission price.
