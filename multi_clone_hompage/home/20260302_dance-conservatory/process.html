Auditions & Enrollment

How to Join
Mouvement

Admission is based entirely on what we see in the studio. We do not consider academic records, prizes, or social media. We watch you move — and we talk with you about why you move the way you do.

The Audition Process

Five Steps to
the Studio

01

Online Application

Submit the online form with your personal details, program preference, and a brief description of your training history (no minimum length — brevity is fine). Attach a short video: three minutes of your best movement, unedited.

Applications reviewed within two weeks
02

Video Review

The faculty watches every video. We are looking for physical potential, musicality, and evidence of genuine engagement with movement — not perfection. Approximately 50% of applicants are invited to audition in person.

03

In-Person Audition

A two-hour audition class in Lyon, conducted by senior faculty. The class format varies by program: ballet barre and centre for classical applicants; a contemporary class and improvisation session for contemporary applicants; a composition exercise for choreography applicants.

Online audition available for international applicants
04

Individual Interview

A thirty-minute conversation with the program director following the audition class. We ask about your training, your influences, what you find difficult, and what you want from the next two or three years. Honesty is the only correct answer.

05

Offer & Enrolment

Offers are made within one week. A deposit of €1,500 secures your place. We will provide full orientation material, studio allocation, timetable, and accommodation guidance for those relocating to Lyon.
