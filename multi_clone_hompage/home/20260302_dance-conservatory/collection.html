Six Paths

Our programs range from three-year professional diplomas to focused one-year certificates. Every path combines technical rigour with creative latitude.

3-Year Diploma — Professional Track

Classical Ballet

The full classical syllabus: barre work, centre practice, pointe work, pas de deux, and character dance. Students train six days per week. Those who complete the diploma with distinction may audition for the Mouvement Resident Company — our professional touring ensemble.

3 Academic Years
Intake
16 Students

15 Years

2-Year Diploma

Contemporary Dance

Release technique, floor work, Limon, Forsythe vocabulary, somatic practices, and improvisation. The program is designed for classical-trained dancers who wish to expand their range, and for contemporary movers who want structural rigour. A separate stream for those new to the classical canon.

2 Academic Years

1-Year Certificate

Choreography

For experienced dancers ready to make their own work. The year proceeds through a series of graduated composition exercises culminating in a 15-minute group piece presented at the Mouvement Platform. Visiting choreographers give two masterclasses per month.

1-Year Certificate

Dance & Music Integration

An interdisciplinary program for dancers who want a deeper understanding of musical structure and its relationship to movement. Covers score reading, rhythmic analysis, improvisation with live musicians, and collaborative performance creation. Two public performances.
