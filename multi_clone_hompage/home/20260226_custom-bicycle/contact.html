After You Submit

What Happens Next

01
We Review Your Submission

Julien personally reads every commission form. Within 48 hours, you will receive a reply from him with initial thoughts on your brief and a link to book your consultation call.

02
Consultation Call

A 60-90 minute call where we deep-dive into your riding profile, goals, and geometry requirements. We may ask for video of your current riding position. At the end of this call, you will have a clear picture of your proposed build.

03
Proposal & Deposit

We send you a detailed build proposal with geometry specification, material choice, component spec, total price, and estimated delivery date. A 30% deposit secures your place in the build queue.
