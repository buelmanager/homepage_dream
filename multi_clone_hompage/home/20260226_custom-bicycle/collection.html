Build Spotlight

Strada Ti MkIII — Deep Dive

47
Build Days

Commissioned by Marco Reyes, a Category 2 amateur racer based in Mallorca, the Strada Ti MkIII represents everything Velox Atelier stands for: geometry derived from 3D motion capture, tube profiles optimised per section, and a finish that speaks before the rider does.

The build uses Shimano Dura-Ace R9200 Di2 throughout, paired with Reynolds 65mm carbon aero clinchers. Total build weight: 6.2kg. The frame ships with a hand-signed geometry certificate from Julien Moreau.
