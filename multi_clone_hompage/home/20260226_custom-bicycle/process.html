The Build Process

8 Steps

How We Build Your Frame

01
Days 1–3
Consultation

Every build begins with understanding the rider. We explore your riding history, goals, event calendar, injury history, and aesthetic preferences. This session happens via video call or at our Lyon workshop.

What We Cover
  • Riding style and primary discipline
  • Goals — comfort, performance, touring distance
  • Previous frame issues and injury history
  • Component preferences and budget
  • Aesthetic direction and color preference
02
Days 4–8
Geometry Fitting

Using rider biometric data — inseam, torso, arm, shoulder, reach, and flexibility — we build a custom geometry worksheet. For local clients, an in-person fit session is conducted on our Guru fitting system.

Fitting Tools
  • Guru Pro dynamic fitting system
  • 15+ biometric measurements
  • Flexibility and mobility assessment
  • Stack and reach calculation
  • Geometry certificate issued
03
Days 9–12
Material Selection

Based on rider goals and budget, we select tube sets and lugs. For most road and gravel builds, we recommend 3Al-2.5V Grade 9 titanium. Track frames use T700 carbon layup. We discuss options with the rider before ordering.

Material Options
  • Grade 9 Titanium (3Al-2.5V) — road/gravel/touring
  • T700 carbon fiber — track/race
  • Reynolds 853 CrMo — touring/commute
  • Custom CNC titanium dropouts (all builds)
  • Hand-selected tube wall thickness per rider weight
04
Days 13–28
Frame Construction

The longest phase. Tube mitering, jig setup, tacking, and full TIG welding. Each bead is inspected under magnification. We weld in three passes on titanium — root, fill, and cap — with argon back-purge throughout.

Construction Details
  • Precision tube mitering to ±0.2mm
  • Argon back-purge TIG welding
  • Three-pass weld sequence
  • Post-weld stress relief
  • AWS D1.1 weld inspection
05
Days 29–33
Component Spec

We work with the rider to finalize component selection — drivetrain, groupset, wheels, cockpit, and saddle. We source at trade pricing and pass savings on to the client. Build sheet signed off before parts are ordered.

Component Partners
  • Shimano, SRAM, Campagnolo groupsets
  • Enve, Zipp, Hunt wheel options
  • Thomson, Ritchey, Deda cockpit
  • Fizik, Brooks, Selle Italia saddles
  • Custom cable routing and housing
06
Days 34–40
Paint & Finish

Frame preparation, surface finishing, and application of chosen color treatment. Options include raw brushed titanium, anodized color, powder coat, or wet paint with custom graphics. Applied by our in-house paint specialist.

Finish Options
  • Raw brushed titanium — no coating required
  • Anodized color — aerospace grade
  • Powder coat — 200+ color options
  • Wet paint with clear coat
  • Custom decals and engraving
07
Days 41–44
Assembly & QA

Full component installation, cable routing, and torque specification. Every bearing, bolt, and interface checked. Final geometry measured on the stand and compared against the geometry certificate. Any deviation corrected before sign-off.

Quality Checks
  • Frame alignment on precision stand
  • All torques to manufacturer specification
  • Shifting and braking calibration
  • Wheel truing and spoke tension
  • Road test by Julien on all builds
08
Days 45–47
Delivery

Your frame is photographed, boxed in our custom foam-lined case, and shipped with full insurance via DHL Express Priority worldwide. Delivery to your door with a geometry certificate, care guide, and a signed letter from Julien.

What's Included
  • Custom foam-lined Velox flight case
  • Signed geometry certificate
  • Frame care and maintenance guide
  • Lifetime structural warranty card
  • Julien's personal letter to the rider

Common Questions

FAQ

How much does a Velox frame cost?
+

Frame-only pricing starts at €4,200 for titanium road builds and €3,600 for steel touring frames. Full custom builds with components range from €8,500 to €18,000 depending on specification. We provide a detailed quote after your initial consultation at no obligation.

Can I visit the workshop for the fitting?
+

Absolutely. We welcome in-person visits to our Lyon workshop and strongly encourage it for riders who can travel. Studio visits are by appointment only — contact us to schedule. Remote fittings via video call are equally effective for international clients.

What is the warranty on a Velox frame?
+

All titanium frames carry a lifetime structural warranty against weld and tube failure under normal riding conditions. Carbon frames carry a 10-year structural warranty. Paint and surface finishes carry a 3-year warranty. Crash damage is not covered, but we offer repair services.

Do you ship internationally?
+

Yes. We ship to 28 countries and counting. All frames are shipped fully insured via DHL Express Priority in our custom foam-lined flight cases. International shipping is typically 3–5 business days. Import duties are the responsibility of the recipient — we advise clients on estimated duty costs for their country.

How far in advance should I book?
+

Our current build queue is 8–12 weeks. This means from your deposit payment to build start is typically 10 weeks, with delivery around 17–18 weeks total. We limit ourselves to 12 frames per month to maintain quality. Enquire early if you have a specific event or season target.

Can I upgrade my components after delivery?
+

Yes. All Velox frames are designed with component upgradeability in mind. We provide a complete component specification document with every delivery so any qualified mechanic can service the bike. We also offer upgrade builds from our Lyon workshop for clients who want to upgrade at the source.
