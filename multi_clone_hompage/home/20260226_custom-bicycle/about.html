The Founder

From Racing
To Building

Julien Moreau raced professionally through his twenties — Tour de Suisse, Paris-Roubaix, six Grand Tours. When a crash in 2003 ended his racing career, he didn't walk away from cycling. He walked into a welding workshop.

Trained under master frame builder Franco Giusti in Ferrara, Julien spent two years learning the fundamentals of titanium TIG welding, geometry mathematics, and rider biomechanics. In 2004, he opened Velox Atelier in Lyon's 1st arrondissement — a single room, a welder, and an obsession with getting geometry right.

Twenty years later, Velox Atelier has delivered over 340 frames to riders in 28 countries. The obsession remains unchanged: every frame is a mathematical problem with a human solution.

What We Believe

Our Philosophy

Four principles guide every decision we make — from tube selection to final paint.

Speed

Every gram eliminated, every angle optimized for aerodynamic efficiency and power transfer. Speed is not a feature — it is the outcome of correct engineering.

Comfort

Road vibration and fatigue are engineering failures. We tune tube wall thickness and geometry to absorb what the road gives, keeping the rider fresh for hour six.

Longevity

We build frames that outlast trends. Grade 9 titanium does not fatigue, does not corrode, does not compromise. We back every frame with a lifetime structural warranty.

Identity

Your frame is not a product — it is a statement. Custom color, engraved details, and a geometry certificate signed by Julien accompanies every delivery.

The Builders

Master Craftsmen

Julien Moreau
Founder & Head Frame Builder

Former pro cyclist, 20 years of frame building experience. Trained in Ferrara under Franco Giusti. Personally oversees geometry on every frame. Holder of European TIG welding certification Level D1.1. Author of three geometry papers published in Cycling Science Quarterly.

Marco Santos
Master Builder — Carbon & CNC

Aerospace engineering background from IST Lisbon, transitioned to frame building in 2012. Specializes in carbon fiber layup, CNC machining of titanium dropouts, and paint finish quality control. Designer of our proprietary bottom bracket shell system introduced in 2019.

Standards We Hold

Certifications

TIG
AWS D1.1 TIG Welding

American Welding Society structural welding certification. The highest standard for titanium TIG weld quality applied to every frame.

EN
EN ISO 4210 Bicycle Safety

All frames tested against European bicycle safety standard EN ISO 4210-6:2015 for frame and fork fatigue and impact resistance.

CF
Carbon Fiber NDT Inspection

Non-destructive testing of all carbon frames using ultrasonic inspection. Zero-defect laminate confirmation before delivery.
