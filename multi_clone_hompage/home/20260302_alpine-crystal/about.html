Our Story

A jeweler who
begins with geology

Manifesto
"A crystal that took 10,000 years to form deserves a setting that took ten months to design."

We are, first, geologists. The jewelry follows from the mineral's nature — not the other way around. This is the fundamental inversion that defines Crestille.

Founder

Élise Crestille-Moreau

Trained in geology at the École des Mines and then in jewelry design at the Beaux-Arts de Paris, Élise Crestille-Moreau spent six years working alpine mineral deposits before opening the atelier in 2003.

Her academic background gives her the capacity to read a crystal's internal structure before she designs its setting — something no purely trained jeweler can do. Each setting is an argument about the mineral's geometry, expressed in gold and platinum.

She prospects personally every season, accompanied by a geologist and a photographer who documents every extraction for the provenance archive.

Élise C-M.
Values

Three Commitments

◇

Geological First

We begin with the mineral. Every design decision follows from what the crystal is — its optical axis, its cleavage planes, its refractive index. We impose nothing.

◆

Single Maker

Élise sets every stone. No production line, no division of labor. The concentration of a single maker working across every stage of a piece cannot be replicated at scale.

◈

Permanent Archive

Every piece is registered in our geological archive. The data lives beyond the owner — a permanent record of where the mineral came from and how it was transformed.

First Commission

The Mont Blanc aquamarine pendant sells within 48 hours of announcement. The provenance certificate concept is introduced from day one.
