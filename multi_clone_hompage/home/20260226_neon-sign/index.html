The Craft

FROM SKETCH TO GLOW

Every piece begins as a conversation and ends as a permanent fixture of light — five meticulous stages of artisanal precision.

01

SKETCH

Your vision translated into detailed technical drawings, exploring typography and spatial placement.

02

TYPOGRAPHY

Letterforms refined for glass bending — every curve, junction and terminus engineered for luminous clarity.

03

GLASS BENDING

Master glassworkers heat borosilicate tubes to 760°C and hand-bend each character with surgical precision.

04

GAS FILL

Sealed tubes filled with noble gases — neon, argon, xenon or krypton — each producing its signature glow.

05

INSTALL

White-glove delivery and professional installation, from intimate desk pieces to full architectural facades.

Noble Gases & Glass

THE SCIENCE OF GLOW

Each gas produces a distinct spectrum of light — chosen not merely for color, but for emotional resonance.

NEON GAS

The original. Pure neon produces the iconic warm orange-red glow that defined the 20th century. Vivid, warm, unmistakably alive. Works with clear glass for maximum intensity.

Warm Red — Orange

ARGON + MERCURY

Argon alone glows a faint lilac. Combined with trace mercury vapour and blue-coated glass, it produces the electric blue-cyan spectrum most associated with modern neon art.

Electric Blue — Cyan

XENON

Premium rare gas producing a brilliant near-white light with a subtle blue-violet undertone. The choice for luxury retail and hospitality where pure luminosity is paramount.

Near-White — Silver

ARGON + PINK GLASS

Argon gas through hand-blown pink-coated borosilicate tubes creates the vivid hot-pink signature associated with contemporary luxury and nightlife culture.

Hot Pink — Magenta

HELIUM

Helium produces a soft golden-amber glow — rarest and most expensive of our gas options. Reserved for collectors and heritage installations seeking warm antiquity.

Amber — Gold
