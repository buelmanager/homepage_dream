Our Story

FROM A SPARK
TO AN EMBER

Ember Studio was founded in 2008 by creative director James Harlow and strategist Lena Voss in a small studio in East London. Their belief was simple: that great brand work could transform businesses and, sometimes, entire industries.

Within three years, Ember had won its first Cannes Lion for its work with a then-unknown spirits brand that went on to become a global category leader. That moment set the template for everything that followed — bold creative thinking, anchored in rigorous strategy.

2008 Founded in Shoreditch, London. First 3 clients, 6 staff.
2013 First Cannes Lions Gold. New York office opens.
2018 Tokyo studio launches. Team grows to 100 globally.
2025 28 Cannes Lions. 340 brands built. 180 staff worldwide.
