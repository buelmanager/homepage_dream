Estate Regulations

Gallop & Grace is a private members' club. All visitors must check in at the Hartfield Lane gatehouse upon arrival. Dogs must be kept on lead at all times on the estate. Vehicle speed limit on estate roads is 10 mph. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times near horses and in stable areas.

Dress Code

Smart country attire is expected within the club rooms, members' lounge, and at formal events. Appropriate equestrian attire — including riding hat and appropriate boots — is required in all yard, arena, and stable areas for the safety of all parties.

Prospective Member Visits

Prospective members are warmly welcomed for a guided estate tour by appointment. Please contact our membership office to arrange a visit. Estate tours take approximately 90 minutes and include the stables, arenas, and members' lounge.

Membership
Application

Membership at Gallop & Grace is by application. All applications are reviewed by the Membership Committee, who consider equestrian background, personal references, and alignment with the estate's values of excellence, welfare, and community.

Application Process

1. Submit application form
2. Membership Committee review (7–14 days)
3. Invitation for estate visit and informal meeting
4. Membership offer issued
5. Annual fee payment and induction day

References

Two personal references are required with all applications. At least one reference should be from within the equestrian community. References are contacted with your permission only.
