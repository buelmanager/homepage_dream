Polo Introduction
Beginner — Intermediate Seasonal Chukka Ponies Provided

Our polo programme — offered from April through September — provides an exceptional introduction to one of the world's most demanding equestrian sports. All sessions begin with stick-and-ball practice on foot, progress to mounted exercises, and culminate in supervised slow chukkas on our purpose-trained polo ponies.

  • Ground polo fundamentals: swing, strike, mallet grip
  • Mounted slow chukkas on trained estate polo ponies
  • Introduction to the rules and tactics of competitive polo
  • Guest chukkas against invited county-level teams (seasonal)

40 Miles of Private Countryside

Estate Trail
Routes

Every trail route at Gallop & Grace has been mapped, maintained, and named over decades of riding across the Surrey Hills estate. They range from a contemplative morning hour through ancient woodland to a full half-day expedition across the open downs.

◊

The Woodland Circuit

8 miles  ·  1.5 hours  ·  All ride levels

The most popular estate route. A gentle loop through ancient oak and beech woodland, crossing two chalk streams and passing the restored 18th-century ice house. Ideal for first trail rides and leisurely hacks.

◊

The Downs Gallop

14 miles  ·  2.5 hours  ·  Intermediate+

A sweeping route across open chalk downland with two long approved gallop stretches. The finest landscape on the estate — views extend across three counties on a clear day.

◊

The Full Estate Circuit

28 miles  ·  4 hours  ·  Experienced riders only

The grand tour of all 340 acres. Combines all terrain types and includes a midpoint rest at the estate's historic hay barn with light refreshments provided. A ride unlike any other in Surrey.

◊

The Dawn Expedition

Seasonal  ·  By arrangement  ·  Patron Members only

A private sunrise ride across the estate's highest ground, departing 45 minutes before dawn and returning at breakfast. A singular experience that members describe as the finest hour in English riding.

The Polo
Programme

Polo is one of the oldest team sports in the world and one of the most exhilarating. Our polo programme was introduced in 2018 on a dedicated grass pitch alongside the estate's eastern meadow. A string of eight purpose-trained polo ponies is available exclusively for the programme.

Whether you seek the exhilaration of a first chukka or are looking to develop your handicap in a private club setting, our resident polo coach — trained at Guards Polo Club — will design a progressive, structured curriculum around your ability.

8-Pony String

Dedicated polo ponies selected for manoeuvrability, speed, and calm temperament. Available for all polo sessions.

Pitch & Boards

Full-size grass polo pitch with permanent boards, mallet racks, and a thatched spectator pavilion.

Resident Coach

Guards Polo Club-trained instructor with playing experience at 4-goal level. Available for individual and group sessions.

Guest Chukkas

Seasonal matches against invited county-level teams. Members may observe or play depending on ability assessment.
