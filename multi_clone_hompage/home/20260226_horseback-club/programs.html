Our Training Programs

Every program at Gallop & Grace is designed with a single philosophy: the correct foundation, patiently built, creates the exceptional rider.

01
Foundation
Beginner Equestrian
Duration: 12 weeks
Level: No experience
Frequency: 2x per week

The foundation of all great riding. Our beginner programme introduces students to horsemanship, stable management, and correct seat and aids in a calm, supportive environment. Sessions are kept small — never more than four students per lesson — ensuring meaningful attention from our instructors.

  • Groundwork and horse handling
  • Walk, trot, and canter basics
  • Stable management certification
  • Use of school horses
02
Intermediate
Intermediate Dressage
Duration: Ongoing
Level: Novice–Medium
Frequency: 3x per week

Classical dressage taught with rigorous attention to the training scale: rhythm, suppleness, contact, impulsion, straightness, and collection. Students progress through British Dressage Novice and Elementary levels toward Medium. Clinics with guest international judges held quarterly.

  • Flatwork clinics and individual sessions
  • Video analysis with trainer review
  • British Dressage affiliation support
  • Competition preparation included
03
Athletic
Show Jumping
Duration: Ongoing
Level: Novice–1.30m
Frequency: 3x per week

From first cross-rails to regional competitions, our show jumping programme develops boldness, accuracy, and an eye for pace and stride. Training takes place on our dedicated outdoor course and indoor school, with regular arena tours to compete at affiliated shows.

  • Grid work and course riding
  • Flatwork for jumping horses
  • BSJA membership support
  • Transport to affiliated shows
04
Endurance
Trail & Endurance
Duration: Seasonal
Level: Intermediate+
Frequency: Weekends

Explore the Surrey Hills on 40 miles of private estate trails. Our trail programme builds horse and rider confidence across varied terrain: open fields, ancient woodland, chalk streams, and managed hills. Endurance conditioning modules available for those preparing for competitive events.

  • Guided estate trail rides
  • Navigation and pace training
  • Horse fitness conditioning plans
  • Multi-day expedition rides (seasonal)
05
Youth
Young Rider Programme
Duration: Annual
Level: Ages 10–18
Frequency: 2x per week

A dedicated pathway for young equestrians aged 10 to 18. The Young Rider Programme combines ridden training across all disciplines with equine science education, stable management, and mentorship from our senior instructors. Alumni have progressed to represent Great Britain at junior international level.

  • Multi-discipline ridden training
  • Equine science and husbandry
  • Pony Club integration
  • Annual Young Rider Championships
06
Elite
Private Coaching
Duration: Bespoke
Level: All levels
Frequency: By arrangement

One-to-one coaching with our senior instructors, tailored entirely to your goals, schedule, and horse. Private coaching sessions are available on your own horse or on our school horses, and can be structured for recreational improvement, competition preparation, or technical rehabilitation.

  • Fully bespoke session structure
  • Video analysis and written reports
  • Inter-session training plans
  • Nutrition and conditioning advisory

Book a Lesson

First Lesson Policy

All new riders complete a complimentary assessment session with a senior instructor before being placed in a programme. This ensures the correct level placement and ideal horse pairing from your very first lesson.

Lesson Fees

Group lessons from £85 per session. Private coaching from £175 per hour. Seasonal packages available with 10% saving. All fees include horse, tack, and arena hire.
