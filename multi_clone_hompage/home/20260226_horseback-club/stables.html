The Heart of the Estate

The Grand
Stables

Built in 1981 by master craftsmen using traditional English timber-frame construction and locally quarried Surrey stone, the Grand Stables at Gallop & Grace stand as one of England's most admired equestrian buildings. Originally designed to house 60 horses, they were expanded in 2014 to their current capacity of 85 heated stalls.

Each stall measures a minimum of 14 by 14 feet — larger than the FEI recommended standard — with deep woodchip bedding, automated water systems, and individual climate monitoring. The stable block is oriented to maximise natural light and cross-ventilation, ensuring exceptional air quality year-round.

85 Individual Stalls

All exceeding FEI minimum dimensions. Heated flooring in winter. Individual ventilation control.

Farriery Bay

Dedicated covered farriery bay with rubber matting and cross-tie rails. Resident farrier on-site twice weekly.

Equine Solarium

Infrared equine solarium for post-exercise recovery and winter coat management. Bookable for all livery horses.

Tack Rooms

Eight climate-controlled tack rooms with individual member lockers, saddle racks, and CCTV security.

Welfare Above All

The Care Programme

The welfare of every horse at Gallop & Grace is a moral commitment, not a marketing claim. Every element of our care programme is designed by specialists, reviewed annually, and monitored continuously.

◆
Daily Turnout

Every horse receives a minimum of six hours in individual or compatible group paddocks each day. Natural movement, social interaction, and access to grazing are considered non-negotiable requirements for equine psychological health.

Minimum 6 hours daily  ·  Individual paddocks
◆
Bespoke Nutrition

Our resident equine nutritionist designs individual dietary programmes for each horse, accounting for breed, age, workload, and seasonal variation. Forage quality is laboratory-tested biannually and supplementation is evidence-based.

Individual dietary plans  ·  Seasonal review
◆
Veterinary Suite

Our on-estate equine veterinary suite houses digital radiography, standing MRI, gastroscopy, and a fully equipped surgery. Four resident veterinarians provide 24-hour care. Bi-annual health MOTs are standard for all horses.

4 resident vets  ·  24-hour coverage
◆
Physiotherapy

Two chartered equine physiotherapists conduct regular musculoskeletal assessments. Hydrotherapy water treadmill sessions, solarium therapy, and magnetic rug protocols are all available and routinely prescribed.

2 physiotherapists  ·  Hydrotherapy on-site
◆
Dental Care

An equine dental specialist visits every six months for routine rasping and examination. Records are maintained digitally for every horse and cross-referenced with behaviour and performance assessments to identify early indicators of discomfort.

Bi-annual dental checks  ·  Full records
◆
Behavioural Science

Our consulting equine behaviourist develops and reviews handling protocols, socialisation strategies, and environmental enrichment plans. Horses displaying anxiety or stereotypies are assessed individually and supported with evidence-based intervention.

Consulting behaviourist  ·  Low-stress handling
Selective Genetics

Breeding
Programme

Our breeding programme operates under a singular philosophy: produce horses of exceptional character, trainability, and longevity. Quality is measured not in championships won, but in decades of useful, harmonious working life.

I

Stallion Selection

We partner with Europe's leading stallion stations in the Netherlands, Germany, and Spain. Sires are selected for documented temperament genetics alongside proven performance. Bloodline diversity is maintained across all four breed programmes to preserve genetic health.

II

Mare Development

Each broodmare completes a four-year ridden assessment before entering the breeding programme. Mares are evaluated for specific genetic traits: bone density, hoof quality, respiratory capacity, and behavioural consistency — not merely athletic performance.

III

Foal Imprinting

A structured imprinting and socialisation programme begins within 24 hours of birth, guided by our equine behaviourist. Every foal is handled daily and exposed progressively to sounds, textures, and human contact to establish a secure psychological foundation from day one.

IV

Young Horse Education

Backing begins at age three with a full season of groundwork and lunging. The first ridden season is unhurried and progressive — confidence is never sacrificed to speed. Our young horse graduates are known for their trust, suppleness, and genuine willingness to work.

Equine Health

Veterinary
Excellence

Our on-estate veterinary facility is among the most comprehensively equipped in private equestrian ownership in the United Kingdom. We have made a deliberate investment in diagnostic technology to ensure that health issues are identified early and treated accurately.

Standing MRI and Digital Radiography

Full diagnostic imaging capability on-site, removing the need for hospital referral in most soft-tissue and orthopaedic cases.

Gastroscopy Suite

Gastric ulcer diagnosis and monitoring for all estate horses. Annual gastroscopy is standard for horses in active training programmes.

Regenerative Medicine

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) and IRAP therapy available for soft-tissue and joint conditions. Stem-cell banking offered for breeding stock.

Quarantine Facilities

Dedicated quarantine block with separate air supply and drainage for incoming horses and disease management when required.

Bi-Annual Health MOT

Every estate horse receives a comprehensive bi-annual health assessment: bloods, dental, weight scoring, vaccination review, and lameness evaluation.
