Foundation · Open Level

Olfactory Foundation

Fifty structured hours of smell training — building the vocabulary, the olfactory memory and the discriminative capacity needed to work seriously with fragrance materials. Taught entirely through direct experience: no chemistry required, no prior training necessary.

Duration
4 Weeks
Hours
50 contact hours
Level
Complete beginner
Materials
200 raw materials

Intermediate · Botanical

Natural Perfumery

Working exclusively with botanical absolutes, essential oils, resins and tinctures. Students compose three original natural fragrances under the supervision of a senior parfumeur and receive formula notation, safety certification and a personal material kit.

Duration
8 Weeks
Prerequisite
Olfactory Foundation
Cohort
Max 12 students
Output
3 original fragrances

Professional · Diploma

Haute Parfumerie Diploma

The school's complete professional training: 200 hours of nose training, accord study, formula development, IFRA compliance, brief interpretation and the creation of a complete original fragrance collection for public presentation. Accredited by the Société Française des Parfumeurs.

Duration
10 Months
Accreditation
SFP & Min. Culture
Intake
September annually
Cohort
8 students only

Intensive · Field Study

Grasse Field Seminar

Five days in the perfume capital of the world — visiting jasmine and rose fields during harvest, distilleries, enfleurage workshops and historic fragrance houses. The olfactory experience of these raw materials in situ is irreplaceable by any classroom exercise.

Duration
5 Days
Location
Grasse, France
Dates
May (rose) & Aug (jasmine)
Level
All levels welcome
