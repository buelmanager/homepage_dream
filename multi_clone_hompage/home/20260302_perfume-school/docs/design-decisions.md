# Design Decisions — L'École du Nez

## Color
Deep rose-black (#1A0A0E) is the color of a darkened salon — intimate, sensory, nocturnal. The dusty pink accent (#C96A8A) evokes faded rose petals, powder and the feminine tradition of French haute parfumerie without being cliché.

## Typography
Bodoni Moda in italic weight carries the tradition of French luxury typography. Its high-contrast strokes suggest both the precision of perfumery and the drama of invisible sensation. Work Sans provides clear, modern body text that does not compete.

## Hero (Type G Scroll Transform)
The scroll-driven text separation — two words diverging as the user scrolls — mimics the experience of a fragrance opening: unified at first impression, then revealing its components as time passes. The extremely dark hero image (brightness:0.25) creates an immersive, sensory environment.

## Animation (A2 Whisper)
The slow, smooth Whisper animation suits a school defined by patience and precision. Nothing rushes; everything arrives with intention — exactly the quality we cultivate in students of the nose.
