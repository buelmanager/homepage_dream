Getting Here

Finding
Hacienda San Marcos

The hacienda is located 4.2 kilometers from the center of Arandas, in the Los Altos Highlands region of Jalisco, Mexico. The drive from Guadalajara's Miguel Hidalgo International Airport takes approximately 90 minutes via the Guadalajara–Lagos de Moreno highway.

We recommend arranging private transportation or renting a vehicle, as the hacienda is not accessible via public transportation. Our reservations team can provide referrals to trusted local transportation services upon request.

From Guadalajara Airport: Take Highway 80 East toward Arandas (approx. 90 min). Follow signs to Camino Hacienda, turn right at km 4.2.
From Guadalajara City: Via Periferico Sur and Highway 80 East. Distance: 118 km. Allow 2 hours during peak hours.
Parking: Complimentary secured parking is available at the hacienda entrance for all guests.
