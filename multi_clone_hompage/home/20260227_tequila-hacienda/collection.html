Our Expressions

Five distinct expressions of the Agavero estate — each a different conversation between agave and time. From the pure fire of our Blanco to the eight-year contemplation of the Fundador.

Unaged
01 — Blanco

Blanco Cristalino

Our Blanco is the purest expression of the AGAVERO estate — distilled from 100% Blue Weber agave piñas harvested at peak maturity and bottled immediately after the second distillation with no aging or modification. It captures the raw, volcanic energy of the highland terroir in its most direct form: clean, bright, and unapologetically complex.

On the nose, green agave, citrus peel, and white flowers. The palate delivers sweet cooked piña, white pepper, and a mineral minerality that speaks directly of the volcanic red soil. The finish is long, dry, and warming — the blue fire of Jalisco in a glass.

02 — Reposado

Reposado Reserva

Rested for fourteen months in American white oak barrels — more than double the legal minimum — our Reposado Reserva represents the first conversation between the AGAVERO spirit and wood. The result is a tequila that remains deeply agave-forward while gaining warmth, roundness, and the first whispers of oak complexity.

Caramel, vanilla, and toasted walnut weave through the bright agave core. A touch of baking spice — cinnamon, clove — appears on the mid-palate. The oak never dominates; it frames. The finish is smooth, medium-long, with a gentle warmth that recalls the amber light of a Jalisco afternoon.

03 — Añejo

Añejo Hacienda

Three years of patience in seasoned American oak barrels produces our most awarded expression — the Añejo Hacienda. At this depth of aging, the agave and the wood have negotiated a profound partnership: neither dominates, and together they produce a spirit of remarkable complexity and balance.

Dark chocolate, dried fig, and toasted oak lead the nose. The palate unfolds over multiple waves — first the rich agave, then the caramel and spice of the wood, then the dried fruit and leather of time. The finish lingers like an amber sunset over the agave fields: long, warming, and inevitable.

04 — Extra Añejo

Extra Añejo Fundador

Named for Don Aurelio Agavero, the founder who first dared to let tequila rest far beyond convention, the Fundador is our magnum opus. Eight years in seasoned American oak — the longest aging in our history — produces a spirit that transcends category. This is not merely tequila. It is a meditation on time itself.

The nose is extraordinary: dark toffee, leather, dried tobacco, and a deep caramel sweetness underpinned by the persistent memory of agave. The palate is lush, dense, and seemingly endless in its layering. Oak dominates, but never obscures — it envelops. The finish lasts minutes, not moments, revealing new notes with every breath.

05 — Cristalino

Cristalino Reserva

Our Cristalino begins as the Añejo Hacienda — three years of patient oak aging — before undergoing a careful charcoal filtration that removes the color while preserving the complex flavor compounds developed during maturation. The result is a spirit of crystalline clarity with the depth of an aged tequila.

Visually pure as the Blanco, but rich with the complexity of years. Vanilla cream and soft caramel on the nose. The palate delivers the roundness and body of the Añejo with a clean, bright finish reminiscent of the Cristalino. An elegant expression for those who appreciate the paradox of depth in clarity.
