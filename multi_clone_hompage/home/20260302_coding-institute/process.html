How We Teach

The Nexus
Method

01

Application & Assessment

We don't look at credentials — we look at how you think. The application process includes a technical problem-solving session and a conversation about your goals. We admit fewer than 15% of applicants.

1 Week
02

Foundations Sprint

Every cohort begins with a two-week intensive covering the fundamentals: data structures, algorithms, and computational thinking. This levels the playing field and ensures everyone builds on solid ground.

2 Weeks
03

Mentorship Pairing

After foundations, each student is matched with a senior engineer from our industry network. Your mentor attends your weekly code reviews, is available on Slack daily, and co-designs your capstone project direction.

Ongoing
04

Project-Based Learning

The heart of Nexus. Each module culminates in a shipping-quality project: a real API, a scalable service, a deployed application. These form the backbone of your engineering portfolio.

6–8 Weeks
05

Capstone & Demo Day

Your final project is reviewed by a panel of senior engineers and potential employers. Demo Day is attended by 60+ engineering leaders — many of whom hire from the cohort.

2 Weeks
06

Career Support (Lifetime)

Nexus graduates never stop being Nexus graduates. Career support — resume reviews, interview prep, referral introductions — is available for life. Our alumni network is our most powerful hiring tool.

Forever

Learning Philosophy

⬡

First Principles

We teach why before how. Understanding the reasoning behind a pattern makes you capable of inventing new ones when the pattern breaks.

⬡

Deliberate Practice

Repetition builds speed. We structure exercises to push slightly beyond current ability — the proven zone of maximal learning.

⬡

Production Mindset

Every project is reviewed as if it were going to production. Code quality, observability, error handling — these are not afterthoughts.
