# Clone Plan — 20260302_public-archive

## Source Concept
Original design inspired by national archive institutions (The National Archives UK, Bibliothèque nationale de France). Not a direct clone of any existing template in this collection.

## Hero Layout
Type G: Scroll-Driven Text Transform. Two large typographic words ("EVERY" / "RECORD") on a sticky background, animated apart as the user scrolls, revealing a sub-heading and CTA. Identical mechanical pattern to 20260302_heritage-museum but with distinct typography (Cinzel vs Bodoni Moda) and copy.

## Differentiation from Heritage Museum
- Typography: Cinzel (architectural, epigraphic) vs Bodoni Moda (editorial luxury)
- Content domain: Documentary records and government papers vs cultural artefacts
- Sub-pages: Archive-specific language (Appraisal, Reader's Ticket, Born-Digital) vs museum language (Exhibitions, Research)
- Animation personality: A3 (x-slide) vs A4 (y-rise with power2.inOut)
- Tier: Free vs Premium
