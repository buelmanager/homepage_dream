Design Philosophy

Type is the voice of thought made visible

Every typeface begins not on screen but in the hand — in pencil sketches, in the study of historical models, in the tension between tradition and the demands of contemporary use. We believe that great typography is invisible in its service and unmistakable in its character.

The letterpress revival is not nostalgia. It is a return to the material truth of type: ink on paper, pressure and texture, the trace of the human hand translated through metal into language. Every digital typeface we create carries this inheritance.
