Biography
Elara Voss
Founder & Type Director, GLYPH Studio

Born in Leipzig — a city whose streets still remember Gutenberg's legacy — Elara Voss began her obsession with letterforms at the age of nine, when her father placed a composing stick in her hands at his small print shop. She has not stopped thinking about type since.

"Type is the first architecture — the structure that organises thought before thought can be communicated at all."

After studying at the Royal College of Art and completing apprenticeships under two of Europe's foremost type designers, Elara founded GLYPH in 2019 with a single conviction: that the best type emerges from the intersection of historical rigour and contemporary need.

Her work spans custom retail typefaces for global brands to bespoke letterpress editions for cultural institutions. Each project begins with the same question: what does this word want to look like?

GLYPH operates from studios in London's Spitalfields and Berlin's Mitte, maintaining a deliberate proximity to the printing presses that continue to inform every digital character drawn.

Design Philosophy

What We Believe About Type

01 / Tradition
Historical Roots

Every great typeface stands on the shoulders of centuries. We study the historical record not to replicate it, but to understand what makes a letterform durable. Garamond, Baskerville, Gill — these are teachers, not constraints.

02 / Craft
Material Truth

We sketch with pencil before we open Glyphs. We proof on press before we issue final files. The act of making type physical grounds every digital decision in a material truth that screen-only designers cannot access.

03 / Function
Purpose Before Style

A typeface must work — at 8pt in a footnote and at 180pt on a billboard. Style is the result of resolving functional problems with elegance. We design for every context a letter might inhabit.
