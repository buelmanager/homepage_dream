Our Process

From Enquiry
to Installation

01
Initial Consultation
We discuss your vision, event, space, and timeline in a 45-minute call or meeting.
02
Concept & Proposal
We develop 2–3 sculptural concepts with sketches, scale references, and a detailed quote.
03
Ice Reservation
On approval, we reserve your ice blocks from the Norwegian harvest and confirm logistics.
04
Carving & Transport
Your sculpture is carved in our atelier, then transported in refrigerated containers to your venue.
05
Installation & Care
Our team installs the piece on-site, with humidity and temperature management throughout your event.
