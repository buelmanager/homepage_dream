Why Workshop With Us

The World's
Finest Ice School

Since 2015, our workshop programme has introduced hundreds of students — from corporate teams to serious craftspeople — to the discipline of ice carving. Every session takes place in our working atelier, using the same −40°C Norwegian ice we carve for royal commissions and five-star hotels.

You will be taught directly by our master carvers, in groups capped at six participants. No compromise on material. No compromise on instruction. You leave with a carved sculpture, professional photographs, and a skill that almost no one on earth has.

The Experience

What Happens
on the Day

01
Arrival & Preparation
Welcome to the atelier. Safety briefing, equipment fitting, and a walk-through of the tools you will be using. Your ice block — already waiting at working temperature.
02
Theory & Demonstration
Your master carver demonstrates the technique before you begin. Learn how ice responds differently to each tool, and why temperature changes everything about the cut.
03
Guided Carving
You carve. Your instructor stands beside you, guiding each cut. There is no template — you draw directly on the ice and follow your own line. Supervised, but never controlled.
04
Critique & Documentation
Your finished sculpture is photographed and filmed by our atelier photographer. You receive a formal critique from your instructor and leave with a portfolio of your work.

Questions

Frequently
Asked

Can't find what you need? Write to us at workshops@frostandco.art

Do I need any prior experience?
No experience is necessary for the Discovery or Artisan tiers. The Master Class is suitable for all levels, though participants with some carving or sculpture background will progress further in the three days.
What should I wear?
The carving studio is maintained at −5°C. We provide insulated overalls, gloves, eye protection, and steel-toed boots. Wear warm base layers and bring thick socks. We recommend avoiding loose jewellery or scarves.
Can I take my sculpture home?
You are welcome to take your sculpture home if you have appropriate transport with a freezer facility. We can also arrange local delivery within 48 hours. Most participants prefer to leave their work in the atelier's display freezer for 24 hours and collect professional photographs instead.
Is this suitable as a corporate team activity?
Absolutely. Our corporate workshop packages are available for groups of 6–24 participants, with dedicated sessions outside our standard timetable. We can incorporate your brand into the carving brief. Contact us for group pricing.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made more than 14 days before the session receive a full refund. Within 14 days, we offer a credit valid for 12 months. No-shows and same-day cancellations are non-refundable.
Are the workshops available in languages other than English?
Sessions in Oslo are available in Norwegian and English. Our London studio operates in English. Dubai sessions are available in English and Arabic. We can arrange French and German interpretation on request for Master Class bookings.
