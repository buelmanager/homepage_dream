Our Origin

The Atelier
Begins

In 2004, master carver Erik Halvorsen returned from the World Ice Art Championships in Fairbanks, Alaska, with a silver medal and an unwavering conviction: that ice sculpture deserved the same reverence as marble, bronze, or glass.

He established FROST & CO in a converted warehouse on the Oslo waterfront, with two chainsaws, a band saw, and three tonnes of Norwegian glacial ice. The early commissions were modest — hotel centrepieces, wedding displays — but the craft was never modest.

Within five years, FROST & CO had been invited to install at the Burj Al Arab in Dubai, the Hôtel de Crillon in Paris, and the Palace Hotel in Tokyo. The name had become synonymous with the highest tier of ephemeral luxury art.

Today, the atelier employs twelve master carvers, operates studios in Oslo, London, and Dubai, and has completed over two hundred and twenty commissions across forty countries. Every piece is still carved from the same −40°C ultra-clear Norwegian ice. Every cut is still made by hand.

The Material

Where Our
Ice Comes From

The source is Hardangerfjord, Norway — one of the world's deepest and clearest bodies of water. For six months each year, from November through April, we work with a single harvesting partner whose family has supplied Norwegian ice for four generations.

The water is drawn from 40 metres below the surface, where particulate matter is virtually absent. It is frozen in climate-controlled chambers over a period of twelve days, at exactly −40°C. The slow freeze eliminates microbubbles entirely, producing optical clarity that borders on the supernatural.

When a block arrives at the atelier, it is stored at −18°C until the day of carving. Working temperature for our sculptors is −5°C — cold enough to preserve the ice, warm enough to allow precise toolwork. The race against time and temperature is what makes ice sculpture unlike any other art form.

Our Principles

The Philosophy
of Ice

01
Clarity
We refuse lesser ice. Every commission begins with the same −40°C Norwegian glacial block, regardless of scale. Optical perfection is not a premium — it is the standard.
02
Precision
Our carvers work to the millimetre. No template, no 3D-printed guide — only a master carver, a hand tool, and forty years of collective experience distilled into each cut.
03
Impermanence
Ice melts. That is not a flaw — it is the point. The most powerful art speaks through its disappearance. We make things beautiful precisely because they will not last.
