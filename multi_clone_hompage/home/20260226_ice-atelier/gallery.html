Installations

"Ice is the only medium that insists on its own end. That insistence is what makes it art."
— Erik Halvorsen

Our fine art practice runs parallel to our commercial commissions. These works are conceived without brief — pure sculptural explorations of form, light, and the passage of time.

They have been installed in the Museum of Contemporary Art Stockholm, the Bergen Kunsthall, the Palais de Tokyo, and the Design Museum London. Several have been commissioned by private collectors for controlled indoor display.
