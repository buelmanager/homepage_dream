The Material

Not All Ice
Is Created Equal

We source exclusively from Norwegian glacial water, frozen under controlled conditions at −40°C. The result is ice of extraordinary optical clarity — so transparent it vanishes, leaving only the art.

Each block weighs between 150 and 400 kilograms, harvested in limited quantities. Our master carvers work against time and temperature to reveal the sculpture within.
