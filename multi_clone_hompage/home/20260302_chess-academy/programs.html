Excellence in Chess

Grandmaster Academy has been producing chess champions for over six decades. Our grandmaster coaching staff, structured curriculum, and tournament support system create the ideal environment for rapid chess improvement at every level.

Whether you are an ambitious club player seeking to break 2000, or a titled player targeting grandmaster norms, we have a program designed for your exact goals and current level.

Our Coaching Staff

All training at Grandmaster Academy is conducted by active or recently retired FIDE grandmasters with proven track records of developing titled students. We employ fourteen grandmasters across our Zürich facility and online platform.
