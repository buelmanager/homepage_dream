Training Philosophy

The Science of Strategic Thinking

Chess mastery is built on three interlocking disciplines, each essential to reaching the grandmaster level.

01

Opening Mastery

A strong opening repertoire is the foundation of competitive chess. We teach principled opening play — understanding the ideas behind every move rather than memorizing lines by rote.

02

Tactical Precision

Combinations, sacrifices, and calculation depth separate average players from masters. Our tactical training system builds pattern recognition through 10,000+ carefully sequenced exercises.

03

Endgame Technique

Games are won and lost in the endgame. We ensure our students master king-and-pawn endings, rook endings, and theoretical drawn positions — converting every won advantage.

Enrollment Journey

Your Path to Mastery

01

Assessment Game

Complete a 15-minute online rapid game for our coaching team to assess your current level, playing style, and key weaknesses to address in training.

02

Coaching Match

We pair you with the grandmaster coach whose expertise and teaching style best matches your goals — whether that's tournament preparation, opening repertoire, or endgame mastery.

03

Personalised Study Plan

Your GM coach crafts a bespoke 90-day training plan addressing your specific weaknesses and setting measurable rating milestones to track progress objectively.

04

Weekly Sessions & Analysis

Regular one-on-one video sessions supplement daily training exercises, self-study, and tournament participation with real-time annotation and feedback.

05

Tournament & Review

Compete in rated over-the-board and online tournaments sponsored by the Academy. Post-tournament game reviews with your GM coach transform each result into accelerated improvement.

Our History

Six Decades of Chess Excellence

1963

Academy Founded

GM Viktor Steinhardt establishes the Academy in Zürich to train the next generation of tournament players with scientific rigour.

1981

First World Junior

Academy alumnus Leon Brandt claims the World Junior Chess Championship — the first of fourteen world-level titles for Grandmaster Academy students.

2004

Digital Platform Launch

The Academy launches its proprietary online training platform, extending grandmaster-level coaching to students in sixty countries worldwide.

2019

GM Candidate Program

An elite track for norm-seekers launches with five participants. To date, eleven students have earned GM or IM titles through the programme.
