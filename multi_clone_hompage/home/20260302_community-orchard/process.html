Getting Involved

How to Join

There is no application process, no interview, and no membership fee. The commons is open to all who want to grow, learn, and share. Here is how most people find their way in.

From First Visit to Site Steward

01

Come to an Open Orchard Day

First Sundays of each month at all our major sites are open to anyone — no registration needed. Walk the food forest, taste what's in season, meet current members and stewards. Most people who join do so after just one visit.

02

Register as a Member (Free)

Fill in a short form online or at any site. Membership gives you access to all 18 growing spaces, all workshops, and the seed library. There are no subscription fees, ever.

03

Choose Your Level of Involvement

Some members come every week. Others come twice a year at harvest. Some contribute to governance. Every level of engagement is equally valued. We accommodate fluctuating availability — life changes, and so can your involvement.

04

Join a Working Group

Soil health, water management, seed saving, new site development, children's programming — there is a working group for nearly every skill and interest. Join one to go deeper and help shape the collective's direction.

05

Apply to Steward a Site

Experienced members can take on site stewardship — coordinating a growing space, mentoring newcomers, and representing the site in collective governance. Stewards receive additional growing resources and formal permaculture training.

06

Start a New Site

If you know of suitable land — disused allotments, neglected parks, housing association gardens — bring it to the collective. Our new sites team can help with negotiations, design, and the first planting day.

Member Resources

Free Workshops

Composting, pruning, seed saving, soil biology, water harvesting — monthly workshops at various sites, always free to members.

Harvest Shares

Active members receive seasonal harvest boxes from communal growing beds. Surplus is shared with local food banks and community kitchens.

Tool Lending

Every site has a shared tool store. Members can also borrow tools for home gardens — a small way the commons extends beyond our shared land.

Start Growing

Show up to an Open Orchard Day or send us a message. The orchard is waiting.
