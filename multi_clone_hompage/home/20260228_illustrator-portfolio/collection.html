Personal Works

Series made for
no one but the work itself

Nocturne Series — 2025

Studies in Absence

12 watercolour studies on the quality of light in empty rooms. Shown at Flowers Gallery, London. A meditation on waiting and the shape of time when no one is present.

The Moth Series — 2023

Drawn to Light

Ink and watercolour on Japanese Kozo paper — 8 works exploring the paradox of attraction to what destroys. Limited edition of 50 prints, hand-signed.

Ghost Cartographies — 2022

Maps of Unmade Places

A series of mixed-media works combining ink drawing and photographic collage. Exhibited at Nanzuka Underground, Tokyo. Concerned with the geography of grief.
