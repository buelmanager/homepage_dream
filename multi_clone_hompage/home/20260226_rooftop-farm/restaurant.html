The Approach

A Menu With
No Distance

Chef Margaux Vidal walks the rooftop every morning at 7am with Dr. Karim Nassar. They taste. They observe. What is ready, what is at peak, what has changed overnight — this determines tonight's menu.

There is no written menu until mid-afternoon. No promises of a specific dish. Only the certainty that every ingredient was alive forty metres above you this morning.

Seven courses, each one a direct translation of what the roof offered. Paired with natural wines chosen to speak the same language as the soil they came from.

Margaux Vidal
Executive Chef, VERT

Natural Wine

Wines That
Speak Soil

Our sommelier, Léa Dumas, selects exclusively from growers who farm biodynamically — growers who, like us, believe that the best flavour lives in honest land.

Pairings are matched to what the rooftop is offering that evening — structured enough to hold the savoury courses, alive enough to sing with the garden.

Loire · Organic
Muscadet Sèvre et Maine, Luneau-Papin
Sea-mineral, citrus bloom — opens the oyster and garden courses
Burgundy · Biodynamic
Chablis Premier Cru, Domaine Pinson
Chalk, white flower, green apple — bridges the fish course
Rhône · Natural
Côtes du Rhône Rouge, La Gramière
Dark berry, wild herb, silky tannin — pairs the pigeon perfectly
Alsace · Biodynamic
Gewurztraminer SGN, Zind-Humbrecht
