Growing Zones

Twelve Distinct
Micro-Climates

Each zone is calibrated for humidity, shade, and substrate depth — creating distinct micro-environments across the rooftop's 2,400 square metres.

Extended Season

Spring–Autumn

Our Methods

How We Grow

I
Living Substrate

600 tonnes of custom substrate — coco coir, perlite, biochar, earthworm castings, and mycorrhizal inoculant. A living ecosystem, not inert growing medium.

II
Closed-Loop Composting

All kitchen waste returns as compost to the growing beds within 8 weeks. The restaurant below feeds the farm above in a continuous biological loop.

III
Rainwater Harvesting

180,000 litre cistern collects Paris rooftop rainfall. Blended with municipal water and filtered through biochar for a gentle, mineral-rich irrigation feed.

IV
Integrated Pest Management

No synthetic pesticide ever. Companion planting, beneficial insect habitat, and the bees themselves create a balanced pest-management ecosystem.

The Apiary

Twelve Colonies,
One Roof

Our Buckfast colonies were selected for gentleness, winter hardiness, and remarkable honey yield. The bees forage Paris parks and gardens within a 5km radius — every jar of VERT honey is a portrait of the city's flora.

Master beekeeper Thomas Roux manages the colonies with the same seasonal attentiveness Karim brings to the growing beds. Each colony is inspected weekly — frame by frame, queen by queen.
