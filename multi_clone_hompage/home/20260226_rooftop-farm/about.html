between field and fork disappeared entirely.

The Beginning

From a Rooftop
No One Wanted

In 2019, architect Élise Fontaine stood on the vacant 2,400 m² rooftop of Tour La Verdure and saw not a concrete wasteland, but the most radical short-supply-chain restaurant possible. Dirt would be hoisted by crane. Seeds would be selected by a sommelier of plants.

The question she asked was simple: what if the distance between soil and plate was measured in steps, not kilometres? Three years later, VERT opened its doors — or rather, its rooftop hatch — to forty guests at a time.

Today, VERT supplies 100% of its table's produce from the farm directly above. Nothing leaves the building. Every course begins not in a kitchen, but in a growing bed.

Our Philosophy

"The city is not separate from nature. It is where nature's ingenuity is most urgently needed."

— Élise Fontaine, Founder

Zero Distance

From harvest to plate in under 45 minutes. No refrigerated truck. No depot. No compromise on freshness.

Living Soil

Our substrate is alive — earthworm castings, mycorrhizal networks, compost from the kitchen below. A closed loop.

Seasonal Truth

No menu. Just what the rooftop offers today. The chef writes the courses each morning after the harvest walk.

The People

The Agronomist,
the Chef & the Founder

Élise Fontaine
Founder & Architect

Former urban planning architect turned rooftop farmer. Élise designed every growing zone and the structural system that supports 600 tonnes of growing substrate over a car park.

Dr. Karim Nassar
Head Agronomist

PhD in urban agronomy from AgroParisTech. Karim manages the twelve growing zones, bee colony health, and seasonal crop rotation across 200+ varieties.

Chef Margaux Vidal
Executive Chef

Two Michelin stars before VERT. Margaux joins Karim for the morning harvest walk every day without exception — the menu is written in the garden, not the kitchen.

Milestones

How We Grew

2019
The Roof is Acquired

Élise Fontaine secures a 25-year lease on the 2,400 m² rooftop of Tour La Verdure after six months of structural assessment and city council negotiation.

2020
First Growing Season

600 tonnes of growing substrate hoisted by crane. First crops germinate in spring — microgreens, herbs, radishes. Dr. Nassar joins as lead agronomist.

2021
The Restaurant Below Opens

Chef Margaux Vidal joins and the 40-cover restaurant opens directly below the farm. First night: full farm-to-table menu in under 45 minutes from harvest.

2022
First Michelin Star

VERT awarded its first Michelin star — cited for "the most radical expression of seasonal cooking in Paris." Twelve bee colonies installed.

2024
Second Michelin Star & Farm Tours Launch

A second star awarded. Guided farm tours open to the public — led by Karim and the beekeeper. Sold out within hours of opening reservations.
