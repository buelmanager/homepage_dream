BALTURA Amber House

Baltic Sea
Amber Atelier

BALTURA has crafted Baltic amber jewelry from Gdańsk since 1961. Founded by Henryk Kasprzak on ul. Mariacka — the historic amber street of the old city — the atelier now spans three generations of the Kasprzak family.

Baltic amber is fossilised tree resin from forests that stood where the Baltic Sea now lies, approximately 44 million years ago. It is found in 180 documented colour varieties, from the common cognac and honey tones to the rarest white and blue specimens.

Each BALTURA piece is made around the stone — not the reverse. Natalia Kasprzak selects every raw amber specimen personally. The setting is then designed to serve the stone's internal light and character. Visit the main page for our full commission form and current collection.
