Living Worlds

Five Ecosystems

Each terrarium is a self-sustaining micro-ecosystem — chosen and curated to thrive within its glass architecture for decades.

🌿
Tropical Rainforest
Humidity-loving ferns, orchids and bromeliads in a lush, layered canopy within glass.
🪨
Desert Diorama
Sculptural cacti, agave and mineral rock formations — arid beauty in geometric glass.
🌱
Moss Garden
Living carpet of cushion moss, stone and dwarf ferns — serene Japanese-inspired landscapes.
🍃
Fern Grotto
Ancient fronds, maidenhair and wood ferns cascading over volcanic basalt and driftwood.
🪤
Carnivorous
Venus flytraps, sundews and pitcher plants — nature's most theatrical micro-predators in glass.
How It Works

The Commission Process

From initial vision to living installation — a four-stage atelier process designed for precision and ease.

01
💬
Consultation
A private session to understand your space, aesthetic vision, light conditions and ecosystem preference. We listen before we design.
02
✏️
Design
Our botanical designers produce hand-drawn plans and material samples — glasswork, soil substrate, plant selection and hardscape elements.
03
🔨
Build
Hand-crafted in our London atelier over 4–12 weeks. Glass blown or cast to specification. Plants propagated and conditioned on site.
04
🌿
Deliver & Maintain
White-glove installation by our team. Optional monthly maintenance subscription ensures your living work of art thrives indefinitely.
