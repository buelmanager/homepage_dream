# Design Decisions — The Loom School

## Color
Deep amber-brown (#1C1008) evokes the earth tones of natural dye and raw timber looms. The rust accent (#D4612A) references cochineal — the most prized natural dye in Oaxacan tradition — and iron-rich ochre.

## Typography
Cinzel's Roman capital forms suggest both classical inscription and the formal structure of a loom's warp. Crimson Pro's warm old-style italics carry the organic qualities of natural materials.

## Hero (Type E Grid)
The canvas grid pattern represents the warp threads of an undressed loom — pure structure awaiting the weft. Corner accents frame the composition like a textile border, grounding the design in the material world.

## Animation (A5 Organic)
Random y-values (20-28px) and random durations (1.0-1.4s) produce a naturalistic, non-mechanical entrance — like threads settling into cloth. No two elements land at exactly the same moment.

## Layout
A preloader with animated warp threads reinforces the school's identity before any content loads. The loom-inspired pattern animation immediately signals textile thinking.
