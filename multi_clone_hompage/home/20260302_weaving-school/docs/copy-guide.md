# Copy Guide — The Loom School

## Voice
- Earthy, patient, deeply knowledgeable
- Respects the cultural contexts of traditions being taught
- Uses technical terminology correctly and without apology
- Never trendy, never folksy — serious craft education

## Key Messages
1. Indigenous Zapotec heritage preserved across three generations
2. Technical mastery before creative expression
3. Natural dye as ecological and historical practice
4. The loom as philosophical object, not just tool
5. Small cohorts, personal mentorship

## Avoid
- Lifestyle imagery or language
- "Handcrafted" as marketing word
- Claims of uniqueness or exclusivity
- Any appropriation of cultural heritage without attribution
