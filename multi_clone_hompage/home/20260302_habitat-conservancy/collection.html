Ancient Woodland
Ashgrove Old-Growth Reserve
12,400 ha · Herefordshire
Our founding reserve — a continuous tract of primary oak-ash woodland supporting red kite, dormouse, and 340+ lichen species.

Wetland & Fen
Broadmere Fen Complex
38,000 ha · East Anglia
Europe's largest rewetted lowland fen — 40,000 tonnes of CO₂ sequestered annually and habitat for bittern, marsh harrier, and fen orchid.

Native Woodland
Penbryn Native Woodland
22,600 ha · Mid Wales
Planted with native provenance oak, birch, rowan, and hazel across former upland sheep pasture. Now 22 years into natural succession.

Upland Heath
Cairn Mor Blanket Bog
64,000 ha · Scottish Borders
Our largest single reserve — rewetted blanket bog storing millennia of peat carbon. Golden plover, curlew, and short-eared owl breed here.

Native Grassland
Chalkdown Wildflower Meadows
8,200 ha · Wiltshire
Traditional unimproved chalk grassland restored from arable. Over 90 wildflower species per hectare — including 12 rare orchid species.

Rewilding
Marches Rewilding Zone
45,800 ha · Welsh Borders
Our flagship passive rewilding experiment — 28 years of hands-off management creating a complex mosaic of scrub, woodland, and wetland.
