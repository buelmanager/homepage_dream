Craftsmanship

The Box is
Part of the Gift

Every Bento Bijou box is a collector's piece made to be kept. We work exclusively with three artisan suppliers to source our containers.

01
Wajima Lacquerware

Seven-layer urushi lacquer applied by master craftsmen in Wajima, Ishikawa. Each piece is polished by hand for 8 hours.

02
Hinoki Cedar Inserts

Food-grade Kyoto hinoki cedar dividers, lightly scented. They regulate moisture and honor the tradition of wooden bento boxes.

03
Nishijin Silk Furoshiki

Each box is wrapped in a seasonal furoshiki woven on looms in Nishijin, Kyoto — yours to keep as a gift in itself.

04
Hand-Printed Cards

Each order includes a seasonal card printed on Echizen washi paper with a hand-brushed ink note from our artisans.
