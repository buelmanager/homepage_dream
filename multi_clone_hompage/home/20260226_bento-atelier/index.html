The Process

The Art of
Every Box

Each bento is composed like a painting. Ingredients are sourced from trusted farmers in Kyoto and Osaka, arranged by color temperature and texture, then sealed inside hand-lacquered wooden boxes made by artisans in Wajima.

01
Seasonal Sourcing

We visit our farm partners each week to select ingredients at peak ripeness. No ingredient travels more than 80km to our studio.

02
Color Composition

Our master artisans study the color temperature of each ingredient — warm, cool, neutral — and compose each layer like a seasonal painting.

03
Lacquer & Seal

Each box is finished in Wajima-nuri lacquer, hand-polished over seven layers. A box that is also a keepsake.

Learn The Art

The Bento
Workshop

Our intimate studio classes teach the principles of Japanese bento — color harmony, ingredient preparation, placement geometry, and the philosophy of ichi-ju san-sai (one soup, three sides).

Seasonal Half-Day Class

3-hour class for up to 6 participants. Includes materials, ingredients, and your finished bento to take home.

Private Master Class

One-on-one with our head artisan. 5-hour immersive experience covering knife techniques, sauces, and box composition.

Team Events

Corporate team-building for 10–30 participants. Available on weekends. Catering and sake pairing add-ons available.
