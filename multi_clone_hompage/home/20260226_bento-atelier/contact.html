Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you deliver outside Kyoto?

We deliver within Kyoto and Osaka prefectures using our refrigerated courier service. For Tokyo and international deliveries, we ship our dry goods (preserved bento elements) via Yamato Kuroneko overnight. Please inquire for details.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Yes. We accommodate vegetarian, vegan, nut-free, and gluten-free requirements. Please specify in your inquiry. Note that our kitchen processes fish and shellfish; severe seafood allergies should be discussed in detail.

What happens to the lacquered box after?

The Wajima lacquer boxes are yours to keep. They are food-safe and can be hand-washed. Many of our guests use them for decades. They are designed to be heirloom objects.

Can I choose the ingredients?

For seasonal editions, our artisans select every ingredient based on the week's market. For bespoke commissions and corporate orders, we work with you on a color and flavor brief. Ingredient-level customization is available for private master class commissions.

How far in advance must I book a workshop?

Seasonal half-day classes: 1 week minimum. Private master class: 2 weeks minimum. Corporate events: 3 weeks minimum. Weekend classes fill up 2–3 weeks in advance, so we recommend booking early.

Is there a minimum order for corporate gifting?

The minimum corporate order is 20 boxes. This allows us to source ingredients in the correct quantities and maintain the quality we require. For orders of 100+ boxes, please contact us at least 8 weeks in advance.
