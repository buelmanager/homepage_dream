About the Classes

More Than
a Cooking Class

Our workshops are not tutorials — they are immersions into a way of seeing food. You will learn to read a season through its ingredients, to balance color with nutrition, to cut a vegetable in a way that honors its geometry.


Led by our studio artisans, every class is limited to six participants to ensure each guest receives genuine personal attention. All skill levels welcome. No prior cooking experience necessary.

Sample Schedule

A Morning in
Our Studio

The Seasonal Half-Day class runs from 9am to noon. Here is what a typical session looks like when the season is autumn.

09:00
Seasonal Briefing

Hana introduces this week's ingredients — their origins, peak ripeness, and what makes them ideal for this season's bento.

09:20
Knife Technique

We practice five foundational cuts — the chrysanthemum radish, maple leaf carrot, feather cucumber, crane egg, and fan ginger.

09:55
Rice & Sauce

Matsutake rice preparation. Making a proper dashi stock. Reducing a teriyaki glaze to the precise consistency.

10:40
Box Composition

The art of placement. We discuss color temperature, height, negative space (ma), and the five-color rule. You arrange your own bento.

11:30
Photo & Tasting

Photograph your creation in our light studio. Enjoy your bento with houjicha tea in the garden. Receive your furoshiki wrap to take it home.
