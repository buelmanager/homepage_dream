Search Methodology

Five Phases.
One Guarantee.

Meridian's search methodology has been refined over 37 years and thousands of engagements. It is built for speed without compromise, depth without delay, and a result that endures long after the placement is made.

We deliver a qualified shortlist in 30–45 days. We stand behind every placement with a 24-month guarantee. And we remain a partner through integration and beyond.

The Five Phases

Built for Precision
at Every Stage.

Each phase is sequential by design, with clear deliverables, defined timelines, and regular client communication throughout. Nothing is left to chance.

I
Days 1–5
Phase One
Discovery & Strategic Alignment
Every search begins with a comprehensive discovery process. We conduct deep stakeholder interviews — board members, direct reports, key investors — to understand not just the role, but the leadership challenge behind it.
We map the organization's strategy, culture, and competitive context. We identify the gap between the current state and the leadership that will close it. The output is a Position Specification that is more than a job description — it is a strategic brief.
Deliverables
  • Stakeholder interview synthesis
  • Organizational culture assessment
  • Strategic role specification document
  • Ideal candidate profile with behavioral competencies
  • Competitive market landscape overview
12+
Stakeholders Interviewed
II
Days 5–18
Phase Two
Market Intelligence & Candidate Identification
Armed with the strategic brief, our research team conducts a comprehensive market mapping exercise. We do not limit ourselves to active candidates — in fact, the most exceptional leaders are rarely looking.
We leverage our proprietary database of 280,000+ executive profiles, sector-specific intelligence, and the personal networks of our partners. Target lists typically include 120–180 potential candidates before initial contact.
Deliverables
  • Comprehensive market map (target organizations and individuals)
  • Prioritized long-list of 120–180 potential candidates
  • Competitive benchmarking of compensation ranges
  • Diversity-conscious candidate universe
  • Initial market feedback report
180+
Candidates Mapped
III
Days 18–35
Phase Three
Assessment & Deep Evaluation
Our assessment process goes beyond credentials and career trajectory. We apply a rigorous multi-stage evaluation that examines leadership judgment, decision-making under pressure, stakeholder management, and cultural adaptability.
Every candidate who advances to our shortlist has completed a minimum of three structured interviews with Meridian partners, including our proprietary Leadership Decision Simulation and a 360-degree reference validation process.
Deliverables
  • 3+ structured partner interviews per candidate
  • Leadership Decision Simulation results
  • 360-degree reference validation (8–12 references)
  • Psychometric and cognitive assessment where appropriate
  • Detailed candidate evaluation reports
8–12
References Per Candidate
IV
Days 35–50
Phase Four
Shortlist Presentation & Client Process
We present a curated shortlist of 3–5 exceptional candidates, each accompanied by a comprehensive profile including our detailed evaluation, competency assessment, and recommendation rationale.
We facilitate the client interview process, provide structured feedback frameworks, and manage candidate experience throughout — ensuring that every finalist's engagement with your organization reflects the same standard of excellence you expect from your leadership.
Deliverables
  • Curated shortlist of 3–5 candidates
  • Comprehensive candidate profile packages
  • Structured interview guides and frameworks
  • Client-candidate interface management
  • Decision support and recommendation advisory
3–5
Shortlisted Candidates
V
Day 50+
Phase Five
Placement, Integration & Guarantee
The search does not end at offer acceptance. Our integration support program is designed to maximize the probability of success in the critical first 24 months — the period when most executive failures occur.
We conduct regular check-ins with both the placed leader and key stakeholders, provide onboarding advisory support, and stand behind every placement with our industry-leading 24-month guarantee. If the placement does not succeed, we conduct a replacement search at no additional fee.
Deliverables
  • Offer negotiation and closing support
  • 90-day onboarding integration plan
  • Quarterly check-ins for the first 12 months
  • Stakeholder alignment facilitation
  • 24-month placement guarantee

The Meridian Guarantee

We Stand Behind
Every Placement.

The 24-month Meridian Guarantee is not a marketing promise. It is a contractual commitment that reflects our confidence in our process and our understanding of what it means to be truly accountable.

If an executive we place departs or is terminated for performance reasons within 24 months of their start date, we will conduct a replacement search at no additional professional fee. No qualifications. No fine print. No exceptions.

In 37 years of practice, our guarantee has been invoked fewer than 6% of the time. Our 94% retention rate speaks to what happens when the right process finds the right person for the right role.

Meridian operates exclusively on a retained basis. Our fee is calculated as a percentage of the placed executive's first-year total compensation. We require one-third upon engagement, one-third upon shortlist delivery, and one-third upon placement. This structure ensures complete alignment between our work and your outcome.
Confidentiality is fundamental to every engagement. We sign mutual NDAs with all clients. We never disclose a search assignment to candidates without explicit client consent. All candidate interactions are conducted under strict confidentiality protocols, and no candidate's name or details are ever shared with third parties.

The guarantee applies to all retained engagements and is contractually binding. It does not apply to departures resulting from organizational restructuring, merger, or acquisition.
