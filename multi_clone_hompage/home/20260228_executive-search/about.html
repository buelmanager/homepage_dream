About Meridian

Nearly Four
Decades of
Leadership.

Founded in 1987, Meridian Executive Search & Advisory has spent nearly four decades at the intersection of leadership, strategy, and organizational transformation. We are not a staffing agency. We are a leadership advisory firm — built to place the leaders who build industries.

With offices in New York, London, and Singapore, our reach is global. Our knowledge is local. And our commitment to every client and every candidate is absolute.

Our History

From One Principle
to a Global Practice.

Every milestone in our history was built on the same foundation: that leadership placement is a sacred responsibility, not a transactional one.

1987
Meridian Is Founded
Reginald Holt and Eleanor Bain establish Meridian Executive Search in Midtown Manhattan with a focus on CEO and board placements for Fortune 100 corporations.
1994
London Office Opens
Expanding into EMEA markets, Meridian London is established to serve the growing demand for cross-border executive placements in financial services and energy.
2001
500th Placement Milestone
Meridian places its 500th executive — a CFO for a NYSE-listed industrial conglomerate. The 93% retention rate at this point surpasses industry norms by 28%.
2008
Leadership Through Crisis
During the global financial crisis, Meridian pioneers its "Crisis Leadership Assessment" methodology, placing 40 turnaround CEOs and restructuring CFOs across 3 continents.
2014
Singapore Office & Asia-Pacific Expansion
Strategic expansion into Asia-Pacific with Singapore as regional headquarters, establishing sector teams in technology, consumer, and healthcare for the region.
2020
Digital Transformation Practice
Launch of dedicated Technology Leadership and Digital Transformation practice, responding to explosive demand for CTOs and Chief Digital Officers in a post-pandemic world.
2024
2,400 Placements & Counting
Meridian celebrates 2,400 cumulative executive placements with a 94% two-year retention rate — the highest in our firm's history and among the highest in the industry globally.
Our Values

The Principles That
Drive Everything.

We hold ourselves to a standard that most search firms do not attempt. These values are not aspirational — they are operational. They define how we work, every day, with every client.

01
Absolute Discretion
Every engagement is conducted with complete confidentiality. We protect the interests of our clients and our candidates with equal rigor. No details are ever disclosed without explicit consent.
02
Intellectual Courage
We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. Our assessments are grounded in evidence, not flattery. The right placement sometimes requires difficult counsel.
03
Long-Term Thinking
Our reputation is measured in decades, not quarters. We place leaders for the organization they are becoming, not the organization they are today. Fit for the future matters as much as fit for the present.
04
Relentless Rigor
Our assessment methodology goes beyond competencies to evaluate judgment, character, and leadership under ambiguity. We do not shortlist candidates we are not prepared to fully endorse.
05
Partnership, Not Transaction
The relationship does not end at placement. We provide 24-month onboarding support, integration advisory, and remain a long-term advisor to every leader we place.
06
Diversity as Strategy
We actively expand the aperture of every search to include candidates who are qualified but historically overlooked. Diverse leadership creates stronger, more resilient organizations.
