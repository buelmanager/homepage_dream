Our Story

Built on Precedent.
Defined by Results.

In the winter of 1891, Archibald Caldwell and Reginald Whitmore established their chambers at 1 Caldwell Court in the City of London. Their founding conviction was simple: that the finest legal minds, operating with absolute integrity, would always command the most consequential mandates.

One hundred and thirty years later, that conviction remains the foundation of everything we do. We have grown from two founding partners to 340 attorneys across offices in London, New York, Hong Kong, Dubai, Frankfurt, and Singapore — yet our mandate has never changed. We counsel those for whom legal excellence is not optional.

Firm History

A Legacy of Firsts

Select milestones in our 130-year history of shaping the global legal and financial landscape.

Founding

Archibald Caldwell and Reginald Whitmore establish chambers at 1 Caldwell Court, London. The firm's first mandate: advising the London & North Western Railway on its merger with the Midland Railway — then the largest transaction in British corporate history.

1891
1924
New York Office Opens

Following the firm's landmark role in the post-WWI reconstruction of European sovereign debt, Caldwell opens its first transatlantic office at Rockefeller Plaza, New York.

Hong Kong Expansion

Recognising the rise of Asian capital markets, Caldwell & Partners establishes its Hong Kong practice, becoming the first Western law firm to advise on a Hang Seng-listed privatisation.

1972
1997
$1 Trillion Milestone

The firm becomes the first law firm in history to have advised on a cumulative $1 trillion in closed transactions, as recognised by The Financial Times.

Global Footprint

Offices open in Dubai and Singapore, completing the firm's coverage of every major financial centre. The firm's attorney count surpasses 300 for the first time.

2008
2024
$2.4 Trillion in Deals Closed

Caldwell & Partners surpasses $2.4 trillion in cumulative deal value across M&A, capital markets, and private equity — cementing its position as the world's leading transactional law firm.
