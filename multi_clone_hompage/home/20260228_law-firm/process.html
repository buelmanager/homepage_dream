Our Approach

A Different
Kind of Counsel

At Caldwell & Partners, we do not simply advise — we immerse ourselves in the objectives, constraints, and character of every mandate we accept. Our engagement model is built around a single principle: the right partner, deeply involved, from day one to completion.

We accept a limited number of new client mandates each year. This deliberate restraint is not a constraint — it is a covenant. It allows each matter to receive the full, undivided attention of our most senior attorneys, from the initial structuring conversation through final execution.

"We do not delegate excellence. Every Caldwell client benefits from partner-led counsel at every stage of their matter — not associate staffing at partner rates."

— Jonathan Caldwell-Whitmore, Senior Managing Partner
The Process

Six Stages of
Engagement

From your initial enquiry to the conclusion of your matter, every stage is governed by the Caldwell Standard.

01
Initiation

Initial Enquiry & Conflict Check

All new enquiries are received by a designated partner — never an associate or receptionist. Within 24 hours, we conduct a comprehensive conflict check across our global client register. You will receive a substantive response, not a form letter, within one business day.

Dedicated Partner Contact
Your enquiry reaches a named senior partner immediately
24-Hour Conflict Resolution
Comprehensive global conflict check completed within one business day
02
Discovery

Strategic Consultation

Before any engagement letter is issued, we invest time understanding your objectives — commercial, legal, and strategic. This consultation is conducted under attorney-client privilege and is typically held in person at one of our global offices or at your location.

Protected by Privilege
All pre-engagement conversations held under full attorney-client privilege
Partner-Led Discovery
Senior partners conduct all initial strategic consultations
03
Engagement

Team Assembly & Mandate Structuring

We assemble a bespoke team for each mandate — cross-practice, cross-border, and always led by a named senior partner with direct accountability. No templates. No generic staffing. A team built specifically for the legal and jurisdictional demands of your matter.

Bespoke Team Assembly
Practice specialists selected by the mandate's specific demands
Partner Accountability
One named senior partner holds ultimate responsibility for your matter
04
Execution

Negotiation & Documentation

The substance of our work. Whether drafting definitive agreements, leading multi-party negotiations, or managing regulatory filings across jurisdictions, we execute with the precision that 130 years of practise has made our defining characteristic.

Drafting Excellence
Documentation of the highest standard, reviewed by multiple senior partners
Multi-Jurisdictional Coordination
Seamless management of regulatory and legal processes across 48 jurisdictions
05
Completion

Closing & Post-Completion

Completion is not the end of our counsel. We manage all closing mechanics, regulatory filings, and post-completion obligations with the same rigour applied to execution. We remain on call for any matter that arises from the transaction.

Closing Management
Full management of closing mechanics, conditions precedent, and completion
Ongoing Availability
Post-completion counsel available at all times via your named partner
06
Relationship

The Ongoing Relationship

The world's most consequential clients do not change counsel with each matter. They build a relationship. Caldwell & Partners becomes the institution that clients call first — not because of obligation, but because of what 130 years of unbroken excellence inspires.

Proactive Intelligence
Regular briefings on legal developments relevant to your business
Annual Review
Formal annual review of your legal risk profile and strategic objectives
Our Commitment

We Are Defined
by Your Result

We accept that the measure of our counsel is not the elegance of our drafting or the sophistication of our analysis — though both are without parallel. The measure is the result.

"At Caldwell & Partners, we do not advise on transactions — we participate in the outcome of them."

Frequently Asked

Caldwell & Partners excels in matters of significant scale, complexity, or strategic importance — typically M&A transactions, private equity mandates, capital markets transactions, high-value litigation or arbitration, and complex restructurings. If your matter involves multiple jurisdictions, material amounts, or exceptional strategic sensitivity, we encourage you to enquire.
We advise on the nature and complexity of the matter, not the standing or tenure of the client. We have represented family offices, entrepreneurs, sovereign entities, and global corporations with equal commitment. What we require is that the matter meets our threshold for scale and strategic interest.
Our fees are agreed at engagement and reflect the complexity, urgency, and strategic weight of the matter. We offer a range of structures including hourly billing, capped fees, and success fee arrangements where appropriate. We are transparent from the outset — you will never receive a surprise invoice from Caldwell & Partners.
Discretion is not a policy at Caldwell & Partners — it is a founding principle. All communications are protected by attorney-client privilege from the moment of first contact. Our information security standards exceed those required by our regulatory obligations, and we operate a strict conflict screen for all matters.
In competitive situations — hostile takeovers, urgent regulatory responses, time-sensitive arbitrations — we can mobilise a full partner-led team within hours of instruction. Our 24-hour global operations across six offices ensure that urgency never compromises quality. Contact our senior partner on call directly at any hour.
