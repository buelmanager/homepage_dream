Suite Category — 03

Cathedral Cave

The most architecturally dramatic space in all of GROTTO. The Cathedral Cave features a naturally formed double-height chamber soaring twelve metres from floor to ceiling — the result of a singular basalt intrusion event that occurred 8 million years ago. This cave was used as a place of Christian worship in the Byzantine era; three original fresco panels survive on the eastern wall and are illuminated at night by a fiber optic constellation installed by our restoration team.

Suite Category — 04

Royal Cavern

The summit of the GROTTO experience. The Royal Cavern comprises three interconnected cave chambers — each carved from a distinct geological stratum — totalling 280 square metres of subterranean living space. The bedroom chamber traces the original path of an ancient underground river, giving the walls their extraordinary smooth, water-carved curvature. The living chamber displays obsidian-rich tuff from the deepest Cappadocian eruption event. The hammam is cut into pure basalt at a depth of thirty metres, directly over the thermal spring.
