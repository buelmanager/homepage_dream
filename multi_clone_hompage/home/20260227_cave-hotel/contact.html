Before You Inquire

Frequently Asked

The minimum stay is two nights for all suite categories. During peak season (April to October) and for Royal Cavern and Cathedral Cave suites, we recommend a minimum of three nights to fully appreciate the experience.
GROTTO is designed for guests aged 16 and above. The underground passage network and cave environments, while perfectly safe, are not appropriate for younger children. We are happy to recommend family-friendly alternatives in Cappadocia upon request.
GROTTO offers fiber-optic Wi-Fi throughout the cave complex, including all suites and the underground restaurant. Mobile phone signal does not penetrate the rock, which many guests find to be a profound feature rather than an inconvenience.
Free cancellation up to 30 days before check-in. Cancellations between 14 and 30 days are subject to a 50% charge. Within 14 days of check-in, the full stay is charged. We strongly recommend travel insurance for Cappadocian stays due to weather-dependent activities.
Breakfast is included in all suite rates. Dinner at the underground cave restaurant is available on request and charged separately (tasting menu: €180 per person). In-suite private dining, hammam sessions, and spa treatments are also charged separately.
Four of our Grotto Suites are accessible with relatively flat passage access and adapted bathrooms. The Cave Pool, Cathedral, and Royal Cavern suites involve stairways and uneven carved surfaces that may not be suitable for all mobility levels. Please detail your requirements in your inquiry and we will advise accordingly.

Getting Here

The Journey
to Göreme

Cappadocia lies in the heart of central Turkey, 300 kilometres south-east of Ankara. The region has two airports, both with excellent connections from major European and Middle Eastern hubs. GROTTO arranges private transfers for all guests.

Air
Nevşehir Kapadokya Airport (NAV) 45-minute private transfer to GROTTO. Direct flights from Istanbul (1h 20m) and Ankara (55m). Seasonal connections from select European cities.
Air
Kayseri Airport (ASR) 75-minute private transfer to GROTTO. More flight options including low-cost carriers and European connections. Recommended for international guests.
Rail
By Train from Ankara High-speed rail to Kayseri (90 minutes), then private transfer to GROTTO (75 minutes). A scenic alternative for guests arriving from Istanbul or Ankara.
Car
Self-Drive Göreme is 5 hours from Istanbul and 3 hours from Ankara via the E80 motorway. GROTTO provides complimentary secure parking at the valley entrance.
