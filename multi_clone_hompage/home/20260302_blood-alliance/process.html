The Donation Journey

How It Works

Step by Step

From Donor to Patient

A blood donation takes as little as 45 minutes from registration to refreshments. In that time, you provide up to four separate life-saving components to patients who may be waiting hours away. This is what happens to your donation.

The Process

Five Steps, Three Lives

01
Registration
Welcome & Health Screening
You complete a confidential health questionnaire and receive a brief clinical assessment from one of our specialist donor nurses. This takes around 10 minutes and ensures that your donation is safe for both you and the patients who receive it.
02
Donation
Giving Blood
Whole blood donation takes 8–10 minutes. Component donations (platelets, plasma, or stem cells) take 45–90 minutes. You will be comfortable throughout, monitored continuously by our clinical team, and offered refreshments immediately afterwards.
03
Laboratory
Testing & Component Separation
Every unit is tested for 13 infectious diseases within 24 hours. It is then processed into up to four components — red cells, platelets, plasma, and cryoprecipitate — each with a different clinical use and shelf life.
04
Logistics
Cold Chain Delivery
Components are stored at precisely maintained temperatures and dispatched via our 24/7 cold-chain logistics network to 98 partner hospitals. Red cells are delivered within 4 days, platelets within 7, plasma within 12 months.
05
Transfusion
Patient Care
Your components are transfused to patients by hospital clinical teams — surgical patients, cancer patients, mothers in childbirth, accident victims. Outcome data returns to our research programme to continuously improve the entire chain.
