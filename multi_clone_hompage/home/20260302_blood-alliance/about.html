Who We Are

About Crimson Alliance

Our Belief
"Blood cannot be manufactured. It can only be given. Our entire existence is dedicated to honouring that extraordinary act of human generosity."
Leadership

Founded by Clinicians,
Driven by Compassion

Crimson Alliance was founded in 2001 by a group of consultant haematologists who had witnessed, too many times, patients deteriorate because blood was unavailable at the critical moment. Their response was not to accept this as inevitable, but to build a system that made it impossible.

Twenty-five years later, our leadership team combines clinical medicine, logistics, research science, and public health expertise. Governance is independent, transparent, and accountable to both our donors and the patients who depend on us.

Professor James Redwell
Chief Medical Officer & Co-Founder
Our Values

What We Stand For

Clinical Safety
Every unit donated is tested to the highest medical standards. Safety is not a compliance exercise — it is the foundation of trust with every donor and patient we serve.
Donor Respect
Blood donors give a part of themselves voluntarily. We design every experience to honour this extraordinary act with the care, comfort, and recognition it deserves.
Scientific Rigour
Transfusion medicine advances through research. We invest significantly in science that will improve how blood is processed, stored, and used — for the benefit of future patients.
Our History

Twenty-Five Years

2001
Alliance Founded
Established by consultant haematologists with a mandate to build a national voluntary blood donation network to clinical excellence standards.
2007
100 Centres
Expansion to 100 donation centres. Launch of mobile donation fleet serving rural and underserved communities.
2015
Research Laboratory
Opening of the Crimson Transfusion Sciences Laboratory. First peer-reviewed research on component separation efficiency published in The Lancet.
2021
1 Million Lives
Cumulative milestone: Crimson Alliance estimated to have contributed to saving 1 million patient lives through blood supply and transfusion research.
2024
180 Centres, 98 Hospitals
Network at full national scale, processing 420,000+ units annually and supporting 98 hospital partners with 24/7 blood supply logistics.
