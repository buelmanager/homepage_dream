Master a Script

Intimate workshops led by master calligraphers. From complete beginners to practising lettering artists.

Script I

Copperplate

The crown jewel of Western penmanship. Hair-thin upstrokes and swelling downstrokes on a 52° slant.

2 Days Max 8 students
Script II

America's historic business script — oval letterforms, delicate shading, and effortless flow.

The Makers

Our Artisans

Eleanor Marsh

Founder & Master Calligrapher

Trained at the Roehampton Institute under the late Sheila Waters, Eleanor has spent 20 years refining her Copperplate and Spencerian hands. Her work has appeared in Vogue, Condé Nast Brides, and the Royal Collection.

Copperplate Spencerian Engrosser's

James Thorne

Resident Calligrapher & Workshop Lead

A graduate of the Calligraphy Centre at Letter Exchange, James specialises in Italic and Uncial scripts. His live event work has graced openings at the V&A, Claridge's, and Harrods.
