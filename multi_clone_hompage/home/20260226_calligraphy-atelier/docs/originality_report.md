## Source of Inspiration (Category-Level Only)

The following real-world industries and aesthetic traditions informed the design direction at a categorical level:

1. **English luxury craft ateliers** — the visual language of Mayfair and Marylebone artisan businesses (tailors, jewellers, binderies)
2. **Classical calligraphy tradition** — visual reference to Copperplate and Spencerian script aesthetics (pen nibs, ink bottles, parchment textures)
3. **Luxury wedding stationery market** — the tone, vocabulary, and hierarchy of high-end stationery brands
4. **Fine pen brands** — dark, refined aesthetic seen in Montblanc and Visconti brand communications
