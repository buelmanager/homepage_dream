How We Work

The Commission Process

01

Consultation

We discuss your requirements, preferred script, paper, ink colour, and timeline over a call or in-person visit to the atelier.

02

Proposal

We send a detailed proposal with script samples, material specifications, per-unit pricing, and a production schedule.

03

Proof

A handwritten sample page is produced and photographed for your approval before any full-scale work begins.

04

Production

Your commission is executed in the atelier. Typical turnaround is 3–6 weeks depending on volume and complexity.

05

Delivery

Completed work is flat-packed in acid-free tissue and dispatched by insured courier, or collected from the atelier.
