Est. 2009
How It Began

From a Single Dip
of the Pen

In 2009, Eleanor Marsh opened the doors of a small Mayfair studio with little more than a collection of oblique pen holders, three bottles of iron gall ink, and an unshakeable belief that Copperplate was the most truthful language ever devised.

What began as a practice — letters written for friends, envelopes addressed for a florist nearby — became, within three years, London's most sought-after calligraphy commission studio.

Today, INK HOUSE occupies a Georgian townhouse in Marylebone. The team — two master calligraphers and three commissioned artisans — has lettered over two thousand wedding suites, performed live calligraphy at events from Claridge's to Kensington Palace, and taught the classical scripts to more than four hundred students.

Every commission begins the same way: a conversation, a cup of tea, and the quiet scratch of a nib on paper.

The Way We Work

Atelier Philosophy

01

Permanence Over Speed

We decline rush commissions. Every piece requires the time it deserves — sometimes three weeks of preparation for a single vow card. The result lasts a lifetime.

02

Material Truth

We use only archival-quality papers, pure carbon and iron gall inks, and hand-crafted steel nibs. The materials speak — we simply hold the pen.

03

Teaching as Practice

Our workshops are not revenue — they are obligation. The classical scripts must be passed forward. Every student who learns Copperplate extends its life by a generation.

The Makers

Master Calligraphers

Eleanor Marsh

Founder & Master Calligrapher

Trained under Sheila Waters and Barbara Getty, Eleanor is a Fellow of the Calligraphy and Lettering Arts Society. Her work has featured in Vogue, the Royal Collection, and the permanent collection of the Victoria and Albert Museum.

She leads all Copperplate and Spencerian workshops, and personally oversees every wedding commission above 200 envelopes.

Copperplate Spencerian Engrosser's Script Roundhand

James Thorne

Resident Calligrapher & Workshop Lead

A graduate of the London College of Communication and the Calligraphy Centre at Letter Exchange, James brings structural rigour to every commission. His Italic hand has been described by the CLAS as "a benchmark for contemporary practice."

James is the studio's primary live event calligrapher, having performed at over 300 events across the UK and Europe.

Milestones

The INK HOUSE Story

2009

The Studio Opens

Eleanor Marsh opens a 300 sq ft studio in Mayfair with four oblique pen holders and an advertisement in Tatler.

2011

First Vogue Feature

Vogue UK features INK HOUSE's envelope addressing work in a six-page wedding editorial. The waiting list reaches 18 months.

2013

The School is Founded

The first Copperplate intensive workshop sells out in four hours. The Atelier School formally opens with James Thorne as Workshop Lead.

2016

The Royal Commission

INK HOUSE receives a Royal Household commission — a milestone that remains confidential in its particulars.

2019

Move to Marylebone

The studio expands into a Georgian townhouse in Marylebone, with two dedicated workshop rooms and a materials library.

2024

2,000 Weddings Lettered

INK HOUSE celebrates its two-thousandth wedding suite — addressed, lettered, and sealed in the classical tradition.
