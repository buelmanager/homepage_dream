How We Work

The Production
Journey

Our five-stage methodology has been refined over 24 years and 2,400 events. It is not a template — it is a discipline. Each stage has defined deliverables, accountability structures, and quality gates that ensure nothing reaches your audience before it is ready.

01
Phase 1

Discovery
& Brief

We begin where every great production should: with questions. Our senior producer and creative lead spend 2–3 sessions with your team conducting deep brand immersion. We learn your audience, your objectives, your culture, and the feeling you want to leave behind. Nothing is assumed.

Deliverables
Brand Immersion Report Event Brief Document Audience Persona Profiles Budget Framework Venue Shortlist
Weeks 1–4
02
Phase 2

Concept
& Design

Our creative team develops the narrative architecture of your event. This is where we translate your brand values into spatial design, sensory experience, and audience journey. We present two complete creative directions — different in approach, equal in ambition — and then build the one you choose into full technical specifications.

Deliverables
Creative Concept Decks ×2 Set Design Renders Run of Show Draft AV Specification Vendor Recommendations
Weeks 5–10
03
Phase 3

Production
& Build

The longest and most demanding phase. Our production team enters full command mode — contracting and coordinating every vendor, managing technical builds, conducting rehearsals, and stress-testing every element until failure is not an option. We hold weekly client progress calls with a live project dashboard accessible 24/7.

Deliverables
Master Production Schedule Vendor Contracts Technical Rehearsal Reports Risk Register Contingency Plans Crew Briefing Pack
Weeks 10–20
04
Phase 4

Execution
& Live Day

The moment everything has been building toward. Our on-site production director carries full command authority — your team leads the room, we manage everything behind it. Communication is seamless. Every cue is called. Every contingency is ready. You will not need to think about logistics — only the people in front of you.

Deliverables
Live Director On-Site Dedicated Comms Network Real-Time Issue Management Full AV Command Hospitality Supervision
Week 20–24 (Event Days)
05
Phase 5

Legacy
& Continuity

We believe the event does not end when the lights come down. Within 72 hours of your event, we deliver a comprehensive impact report. Within two weeks, we hold a full retrospective with your team. We capture every learnable insight and embed it into a relationship brief that makes next year's event even better than this one.

Deliverables
72-Hour Impact Report Attendee Sentiment Analysis Full Content Archive Strategic Retrospective 2026 Relationship Brief
Weeks 25–28
Our Standards

Non-Negotiable
Principles

01

Single Point of Accountability

Every project has one named Luminary producer who is accountable for every outcome. You will never be passed from person to person. One relationship. One responsibility.

02

Zero Surprise Costs

Our budget frameworks are conservative by design. We build contingency in — not as a buffer to surprise you with at invoice, but as a discipline that keeps us honest and your costs predictable.

03

Rehearse Everything

No element of your event is performed live for the first time on the day of the event. If we cannot rehearse it, we redesign it. This is absolute.

04

Contingency for Everything

For every primary plan, we carry a secondary. For every technical dependency, we carry a backup. The world does not always cooperate — our job is to ensure your guests never notice when it does not.
