Our Origin

Born From a
Singular Belief

Luminary Events was founded in London in 2001 by Alastair Pemberton-Hale, a former theatrical director who believed that corporate events deserved the same creative rigour as the world's finest theatrical productions. His premise was simple: if you are gathering brilliant people, the environment should match their ambition.

That founding philosophy has guided every event we have ever produced. We do not approach corporate experience as logistics management — we approach it as narrative architecture. Every room we enter, we redesign. Every moment we produce, we rehearse until it is flawless.

Today, our teams operate across seven global studios — London, New York, Dubai, Singapore, Sydney, Tokyo, and São Paulo — producing experiences for the world's most ambitious companies and leaders.

What Drives Us

The Principles That
Shape Everything

Creative Sovereignty

We do not recycle formats. Every brief begins with a blank canvas. Our creative directors challenge every assumption about what a corporate event can — and should — be.

Temporal Precision

Time is the only non-renewable resource an event has. We engineer every schedule, every transition, and every moment to eliminate waste and amplify impact.

Human-Centred Design

The most sophisticated production means nothing if it does not move the people inside it. We design for human emotion first — wonder, connection, inspiration, belonging.

Global Perspective

With studios on six continents, we understand local culture, regulatory environments, and supplier landscapes — giving you borderless execution without cultural compromise.

Uncompromised Quality

We have one standard: the best that has ever been done. Every vendor, every material, every talent is selected against this standard. We do not negotiate it for budget or convenience.

Accountable Delivery

We stand behind every promise. Our live-day production teams carry full command authority — there is always a Luminary director in the room, accountable for every outcome.
