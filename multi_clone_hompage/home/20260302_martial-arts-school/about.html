ABOUT

Four decades of transmitting the warrior arts with integrity. A school where the ancient disciplines of Japan are taught without compromise.

The Origin

FORGED IN TOKYO

Bushido was founded in 1987 by Sensei Haruki Tanaka in Shinjuku, Tokyo. Tanaka had trained under three masters of different disciplines — kendo under Nakamura Toshio, judo under Yamamoto Kenji, and taekwondo under Grand Master Park — and was struck by the unifying philosophy that ran beneath all three arts.

His conviction was that these disciplines, when taught together under a coherent philosophical framework, produced a practitioner whose mastery was qualitatively different from those who trained in a single art. He called this framework Bushido — the way of the warrior.

Today, Bushido operates from its purpose-built London dojo with a faculty of six and a student body drawn from over twenty countries. The school has produced 22 black belt graduates and multiple national champions while maintaining the intimate, rigorous character of its founding vision.

Our Standards

WHAT WE
DEMAND

I

INTEGRITY

We do not bend grading standards for any student. A rank at Bushido means exactly what it says. This is non-negotiable and forms the basis of our reputation.

II

RESPECT

The bow is not ceremony — it is recognition of what came before us. Every class begins and ends with a bow. This is observed by instructors and students alike without exception.

III

PERSEVERANCE

There are no shortcuts. The curriculum is designed to be challenging. Students who leave because it is hard are welcome to return when they are ready to stay.

IV

SELF-CONTROL

The purpose of martial arts training is not to become dangerous but to become controlled. A student who cannot govern their own force has not understood what they are learning.
