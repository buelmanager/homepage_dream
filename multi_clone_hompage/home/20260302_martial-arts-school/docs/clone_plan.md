# Clone Plan — Bushido Martial Arts School

## Source Inspiration
Original design concept inspired by premium sports academies and traditional Japanese martial arts institutions. No direct website clone.

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Bushido Martial Arts School
- **Tagline:** Discipline is the Only True Strength
- **Established:** 1987, Tokyo (fictional)
- **Position:** Premium martial arts school with traditional values

## Target Audience
- Adults seeking disciplined physical and mental training
- Competitive athletes wanting structured advancement
- Professionals seeking stress management through martial practice
- Philosophical seekers interested in warrior tradition

## Key Differentiators
1. Multi-discipline curriculum under one code
2. Tokyo lineage with authentic Japanese pedagogy
3. Philosophy integrated alongside physical training
4. Strict grading — no shortcuts permitted
5. Purpose-built 4,000 sq ft dojo

## Page Architecture
- `index.html` — Showcase landing page
- `about.html` — School history and faculty
- `collection.html` — All disciplines
- `process.html` — Training methodology
- `contact.html` — Enrollment portal

## Design Decisions
- Hero Type C (Diagonal Split) chosen to convey confrontation and strength
- Onyx Stone palette reflects discipline, seriousness, and steel
- Bebas Neue conveys authority and impact
- DM Sans body for clarity and readability
- Silver/grey accent rather than gold — reflects steel of weapons
