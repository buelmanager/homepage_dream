Our Story

The Studio
Behind the Light

Founded 2012

Where Engineering Meets Architecture

Luminos Solar was born from a question: why should clean energy be ugly? Two solar engineers and a Harvard-trained architect spent two years developing a methodology that places equal weight on system performance and visual integrity.

Today, Luminos is recognized as North America's premier solar design studio, completing over 340 installations across residential estates, commercial campuses, and hospitality properties from California to New England.

Every project passes through our rigorous SolarSense protocol — a 47-point assessment that evaluates solar potential, structural capacity, aesthetic alignment, and ROI before a single panel is specified.
