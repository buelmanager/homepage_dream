Our Process

Five Stages to
Perfect Solar

01

Solar Feasibility Study

Comprehensive property analysis including satellite solar mapping, on-site shading assessment, roof structural review, and electrical infrastructure evaluation. We build a full energy model before committing to design.

1–2 Weeks
02

Architectural Design Integration

Our architects collaborate with your project team to develop panel layouts that complement the building's aesthetic language. We explore monocrystalline flush-mount, frameless, and BIPV options to find the ideal solution.

2–3 Weeks
03

Custom Equipment Specification

We curate a bespoke equipment package from tier-1 manufacturers — selecting the optimal combination of panels, inverters, mounting hardware, and storage systems for your specific project requirements.

1 Week
04

Permitting & White-Glove Installation

We manage all permitting and utility interconnection paperwork. Our certified installation team executes with craftsman-level precision: every wire concealed, every penetration waterproofed, every panel aligned perfectly.

4–8 Weeks
05

Luminos Guardian Monitoring

Your system is enrolled in our Guardian programme — 24/7 remote monitoring, proactive fault detection, scheduled maintenance visits, and quarterly performance reporting for the lifetime of your installation.
