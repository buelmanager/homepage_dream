Our Studio

Where Science
Meets Craft

Luminos Solar was founded on a singular belief: that clean energy systems should be as beautiful as they are efficient. Our design studio partners with architects, landscape designers, and engineers to create solar installations that enhance rather than compromise the aesthetic integrity of your property.

Each project begins with our proprietary SolarSense analysis — a combination of satellite mapping, on-site assessment, and architectural review that ensures every panel placement is optimal for both performance and visual harmony.

The Process

From Vision to
Clean Power

01
Solar Feasibility Study

We conduct a detailed analysis of your property — solar irradiance, shading, roof structure, and electrical infrastructure — to establish the optimal system design.

02
Architectural Design Integration

Our designers collaborate with your architect to produce panel layouts that complement the building's aesthetic, using premium monocrystalline or BIPV solutions.

03
Custom Specification

We curate equipment from a selection of tier-1 manufacturers, specifying panels, inverters, and mounting systems to your project's precise requirements.

04
White-Glove Installation

Our certified technicians execute the installation with the care and precision of a master craftsman, ensuring every junction is sealed, every wire concealed.

05
Perpetual Monitoring

Post-installation, your system is enrolled in our Luminos Guardian programme — real-time monitoring, proactive maintenance, and annual performance reporting.

Our Heritage

A Decade of
Solar Excellence

2012

The Studio is Founded

Two solar engineers and an architect unite to establish Luminos Solar in San Francisco, with a mission to redefine the aesthetics of renewable energy.

2015

The Meridian Award

Luminos receives the American Solar Energy Society's Design Excellence Award for the integrated BIPV system at the Holloway Estate, Carmel.

2018

Commercial Division Launch

Expanding beyond residential, Luminos completes its first commercial megawatt installation for a Fortune 500 headquarters in Silicon Valley.

2021

SolarSense Technology

Luminos launches its proprietary AI-driven solar assessment platform, reducing design time by 60% while improving system accuracy to within 2% of projection.

2024

25MW Milestone

The studio surpasses 25 megawatts of installed capacity across residential, commercial, and hospitality sectors throughout North America.
