Frequently Asked Questions
How long does a typical residential installation take?
From initial feasibility study to final interconnection, most residential projects take 8–14 weeks. Larger commercial projects may require 16–24 weeks.
What makes Luminos different from other solar installers?
We specialize in architecturally integrated solar systems for high-value properties. Our team includes licensed architects and engineers who collaborate directly with your design team.
Do you offer battery storage solutions?
Yes. We specify premium home energy storage systems from Tesla, SolarEdge, and Enphase, integrated seamlessly with your solar array.
What is your service area?
We primarily serve clients in California, Oregon, Washington, and Nevada, though we undertake select projects nationally for qualifying properties.
