# Clone Plan — Open Canvas Studio

## Design Direction
A deep violet-purple luxury page evoking creative imagination, accessibility, and artistic ambition. The #130F1A dark purple background is rich and distinctive; #9B6EDB accent creates a vivid, energized presence without aggression. Spectral's elegant weight adds literary seriousness; Mulish keeps body text clean and accessible.

## Hero Approach
Type D portraits-and-stats grid. Left panel positions the studio's identity and presents three key impact statistics with animated counters. Right panel shows a 2x2 portrait grid of artworks, with the main feature card spanning the full left column. The grid composition communicates creative multiplicity — many voices, many shapes.

## Content Strategy
- Stats foreground artistic output (220 members, 48 workshops, 1,400 works, 14 years)
- Philosophy positioned as artist-led, not charity-led
- Programs presented as genuinely accessible pathways, not therapeutic alternatives
- Process framed as building toward exhibition and artistic recognition
- Heritage timeline from 2012 community centre to 2025 national award
- Press quotes challenge patronising framing; third quote is an artist's own testimony
