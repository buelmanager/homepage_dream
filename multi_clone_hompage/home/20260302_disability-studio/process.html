Getting Started

How to Join

Every artist at Open Canvas Studio started with a first visit. There is no audition, no prior experience required, and no paperwork before you arrive. Just come and make something.

Five Steps to Full Studio Practice

01

Come for a Welcome Visit

Drop in during any open session or book a dedicated welcome visit. You will meet our team, see the studio, try out some of the adaptive tools, and talk through any questions about access, programs, or your creative interests. No commitment required.

02

Access Assessment and Studio Plan

We sit down with you to understand your specific access requirements — physical, sensory, cognitive, and scheduling. This is a collaborative conversation to design a studio experience that genuinely works for you, not a tick-box exercise.

03

Choose Your Program Entry Point

Join the Adaptive Open Studio, the Digital Arts program, an art therapy stream, or a group workshop. You can change, combine, or step back from programs at any time — your engagement is always on your terms.

04

Build Your Practice

Over time, with one-to-one mentorship from practicing artist facilitators, studio members develop coherent bodies of work and a clear artistic voice. We support you in articulating and developing your practice at whatever pace suits you.

05

Exhibit and Be Seen

When you are ready, we provide full production support for public exhibition — from curation and installation to artist statements and publicity. Your work will be seen on its own merits, in real gallery and public spaces.

Studio Resources

Adaptive Equipment

Mouth-held tools, grip aids, weighted implements, adjustable-height stations, sensory-calm areas, and specialist surfaces. If we do not have what you need, we will source it.

BSL and Interpretation

BSL interpretation is available for all sessions on request. Written materials are available in plain language, large print, and audio formats.

Remote Participation

Every program is available remotely for members who cannot attend in person. Live-streamed sessions and asynchronous participation options are built into all program tracks.

Come Create With Us

The studio is waiting. Your first visit is completely free and commitment-free.
