Our Story

Born from the Desert

Foundation

A Geologist's Discovery

In 1998, desert geologist Dr. Amara Rhosia was mapping selenite formations in the sub-Saharan rock when she uncovered something extraordinary — a vast vein of desert rose crystals unlike any documented formation in the scientific literature.

The crystals, formed over millions of years in arid, mineral-rich conditions, possessed a structural purity that caught her attention. She spent five years learning from Berber healers the traditional ceremonial uses of these minerals before founding the spa that bears her name.

Today, Rhosia Spa sits directly above that original crystal site. Our buildings are constructed from desert stone quarried on-site, our water flows from a volcanic aquifer 400 metres below, and every ritual we offer traces its lineage to documented ancestral practices.

Our Values

What We Stand For

I
Authenticity Above All

Every crystal is authenticated by our resident geologist. Every ritual is backed by documented ancestral practice. We do not use synthetic materials, manufactured scents, or borrowed traditions.

II
Radical Presence

We accept a maximum of 8 guests per day. No phones in ritual spaces. No group sessions. Every guest receives the undivided attention of a dedicated practitioner for the full duration of their ceremony.

III
Desert Stewardship

Our crystal sourcing operates under a strict annual extraction quota. We replant native species across our 12-hectare site and contribute 8% of revenue to Saharan conservation initiatives.

IV
Living Tradition

We employ three Berber master healers on permanent staff and fund apprenticeship programmes to ensure these traditions are preserved and transmitted to future generations of practitioners.
