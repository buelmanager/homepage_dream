Our Collection

Sacred Desert Minerals

Sacred Minerals

Crystals formed by
millions of years

The desert rose is a selenite gypsum crystal that forms in arid, sandy conditions — its petals created by the very sand in which it grows. Each formation is unique, unrepeatable.

  • 01
    Desert Rose Selenite

    Forms in arid desert conditions. Deeply calming, clarity-promoting. Native to Morocco and Tunisia.

  • 02
    Fulgurite Glass

    Created when lightning strikes desert sand. Each piece is a hollow tube of petrified lightning — extraordinarily rare.

  • 03
    Libyan Desert Glass

    Ancient silica glass formed by a meteorite impact 29 million years ago. Used in pharaonic jewellery. Deeply energising.

  • 04
    Saharan Amethyst

    Found in the sub-Saharan volcanic belt. Darker and more potent than Brazilian amethyst. Used in our evening crystal ceremonies.

Our Story

Born from the desert's deepest wisdom

In 1998, desert geologist Amara Rhosia discovered an extraordinary vein of selenite crystals beneath the Moroccan Sahara — a formation unlike any documented before. She spent five years learning from Berber healers the traditional uses of these minerals.

Today, Rhosia Spa sits directly above that original crystal site. Our buildings are constructed from desert stone, our water flows from an ancient volcanic aquifer, and every ritual is guided by documented ancestral practice.
