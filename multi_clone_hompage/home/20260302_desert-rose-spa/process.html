How It Works

The Rhosia Method

01
Before You Arrive
Intention Setting

Upon booking, you receive our pre-ritual questionnaire. Our practitioners review your responses and design a personalised ceremony that addresses your specific needs and intentions.

You are also sent our preparation guide — a simple set of practices to begin in the days before your visit: reduced screen time, mineral water intake, and a brief daily breath practice. These prepare the nervous system to receive the ceremony fully.

02
On Arrival
Welcome Consultation

A 30-minute private consultation with your assigned practitioner. We discuss your health, energy, and intentions in depth. We select the crystals and temperature calibrations specific to your session.

You are offered a ceremonial welcome drink — a blend of rose water, desert herbs, and mineralised spring water from our 400-metre aquifer — to begin the transition from the outside world to the desert sanctuary.

03
The Ritual Threshold
Ceremonial Preparation

Foot cleansing in a rose water and volcanic mineral salt bath. Transition into hand-woven linen robes made by Berber artisans. A 10-minute guided breathwork practice with your practitioner.

No phones, no conversation beyond what is necessary. The threshold ritual exists to mark the boundary between ordinary time and sacred time. Every gesture is intentional and deliberate.

04
The Core
Your Chosen Ceremony

Performed in your private desert pavilion, open on one side to the desert. The ceremony itself is conducted in near-silence, guided by your practitioner's hands, the temperature, and the resonance of the crystals.

Every placement, every temperature, every mineral chosen is specific to you — to what emerged in your consultation. There are no standard-issue rituals at Rhosia. Every ceremony is composed fresh.

05
After
Integration & Return

A minimum one-hour recovery period in our crystal garden, surrounded by selenite formations and volcanic stone. Rose-infused tea. Complete silence. No obligation to speak or socialise.

Before you leave, your practitioner provides a written integration note — their observations from your session, and recommendations for the days that follow. Many guests return this note as the single most valuable thing they took home.

Ready to Begin?

Reserve your place in the desert. Maximum 8 guests per day.
