Sacred Minerals

Our Crystal Collection

Every crystal used at Rhosia is sourced from the Saharan sub-surface, authenticated by our resident geologist, and assigned a unique provenance record. We maintain a permanent collection of 847 individual specimens.

Morocco · Atlas Mountains
Desert Rose Selenite

Lenticular selenite crystals that grow in sandy evaporite environments. Their petal-like formations are created by the very sand in which they form. Deeply calming and clarity-promoting.

Sahara · Sub-Saharan Desert
Fulgurite Glass

Hollow glass tubes formed when lightning strikes desert sand at temperatures exceeding 1,800°C. Among the rarest natural glasses on earth. Used as resonance vessels in sound healing.

Libya · Great Sand Sea
Libyan Desert Glass

Silica glass formed 29 million years ago by a meteorite impact or airburst. Found only in the Great Sand Sea. Used in pharaonic jewellery. Extraordinarily energising in thermal ceremonies.

Mali · Volcanic Belt
Saharan Amethyst

Darker and more potent than Brazilian varieties. Found in the sub-Saharan volcanic belt where ancient lava flows created perfect crystallisation chambers. Used in our evening Midnight Ritual.

Algeria · Hoggar Mountains
Hoggar Obsidian

Volcanic glass from the Hoggar Mountains of southern Algeria. Grounding, protective, and deeply reflective. Used in facial treatments for its naturally smooth surface and mineral content.

Morocco · Marrakech Region
Atlas Red Ochre

Iron oxide-rich clay from the Atlas Mountain foothills. Used in our botanical facials and body wraps. Rich in iron, magnesium, and calcium. The base of the traditional Moroccan ghassoul.
