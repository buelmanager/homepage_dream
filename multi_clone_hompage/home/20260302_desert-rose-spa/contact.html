Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking a minimum of 2 weeks in advance. Due to our 8-guest daily limit, peak season dates (October–March) often book 4–6 weeks ahead.

Are the rituals suitable for first-time spa guests?

Absolutely. Our consultation process is thorough enough to design an appropriate first ritual for any level of experience. We have hosted guests who had never visited a spa before.

Can I visit for a day or must I stay overnight?

Day visits are available. We also have a partnership with two desert camps nearby if you wish to stay overnight and experience a sunset and sunrise over the dunes as part of your journey.

Are your crystals ethically sourced?

Yes. All crystals are sourced under a strict extraction quota from our own site, supervised by our resident geologist. We maintain full provenance records for every specimen in our collection.
