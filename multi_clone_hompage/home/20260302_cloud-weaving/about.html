Our Story

Founded 2011

A Studio Born from Cloud Obsession

NIMBUS was founded in 2011 by textile artist Fiona Mackinnon, who had spent a decade studying traditional Highland weaving techniques before an apprenticeship in Kyoto changed her understanding of silk forever.

Returning to Scotland with a profound new reverence for fine fibre, she established a studio in a Victorian mill on the River Tay — within sight of the clouds that had always inspired her colour palette.

Today NIMBUS employs fourteen master weavers and a dedicated natural dyer, all working on heritage looms to produce some of the lightest, most extraordinary textiles in the world.

Our Principles

What We Believe

Slowness is Quality

We refuse production targets. Each weaver works at the pace the material demands. A single stole requires two to three days; a large throw may take a week.

Nature Leads

Our colour palette is derived entirely from the Highland landscape. We do not chase seasonal fashion — we follow what the land provides.

Heirloom Thinking

Every NIMBUS piece is made to outlast its owner. We design for longevity — not trends — and provide lifetime repair service for all pieces we have woven.
