# Clone Plan — VAULTTEC Energy Storage Vault

## Concept
Grid-scale energy storage engineering firm. Hero C diagonal split: left panel shows industrial battery installation (filtered dark), right panel carries brand story and CTA. Sophisticated, institutional tone.

## Design Decisions
- P8 Onyx Stone: near-black bg (#141416) with cool silver-grey accent (#B0B0C0) — industrial precision, materials-science aesthetic
- EB Garamond (classical serif) + Nunito (humanist sans) — engineering authority with readability
- Hero C: diagonal clip polygon creates visual tension between industrial image and brand voice
- A1 Standard animation: measured, confident reveals suit institutional engineering brand

## Section Structure
1. Preloader — VAULTTEC wordmark
2. Nav — fixed, blur-backdrop with CTA button
3. Hero C — diagonal split (hero-1 + right panel content)
4. Stats Band — 4200 MWh, 32 projects, 14 countries, 98% uptime
5. Philosophy — 3-column grid (Reliability, Technology-Agnostic, Whole-Life Value)
6. Products — 2x2 grid (VaultCore LFP, VaultFlow VRB, VaultAir CAES, VaultHydro PHES)
7. Vault Feature — split image + VAULT OS content + specs grid
8. Process — 5-step horizontal steps with connector lines
9. Heritage Timeline — 7-entry vertical timeline 2015–2026
10. Press — swiper carousel with 4 press quotes
11. Commission — side-by-side form + intro text
12. Footer — 4-column grid
