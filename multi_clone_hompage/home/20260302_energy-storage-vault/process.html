From Grid Study to
Full Operation

Every VAULTTEC project follows a rigorous five-phase development process designed to maximise performance, minimise risk, and deliver reliable stored energy from day one of operation.

01

Grid Needs Assessment

Our power systems engineers model your grid's constraints, existing generation mix, renewable penetration targets, and ancillary service obligations to define the optimal storage capacity, power rating, and dispatch profile.

02

Technology Selection

Based on your duration requirements and economics, we recommend the optimal chemistry or storage mechanism — LFP for 2–4 hour cycling, vanadium flow for 4–12 hour applications, compressed air or pumped hydro for multi-day or seasonal storage needs.

03

Engineering Design

Full electrical, civil, and control system engineering. Grid interconnection studies, protection relay coordination, safety analysis, and planning application support. Factory acceptance testing protocols defined in advance.

04

Construction & Commissioning

Our construction management team oversees procurement, site preparation, equipment installation, and system integration. Full capacity testing and grid compliance demonstration before commercial operation date.

05

Operations & O&M

24/7 remote monitoring from our Zurich NOC, scheduled and predictive maintenance, performance reporting, and continuous VAULT OS optimisation to maximise revenue from energy arbitrage, frequency response, and capacity market participation.
