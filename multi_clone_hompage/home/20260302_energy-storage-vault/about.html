Engineering the
Energy Transition

VAULTTEC was founded in 2015 by a team of electrochemists and power engineers with a shared conviction: that affordable, large-scale energy storage is the central challenge of the 21st-century electricity system.

Technology-Agnostic, Performance-Obsessed

Unlike manufacturers tied to a single chemistry, VAULTTEC designs with four distinct storage technologies — lithium iron phosphate, vanadium redox flow, compressed air, and pumped hydro. We select the optimal solution for each project's duration, dispatch profile, and economics.

This independence from any single supplier allows us to deliver genuinely best-in-class performance at every scale.

Global Portfolio

From our first 48 MWh installation in Bavaria to a 2.4 GWh pumped hydro project in the Swiss Alps, VAULTTEC's 32 operational projects span 14 countries and represent 4.2 GWh of installed capacity.

Our systems collectively displace over 2 million tonnes of CO2 equivalent per year by enabling higher renewable penetration on the grids they serve.
