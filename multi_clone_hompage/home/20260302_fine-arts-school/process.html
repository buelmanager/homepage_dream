Admissions

How to
Join the Atelier

We admit students on the basis of demonstrated artistic potential and intellectual seriousness. Degrees, academic records, and credentials carry no weight. Work and conversation do.

The Process

Five Steps to
Admission

01

Initial Application

Complete the online application form with your personal details, selected program, and a brief artist statement (300–500 words). Tell us what you make, why you make it, and what you believe is missing from your current practice.

Rolling intake — no deadline
02

Portfolio Submission

Submit 10–15 images of recent work. We are looking for consistency of intent and evidence of serious engagement with your chosen medium. Do not submit your best work — submit the work that shows us how you think. Include any process documentation if relevant.

Digital submission via our online portal
03

First Review

The admissions committee — comprising the program director and two faculty members — reviews all portfolios. Approximately 40% of applicants proceed to interview. You will receive a response within three weeks of submission.

04

Interview

A one-hour conversation with two faculty members, conducted in person (Paris) or via video call. We talk about your work, your influences, your questions, your uncertainties. This is not an audition — it is a conversation between people who take art seriously. Bring work to discuss, or be prepared to discuss it from memory.

In-person or video call
05

Acceptance & Enrolment

Successful candidates receive a formal offer letter within one week of the interview. A non-refundable deposit of €2,000 secures your place. We will confirm your studio allocation, program start date, and provide a full orientation pack.

What We Need

Requirements
for Application

Portfolio

  • 10–15 images of original work
  • High resolution (minimum 1500px on long edge)
  • Work from the past two years preferred
  • Include titles, dates, dimensions, and media
  • Process documentation welcomed but not required

Artist Statement

  • 300–500 words in English or French
  • Describe your current practice honestly
  • Identify what you find difficult or unresolved
  • State why the Atelier specifically, not art school generically
  • Avoid biographical narrative — focus on the work

Practical Requirements

  • Age 18 or above (no upper limit)
  • Proof of identity
  • Basic French or English language ability
  • No prior formal qualification required
  • Valid student visa if non-EU national

What We Do Not Consider

  • Academic grades or prior degrees
  • Recommendations or references
  • Exhibition history or prizes
  • Social media following
  • Commercial success of prior work
Common Questions

Frequently
Asked

I have no formal art training. Can I apply?

Yes. Some of our most significant alumni came to the Atelier entirely self-taught. What matters is that your work demonstrates a sustained engagement with your medium and that you can articulate — however roughly — what you are trying to do.

Are there scholarships available?

We offer three full-scholarship places per year through the Fondation Vasseur. The selection criterion is artistic merit exclusively. Applications for scholarship funding are reviewed alongside admissions.

Can I apply to more than one program?

You may indicate a secondary program preference in your application. The faculty will advise during interview if they feel a different program is a stronger fit for your current work.

What is the language of instruction?

The primary language of instruction is French, with most sessions also available in English. We strongly encourage students to work towards basic French during their first semester; Paris itself is an essential part of the education.
