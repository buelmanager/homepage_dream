Every program at the Atelier is designed around intensive, sustained practice. We do not offer survey courses. We offer formation — the deep embedding of skill, judgment, and artistic consciousness.

Painting

The foundational discipline of the Atelier. We teach painting not as a style but as a problem — one that has no final solution. Students work through historical techniques from the Flemish masters to contemporary abstraction, developing a personal position within that tradition. Oil, encaustic, fresco, acrylic, and experimental media. We have a fully equipped colour laboratory where students grind their own pigments.

Sculpture

Sculpture at the Atelier means contending with mass, gravity, surface, and time. Students learn modelling, carving, casting, and fabrication. Our professional bronze foundry is a rarity in art education — students can take work through the full lost-wax process. A dedicated stone yard and woodworking studio complete the material offer. Installation and site-specific work is embedded throughout.

Printmaking

The most overlooked discipline in contemporary art — and the most technically demanding. Our students learn etching, aquatint, lithography, screen printing, relief printing, and digital offset. The Atelier's print studio runs a professional edition service; students are expected to produce a documented limited edition as their final project. Several have gone on to establish their own studios.

Contemporary Practice

For artists whose work refuses categorisation. Video, performance, sound, text, installation, and expanded photography are all valid media here. The program is organised around a single question that shifts annually. Recent questions have included: What does it mean to make an object in 2026? Who is the audience for art that refuses to be seen? Rigorous seminars alternate with studio time.
