Essential Information
Response Time
We reply to all enquiries within two business days. Initial consultations are conducted by Éléonore Voss personally.
Season Availability
We accept up to twenty new commissions per season. Current availability: Spring 2026. Wait list open for Summer 2026.
What Happens Next
After your enquiry, we schedule a video or in-person consultation. No commitment required until you approve the initial concept document.

Small framed piece (up to 30×30 cm) From £1,800

Large study (60 cm +) From £5,500

Heirloom textile set From £4,000
