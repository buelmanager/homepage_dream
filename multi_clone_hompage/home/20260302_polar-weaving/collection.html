Tundra Grand Throw

Our flagship piece — a 200×250cm blanket woven in 4-shaft twill from hand-spun Svalbard reindeer underhair. No two are identical. Pattern variants shift seasonally with the dye harvest.

Each throw requires 280 hours of loom time and six weeks of fibre preparation. The yarn is hand-spun to a consistent 15-micron thread count — finer than the finest cashmere.
