The Commission Process

Step 1

Enquiry Review

Our studio director reviews all commission enquiries personally. We accept a maximum of 40 bespoke commissions per year. You will receive a response within 5 business days.

Step 2

Weaver Assignment

Based on the nature of your commission, we assign the most suited master weaver from our cohort of twelve. You will be introduced by correspondence and may correspond directly about the work throughout the process.

Step 3

Fibre & Colour Selection

We send a physical sample pack of available fibre lots and current season's dye palette. Your selections are discussed with the assigned weaver before work begins.
