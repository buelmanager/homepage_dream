Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a commission take from initial enquiry to completed edition? +
Most commissions take between 3 and 8 months from initial consultation to the delivery of signed impressions. This includes time for stone preparation (1 week), drawing phase (1–3 weeks depending on complexity), etching and proofing (1–2 weeks), and the print run itself (1–4 weeks). Scheduling around the press's existing commitments can add time at the start.
Do I need to be present at the press for the entire print run? +
You must be present for the drawing phase and for the approval of the bon à tirer proof — the standard impression against which the entire edition is measured. For the print run itself, your presence is welcome but not required. We document each stage with high-resolution photography and can share progress remotely.
What does a commission at OFFSET cost? +
Commission costs vary based on edition size, image complexity, number of stone passes (for multi-tonal work), and paper requirements. A standard single-stone edition of 20–30 impressions typically starts at $8,000–$12,000 for the printing fee, exclusive of the artist's own materials and travel. We provide a detailed quote after the initial consultation.
Can I commission a colour lithograph at OFFSET? +
Yes. Colour lithography requires a separate stone for each colour, with careful registration between passes. We have produced editions using up to six stones. Colour commissions require significantly more time and press days than monochrome work, and are priced accordingly. We discuss colour requirements fully at the initial consultation.
How are editions shipped and insured? +
All impressions are shipped flat or rolled (depending on size) in archival tissue and acid-free cardboard. Shipments are fully insured at replacement value. International shipments are accompanied by full customs documentation specifying the work as original fine art. We work with specialist art shippers for oversized or high-value consignments.
What happens to the stone after the edition is complete? +
After the completed edition is signed and registered, the stone is ground flat within 30 days using progressively finer carborundum. The grinding is recorded in the edition archive with a date and the initials of the printer who performed it. The stone then returns to our limestone library for future use. This is our foundational commitment to edition integrity — no stone is reprinted.
