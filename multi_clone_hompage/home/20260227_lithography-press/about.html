About The Press

Fifty Years Of
Stone & Ink

OFFSET was founded in 1970 in a converted warehouse in Lower Manhattan. For over five decades, we have printed editions for some of the most significant artists working in contemporary fine art — always on stone, always by hand.

Founder & Master Printer
Marcus Veil
Master Printer, Founder

Marcus Veil trained under the German printmaker Karl Heinz Rupprecht in Munich from 1965 to 1969, learning stone lithography at its source — in the city where Alois Senefelder invented the medium in 1796. He returned to New York with a single limestone slab and the conviction that the medium deserved a dedicated press in America.

He founded OFFSET in 1970 with three secondhand presses and an unwavering technical standard. His first major collaboration was with the painter Agnes Forsythe in 1971, a series of twelve landscape lithographs that sold out within weeks of publication and established the press's reputation for quality.

Over fifty years, Marcus has pulled impressions for painters, sculptors, photographers, and installation artists — adapting stone lithography's centuries-old chemistry to the demands of contemporary visual language. He continues to oversee every edition printed at OFFSET personally.

Press History

Five Decades
In Brief

1970
OFFSET Founded — Lower Manhattan
Marcus Veil opens the press in a converted warehouse on West 26th Street with three presses acquired from a closing printmaking studio in Berlin. The press initially takes commissions from local painters seeking to explore printmaking.
1974
First Major Exhibition — MoMA Print Project
OFFSET editions are included in the Museum of Modern Art's survey of contemporary American printmaking. The press receives commissions from four museum-represented artists following the exhibition.
1982
Voirin Press Acquisition
The 1922 Voirin flatbed lithographic press — our finest instrument — is acquired from a Paris atelier closing after the death of its master printer. The press is disassembled, shipped to New York, and restored over eighteen months.
1995
European Exchange Programme
OFFSET establishes a formal exchange programme with three European atelier lithography studios. Artists can complete drawings in New York and print in Munich, Paris, or London, and vice versa. The programme runs annually.
2008
Second Printer Appointed — Soo-Yeon Park
After eight years of training under Marcus Veil, Soo-Yeon Park is appointed as a second master printer — only the second person to hold that designation at OFFSET in the press's history.
2016
100th Edition Published
The hundredth edition printed at OFFSET — a six-stone colour lithograph by the painter Elias Müller — is published in a run of fifteen impressions and acquired by three international museum collections.
2024
Archive Digitisation Project
OFFSET begins digitising its complete edition archive — more than 150 publications and the stones, proofs, and correspondence associated with each. The archive will be accessible to scholars and collectors by 2026.
The Team

The People
Of The Press

Marcus Veil
Founder & Master Printer

Trained in Munich under Karl Heinz Rupprecht. Over fifty years of continuous practice in stone lithography. Expert in multi-stone colour printing and tusche technique.

Soo-Yeon Park
Master Printer

Joined OFFSET as an apprentice in 2000. Appointed master printer in 2008 after eight years of training. Specialist in fine-line and crosshatch lithographic drawing methods.

Daniel Reiss
Press Manager & Stone Grinder

Responsible for the preparation and maintenance of OFFSET's limestone library. Trained in stone levigation and surface chemistry. Manages the press's archive of over 200 active slabs.

Collaborating Artists

Artists We Have
Printed With

Agnes Forsythe
Landscape Painting · 1971–1984

OFFSET's first major collaborator. Twelve landscape editions printed between 1971 and 1984. Her 1973 series remains one of the most collected editions in the press's history.

Elias Müller
Abstract Painting · 2012–Present

Berlin-based painter known for gestural abstraction. The hundredth OFFSET edition, a six-stone colour lithograph, was Müller's work. Currently in residence for a new edition.

Takashi Noda
Printmaking · 2018–Present

Tokyo-born printmaker whose practice spans etching, woodblock, and lithography. His OFFSET editions are characterised by complex layering achieved through multiple stone passes.

Clara Muñoz
Figure Drawing · 2022–Present

Madrid-based figure draughtsperson working in charcoal and lithographic crayon. Her recent Portrait Study edition sold out within 48 hours of publication.

Elena Harrow
Landscape & Environment · 2020–Present

British landscape artist whose lithographic practice explores the relationship between geological time and atmospheric light. Winner of the Turner Prize for Print, 2021.

James Okafor
Conceptual Art · 2019–2023

Nigerian-American conceptual artist whose 2019 OFFSET edition, Inventory, interrogated the archive and the edition as simultaneous objects. Works in institutional collections worldwide.
