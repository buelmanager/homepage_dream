Our Foundations

The Dusk Hour in Every Piece

At CRÉPUSE, we chase the exact moment when the sky transforms — when indigo meets amber, when the first star appears, when everything becomes both more beautiful and more fragile than it will ever be again. Our jewelry captures that instant.

✦
Transitional Beauty

We are drawn to the moments of transformation — the liminal spaces between states. Each CRÉPUSE piece is designed to evoke that sense of beautiful impermanence.

◇
Haute Joaillerie Standards

Every piece is made in our Paris atelier to the strictest haute joaillerie standards. We work with the same gemological suppliers as the great French houses — insisting on the same quality they accept.

〜
Color as Language

The twilight palette — rose, amber, violet, midnight blue — informs every gemstone selection. We create combinations that reference the sky, not the trend reports.

The Story

A Paris Atelier,
Built at Dusk

"I designed the first piece at sunset. I haven't been able to work at any other time of day since."

CRÉPUSE was founded in 2016 by gemologist and designer Isabelle Vernet, whose decade at Van Cleef & Arpels convinced her that the most extraordinary jewelry emerges from a single, sustained obsession. Hers is the twilight hour.

The Craft

From Concept to Commission

01
Gemstone Selection

Every commission begins with gemstone selection. We work with four preferred traders in Antwerp and one in Bangkok to source sapphires, spinels, and alexandrites that reference our twilight palette.

02
Sketch & Design

Isabelle creates all initial designs by hand, in pencil and watercolour, inspired by the stone itself. Technical drawings are prepared in-house and refined through multiple client consultations.

03
Fabrication

All fabrication takes place in our Marais workshop. We work with three master goldsmiths trained at the Chambre Syndicale de la Haute Couture alongside our dedicated setter.

04
Presentation

Each completed piece is presented at dusk in our atelier — always in the natural light that defined it. The presentation is accompanied by a full gemological report and certificate.
