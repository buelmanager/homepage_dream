Twilight Atelier

Jewelry Born
from Dusk

CRÉPUSE was founded in Paris in 2016 by gemologist Isabelle Vernet. Every piece is a meditation on the twilight hour — capturing the transition between day and night in gold, platinum, and rare gemstones.

Twilight Palette

Rose, amber, violet, midnight blue — our gemstones are selected to reference the sky at dusk, not the trend reports.

Paris Atelier

All pieces are made in our Marais workshop by master goldsmiths trained at the Chambre Syndicale de la Haute Couture.

Haute Joaillerie

We work to the strictest standards — the same gemological suppliers and fabrication methods as the great French houses.
