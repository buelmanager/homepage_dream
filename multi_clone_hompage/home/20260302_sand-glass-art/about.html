About Fulgurite

Founded in 2008 in the Tassili n'Ajjer desert. Sixteen years of lightning storms, desert expeditions, and radical glass art.

Origin

A Desert Encounter

In 2006, geologist Dr. Karim Ouali was conducting a survey of Tassili n'Ajjer sandstone formations when he came across a cluster of hollow glass tubes embedded in a sand dune — remnants of a lightning strike that had occurred months earlier. He had seen fulgurite in textbooks. He had never expected to find a specimen this pure, this large, this beautiful.

He returned the following year with his partner, sculptor Yasmine Chafik, and they spent two weeks excavating the site. From that collection, they made their first three works. A Paris gallery showed them in 2008. Everything sold in the first hour of opening.

Fulgurite Studio was founded the following month. They have never left the desert.

The Team

Founders

Dr. Karim Ouali
Co-Founder · Desert Geologist

PhD in desert geology from the University of Algiers. Twenty years of Saharan fieldwork. Leads all specimen collection, geological assay, and provenance documentation. Publishes annually in the Journal of Arid Geology on fulgurite formation.

Yasmine Chafik
Co-Founder · Sculptor

Trained at École des Beaux-Arts de Paris. Works exclusively with natural silica glass — fulgurite, obsidian, and desert glass. Her Wall Panel installations are held in collections across 12 countries. Directs the artist residency programme.

Mohammed Tineghir
Head of Desert Operations

Tuareg guide and desert expert born in Tamanrasset. Has navigated the Tassili for thirty years. Leads the post-storm collection expeditions. Knows where the lightning hits before the storm arrives.

Elsa Kristiansen
Curator & Gallery Liaison

Former curator at Munch Museum, Oslo. Joined Fulgurite in 2018 to manage gallery relationships and collector communications. Based in Paris, travels to the studio twice annually.
