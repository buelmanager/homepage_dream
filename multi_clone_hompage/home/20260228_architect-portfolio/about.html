Our Manifesto

Architecture Is an Argument

We founded FORMA ATELIER on a single conviction: that every space is a position taken in the world. A building is not neutral. It asserts. It presses against its site, its neighbors, its era. It argues for something.

Our practice begins before the drawing. It begins with a question: what does this space need to say? We call this the primary proposition — and everything that follows is its elaboration.

We are not stylists. We do not apply aesthetics to structures. We find the form that is latent in the problem itself.

"The details are not the details. They make the product."
— FORMA ATELIER Studio Principles, 2006
  • I Structure is honest. We refuse to hide engineering behind decoration. What holds the building tells its own story.
  • II Silence is a material. Empty space is as carefully designed as any wall or column. The interval matters as much as the object.
  • III Light is the primary medium. All architecture is the art of modulating natural light. We design shadow as much as we design form.
  • IV Time is a dimension. Buildings age. We design for the patina — for the way concrete weathers, how wood deepens, how steel oxidizes with dignity.
Principal Architect

Anders
Forma

RIBA Chartered Architect · AA Graduate

Anders Forma established FORMA ATELIER in Oslo in 2006, following formative years at Snøhetta and Herzog & de Meuron. His approach to architecture synthesizes the structural clarity of Nordic modernism with the spatial philosophy of Japanese ma — the idea that absence is as significant as presence.

His built work spans private residences in the Norwegian fjords, a cultural center in Copenhagen recognized by the RIBA, and a series of artist studios in Tokyo's Yanaka district that have become seminal examples of contextual integration.

Anders lectures at the Oslo School of Architecture and Design and has been a visiting critic at the Architectural Association, London.
