Our Origin

An Oasis Born from Obsession

Qarmid began not as a business, but as a quest — to create the one place on earth where total privacy and extraordinary beauty exist simultaneously.

The Founding

Built for Solitude

In 2001, architect and desert traveller Yassine Qadir spent six months on camelback traversing southern Morocco, searching for a site that possessed both a reliable underground spring and a compelling relationship to the sand sea. He found it 80km south of Zagora.

Construction of the first four villas took three years, using only local rammed earth and stone. No machinery. The builders were artisans from the nearby village of Tamegroute, trained in the region's 400-year-old earth-building tradition. Qarmid opened quietly, with no press, in November 2004.

Two decades later, it remains the same 24-villa estate. Yassine refused all expansion offers. "To add a room would be to lose the idea," he says. "The idea is that it is entirely yours."

Our Principles

Values

I
Deliberate Smallness

24 villas is not a constraint — it is the design. At that scale, we know every guest by name, anticipate every need, and ensure that the estate never feels crowded.

II
Local Sovereignty

Every material in our villas is Moroccan. Every ingredient on your table is sourced within 200km. Every artisan who built or decorated this place is from this valley.

III
Water Stewardship

Our spring is ancient and finite. We track every litre used on the estate and operate our pools and gardens on a strict regenerative water cycle. The desert gives; we return.
