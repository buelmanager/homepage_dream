Reserve
Before You Arrive

Planning Your Desert Stay

01
6–12 Weeks Before
Private Enquiry

Contact Fatima, our guest relations director, directly. There is no online booking engine. Every reservation begins with a conversation. We will discuss your dates, your preferences, your intentions for the stay, and the villa that suits you best.

02
4 Weeks Before
Pre-Arrival Curation

You receive our pre-arrival questionnaire. Food preferences, pillow firmness, flower scents for your villa, book genres, music preferences, whether you want absolute silence or curated background audio. We use this to prepare your villa before you arrive.

03
Transfer Day
The Desert Approach

Our helicopter meets you at Marrakech or Fez Saiss airport. The 80-minute flight over the High Atlas and into the desert is itself an experience. Your villa team is waiting at the landing pad — not in a lobby. There is no check-in process.

04
Upon Arrival
Your Villa, Ready

You are walked to your villa by your personal concierge. Your preferred welcome drink is waiting. The temperature is set. The flowers you chose are arranged. The music you love is already playing softly. You have arrived somewhere that knows you, somehow.

05
Your Days
Unscheduled Time

There is no programme unless you want one. Your chef prepares meals at hours of your choosing. Experiences are available on request — dune treks, astronomy, camel journeys, hammam ceremonies. Or you may sit by your pool and do absolutely nothing for a week.
