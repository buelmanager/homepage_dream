The Commission Journey

Seven Stages of
Masterwork

Every LUMINIS commission follows a rigorous process developed over four decades — ensuring the final installation exceeds every expectation and endures for centuries.

01
Weeks 1–2

Initial Consultation

We begin every commission with an in-depth consultation — in our London studio or on-site — to understand the architectural context, liturgical programme, and the client's vision.

  • Site survey and light measurement (all seasons where possible)
  • Architectural brief review with your architect
  • Review of heritage authority requirements if applicable
  • Budget and timeline discussion
Weeks 3–8

Design Development

Our design director develops concept sketches, colour studies in gouache, and preliminary cartoons. Multiple iterations are reviewed with the client before any design is approved.

  • Concept sketches and narrative programme development
  • Colour studies using actual glass samples in natural light
  • Full-size cartoon sections for complex passages
  • Presentation to conservation or planning authorities where required
02
03
Weeks 8–12

Glass Sourcing

Edmund Voss personally visits Lamberts of Bavaria and other specialist suppliers to hand-select each sheet of glass — examining texture, colour depth, and light transmission in natural daylight.

  • Antique mouth-blown glass from Lamberts, Bavaria
  • Opalescent and cathedral glass from Uroboros and Wissmach
  • Medieval-recipe enamels and silver stain pigments
  • Complete batch documentation for future conservation reference
Months 3–8

Cutting & Painting

Glass is cut to full-size cartoons using traditional iron-handled glass cutters. All grisaille painting and silver staining is applied by hand, then kiln-fired multiple times to achieve permanence.

  • Full-scale cutting from approved cartoon
  • Grisaille and vitreous paint application — multiple layers
  • Silver stain for amber tones and golden accents
  • Multiple kiln firings at 640–680°C for paint fusion
04
05
Months 7–12

Lead & Copper Work

All panels are leaded using hand-cut lead came, soldered at every intersection, and treated with a traditional glazing compound. Panels are assembled on light tables for continuous reference to the overall design.

  • Hand-cut lead came in flat, beaded, or decorative profiles
  • 60/40 tin-lead solder at every joint
  • Glazing compound tamped by hand for weatherproofing
  • Copper foil used for residential and small-scale commissions
Month 11–12

Studio Assembly

Completed panels are assembled in our studio under high daylight conditions, allowing a final quality review of the entire composition before despatch. Clients are invited to a private studio viewing.

  • Full assembly on back-lit table in natural north light
  • Client studio viewing by appointment
  • Final conservation-grade protective glazing if specified
  • Bespoke timber crating for transport
06
07
Final Phase

Installation & Handover

Our installation team works with your site team to fix and bed each panel. For significant commissions we remain on-site through the first full cycle of natural light conditions, making fine adjustments as needed.

  • Installation by LUMINIS specialist team
  • Structural fixings specified by our engineer
  • Full handover documentation and glass sample archive
  • 10-year structural guarantee; lifetime consultation

What We Use

Materials of
Permanence

We use only the finest materials — sourced from specialist suppliers whose own histories span centuries of glass-making tradition.

Lamberts Mouth-Blown

Bavaria, Germany · Since 1622

Hand-blown antique glass featuring natural surface variation, bubbles, and striations that interact with light in ways no machine-made glass can replicate.

Vitreous Enamel Paints

Reusche & Co · Medieval Recipe

Iron oxide-based glass paints mixed with gum arabic and oil, kiln-fired at 640°C for permanent fusion with the glass surface. Guaranteed for 500+ years.

Hand-Cast Lead Came

Lead & Light, London · Hand-drawn

Lead came hand-drawn from pure alloy lead to our specified profiles — flat, beaded, or ornamental. Provides structural integrity and longevity exceeding 300 years.

Silver Stain

Silver nitrate compound · Applied cold

Applied cold to glass and fired, silver nitrate penetrates the glass matrix to produce tones from pale lemon to deep amber. Used since the 14th century for canopies and flesh tones.
