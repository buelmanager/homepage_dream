Founding Story

Born From a Reverence
for Ancient Light

In 1987, Edmund Voss — a young architectural conservationist — walked into a derelict Somerset chapel and encountered a shattered lancet window of breathtaking medieval complexity. Rather than see it lost, he spent three years restoring it by hand, teaching himself the techniques of 13th-century Bavarian glaziers.

From that singular act of devotion, LUMINIS was born. What began as a conservation practice in a single London workshop gradually became one of the world's most sought-after studios for original architectural glass commissions.

"I did not set out to build a studio. I set out to understand light — and everything followed from that."

Today, Edmund leads a team of six master glaziers and works in close collaboration with architects, conservationists, and private clients across nineteen countries. Every commission, regardless of scale, passes through his hands.

Our Philosophy

Three Pillars of
Enduring Practice

Every decision at LUMINIS is guided by convictions formed over four decades of working with light, glass, lead, and stone.

I

Material Integrity

We use only antique mouth-blown glass from Lamberts of Bavaria, hand-cast opaque glasses, and medieval-recipe enamels. No machine-made float glass appears in our commissions.

II

Architectural Dialogue

Glass does not decorate architecture — it is architecture. Every commission begins with weeks of site study, measuring light angles at every hour across every season before a single design line is drawn.

III

Permanence of Craft

We design for centuries, not decades. Every panel is executed with the expectation it will outlast its commissioners. Conservation-grade materials and time-tested construction methods are non-negotiable.
