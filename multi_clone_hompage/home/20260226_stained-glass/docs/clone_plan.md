## Concept Origin

LUMINIS is an original concept inspired by the aesthetic and craft heritage of real-world luxury glass studios such as:
- **Whitefriars Glass** (London, est. 1680) — heritage craft positioning
- **Lamberts Glashütte** (Bavaria, est. 1622) — artisan glass supplier aesthetic
- **The Stained Glass Museum** (Ely Cathedral) — institutional presentation style

This is NOT a direct clone of any existing website. Design language, copy, brand positioning, glazier bios, project names, and all content are entirely fictional and original.

---

## Design Direction

### Hero Approach
- Type G: Text-driven scroll transform
- Large typographic display with SplitText word-reveal
- Floating glass fragment SVG shapes in parallax
- Dark cinematic atmosphere

### Palette Rationale
- Deep navy `#131820` — evokes cathedral shadow, leaded glass shadows
- Amber gold `#F0B050` — references amber leaded glass and candlelight
- Cool blue `#80C8F0` — references sky-blue cathedral glass panels
- Warm white `#F0EADC` — aged parchment / illuminated manuscript warmth

### Typography
- Bodoni Moda — classical editorial serif, used in high-end architecture and art publishing
- Inter — clean technical clarity for body copy, contrasting the classical heading choice
