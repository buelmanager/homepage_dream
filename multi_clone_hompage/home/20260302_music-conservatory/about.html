Our Story

A Century of Musical Excellence

Founded in Vienna in 1892 by Maestro Ludwig Ehrhardt, Altissimo Conservatory began as a small but intensely dedicated studio for advanced pianists. Ehrhardt's conviction — that musical mastery required both exceptional technique and profound artistic sensitivity — shaped an educational philosophy that persists to this day.

Over more than a century, Altissimo has expanded its reach and its curriculum while preserving the intimacy and rigor that define the Altissimo Method. Alumni include principal musicians of the world's greatest orchestras, Grammy-winning recording artists, and celebrated opera singers performing at the Metropolitan Opera, La Scala, and the Royal Opera House.

Today, our three campuses — in New York, London, and Vienna — offer Bachelor of Music, Master of Music, and Artist Diploma programs across piano, strings, voice, and composition. Each student is assigned to a faculty mentor upon admission, beginning an apprentice-style relationship that continues throughout their studies.

The Altissimo faculty comprises 48 active performing artists and scholars, each bringing the demands and perspectives of the contemporary concert stage into the studio. Our students do not merely study music history — they participate in it.

Distinguished Faculty

Prof. Ingrid Larssen
Chair, Piano Studies

Former soloist with the Oslo Philharmonic, Professor Larssen has won the Leeds International Piano Competition and performed at Carnegie Hall over forty times.

Dr. Marco Antonelli
Professor of Composition

Composer of two symphonies, three string quartets, and an opera premiered at La Scala. His scholarly work on Brahmsian counterpoint is internationally recognised.

Madame Hélène Dubois
Director of Voice Studies

Retired mezzo-soprano who performed with every major European opera company. Her students have won the Queen Elisabeth Competition and the Operalia Prize.
