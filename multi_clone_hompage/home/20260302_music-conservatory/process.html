The Audition Process

Admission to Altissimo is highly selective. Each year, we welcome a small cohort of exceptional students whose technical excellence, musical intelligence, and artistic potential align with the Altissimo standard. The audition process is designed to reveal these qualities comprehensively.

STEP 01
Online Application
Submit your application through our online portal. Include your curriculum vitae, academic transcripts, two letters of recommendation from music educators, and a video recording of two contrasting pieces.
STEP 02
Preliminary Review
A faculty panel reviews all submitted materials within four weeks of the application deadline. Approximately 30% of applicants are invited to attend the live audition.
STEP 03
Live Audition
Invited candidates perform before a jury at one of our three campuses. The audition includes prepared repertoire, sight-reading, and a brief interview with the faculty committee.
STEP 04
Final Decision
Admission offers and scholarship notifications are dispatched within three weeks of the live audition. All admitted students receive a personalised studio placement letter from their assigned faculty mentor.
