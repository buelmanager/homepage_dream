Piano Performance

Our Piano Performance program is among the most rigorous and celebrated in the world. Students work one-on-one with master teachers, exploring the full range of the solo, chamber, and concerto repertoire from Bach to Boulez.

The curriculum integrates historical performance practice, score analysis, and regular master classes with guest artists from the world's leading concert venues.

Duration4 Years (BM)
DegreeBM / MM
Intake12 / Year
Orchestral Studies

Strings & Orchestra

From solo Bach suites to the great Romantic concertos, our strings program demands virtuosity and intelligence. Students perform in the Altissimo Chamber Orchestra, preparing them for the realities of ensemble life.

The program includes chamber music coaching, orchestral audition preparation, and a residency with a professional orchestra in the final year.

Duration4 Years (BM)
DegreeBM / MM
Intake16 / Year
