Our History

A DECADE OF
H₂ INNOVATION

2013

Nexus Founded in Zurich

Three ETH Zurich electrochemists found Nexus with seed funding from a Swiss family office, pioneering premium PEM electrolysis systems.

2016

First Marine Deployment

The Poseidon prototype fuel cell system is installed aboard a 60m superyacht — proving H₂ power at sea for the first time in the luxury market.

2019

Solid-State Storage Breakthrough

Nexus patents its metal hydride storage module, enabling ambient-pressure H₂ storage at energy densities 40% higher than competing technologies.

2022

Industrial Scale

First 10MW electrolyser deployment at an Austrian steel mill marks Nexus's entry into the industrial decarbonisation market.

2025

100MW Milestone

Nexus surpasses 100MW of cumulative electrolyser capacity deployed globally — a milestone for the premium green hydrogen industry.
