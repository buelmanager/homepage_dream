Astronomy Education

The Science Beneath the Stars

Our expert team of professional astronomers brings the universe to life. Each stay includes curated educational sessions tailored to your level of knowledge.

01 🌌

Stellar Evolution

Follow the lifecycle of stars from nebular nurseries to white dwarf remnants. Our telescopes reveal star-forming regions in real time, connecting cosmic theory to breathtaking reality.

02 🔭

Galactic Structure

Trace the spiral arms of the Milky Way and peer at neighboring galaxies millions of light-years distant. Our astronomers contextualize humanity's place in the grand cosmic web.

03 💫

Nebulae & Remnants

Observe the Orion Nebula — a stellar nursery 1,344 light-years away — in breathtaking detail. See planetary nebulae, supernova remnants, and the very elements that built the solar system.

The Research Program

Stellaris Lodge partners with professional observatories worldwide to offer an exclusive citizen-science research program. Amateur astronomers can participate in real scientific observations, contributing data to active research projects.

📡
Real Scientific Data Collection
Contribute to exoplanet transit timing, variable star monitoring, and asteroid photometry under professional guidance.
🎓
Resident Astronomer Mentorship
Work side-by-side with PhD-level astronomers. Learn reduction techniques, data analysis, and celestial coordinate systems.
📊
Published Contributions
Significant discoveries made by program participants may be credited in academic publications and reported to the IAU.
🌐
Remote Access Continuation
After your stay, continue observing remotely via our robotic telescope network — your Stellaris journey never truly ends.
