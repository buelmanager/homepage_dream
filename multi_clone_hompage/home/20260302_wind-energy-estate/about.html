WIND AS LEGACY

Aether Wind was established in Edinburgh in 2009 by three turbine engineers and a land agent who shared a conviction that wind energy development could be done better — more rigorously, more transparently, and with greater respect for the landscapes we work within.

Today Aether manages over 600MW of wind energy capacity across the British Isles, Ireland, Sweden, and Australia — delivering clean, reliable power to hundreds of thousands of homes.
