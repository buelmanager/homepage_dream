Development Pathway

FROM LAND
TO MEGAWATTS

01
WIND RESOURCE ASSESSMENT

12-month LiDAR campaign combined with mesoscale modelling delivers bankable energy yield estimates with P50/P90 confidence levels.

02
PLANNING & CONSENTING

We manage environmental impact assessment, community consultation, and planning applications through to consent — a process typically spanning 18-36 months.

03
GRID & FINANCING

Aether secures grid connection agreements and structures project financing — debt, equity, or our proprietary co-investment framework — to suit the landowner's objectives.

04
CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Our construction management team oversees civil works, turbine erection, electrical infrastructure, and substation commissioning to programme and budget.

05
LONG-TERM O&M

Post-commissioning, our O&M team maximises availability and revenue through predictive maintenance, real-time SCADA monitoring, and annual performance reviews.

Our History

A DECADE OF
WIND MASTERY

2009

Aether Wind Founded

Three turbine engineers and a land agent establish Aether in Edinburgh, commissioning their first 6-turbine community wind farm in Aberdeenshire.

2013

First 100MW Milestone

Aether surpasses 100MW of commissioned capacity across five Scottish estates, attracting first institutional investment from a major pension fund.

2017

Offshore Expansion

Aether enters the offshore market with a 40MW nearshore development in the Moray Firth — the studio's most technically complex project to date.

2021

International Portfolio

Expansion into Ireland, Sweden, and Australia adds 200MW of international capacity under the Aether management platform.

2025

600MW Under Management

Aether Wind reaches 600MW of total capacity under management — delivering clean power to over 400,000 homes annually.
