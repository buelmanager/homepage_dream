The Atelier

Engineering
as Fine Craft

Aqua Atelier was founded on the conviction that hydropower engineering is both a science and an art. Our team combines civil, mechanical, and electrical engineering expertise with a deep respect for the watercourses and landscapes we work within.

Every project is personally managed by a principal engineer from feasibility through commissioning — ensuring that the vision established at conception is faithfully realised in every detail of construction.

How We Work

From Catchment
to Current

01
Hydrological Assessment

Flow gauging, catchment analysis, and 3D modelling establish the available water resource and optimal generation profile for your site.

02
Concept Design

Civil, mechanical, and electrical concept design explores intake, penstock, powerhouse, and tailrace options — optimising yield, cost, and environmental impact.

03
Environmental Consenting

We prepare environmental impact assessments and manage regulatory consenting, including fish pass design and abstraction licensing.

04
Procurement & Construction

Turbine and generator procurement, civil works supervision, and commissioning management — delivered to programme and budget.

05
Operations & Optimisation

SCADA-monitored operations with real-time generation reporting, fish pass monitoring, and scheduled maintenance programmes.

Our Story

Two Decades of
Flowing Power

2006

Aqua Atelier Founded

Three civil engineers establish the atelier in Inverness, commissioning their first 1.2MW run-of-river scheme on the River Findhorn.

2010

First International Project

A 6MW Alpine scheme in Haute-Savoie marks Aqua's first international commission, establishing a pattern of European excellence.

2015

100MW Milestone

The completion of Loch Rannoch Power pushes total commissioned capacity beyond 100MW — a landmark in the studio's history.

2020

Micro Hydro Division

Aqua launches a dedicated micro hydro service for estates, farms, and private landowners — making premium hydropower accessible at any scale.

2025

35 Schemes Worldwide

A global portfolio of 35 completed hydropower schemes delivering 480MW of clean, renewable baseload electricity from flowing water.
