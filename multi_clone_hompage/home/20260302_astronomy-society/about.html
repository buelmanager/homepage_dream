About the Society

Looking Up, Together
Since 1987

We are a community of amateur astronomers who believe the night sky belongs to everyone. Our mission is to share the wonder of the cosmos, preserve dark skies, and cultivate a new generation of observers.

Our Origin

Twelve Stargazers and a Borrowed Telescope

The Celestial Society began informally in 1987 when twelve friends — teachers, engineers, and one retired ship captain — started gathering on a hillside outside the city on moonless nights. Their only equipment was a 6-inch refractor borrowed from a local school.

Within five years the group had grown to sixty members, established a formal charter, and began fundraising for a permanent home. What made us grow was not equipment or expertise — it was the shared feeling of standing beneath the stars and knowing you were not alone in your sense of wonder.

Our Mission

Science, Community, and Wonder

Today we serve over 480 active members across three regional chapters. Our programming spans beginner observation nights and advanced astrophotography workshops to citizen science campaigns contributing data to international research databases.

We run an active youth outreach programme that reaches more than 800 schoolchildren each year, introducing them to telescopes, the scientific method, and the humbling perspective that comes from knowing your place in the universe.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

01

Curiosity Without Borders

The universe does not discriminate. We welcome anyone who looks up and feels that pull of curiosity, regardless of age, background, or experience level.

02

Rigour and Humility

We pursue accuracy in our observations and honesty in what we do not yet know. Precision and wonder are not opposites — they reinforce each other.

03

Stewardship of the Sky

We have a duty to protect the resource we love most. Advocacy for dark skies and responsible outdoor lighting is central to our identity as a club.
