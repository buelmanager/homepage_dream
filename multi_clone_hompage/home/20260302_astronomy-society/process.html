Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to own a telescope to join?

Not at all. We have a fleet of shared telescopes available for all members, from small refractors for beginners to large Dobsonians for experienced observers. Equipment loan is included in Astronomer and Fellow memberships.

Can I join if I have no astronomy experience?

Absolutely. The majority of our members started with zero knowledge. Our orientation night and beginner programme are specifically designed to welcome people at the very beginning of their journey.

How often do events run?

We run observation nights every new moon (roughly monthly), plus workshops, special events for celestial phenomena, and our annual dark sky weekend. Members typically have 8–12 events available per month.

Is there a family or youth membership?

Yes — we offer family memberships at £130/year covering two adults and up to three children under 18. Youth memberships (under 25) are available at £32/year with full Observer benefits.
