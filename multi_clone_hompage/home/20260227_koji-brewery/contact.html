Koji Masterclasses

Hands-on workshops led personally by Hayashi Kenji. Limited to four participants per session to allow full engagement with the material and individual feedback.

Foundations

The Koji Intensive

A two-day immersive workshop covering the complete koji cultivation process from grain selection through harvest. Participants produce their own shio koji to take home.

Duration: 2 days (Saturday–Sunday) Location: Nishiki Studio, Kyoto Group size: Maximum 4 participants Investment: ¥180,000 per person
Advanced

Miso Architecture

A focused half-day workshop exploring the variables that shape miso character: grain selection, koji strain, salt ratio, aging duration, and environmental conditions.

Duration: 5 hours Location: Nishiki Studio, Kyoto Group size: Maximum 4 participants Investment: ¥65,000 per person
Corporate / Restaurant

On-Site Workshop
