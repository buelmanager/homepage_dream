What We Do

Everything on
the Spectrum

From weekly HF nets and contest operations to digital mode evenings, ARES emergency exercises, and hands-on antenna construction — Frequency ARC keeps busy all year.

HF
Tuesday Night HF Net

Nightly check-in net on 14.225 MHz. Members exchange signal reports, traffic, and club news. Open to all Technician+ operators via phone patch.

Nightly
Digital
FT8 / FT4 Evening

Monthly gathering using WSJT-X software modes. Contest exchange practice, DX contact logging, and weak signal propagation analysis.

Monthly
Emergency
ARES Quarterly Drill

Full emergency communication exercise including ICS message passing, portable station deployment, and cross-agency coordination simulation.

Quarterly
Antenna
Antenna Build Day

Hands-on construction of Yagi, EFHW wire antenna, or portable dipole kits. Materials provided. Previous builds have been tested on-air immediately after.

Monthly
Digital
SDR & Spectrum Night

Software-defined radio demonstrations and workshops covering RTL-SDR, GQRX, aircraft tracking, weather satellite decoding, and signal analysis.

Monthly
HF
ARRL Field Day

Annual ARRL Field Day emergency preparedness event. W0FRQ consistently places in the top 50 nationwide for our category. All members participate.
