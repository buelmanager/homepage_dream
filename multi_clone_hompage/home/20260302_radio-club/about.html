Our Story

Sixty Years
on the Air

Founded in 1962 by 14 passionate operators sharing a basement and a surplus HF rig, Frequency Amateur Radio Club has grown into one of the most active amateur radio organisations in the region — with over 840 licensed members spanning every operator class.

Our club station W0FRQ operates continuously, hosting weekly nets, emergency preparedness exercises, digital modes events, and contesting weekends. We hold ARRL affiliated club status and maintain an active ARES emergency group recognised by county emergency management.

We believe amateur radio is more than a hobby — it is a public service, a technical discipline, and a community. Whether you're chasing DX across the 20-metre band or building your first wire antenna, Frequency ARC is your home on the air.

Leadership

Club Officers
2026

Club President
Raymond Xu
W0RXU — Extra Class

Raymond has held his Extra class license for 22 years and served as club president since 2020. A retired RF systems engineer, he manages club operations, partnerships with county emergency management, and the annual hamfest.

Technical Director
Priya Nakamura
KD0PNK — Extra Class

Priya oversees all club station upgrades, the repeater network, and the digital infrastructure including APRS, Winlink, and SDR systems. She runs the monthly antenna construction workshops attended by over 40 members.

ARES Emergency Coordinator
Tobias Malone
N0TBM — General Class

Tobias coordinates Frequency ARC's emergency communication team — 38 active ARES members trained in ICS communications, portable operation, and Winlink digital traffic handling for activated emergencies.

What Drives Us

Our Club
Values

→

Elmer Culture

Every new member is paired with an experienced operator. We believe passing knowledge forward is the cornerstone of the amateur radio tradition.

→

Public Service

We exist to serve our community. From emergency nets to public events communication, W0FRQ is always ready when needed most.

→

Technical Growth

Amateur radio is a discipline as much as a hobby. We push the boundaries of RF, digital modes, antennas, and software-defined radio together.

→

Inclusivity

From YLs and newcomers to seasoned contesters, every operator brings value. Our club welcomes all who share a love for the spectrum.
