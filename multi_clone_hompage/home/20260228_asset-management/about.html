Our Story

Four Decades of
Disciplined Capital

Founded in 1983 by former Goldman Sachs fixed income chief Richard Harlow and quantitative economist Dr. Elena Vasquez, Stratum Capital was built on the premise that institutional investors deserve a fundamentally different kind of manager — one whose interests are structurally aligned with client outcomes, not asset growth for its own sake.

Milestones

A History Built on
Consistent Conviction

Every year in Stratum's history has been defined not by the markets, but by our decisions within them. From our founding with $420M in seed capital to managing $340B across five asset classes, we have grown by earning trust — one mandate at a time. Below are the moments that shaped who we are.

1983
Founding with $420M Seed Capital

Richard Harlow and Dr. Elena Vasquez launch Stratum Capital Management in New York with a global equity mandate from three university endowments. The core thesis: research-driven active management can systematically outperform passive benchmarks across full market cycles.

1991
Launch of Fixed Income & Structured Credit Platform

As institutional demand for liability-driven investing grew, Stratum launched its fixed income platform under Chief Fixed Income Officer Margaret Osei. The platform crossed $10B AUM within four years of inception.

1997
Navigated Asian Financial Crisis with Zero Mandate Losses

Stratum's systematic risk management framework was tested during the Asian contagion. Every client mandate met or exceeded its benchmark. The experience cemented our stress-testing protocols that remain in use today.

2005
Private Credit Platform Established

Stratum entered private markets with a direct lending mandate targeting mid-market companies in North America. The illiquidity premium thesis proved prescient as the platform now manages $62B across 14 vintage years.

2008–2009
Global Financial Crisis — Preservation and Opportunity

Stratum reduced equity beta in client portfolios 6 months before the Lehman collapse. Subsequently deployed $18B in distressed credit opportunities, generating exceptional vintage-year returns for clients who maintained their mandates.

2018
Crossed $200B AUM — Opened Singapore and Abu Dhabi Offices

Stratum's Asia-Pacific and Middle East growth necessitated dedicated regional infrastructure. Singapore now hosts our regional CIO and 60+ analysts covering Pan-Asian markets and sovereign wealth fund relationships.

2024
$340B AUM — Launch of Quantitative Systematic Strategies

Stratum's newest platform combines 40 years of fundamental research with machine learning and alternative data. The systematic strategies complement our discretionary mandates as a true diversifier with near-zero correlation to our equity book.

What We Stand For

Principles That
Outlast Markets

I
Intellectual Integrity

We dissent when evidence demands it. Our investment committees are structured to reward analytical courage — the ability to hold a contrarian position in the face of consensus pressure when our research supports it. Career risk is never a factor in portfolio decisions at Stratum.

A
Alignment of Interests

Every senior professional at Stratum invests personally in the strategies they manage. Our fee structures are performance-linked, not purely asset-based. When clients prosper, we prosper. When they don't, we don't. This alignment is non-negotiable.

T
Transparent Communication

We deliver quarterly attribution reports that explain not only what performed, but why — including honest post-mortems on positions that detracted. Institutional clients deserve complete transparency, not curated narratives.

S
Stewardship Mindset

We are stewards of capital that represents pension obligations, endowment missions, and sovereign mandates. The weight of that responsibility shapes every decision. We measure our success in decades, not quarters.
