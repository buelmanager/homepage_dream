The Four Stages

A Process Built for
Institutional Scale

Every investment decision at Stratum — from a $25M direct lending commitment to a $2B equity allocation — passes through the same four-stage process. The stages are not sequential silos; they are iterative loops. Research continuously feeds risk modeling; risk modeling refines portfolio construction; monitoring generates new research. The process is circular by design.

01
Research & Idea Generation

120 analysts. 47 markets. Proprietary fundamental scoring, ESG integration, alternative data. Ideas enter a centralized funnel, ranked by conviction and risk-adjusted expected return.

02
Risk Modeling & Stress Testing

Monte Carlo simulation, tail-risk analysis, cross-asset correlation stress testing. Every idea is subjected to a formal Investment Risk Report before committee review.

03
Portfolio Construction

Mean-variance optimization constrained by conviction weights, risk budget, and liquidity requirements. Position sizing reflects both upside potential and downside scenario analysis.

04
Monitoring & Attribution

Real-time risk monitoring. Quarterly performance attribution. Annual strategy reviews. Formal post-mortems on every position that detracted more than 50 basis points.

01
Stage One

Research & Idea Generation

The foundation of every Stratum allocation is proprietary fundamental research. Our 120-analyst global research platform is organized by sector and geography, with each analyst responsible for deep, ongoing coverage of a defined universe. We do not rotate analysts between sectors or geographies — continuity of coverage creates institutional memory that is a genuine competitive advantage.

  • Proprietary Fundamental Quality Score (FQS) rates every covered security on 18 factors including management capital allocation track record, return on invested capital trajectory, and balance sheet quality
  • Alternative data integration: satellite imagery, credit card transaction data, job posting data, and web traffic analytics supplement traditional financial analysis
  • Forensic accounting review on every potential investment: we look for earnings management, off-balance-sheet liabilities, and related-party transaction red flags before any capital commitment
  • Monthly sector meetings across all 12 offices to share insights, identify cross-asset opportunities, and challenge existing theses
Research Coverage Universe
10,000+ securities tracked globally. 2,400 with active Fundamental Quality Scores. 600 in the actively monitored watch list at any time.
Idea Submission Threshold
Analysts submit to the idea funnel only when: FQS > 70th percentile, expected 3-year risk-adjusted return > 250bps above benchmark, and ESG risk rating is "Acceptable" or above.
Research Independence
Research compensation is based on idea quality (measured by subsequent portfolio performance), not client relationships or trading volume. No research analyst has any client-facing responsibilities.
Contrarian Protocol
Ideas that diverge significantly from consensus are tagged for elevated scrutiny — but also receive a formal "advocate" assignment: a senior analyst whose sole role is to steelman the contrarian case before the committee.
02
Stage Two

Risk Modeling & Stress Testing

Stratum's risk management framework was designed by Chief Risk Officer Sunita Chandra, who pioneered tail-risk hedging for institutional portfolios at Deutsche Bank before joining Stratum in 2003. The framework treats risk management as an investment activity — not a compliance function. The Risk Management Committee is independent of the Portfolio Management team and has veto power over any allocation.

  • Every investment idea receives a formal Investment Risk Report (IRR) before Portfolio Review Committee consideration. The IRR documents: base case, bear case, and tail risk scenarios with probability estimates
  • Monte Carlo simulation using 10,000 portfolio paths to quantify the distribution of potential outcomes, including CVaR at 95th and 99th confidence levels
  • Cross-asset correlation stress tests calibrated to historical crisis periods: 1987, 1997-98, 2001, 2008-09, 2011, 2020
  • Liquidity stress testing: each position is evaluated for time-to-liquidation under normal market conditions and stressed market conditions (modeled as 50% of 30-day average volume)
Portfolio Risk Limits
Position-level VaR limit: 0.5% of portfolio. Sector-level risk budget: 3% TE contribution maximum. Portfolio-level VaR (95%, 1-day): must not exceed 1.5% of NAV without IC approval.
Risk Committee Independence
The Risk Management Committee is chaired by the CRO and reports directly to the Board of Directors. The CRO's compensation is partially determined by post-hoc accuracy of risk forecasts — not portfolio returns.
Real-Time Risk Monitoring
Portfolio risk is monitored in real-time during market hours using Stratum's proprietary STRAND risk system. Automated alerts trigger at 80% of VaR limits; escalation protocols activate at 95%.
03
Stage Three

Portfolio Construction

Stratum's portfolio construction methodology rejects naïve mean-variance optimization. We use a robust optimization framework that accounts for estimation error in expected returns and covariance matrices. Position sizes reflect conviction, risk budget utilization, liquidity constraints, and mandate-specific guidelines — all simultaneously solved through our proprietary EDIFICE system.

  • Robust optimization using Black-Litterman framework with Stratum's fundamental views as "views" — combining market equilibrium with analyst conviction in a mathematically principled way
  • Factor exposure constraints: portfolio factor tilts (value, growth, momentum, quality, low volatility) are monitored and bounded to ensure returns are driven by stock selection, not unintended factor bets
  • Maximum position size: 5% of portfolio in liquid strategies, 10% in concentrated mandates (with separate client consent). Minimum position size: 50bps (below this, risk is not worth the complexity)
  • Rebalancing is trigger-based (drift > 200bps from target weight) rather than calendar-based, reducing unnecessary transaction costs
Portfolio Review Committee
All portfolio changes > 1% of NAV require PRC approval. PRC meets weekly (Tuesdays, 7am). Decisions require a supermajority (4 of 6 members). Minutes are published to all senior investment staff within 24 hours.
Implementation Discipline
Stratum's trading team implements all portfolio changes using algorithmic execution with VWAP/TWAP benchmarks. Transaction cost analysis is reviewed quarterly and factored into position sizing decisions.
Mandate Customization
Client mandates with specific requirements (ESG exclusions, sector limits, currency hedging specifications) are implemented through a secondary optimization layer that applies client constraints on top of the base portfolio construction.
04
Stage Four

Monitoring & Attribution

Continuous monitoring is not performance review — it is thesis validation. Every position in every portfolio has a documented investment thesis with specific milestones. When a position diverges from its thesis, the protocol is re-evaluation, not panic. We distinguish between thesis-affirming price moves (hold or add) and thesis-violating price moves (review and potentially exit) with rigorous consistency.

  • Quarterly performance attribution reports for all client mandates: sector attribution, security selection attribution, factor attribution, and currency attribution — reported separately and together
  • Every position that detracted more than 50 basis points from benchmark receives a formal post-mortem within 90 days: what did we get wrong, at what stage, and what process change (if any) does this warrant?
  • Annual Strategy Reviews: each strategy platform undergoes a comprehensive annual review covering performance attribution, risk budget utilization, positioning vs. mandate constraints, and competitive benchmarking
  • Formal client reporting cadence: monthly NAV reports, quarterly attribution reports with CIO commentary, annual strategy outlook letters
Monitoring Frequency
Real-time: VaR, factor exposures, liquidity. Daily: position drift, sector weights, risk budget utilization. Weekly: relative performance vs benchmark. Monthly: attribution analysis. Quarterly: full mandate review.
Post-Mortem Protocol
Post-mortems are presented to the full Investment Committee, not just the responsible portfolio manager. Findings are archived in our institutional knowledge base and reviewed annually to identify systemic process improvement opportunities.
Client Transparency
All client reports include a "What Hurt Performance" section alongside "What Helped." We believe institutional clients deserve complete transparency — not curated narratives that obscure detractors.
ESG Integration

A Financial Lens,
Not an Ethical Filter

Stratum's approach to ESG integration is fundamentally financial. We do not exclude industries or companies on ethical grounds. We integrate ESG data because environmental liabilities, social license to operate, and governance quality are material financial variables that affect long-term risk-adjusted returns. Our analysts are trained to quantify ESG risk, not just score it.

E
Environmental

Carbon emission trajectories, physical climate risk exposure, regulatory compliance costs, and energy transition capex requirements are quantified as financial variables in our DCF models and stress tests.

S
Social

Labor relations, supply chain vulnerability, community license to operate, and product safety litigation history are evaluated for their potential to create financial liability, operational disruption, or reputational discount.

G
Governance

Executive compensation alignment, board independence and composition, audit quality, related-party transactions, and capital allocation track record. Poor governance is our most reliable predictor of long-term underperformance.

Risk Architecture

Risk Is an Investment
Not a Constraint

At Stratum, risk management is not the department that says "no." It is the department that ensures every "yes" is worth saying. Our risk management framework, developed over 40 years and stress-tested through every major market disruption of the modern era, is one of our most durable competitive advantages.

The distinction between risk control (limiting losses by restricting positions) and risk management (maximizing the return earned per unit of risk taken) is fundamental to Stratum's approach. We focus relentlessly on the second, because institutional clients hire us to earn risk-adjusted returns — not to avoid risk entirely.
