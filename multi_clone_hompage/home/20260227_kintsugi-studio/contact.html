Before You Write

What We
Need to Know

Tell us the story, not just the damage

The best commissions begin with a story. Where was the piece made? Who owned it before you? How did it break? The more context you provide, the better we can understand how to approach the repair — and the more meaningfully we can honor the object's history.

We are not simply mending ceramics. We are respecting objects that have been loved. When you write to us, write as you would write to someone who cares — because we do.
