Service 01

Emergency Repair

For precious pieces broken recently — an heirloom dropped, a cherished vessel chipped in the move. We receive your piece, assess the fractures, and begin stabilization with authentic urushi lacquer within 48 hours. Emergency repair is not a lesser service — it is simply a different rhythm of urgency applied to the same patient craft.

Fresh breaks are often easier to repair well. The edges are clean, the fragments complete. If your piece has broken recently, do not wait — contact us and we will guide you through safe packaging and delivery.

Service 02

Full Kintsugi Restoration

Our flagship offering — the complete kintsugi journey from first assessment to final burnished gold. We receive your piece, examine every fracture with the care of a physician, clean the edges of decades of accumulated debris, bond the fragments with traditional hon-urushi lacquer harvested in Japan, and finally apply pure 24-karat gold powder with a bamboo brush — stroke by patient stroke.

The full restoration takes six to twelve weeks. We send photographic updates at each stage. The piece that returns to you is not the piece you sent — it is something more than it was before it broke.

Service 04

Gold Type Selection

The choice of metal profoundly shapes the character of a kintsugi repair. 24-karat gold brings luminous warmth, a solar radiance that transforms every seam into a line of light. Silver is quieter, more lunar — a reflective restraint that suits celadons and pale stonewares. Platinum reads as contemporary and architectural. Copper offers autumnal warmth, the color of autumn leaves and aging bronze.

We offer a complimentary consultation to help you choose. We may suggest a different metal from the one you imagined — and we always explain why. The right choice transforms a repair into a revelation.

Metal Selection

Choose Your
Transformation

Each metal brings a different mood to the repaired seam. There is no hierarchy of beauty here — only different expressions of the same philosophy.

24k Pure Gold

Kintsugi — 金継ぎ

The classic. Luminous, warm, and undeniably precious. Pure gold seams glow with their own light, transforming every fracture into a solar line. The traditional choice of Japanese masters for five centuries.

Most Popular

Silver

Gintsugi — 銀継ぎ

Lunar and restrained. Silver seams carry a quiet dignity — reflective and cool, they complement celadons, white porcelains, and pale stonewares with understated grace.

For Pale Ceramics

Platinum

Shirotsugi — 白継ぎ

Contemporary and architectural. Platinum brings a refined modernity to the ancient technique — colder than silver, harder, more precise. The choice for sculptural and industrial ceramic forms.

Modern Character

Copper

Akagane — 赤金

Autumnal and warm. Copper seams carry the color of autumn, of aged bronze, of oxidized earth. A deeply organic choice that pairs beautifully with terracotta, earthenware, and ochre-toned ceramics.
