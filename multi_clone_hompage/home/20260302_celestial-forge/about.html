Our Story

Founder
Callum Rowe
Master Metallurgist & Founder

Callum Rowe spent the first decade of his career as a materials scientist at the Natural History Museum in London, cataloguing metallic meteorites and studying the crystalline Widmanstätten patterns that form only over millions of years of slow cooling in space.

In 2009, struck by the profound beauty of a pallasite meteorite he was preparing for display — its olivine crystals suspended in a lattice of iron-nickel alloy — he asked a simple question: could this material become the finest jewelry ever created?

Seventeen years later, EXON has provided the answer. Working from a converted forge in London's Bermondsey, Rowe and his team of six master smiths have created over 3,000 pieces that are owned and treasured by collectors across six continents.

Our Journey

Fifteen Years of Celestial Craft

2009
The Forge Opens

Callum Rowe establishes EXON in a 400 sq ft Bermondsey workshop. The first piece — the Meteor Ring No.1 — sells to a private collector in Zurich within a week of completion.

2013
First International Exhibition

EXON presents at Masterpiece London and sells out its entire debut collection of twelve pieces. The New York Times covers the exhibition, calling EXON "the most provocative jeweler working today."

2017
Pallasite Collection Launch

The landmark Pallasite Collection — 24 pieces set with olivine crystals from the Esquel meteorite — sells out within 48 hours. Demand triggers an 18-month waiting list for commissions.

2021
Scientific Partnership

EXON formalizes its partnership with the Meteoritical Society, becoming the first jeweler to contribute to the scientific classification of new meteorite falls. A portion of every commission funds meteoritical research.

2024
The Celestial Forge Collection

EXON's most ambitious collection to date: 36 pieces crafted from six different meteorite types, each accompanied by a 40-page bound scientific monograph. All pieces are reserved within two months of announcement.

What We Stand For

The EXON Principles

01
Scientific Integrity

We never use meteorite in ways that compromise the scientific record. Every specimen we acquire was legally obtained from verified private falls, and its provenance is fully documented.

02
Artisanal Excellence

All EXON pieces are made entirely by hand in our London forge. We refuse to scale production, automate fabrication, or accelerate our process for commercial reasons.

03
Permanent Record

Every piece enters our archive permanently. We maintain lifetime repair service, resizing, and the right of reacquisition for future generations. An EXON piece is never abandoned.
