Our Foundation

Every Piece Carries the Memory of a Star

We harvest meteorites from the world's most remote crater fields — materials that crossed the void of interstellar space to arrive on Earth. We then work with master metallurgists to transform them into objects of transcendent beauty.

◈
Celestial Origins

Every meteorite we source is authenticated by gemological and meteoritical experts. We trace the provenance of each specimen to its fall site, cataloguing its cosmic journey.

⬡
Master Metallurgy

Our forge masters have trained under the finest European metalworking traditions. The pairing of ancient craft with extraterrestrial material yields jewelry unlike anything on Earth.

✦
Eternal Signatures

Each EXON piece is registered in our permanent archive, complete with its meteorite certificate of authenticity, spectroscopic analysis, and numbered provenance document.

How We Work

From Cosmos to Craft

01
Meteorite Sourcing

We work with certified meteorite hunters and scientific institutions worldwide to ethically source authenticated specimens — from Gibeon iron meteorites to pallasite olivine.

02
Gemological Analysis

Each specimen undergoes rigorous testing — spectroscopic analysis, Widmanstätten pattern examination, and chemical composition profiling before a single cut is made.

03
Forge & Fabrication

Working in our London atelier, our master smiths integrate the meteorite with 18k gold, platinum, or blackened silver using techniques refined over two decades.

04
Archive & Certification

Your piece is permanently numbered, photographed, and registered in the EXON archive. A bound provenance document accompanies every commission.

Our Story

Fifteen Years at the Intersection of Sky and Earth

EXON was founded in 2009 by metallurgist and astronomer Callum Rowe, whose lifelong obsession with celestial materials led him to the realization that the most extraordinary jewelry on Earth would be made from beyond it. Our London forge has since created over 3,000 singular pieces for collectors on six continents.

The Studio

A Forge Built for the Extraordinary

Our Bermondsey atelier houses a complete gemological laboratory alongside traditional forging hearths. Every tool, technique, and material choice is made with a single purpose: to honor the celestial matter we are privileged to work with.

⚡
