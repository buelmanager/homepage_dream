How We Work

Four Stages, Ten Weeks,
One Singular Object

Every EXON commission follows the same rigorous sequence — from meteorite selection to final certification. We never rush a single stage.

01
Meteorite Sourcing & Authentication

Before any design work begins, we select the specific meteorite specimen for your commission. We maintain relationships with nine certified meteorite dealers across five continents, and we hold a curated inventory of 30–50 authenticated specimens at any time.

For each commission we present you with three to five candidate specimens, with full classification data for each — fall date, fall location, class, and chemical composition. You choose the material that resonates.

02
Gemological Analysis & Design

Your selected specimen undergoes full XRF spectroscopic analysis in our in-house laboratory. We identify the unique crystalline structures and inclusion patterns that will guide our design decisions.

Callum Rowe personally designs each commission in close dialogue with the client. We produce three initial sketches, refined into final technical drawings before any material is touched.

03
Forge & Fabrication

Our forge masters work exclusively by hand using traditional techniques — bench fabrication, hand engraving, and stone setting. No CNC machining or 3D printing is employed at any stage.

The integration of meteorite with noble metals is the most technically demanding phase. Each alloy combination requires custom temperature protocols developed through years of experimentation.

04
Archive & Certification

Upon completion, your piece is photographed against a calibrated background, measured, and entered into the EXON Permanent Archive with its unique registration number. This record is maintained indefinitely.

A bound provenance document — typically 20–40 pages — accompanies each piece. It includes the meteorite classification certificate, spectroscopic data, design drawings, fabrication notes, and care instructions.

Commission Timeline

Week by Week

Weeks 1–2
Consultation & Selection

We present meteorite candidates, discuss your intentions, and confirm the design direction. A 25% deposit secures your commission slot.

Weeks 3–5
Analysis & Design

Laboratory analysis of your specimen, sketch development, and finalisation of technical drawings with your approval.

Weeks 6–14
Fabrication

Hand fabrication begins. A progress update with photography is provided at the midpoint of this phase.

Weeks 15–18
Finishing & Delivery

Final polishing, quality review, archiving, documentation binding, and delivery in our bespoke mahogany presentation case.
