Our Commitment to You

What to Expect When You Reach Out

Every inquiry to Ashworth Private is treated with the discretion and seriousness it deserves. You will speak directly with a Senior Partner — not an assistant, not an automated system. We take on very few new clients each year, which means every inquiry receives our full attention.

There is no sales pressure, no obligation, and no rush. Great wealth management relationships are built on trust — and trust begins with a genuine conversation.

I
Response Within 24 Hours

A Senior Partner reviews every inquiry personally and will contact you directly within one business day.

II
Absolute Confidentiality

Your information is never shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party under any circumstances.

III
No Obligation, Ever

The discovery consultation carries no obligation. We believe the right decision takes time and trust.

IV
Senior-Level Access from Day One

From your first call to your last, you work directly with the senior partners who lead our firm.
