The Wealth Planning Journey

Five Stages to Clarity and Confidence

We believe the quality of your wealth strategy is only as good as the quality of the process behind it. Our five-stage engagement model — refined over nine decades of service to extraordinary families — ensures that no detail is overlooked, and that every strategy is deeply personal, rigorously constructed, and built to endure.

I
Stage One

Discovery Consultation

Every relationship begins with listening. Your dedicated Senior Partner meets with you — privately, confidentially, without obligation — to understand the full scope of your wealth, your family structure, your values, and the legacy you envision. This is not a sales meeting. It is the beginning of a partnership.

Typical duration: 90–120 minutes
a
Confidential introduction with a Senior Partner and our Chief Investment Officer
b
Deep-dive conversation on family structure, goals, values, and existing advisors
c
Preliminary discussion of your wealth planning priorities and timeline
d
Mutual assessment: we confirm whether Ashworth is the right fit for your family
02
II
Stage Two

Comprehensive Wealth Assessment

Our team conducts an exhaustive review of your complete financial picture: every holding, liability, tax position, estate structure, and insurance policy. We identify opportunities your current advisors may have missed, and vulnerabilities that demand immediate attention.

Typical duration: 2–3 weeks
a
Complete inventory of assets, liabilities, and cash flows across all entities
b
Tax position analysis: current year and five-year forward projections
c
Estate structure review: trusts, beneficiary designations, ownership structures
d
Risk and insurance gap analysis: life, liability, and property coverage
03
III
Stage Three

Bespoke Strategy Development

Armed with a complete understanding of your situation, our team — investment, estate, tax, and family office specialists working in full coordination — develops a fully integrated, bespoke wealth strategy. No templates. No off-the-shelf solutions. Everything built from the ground up for your family.

Typical duration: 2–3 weeks
a
Custom investment policy statement with target allocation and risk parameters
b
Multi-generational estate restructuring proposals with tax impact modeling
c
Integrated tax reduction strategy across income, estate, and philanthropic channels
d
Comprehensive wealth plan presentation with your Senior Partner and full advisory team
04
IV
Stage Four

Precision Implementation

We execute your strategy with methodical care, transitioning assets to minimize tax and market impact, establishing or modifying trust and estate structures, and ensuring every element of your plan is in place before moving forward. You are kept fully informed at every step.

Typical duration: 3–6 weeks
a
Tax-efficient asset transition and consolidation executed in coordinated phases
b
Trust establishment, beneficiary updates, and ownership structure modifications
c
Onboarding to Ashworth client portal with full portfolio transparency
d
Introduction to your dedicated relationship team and all service partners
05
V
Stage Five — Ongoing

Lifelong Partnership

Our relationship does not end at implementation — it deepens over time. Quarterly investment reviews, annual comprehensive wealth audits, and always-available access to your Senior Partner ensure your strategy evolves fluidly with your life, your family, and the world around you.

Continuous — year after year
a
Quarterly portfolio reviews with Senior Partner and investment team
b
Annual comprehensive wealth audit: investment, tax, estate, and family office
c
Life-event advisory: business sales, inheritances, divorce, next-generation transitions
d
Ashworth Family Symposium: annual private gathering for clients and their families

Frequently Asked Questions

What Families Ask Us

What is the minimum investment to work with Ashworth Private?

We work exclusively with families with investable assets of $25 million or more. Our multi-family office services begin at $50 million. We believe in maintaining a highly select client roster so that every family receives the depth of attention they deserve.

How long does the onboarding process typically take?

From your first discovery consultation to full implementation, the process typically spans six to eight weeks. We move deliberately — careful transitions matter more than speed when generational wealth is at stake.

Do you work with existing attorneys and accountants?

Absolutely. We serve as the coordinating hub of your advisory ecosystem. We collaborate closely with your existing legal, tax, and accounting professionals — and where gaps exist, we can introduce our network of trusted specialists.

How often will I hear from my Senior Partner?

Your Senior Partner is always available by phone. Beyond that, you receive formal quarterly reviews, annual comprehensive audits, and ad-hoc communications whenever markets, tax law, or life events create meaningful planning opportunities.

Is Ashworth a fiduciary?

Yes, unconditionally. As an SEC Registered Investment Adviser, we are legally obligated to place your interests above all others. We receive no commissions, referral fees, or incentive payments of any kind. Our sole compensation is the fee you pay us directly.

Can you work with international or multi-jurisdictional families?

Yes. We have deep experience serving families with assets, residency, and beneficiaries across multiple countries. We coordinate with legal and tax specialists in all relevant jurisdictions to build strategies that function seamlessly across borders.
