Our Story

Since 1929

Edward Ashworth Sr.
Founder — 1929
The Founding Vision
Built on discretion, driven by legacy.

In 1929, on the eve of the Great Crash, Edward Ashworth Sr. founded this firm with a singular conviction: that great wealth demands great stewardship, not speculation.

While others chased the markets, Ashworth protected his clients. That founding instinct — preservation before growth, relationship before transaction — has guided every generation of leadership since.

Today, under the stewardship of Edward Ashworth III, the firm manages $48 billion in assets for 1,200 families across five continents. The principles remain unchanged.

Our Charter
"We do not manage money. We steward legacies — the accumulated ambitions, sacrifices, and dreams of families who trusted us with what matters most."

Our Values

What We Stand For

I
Integrity Without Compromise

We are bound by a fiduciary standard that places your interests absolutely first — in every recommendation, every strategy, every conversation.

II
Absolute Discretion

The affairs of our clients have never been disclosed and never will be. Our reputation for privacy is not a policy — it is a covenant.

III
Generational Thinking

Every strategy is designed with your grandchildren in mind. We measure success not in quarters, but in the enduring strength of family legacies.

IV
Disciplined Stewardship

We are rigorous in risk management, careful in execution, and steady in our convictions — particularly when markets test resolve.

V
Human-Centered Counsel

Behind every portfolio is a family with hopes, fears, and values. We never forget the human dimension of every financial decision we make together.

VI
Excellence Without Ego

We are a quiet firm. We do not seek recognition. We seek results — and the deep satisfaction of clients who trust us completely.

Our History

Nine Decades of Stewardship

1929
Founding

Edward Ashworth Sr. establishes the firm at 52 Wall Street with a founding belief that preservation of capital is the highest duty of a wealth advisor.

1952
Second Generation

Edward Ashworth Jr. assumes leadership, expanding the firm's international capabilities and establishing the firm's first European office in Geneva.

1974
Multi-Family Office Launch

Ashworth pioneers the multi-family office model, offering comprehensive family governance, estate coordination, and tax services under one roof.

1998
Alternative Investments

Launch of Ashworth Private Capital, providing clients exclusive access to private equity, real assets, and hedge fund strategies unavailable in public markets.

2011
Third Generation

Edward Ashworth III assumes the role of Managing Partner, introducing next-generation advisory technology while honoring the firm's founding values.

2024
$48 Billion Milestone

Ashworth Private crosses $48 billion in assets under management, serving 1,200 families across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.
