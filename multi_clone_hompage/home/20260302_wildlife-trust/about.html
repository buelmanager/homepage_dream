About Verdania

Our Manifesto
"The wild is not a commodity to be managed — it is a living inheritance to be protected, restored, and passed on intact to every generation that follows. Verdania exists to honour that obligation."
Leadership

Guided by Scientists
and Stewards

Verdania Wildlife Trust was founded by a collective of field ecologists, conservation lawyers, and community land advocates who shared a single conviction: that the destruction of wild habitat was not inevitable, and that organised, science-led action could reverse it.

Today, our leadership team brings together over 200 years of combined conservation experience spanning six continents. Every strategic decision is informed by on-the-ground data from our field programmes.

We are governed by an independent board of trustees drawn from ecology, law, finance, and indigenous land stewardship — ensuring that Verdania remains accountable to both the ecosystems we protect and the communities who depend on them.

Dr. Eleanor Marsh
Chief Executive, Verdania Wildlife Trust
Core Values

What Guides Us

◉
Scientific Rigour
Every programme is designed, executed, and evaluated using peer-reviewed methodology. We publish our data openly and submit to independent audit.
◈
Community First
Lasting conservation requires local ownership. We invest in community capacity and share governance with the people who live alongside the habitats we protect.
◇
Adaptive Management
Nature does not follow fixed plans. Our teams respond to changing ecological conditions with agility, revising strategies based on continuous monitoring data.
Timeline

Thirty Years in the Field

1994
Foundation
Verdania Wildlife Trust established by a coalition of ecologists and conservation advocates, with an initial mandate to protect threatened forest systems in northern Europe.
2000
First Reserves Secured
Acquisition of the Miredale and Kestrel Hill nature reserves, providing permanent protection for 14,000 hectares of critical wetland and upland habitat.
2003
Thornfield Reserve
The Trust's landmark acquisition: 56,000 hectares of temperate old-growth forest, securing habitat for 14 endangered mammal species.
2011
100 Species Milestone
Verdania's recovery programmes achieved measurable population increases for 100 distinct species — a milestone recognised by the International Union for Conservation of Nature.
2022
Global Partnerships
Formal partnerships with four international conservation bodies extended Verdania's reach to 12 countries, creating transboundary habitat corridors across 280,000 hectares.
