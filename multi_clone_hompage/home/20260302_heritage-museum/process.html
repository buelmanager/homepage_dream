Research & Conservation

Knowledge at the Heart of Heritage

The National Heritage Museum is not merely a place of display — it is an active centre of research, conservation, and knowledge creation. Our curatorial and scientific staff conduct original scholarship that advances the field of cultural heritage studies worldwide, publishing hundreds of peer-reviewed articles, books, and catalogues each year.

I

Archaeological Science

Isotopic analysis, radiocarbon dating, and spatial archaeology reveal the origins, provenance, and biography of objects in our collection, transforming our understanding of the ancient past.

II

Conservation Science

Our conservation laboratories develop and apply cutting-edge materials science to preserve fragile objects, sharing knowledge with institutions worldwide through publications and training programmes.

III

Digital Scholarship

3D scanning, multispectral imaging, and machine learning open new possibilities for understanding and sharing our collection — making objects accessible to researchers who cannot visit in person.

Conservation

The Art and Science of Preservation

Our conservation studios work on hundreds of objects each year — from the microscopic cleaning of ancient ceramics to the structural stabilisation of monumental stone sculptures. Every intervention is documented, reversible where possible, and guided by the principle of doing as little as necessary to ensure long-term survival.

The Studios also run an internationally recognised training programme, developing the next generation of conservation professionals, and accept applications from visiting conservators from institutions worldwide.

Research Access

Access for Scholars

Study Rooms

Accredited researchers may apply for access to objects not on public display in our supervised study rooms. Prior appointment required; access is granted for bona fide scholarly purposes.

Archive Access

Our institutional archive holds over 160 years of acquisition records, correspondence, and photographs. The archive is open to qualified researchers by appointment.

Fellowships

We offer six research fellowships annually, hosted across our curatorial and scientific departments. Applications are accepted each autumn for the following year.

Photography

High-resolution images of objects in the permanent collection are available for scholarly use via our online catalogue. Commercial licensing is also available.
