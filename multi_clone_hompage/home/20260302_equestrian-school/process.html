How to Join
Cavalier

We admit students at all levels — from novice riders to those with competition experience. What we ask for is a genuine connection with horses, a capacity for patience, and the seriousness of purpose that this kind of training demands.

The Process

Five Steps to
the Saddle

01

Initial Enquiry

Send us an enquiry form or email with your name, program of interest, current riding level, and any horses you own or lease. We will respond within five working days with further information and an invitation to visit.

02

Visit & Yard Assessment

We strongly prefer that all prospective students visit the Academy before applying. A half-day visit includes a tour of the estate, meeting the horses, and an informal conversation with a faculty member. There is no charge for this visit.

Visits available throughout the year
03

Riding Assessment

A 45-minute riding assessment on one of our Academy horses, observed by two faculty members. We assess balance, empathy with the horse, and the quality of the rider's aids. We are not looking for technical perfection — we are looking for feel, and the potential to develop it.

Casual clothing acceptable — no formal attire required
04

Formal Application

Following a successful assessment, we invite formal application: full personal details, program preference, and a brief statement of intent. What do you want from two years at Cavalier? What do you expect will be hardest?

05

Offer & Placement

Offers are made within one week of application. A deposit of £2,000 reserves your place. We will confirm your horse placement, accommodation options, and provide a full pre-arrival guide.
