## Brand

Cavalier Equestrian Academy is a classical equestrian academy on a 280-acre Cotswolds estate, England, established in 1884 by Colonel William Ainsworth. Programs: Dressage, Show Jumping, Horse Care & Management, Equine Sports Management.
