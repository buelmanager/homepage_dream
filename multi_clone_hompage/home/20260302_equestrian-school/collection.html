Programs

Four Disciplines,
One Estate

From the two-year professional diplomas in Dressage and Show Jumping to specialist certificates in Horse Care and Equine Sports Management — each program is shaped by the land and horses of the Ainsworth Estate.

2-Year Diploma — Professional Track

Dressage

The most classical of our programmes. Students work through the scales of training from rhythm and relaxation through to collection and straightness. Lessons on Academy horses are supplemented with work on students' own mounts where applicable. The programme culminates in an FEI examination at Advanced Medium level.

Duration
2 Academic Years
Horses Provided
Yes, Academy horses
Qualification
BHS Stage 4 / BHSI track
Tuition
£18,500 Per Year
2-Year Diploma — Professional Track

Show Jumping

From gymnastic gridwork at 80cm to affiliated competition at 1.20m and above. Students develop adjustability, eye, and the mental composure required for competition. Monthly outings to affiliated competitions under the Academy name are included from Semester Two.

2 Academic Years
Competitions
Included (monthly)
Qualification
BHS Stage 4 + BSJA membership

1-Year Certificate

Horse Care & Management

A comprehensive year in equine care: stable management, nutrition, equine anatomy and physiology, veterinary first aid, farriery, breeding and foaling, and yard management. Students are responsible for a string of Academy horses throughout the year. Prepares graduates for positions as grooms, stud hands, or head yard personnel.

Duration
1 Academic Year
Riding
3 times per week included
Qualification
BHS Stage 2 minimum
Tuition
£12,500
1-Year Certificate

Equine Sports Management

The business and logistics of equestrian sport. Event organisation, FEI regulations, sponsorship and commercial management, stable operations as a business, and the international competition circuit. Students complete a work placement with a recognised equestrian organisation during the programme.

Duration
1 Academic Year
Work Placement
6 weeks included
Format
Lectures + placement
Tuition
