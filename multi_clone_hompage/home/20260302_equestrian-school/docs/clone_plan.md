# Clone Plan — Cavalier Equestrian Academy

## Concept

A premium equestrian academy template with a Type B parallax hero — the horse image fills the viewport with depth and movement, reinforcing the outdoor, land-based character of the school. Steel blue/navy palette evokes the English countryside, morning mist over fields, and the formal attire of classical equestrian sport.

## Hero (Type B Parallax)

- Single full-bleed background image with mouse parallax effect
- Gradient overlay darkens bottom to ensure text legibility
- Content panel left-aligned with eyebrow, title, tagline, and dual CTA

## Color

- Deep navy (#141820 bg) with steel blue accent (#A0C4D8) — cool, refined, English
- Suggests morning light on dew, country estate formality, the palette of English equestrian sport

## Typography

- Playfair Display (elegant italic serif) + Lato (clean humanist sans) — establishes both heritage and professionalism
