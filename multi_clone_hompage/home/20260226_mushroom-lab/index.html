Cultivation Process

The Laboratory

Five controlled stages transform raw substrate into extraordinary fungi. Every variable — temperature, humidity, light spectrum, CO₂ concentration — is monitored in real time.

01
Substrate Preparation
Hardwood sawdust, wheat bran, gypsum, and purified water are blended, sterilized at 121°C, and vacuum-sealed in polypropylene bags.
02
Inoculation
Lab-isolated liquid culture is injected under laminar flow. Zero contamination tolerance. Each batch is tracked by DNA fingerprint.
03
Incubation
Bags colonize in darkness at 23°C over 18–24 days. Mycelium threads weave through substrate, building a dense white network.
04
Fruiting
Temperature drops to 16°C. Humidity rises to 90%. Fresh air exchange triggers pinning. Mushrooms emerge within 5–8 days.
05
Harvest & Cold-Chain
Hand-twisted at peak development. Packed within 2 hours in breathable paper at 2°C. Delivered to your door within 96 hours of harvest.

Research & Wellness

The Network Beneath

Mycelium isn't just root — it's intelligence. A single cubic centimeter of forest soil holds 8 kilometers of fungal threads transmitting nutrients, signals, and chemical defense across the ecosystem.

01
Cognitive Enhancement
Lion's mane stimulates Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) synthesis. Clinical studies show measurable improvement in memory and focus after 8 weeks of daily consumption.
02
Immune Modulation
Beta-glucans in shiitake, reishi, and turkey tail activate macrophages and NK cells. Chaga contains the highest known antioxidant density of any food source.
03
Gut Microbiome Support
Fungal polysaccharides serve as prebiotic fuel for Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus strains. Shown to reduce systemic inflammation markers in 4 weeks.
04
Adaptogenic Stress Response
Reishi triterpenes regulate cortisol rhythms and support deep sleep architecture. Used in integrative oncology programs at leading research hospitals.
