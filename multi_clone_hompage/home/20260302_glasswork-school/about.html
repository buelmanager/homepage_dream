About Ignis
Glass School

From a converted factory in Prague to a UNESCO-recognised institution, Ignis Glass School has spent thirty years teaching artists to work with fire's most demanding, most beautiful material.

Born in the Baroque, Built from Fire

When Marco Veltri left Murano in 1991, he carried with him something the island's glassmaking guilds had kept for four centuries: the complete knowledge of how to make glass by breath, by heat, by the intelligence of experienced hands. He intended to give it away.

Ignis Glass School was never meant to be exclusive. Its founding principle — stated in the first prospectus printed in Czech and Italian — was that technical mastery should be teachable, and that art made from fire deserved a proper institution in which to be learned.

Thirty years later, Ignis has trained over 1,800 artists in four glass disciplines and built one of the finest teaching hot shops in Europe. But the founding conviction has not changed: the knowledge of glass belongs to those willing to learn it.

What We Believe

The Principles of Ignis

Material Truth First

Before artistic expression, students must understand how glass actually behaves — its viscosity curves, its thermal expansion, its relationship with colour and light. Design from ignorance produces inferior work.

Safety as Discipline

The hot shop environment demands constant awareness. We teach safety not as a set of rules but as a quality of attention — one that serves students well in the studio and beyond it.

The Lineage Matters

Glassmaking traditions represent centuries of embodied research. We trace and teach the lineages behind our techniques — Murano, Bohemian, Art Nouveau, Studio Glass — because understanding the past makes better artists.

Small Cohorts, Real Mentorship

Glass cannot be taught to large groups. Our maximum cohort size of twelve ensures that every student receives the individual attention their practice requires and that the hot shop remains a productive, safe environment.

Come Work with Fire

Applications are open year-round. Hot Glass and Lampwork cohorts start monthly. Contact us to discuss which programme suits your level and ambitions.
