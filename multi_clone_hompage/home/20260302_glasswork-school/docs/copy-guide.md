# Copy Guide — Ignis Glass School

## Voice
- Technical authority without intimidation
- Honest about the demands of hot glass work
- Celebrates fire, heat and material truth
- European sensibility — understated excellence

## Key Messages
1. Murano lineage — verified and unbroken
2. Four complete glass disciplines under one roof
3. Small cohorts, personal instruction
4. Prague as a European craft capital
5. Material mastery before artistic expression

## Tone Examples
- "The hot shop is no place for impatience."
- "Understanding heat is the core skill of every glass discipline."
- "Work held in the collections of the Corning Museum of Glass."

## Avoid
- Wellness/lifestyle language
- "Unique" claims without specifics
- Hyperbole about fire or danger
