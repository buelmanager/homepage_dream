# Design Decisions — Ignis Glass School

## Color
Shared amber-brown palette with The Loom School but applied differently — here, it evokes the warm glow of a furnace interior and molten glass rather than earthen textiles. The rust accent reads as glowing metal, fire and heat.

## Hero (Type C Diagonal)
The diagonal split creates visual tension — exactly right for a glass school, where the material exists in a state of perpetual tension between solid and liquid, structure and dissolution. The right panel's fire image bleeds under the hard-edged clip-path, softened by an overlay gradient.

## Preloader
A flickering flame animation identifies the school's identity before content loads — minimal, purposeful and thematically precise.

## Animation (A5 Organic)
Random values simulate the unpredictable, beautiful chaos of glass under heat — no two elements settle in exactly the same way.
