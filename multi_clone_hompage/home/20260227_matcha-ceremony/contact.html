Finding Us

Visit the
Tearoom

CHADO Studio is located in a converted traditional townhouse (machiya) in a quiet residential quarter. Guests are provided with precise directions and a gate code upon confirmation of their reservation.

  • LocationNear the Botanical Garden, accessible by subway (15 min from city centre)
  • ParkingOn-street parking available. We recommend public transit for the full approach.
  • AttireComfortable, modest clothing. Socks required; no strong fragrance.
  • ArrivalPlease arrive 10 minutes before your ceremony to allow for transition.

Booking Policies

Good to Know

Cancellation

Cancellations received more than 14 days in advance receive a full refund of any deposit paid. Cancellations within 14 days may be rescheduled within 6 months. Cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the deposit.

Payment

A 25% deposit is required to confirm your reservation. The remainder is due 7 days before the ceremony date. Gift certificates may be redeemed in lieu of payment at time of booking. We accept major cards and bank transfer.

Late Arrival

The tearoom maintains silence once the ceremony has begun. Guests arriving more than 10 minutes late may be asked to join at the next natural pause in the ceremony. We recommend arriving 10 minutes before your scheduled time.
