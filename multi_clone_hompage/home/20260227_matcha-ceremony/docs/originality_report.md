# Originality Report — CHADO Matcha Ceremony Studio

## Summary
This site is an entirely original design and brand concept. No existing website was cloned or directly referenced. The visual identity, copy, color palette, layout compositions, and brand narrative were created from scratch.

## Brand Originality
- **Name:** CHADO is an original studio brand name derived from the Japanese word 茶道 (the Way of Tea). No existing business with this exact brand identity was referenced.
- **Tagline:** "Harmony in Every Bowl" — original.
- **Founder:** Sensei Yuki Harada is a fictional persona created for this template. All credentials, lineage details, and biographical information are invented.
- **Studio:** No real physical studio was used as a reference. The studio details (location, rooms, pricing structure) are fictional.

## Content Originality
All written content is original, including:
- Six Chado philosophy card descriptions
- Seven-step ceremony process with timing and Japanese terminology
- Six utensil descriptions (Chawan, Chasen, Chashaku, Chakin, Hishaku, Natsume)
- Lineage timeline events (partially fictional, set within authentic Urasenke historical context)
- Four testimonials (fictional guests)
- FAQ answers
- Visit and policy copy

## Cultural Authenticity
While this is a fictional brand, the following content is historically and culturally accurate:
- Sen no Rikyu (1522–1591) biographical dates and role
- Urasenke school founding (c. 1622, Sen Sotan)
- Four principles of Chado: Wa, Kei, Sei, Jaku (correct Japanese characters used)
- Ichi-go Ichi-e philosophy attribution
- Usucha vs. Koicha preparation differences
- Chasen composition and Takayama origin
- Uji as the primary source of ceremonial matcha tencha
- Water temperature for usucha (80°C)
- Nijiriguchi door description and purpose
- Matsukaze — the sound of kettle water

## Design Originality
- **Color palette** Forest Night was designed specifically for this template; no existing palette was copied
- **Hero TYPE G** scroll-driven text transform is an original layout type developed for this template system
- **Layout compositions** across all five pages are original combinations; no specific website was referenced as a visual model
- **Typography pairing** DM Serif Display + Karla selected for this brand's specific tone

## Reference Research (General Knowledge Only)
The following were consulted as general cultural/historical references, not as design sources:

- General knowledge of wabi-cha aesthetic and chado philosophy
- General knowledge of traditional Japanese ceramics (Iga, Raku, Hagi, Shigaraki)

CHADO — Harmony in Every Bowl is an original luxury template created without reference to or reproduction of any specific existing brand, website, or protected intellectual property.
