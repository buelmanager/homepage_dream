Common Questions

Before You Breathe

Is halotherapy safe for everyone? +

Halotherapy is safe for most adults and children. We advise that those with active pulmonary infections, severe COPD, or cardiac conditions consult their physician before booking. During your pre-session consultation, our therapists will assess your suitability.

What should I wear to my session? +

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing is ideal. We provide clean cave robes if you prefer. Outdoor footwear is not permitted in the cave. Salt can lightly settle on clothing, which rinses out easily with water — there is no permanent staining.

How many sessions do I need to feel results? +

Many guests report noticeable improvement in breathing and sinus clarity after their very first session. For chronic conditions such as asthma, eczema, or COPD, a course of 8–12 sessions is typically recommended for sustained clinical benefit — consistent with published research protocols.

Can I bring my phone into the cave? +

We gently encourage guests to leave devices in our secure lockers. The salt environment is part of the healing — the disconnection from screens supports the neurological benefits of the negative ion atmosphere. Our caves maintain a sanctuary of calm.

Is there a minimum age for sessions? +

Children from age 3 may participate in our dedicated Children's Cave sessions. Infants and toddlers under 3 may be accommodated with advance arrangement — please contact us to discuss. Adults accompany children under 8 at no additional charge.
