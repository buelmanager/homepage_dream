Find Us

In the Heart of Harrogate

HALITE is located in the historic spa quarter of Harrogate — a town with a centuries-long tradition of mineral healing. We are 5 minutes from Harrogate train station and have ample parking immediately adjacent to the facility.

Parking

Free parking for up to 3 hours directly adjacent to HALITE. Additional parking available in the Crown Place multi-storey, 2 minutes walk.

Public Transport

Harrogate railway station: 5 minutes walk. Bus stops for routes 1, 6, and 36 within 100 metres of our entrance.

Accessibility

HALITE is fully wheelchair accessible. Please contact us in advance to discuss any specific accessibility requirements — we will ensure your visit is comfortable.
