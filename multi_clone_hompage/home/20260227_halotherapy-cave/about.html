The Discovery

Wieliczka to
the Himalayas

In 1843, Polish physician Felix Boczkowski made a remarkable observation: workers in the Wieliczka Salt Mine near Krakow had remarkably low rates of lung disease compared to workers in other industries. He published his findings, establishing the foundation of what we now call halotherapy.

In the 20th century, Soviet researcher A.V. Sokolov documented similar findings in Ukrainian salt mines, leading to the development of speleotherapy — the therapeutic use of salt caves. By the 1980s, the first purpose-built salt rooms were appearing across Eastern Europe, replicating the microclimate of natural salt caves.

HALITE draws on Himalayan pink salt — formed 250 million years ago when ancient seas evaporated and were sealed beneath tectonic pressure, preserving 84 trace minerals in crystalline form untouched by modern pollutants.

Historical Record

A Timeline of Therapeutic Discovery

1843

First Clinical Documentation

Dr. Felix Boczkowski publishes "On the mineral waters and salt baths of Wieliczka," noting dramatically reduced respiratory disease among salt mine workers.

1945

Wartime Salt Cave Discovery

During World War II, civilians sheltering in German salt mines observed improvement in their respiratory conditions following extended time underground.

1968

Soviet Medical Research

The Soviet Ministry of Health begins systematic speleotherapy research. Sanatoria are established in salt mines across Ukraine and the Caucasus.

1985

The Halogenerator

Russian researcher Dr. Alesandr Trofimenko develops the first pharmaceutical-grade halogenerator, enabling precise control of salt aerosol concentration in purpose-built rooms.

2010

Western Clinical Validation

The New England Journal of Medicine and the European Respiratory Journal publish controlled studies validating halotherapy's efficacy for asthma, COPD, and chronic bronchitis.

2026

HALITE

HALITE opens its Himalayan salt cave facility in Harrogate, bringing 180 years of therapeutic science into a luxury, clinically-validated healing environment.
