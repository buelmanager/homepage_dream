Behind the Image

The Process

How It Works

From Concept to
Iconic Image

Every great image is the result of meticulous preparation, creative courage, and flawless execution. Here is how we build something unforgettable together.

01
Concept & Vision
The first step is the most important: understanding your vision. We meet — in person or virtually — to explore your brand identity, the story you want to tell, the emotion you want to evoke, and the world you want to create. I come to every brief with a mood board, reference images, and initial ideas ready to share.
This Stage Includes
  • Brand and creative brief review
  • Concept mood board development
  • Visual reference library
  • Colour palette and tone alignment
  • Initial budget and timeline scoping
02
Casting & Styling
The right team transforms a shoot from good to extraordinary. I work with an exclusive network of the world's best talent — models, fashion stylists, hair and makeup artists, and set designers — all selected specifically for each project's unique requirements and aesthetic.
Team Assembly
  • Model casting and agency negotiation
  • Fashion stylist and wardrobe sourcing
  • Hair and makeup artist selection
  • Set designer and prop coordination
  • Production manager appointment
03
Location Scouting
Location is character. I work with specialist location agencies in London, Paris, Milan, New York, and beyond to find spaces that elevate the creative concept — whether that is a brutalist warehouse in East London, a Haussmann apartment in Paris, or a rooftop overlooking Manhattan.
Location Services
  • Global location library access
  • In-person recce and scout
  • Permit and licensing coordination
  • Studio hire alternatives
  • Weather and logistics planning
04
The Shoot
On set, I create an environment where creativity flows freely and every moment is captured with intention. My directing style is bold but collaborative — I know what I want, and I know how to bring the best out of every person in front of my lens. Shoots typically run 8–12 hours with a full production crew.
On Set
  • Precision lighting design and setup
  • Art direction throughout the day
  • Polaroid and digital proofing
  • Client live review via tethered monitor
  • BTS content capture for social media
05
Post-Production
The final images are where the magic is completed. My post-production team works with Capture One and Photoshop to produce world-class retouching and colour grading. We deliver a first edit within 5 business days, with final retouched selects within 14 days. Every image is delivered print-ready.
Delivery
  • Capture One colour grading
  • High-end Photoshop retouching
  • Print-ready TIF and web JPEG delivery
  • Private client gallery (90-day access)
  • Clear licensing and usage rights documentation

Collaboration Philosophy

Every Project is
a Partnership

"The best images are made when trust exists between photographer and subject. My job is to create that trust instantly."

I believe great photography begins with listening. Before I pick up a camera, I spend time truly understanding my collaborators — what drives them, what scares them, what they want the world to see when they look at the image.

My studio operates as a safe, creative space. I work with a small, permanent core team who are extensions of my creative vision — not hired day players. This continuity produces better work and a better experience for everyone involved.

Transparency
Clear communication at every stage — no surprises on brief, budget, or delivery.
Integrity
I only take projects I believe in and can do justice to.
Precision
Every frame, every light placement, every post-production decision is intentional.
Boldness
I push creative boundaries — that is where the iconic images live.
