About Elara Lens

Elara Lens
London / Paris / NYC
Biography

12 Years of Bold,
Fearless Imagery

Elara Lens is one of the most sought-after fashion and editorial photographers working today. Based between London, Paris, and New York, she has spent over a decade crafting images that are bold, unapologetically feminine, and impossible to ignore.

Her work has appeared on 48 Vogue covers across 12 international editions, and her campaigns for houses including Dior, Chanel, Gucci, and Valentino have set the visual tone for an era of fashion. She has photographed the world's most iconic models, actresses, and cultural figures.

"I don't take photographs of women. I take photographs of power."

Born in Lagos and raised in London, Elara studied at the Royal College of Art before assisting legendary photographers Mario Testino and Nick Knight. She launched her independent studio in 2012 and has since built an unmatched body of work spanning editorial, advertising, and fine art.

Career

Milestones

2012
Studio Launch
Opened independent studio in Shoreditch, London. First major commission: Burberry Spring/Summer campaign.
2014
First Vogue Cover
Shot the September issue cover for British Vogue — the beginning of a long-standing collaboration with Condé Nast.
2016
Dior Global Campaign
Appointed as lead campaign photographer for Dior's global advertising — a three-year exclusive partnership.
2018
Photography of the Year — Harper's Bazaar
Awarded Photographer of the Year at the Harper's Bazaar Women of the Year ceremony in London.
2020
New York Studio Opens
Expanded with a permanent studio in Tribeca, NY. Launched the Elara Foundation supporting emerging Black women photographers.
2022
MoMA Retrospective
"Fearless: A Decade of Fashion Photography" — a 10-year retrospective at the Museum of Modern Art, New York.
2024
48th Vogue Cover
Shot the 48th international Vogue cover. Named to TIME Magazine's 100 Most Influential People in the World.
