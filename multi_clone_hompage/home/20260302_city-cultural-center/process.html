Purpose & Practice

Our Mission

We exist to serve the city — through extraordinary programming, inclusive education, and a deep commitment to the transformative power of art.

How We Work

Three Pillars of Our Practice

01

Commission & Present

We fund, develop, and present original work by the world's most exciting artists — from emerging local voices to internationally acclaimed companies. Our curatorial approach prioritises artistic risk and social relevance.

02

Educate & Engage

Our education programmes reach 30,000 schoolchildren annually, while our community engagement work brings art-making into neighbourhoods, hospitals, and care settings across the city.

03

Archive & Preserve

We document and preserve the city's cultural life — our archive holds over 45 years of performance records, photographs, oral histories, and artists' materials, all now available digitally.

Education

Shaping the Next Generation

We believe that creative education is fundamental to a well-rounded life. Our education programmes are designed in close collaboration with teachers and youth workers to complement school curricula and provide experiences that go far beyond what the classroom can offer.

From primary school visits to our Young Creators Lab, from theatre-in-education tours to our graduate residency programme, we invest heavily in the artists and audiences of tomorrow.

Community

Art Without Walls

◈

Neighbourhood Commissions

We fund artists to create new work in and for specific neighbourhoods — murals, performances, installations, and participatory projects that celebrate local identity.

◉

Access Programmes

Subsidised tickets, British Sign Language-interpreted performances, audio-described exhibitions, and relaxed performances make Lumina genuinely accessible to all.

◌

Artist Development

Our studio residency programme provides 20 local artists per year with free workspace, mentoring, and development support to help them grow their practice.

◎

Social Prescribing

In partnership with local NHS trusts, we offer arts-based wellbeing programmes for people experiencing mental health challenges, isolation, and chronic illness.
