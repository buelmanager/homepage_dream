The City Speaks — A Landmark New Commission

This sweeping, immersive theatrical work draws on 200 oral history interviews with city residents. Directed by three-time Olivier Award winner Thomas Reed, it features an ensemble of 40 performers and a live orchestra in a once-in-a-generation production exclusive to Lumina.

Running March 15 through May 5, tickets from £12 with subsidised pricing available.
