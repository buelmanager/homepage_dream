Our Story

The Vulcan
Laboratory

Founded 2008

Born from
the Earth

Vulcan Geothermal was established in Reykjavik in 2008 by three volcanologists who had spent their careers mapping Iceland's extraordinary geothermal fields and saw an opportunity to bring this scientific mastery to the global energy transition.

Today, Vulcan operates across three continents, completing projects from luxury estate direct-use systems to utility-scale power plants — always with the same scientific rigour and genuine respect for the geological environments we work within.

Core Values

The Principles of
Vulcan Science

I

GEOLOGICAL TRUTH

We never overstate geothermal resource potential. Our estimates are conservative, bankable, and scientifically defensible.

II

LANDSCAPE STEWARDSHIP

Geothermal plants are designed to minimise surface impact. Where possible, we make them invisible from surrounding terrain.

III

BASELOAD COMMITMENT

Unlike intermittent renewables, geothermal delivers 24/7 power. We engineer for 92%+ capacity factors across all our plants.
