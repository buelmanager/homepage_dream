Our Story

About Bloom & Form

We are a school born from a love of living material and a belief that floral art deserves the same serious pedagogy as any other creative discipline.

Our Origin

Rooted in Intention

Bloom & Form was founded in 2008 by master florist Adrienne Cole in a small Marylebone studio. Adrienne had trained under ikebana masters in Kyoto and European floristry schools in Amsterdam, and found herself frustrated by the fragmentation of knowledge in the field.

Her vision was a school that treated flowers as both material and message — one that drew from Eastern and Western traditions with equal reverence, and trained students not just to arrange, but to see.

Sixteen years later, Bloom & Form has become one of the most respected floral education institutions in Europe, with alumni working at the world's leading hotels, design houses, and botanical art galleries.

What We Stand For

Our Values

01

Slow Practice

We resist the culture of speed. Mastery in floral arts requires patient observation, repetition, and a willingness to stay with difficulty until understanding arrives organically.

02

Cultural Respect

We teach across traditions with deep respect. Ikebana and European floristry carry different philosophies; we honour both rather than conflating or ranking them.

03

Seasonal Thinking

Our curriculum is structured around the natural calendar. Students work with what is available, building an intuition for seasonal variation that lasts a lifetime.

04

Material Honesty

We do not hide the impermanence of our medium. A petal that falls, a stem that bends — these are not failures but revelations of what it means to work with living things.
