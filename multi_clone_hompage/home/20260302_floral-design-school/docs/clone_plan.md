# Clone Plan — Bloom & Form Floral School

## Source Inspiration
Original design concept inspired by luxury botanical and art school aesthetics. No direct website clone — original design built from brief.

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Bloom & Form Floral School
- **Tagline:** Nature, Arranged with Intention
- **Established:** 2008, London (fictional)
- **Position:** Premier floral arts education institution

## Target Audience
- Aspiring florists (beginners to intermediate)
- Working florists seeking advanced training
- Creative professionals exploring botanical arts
- Event designers expanding into floral work

## Key Differentiators
1. Eastern-Western pedagogy (Ikebana + European)
2. Small cohorts (max 14 students)
3. Five-stage progressive curriculum
4. Public installation as final assessment
5. Marylebone atelier with private garden

## Page Architecture
- `index.html` — Showcase landing page
- `about.html` — Institutional story and faculty
- `collection.html` — Full program listings
- `process.html` — Teaching methodology
- `contact.html` — Application portal

## Design Decisions
- Hero Type E (Grid Canvas) chosen for botanical precision aesthetic
- Forest Night palette reflects organic, nature-rooted brand
- DM Serif Display conveys editorial luxury
- Karla body for clean readability
- Canvas grid echoes the precision of botanical illustration
