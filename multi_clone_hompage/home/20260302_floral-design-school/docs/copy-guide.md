# Copy Guide — Bloom & Form Floral School

## Voice & Tone
- Meditative, unhurried, deeply knowledgeable
- Bridges East and West with equal fluency and respect
- Talks about plants as living subjects, not decorative objects
- Botanical precision without jargon — accessible to beginners, satisfying to experts
- Never uses words: "stunning", "gorgeous", "beautiful", "amazing", "transform"

## Key Messages
1. Ikebana and European floristry are conversation partners, not competitors
2. Twelve students per cohort — serious, unhurried, personal
3. Seasonal intelligence: the harvest calendar is the curriculum
4. Material honesty — no artificial, no dyed, no foam
5. Floral design is a discipline deserving the same rigour as any fine art

## Brand Voice Examples
- "Before you can arrange a flower, you must know it — not its name, but its weight in your hand."
- "We design only with what grows. Seasonality is not a limitation — it is the grammar of this art."
- "Our curriculum follows the harvest calendar. So does nature."
- "Slow looking precedes every cut. Perception before composition."

## Programme Copy Register
- Foundation: welcoming, patient, encouraging — "begin your journey with intention"
- Diploma: serious, ambitious — "a year of sustained practice"
- Masterclass: peer-to-peer, collegial — "for working florists ready to expand"

## Avoid
- Words: stunning, gorgeous, beautiful, amazing, magical, transform, journey
- Claims: "world-class", "award-winning" (unless specifically true)
- Urgency language: "Don't miss out", "Last chance", "Act now"
- Generic floristry clichés: "blooming", "blossoming potential", "flower power"
