# Design Decisions — Bloom & Form Floral School

## Color Strategy
Forest Night palette (#0F1A10 background) creates a deep, nocturnal botanical studio atmosphere — the feeling of a glass conservatory at dusk. The sage-green accent (#4DAF6A) is botanical-true: the precise hue of new growth, neither yellow-warm nor blue-cool. It reads as living, organic and intelligent against the dark ground.

## Typography
DM Serif Display is an editorial serif with strong Italian influence — its italic form has genuine calligraphic energy appropriate for a school where beauty in hand and form is the subject. Karla body provides clean, contemporary legibility without disrupting the luxury register.

## Hero Choice (Type E Grid/Pattern Canvas)
The HTML5 canvas grid with diamond markers at intersections references botanical illustration grid-paper — the precise, measured backdrop of scientific plant drawing. This hero communicates precision and discipline before a single word is read. The faint #4DAF6A grid lines dissolve into the dark hero image, creating depth without competition.

## Layout Logic
- About strip places the founding year (2009) as a large, ghosted numeral — establishing heritage without a bulleted list
- Philosophy grid (display:grid 3-col, never flex) presents three principles at equal visual weight
- Programme rows alternate image-left/image-right for visual rhythm across the collection page
- Stats band uses DM Serif Display numerals at display scale — numbers as typographic objects

## Animation
A5 Organic (y: random 20–28px, duration: random 1.0–1.4s, ease: power2.out) mimics the irregular timing of flowers opening — never mechanical, never identical. The stagger (0.09) creates gentle sequential reveal that feels cultivated, not engineered.

## Preloader
Six-petal spinning bloom in accent color — thematically precise, technically minimal. The rotation is continuous and calm, suggesting patient growth rather than loading anxiety.
