Our Heritage

A Tradition of
Quiet Excellence

Founded in 1887 by a coterie of statesmen, artists, and industrialists who believed that true civilization demanded a space beyond the ordinary — SANCTUM was conceived as a refuge for minds of consequence.

Over thirteen decades, we have hosted prime ministers and poets, Nobel laureates and philanthropists, each bound by a single covenant: the pursuit of excellence and the cultivation of meaningful connection.

"SANCTUM is where the world's most interesting people become the world's most interesting friends."

— Lord Alistair Pemberton, Founding Patron, 1887

Today, across twelve cities on five continents, SANCTUM remains what it has always been: the world's most private, most purposeful, and most distinguished gathering place.

Begin Your
Application

Membership of SANCTUM is extended by invitation and by application. All applications are reviewed by our Membership Committee, which meets quarterly.

We respectfully ask that applicants provide a brief account of their background and their expectations of membership. References from existing members are welcomed but not required.

All applications are treated in the strictest confidence. SANCTUM does not disclose membership details or application statuses to any third party.

Your information is handled with absolute discretion and is never shared with any third party. View our Privacy Charter.
