Our Miso

The Complete
KOJI Range

Every miso in our atelier is pressed by hand into aged cedar barrels and left to ferment at its own pace. We do not accelerate, adjust, or interrupt. When the miso is ready, we open the barrel. Not before.

Our twelve varieties span a continuum from the delicate three-month white shiro to the near-mythic three-year Genmitsu Kuroku — a paste of absolute umami depth that we produce in quantities of fewer than 80 kilograms per year. Each variety is available in 200g artisan jars and 1kg atelier tins.

We also produce koji culture starters — our living 250-year-old Aspergillus oryzae lineage — available to certified practitioners who complete our Koji Mastery program.

Shiro Shun
白旬 — White Season

The youngest miso in our range, pressed in late spring and opened in late summer. Shiro Shun is cloud-white in color with a paste consistency almost as light as cream cheese. The flavor is intensely floral — sake lees, fresh rice, and a faint sweetness from the high koji ratio. Salt is minimal. Umami is gentle, present like a whisper rather than a declaration.

Aki Mugi
秋麦 — Autumn Barley

One complete mountain year. Spring pressing, summer transformation, autumn opening. Aki Mugi is our most requested variety by professional chefs — a deep amber paste that balances savory depth with a surprising caramel sweetness, driven by the Maillard reactions of the summer heat acting on barley sugars. Long, mineral finish from the Nagano granite water.

Dark · Charcoal
Sumi Koji
炭糀 — Charcoal Koji

Two full mountain years using a proprietary activated-charcoal koji culture developed by Haruka Yamamoto. The charcoal modifies the enzymatic environment, suppressing certain lactic acid pathways and amplifying protease activity. The result is an extraordinarily dense umami with pronounced smokiness, dark chocolate bitterness, and a finish that lingers for over a minute on the palate.

Genmitsu Kuroku
原蜜黒久 — Origin Amber Black · Three Year

The pinnacle of six generations of craft. Three full years in ancient cedar barrels — the same barrels Koji Yamamoto I carved by hand in 1769. The miso has long since turned a color beyond brown: a near-obsidian black with flecks of amber gold from amino acid crystallization. A single gram contains more glutamate than an entire bowl of standard dashi. This is not miso as seasoning. It is miso as a destination.

Culture Starters

The Living
250-Year Lineage

Our Aspergillus oryzae culture has been maintained without interruption since the founding of the atelier. Six generations have passed this invisible thread forward — a living organism whose characteristics are shaped by two and a half centuries of continuous cultivation in Nagano's specific microclimate.

We make our culture starter available in limited quantities to professional practitioners and serious home fermenters who have completed the Koji Mastery certification program. Each starter comes with full lineage documentation and cultivation guidance from Haruka Yamamoto.

The starter is not sold commercially. It is entrusted — and we follow up with all recipients to ensure the lineage continues.

Seasonal Preserves

Beyond Miso — The
Garden in Jars

From our surrounding mountain garden, we produce small-batch seasonal preserves using koji fermentation techniques — available only while the harvest allows.

Spring
Shio-Koji Bamboo

Tender spring bamboo shoots fermented in shio-koji brine for 30 days. Clean, mineral, with deep vegetal sweetness.

Summer
Amazake Plum

Ume plums preserved in our house amazake. The natural sweetness of rice koji wraps the sour plum in a round, golden flavor.

Autumn
Koji Persimmon

Mountain persimmons fermented in barley koji paste for 60 days. A revelatory pairing of fruit tannin and umami depth.

Winter
Kuro Daikon

Black radish aged in our 18-month mugi miso for the full winter season. Dense, complex, extraordinary with sake.
