Find Us

Coming to
Shiojiri

The atelier sits in the mountains above Shiojiri city, a 3-hour shinkansen ride from Tokyo. We are not signposted — please contact us for precise directions ahead of your visit. We are not open for unannounced drop-ins.

  • 01
    Take the JR Chuo Line to Shiojiri Station from Shinjuku (Approx. 2h 45min via Azusa Limited Express).
  • 02
    Taxi from Shiojiri Station to the atelier — approximately 25 minutes. Share our address when booking: 2-4-8 Yamamoto-cho, Shiojiri.
  • 03
    By car from Nagano City: National Route 19 south to Shiojiri, then follow our specific approach directions (provided on booking confirmation).
  • 04
    All course and workshop guests receive a detailed arrival guide 7 days before their program begins, including seasonal travel tips and what to wear in the atelier.
For Professionals

Wholesale &
Restaurant Supply

We supply selected restaurants, fine food retailers, and culinary professionals. Our wholesale program is limited to ensure consistent quality and availability for existing accounts.

Restaurant Accounts

We maintain ongoing supply relationships with selected fine dining restaurants in Japan, South Korea, and Europe. Minimum monthly order of 5kg across varieties. Dedicated account manager — Haruka herself for the first year.

Fine Food Retail

KOJI miso is stocked in a very small number of specialty food retailers. We review new retail applications quarterly. Minimum opening order and specific packaging and display requirements apply.

Custom Aged Batches

For restaurants or chefs who wish to serve KOJI miso as a featured ingredient under their own narrative, we offer custom aged batches — pressed to your specification, aged in a dedicated barrel, with documentation of your batch's entire fermentation timeline.
