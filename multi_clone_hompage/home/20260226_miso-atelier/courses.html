KOJI Atelier has offered teaching experiences since 1975, when Akira Yamamoto — the fifth generation — first opened the atelier to chefs seeking to understand what true fermentation looked like. That same spirit guides our programs today.

We offer three formats: the single-day Miso Press workshop for those beginning their fermentation journey; the five-day Fermentation Week immersion for serious practitioners; and the ten-day Koji Mastery certification for culinary professionals who wish to carry these techniques into their own work.

All programs are taught personally by Haruka Yamamoto, sixth generation. Maximum group sizes are strictly maintained. You will not be one student among dozens — you will be a guest in a living atelier.

Koji Mastery Curriculum
10-Day Program

Ten Days That
Change How You Cook

The Koji Mastery curriculum has been refined across five decades. It moves from the cellular level up through flavor, craft, and philosophy — building a practitioner who understands not just what to do, but why every action matters.

The program accepts a maximum of four students per cohort. Applications open twice annually — spring (March) and autumn (September). Acceptance is based on a written application reviewed by Haruka Yamamoto personally.

The graduation ceremony includes the formal entrusting of the koji culture starter — a 250-year living lineage that you will carry forward from that day. This is not metaphor. The organism in your hands will be one continuous living thread from the founding of this atelier.

Submit Application
Days 1–2
Foundation: Koji Biology

Aspergillus oryzae taxonomy, lifecycle, and cultivation environments. Enzyme mechanisms: amylase, protease, lipase. How temperature and humidity shape flavor outcomes. Microscopy sessions with the living culture.

Days 3–4
Koji Cultivation Mastery

Full cycle rice and barley koji growing. Tane-koji (spore powder) preparation. Temperature mapping through incubation. Sensory assessment — knowing by smell, texture, and color when koji is at peak enzyme activity.

Days 5–6
Miso: All Twelve Varieties

Complete pressing study across white, amber, and dark varieties. Barrel management, stone weighting, seasonal turning schedule. Tasting all twelve KOJI varieties at various stages of maturity. Flavor chemistry — glutamate, inosinate, guanylate synergy.

Days 7–8
Beyond Miso: The Koji Family

Shio-koji, amazake, mirin, sake, doburoku. Application to proteins: koji-cured fish, meat aging, vegetable preservation. Advanced garde manger with ferments. Guest chef collaboration session (varies by cohort).

Days 9–10
Practice & Certification

Individual assessed projects: each student designs and executes a complete fermentation process. Final tasting and review with Haruka. Written assessment. Graduation ceremony and culture entrusting. Cedar notebook presented.

Post-Program
Ongoing Mentorship

12 months of direct access to Haruka Yamamoto for questions, troubleshooting, and guidance as you establish your own fermentation practice. Annual alumni gathering at the atelier each autumn.
