Feu Sacré Culinary Academy

The Art of Cuisine

Founded in Lyon in 1972, Feu Sacré Culinary Academy has been producing world-class chefs for over five decades. Our alumni hold 38 Michelin stars across restaurants in Paris, Tokyo, London, New York, and beyond.

All instruction is conducted in professional kitchen environments by Michelin-trained chef-instructors. Class sizes are limited to 12 students per chef to ensure individual attention and genuine professional development.
