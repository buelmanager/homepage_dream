The Founder

Marek Holden

Lead Practitioner & Studio Director

My journey into sound healing began not in a studio, but in a moment of personal crisis. After a decade as a recording engineer in London's music industry, chronic burnout brought me to a Tibetan sound ceremony in Bali in 2013. What I experienced in that hour changed the direction of my life irrevocably.

Upon returning to New York, I trained in crystal singing bowl therapy, acoustic medicine, and quantum resonance protocols — ultimately founding RESONANCE in 2019.

Our studio is not a wellness commodity. It is a dedicated healing space, built from the ground up with acoustic precision and devotional care. Every session I offer carries the full weight of that lineage — and the full presence of my training.

The Science

Why Crystal Bowls Heal

The therapeutic effects of crystal singing bowls are supported by decades of research in acoustic biology, neuroscience, and psychoacoustics. Here is what science tells us about vibrational medicine.

432

Hz — The Natural Frequency

Our bowls are tuned to 432 Hz rather than the standard 440 Hz. Research suggests 432 Hz aligns with the mathematical geometry of nature, creating a more resonant and harmonically coherent listening experience that promotes relaxation rather than stimulation.

δ

Delta Wave Induction

Sustained crystal bowl frequencies in the 0.5–4 Hz range entrain brainwaves into delta states — the deepest healing state associated with immune system restoration, tissue repair, and the release of human growth hormone. Sleep Journey sessions specifically target this band.

70%

Water Resonance

The human body is approximately 70% water. Sound waves travel through water at a velocity that creates standing wave patterns within tissues and cells. Crystal bowl frequencies are particularly efficient at creating constructive resonance within biological water structures.

HRV

Heart Rate Variability

Clinical studies measuring Heart Rate Variability (HRV) before and after sound bath sessions have found statistically significant increases in parasympathetic tone — indicating genuine nervous system regulation, not merely subjective relaxation.

7

Chakra Frequencies

Each of the seven primary energy centres corresponds to a specific musical note and solfeggio frequency. Our Chakra Alignment sessions use bowls tuned to C, D, E, F, G, A, and B — methodically addressing each energy centre in ascending sequence.

SiO₂

Quartz Crystal Purity

Our bowls are made from 99.9% pure quartz crystal. Silicon dioxide oscillates at a precise, stable frequency when struck — unlike metal bowls whose harmonics drift. This purity means every bowl sustains a clean, consistent, medically reproducible tone.
