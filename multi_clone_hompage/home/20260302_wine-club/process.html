The Programme

The Five
Formats

Our tasting programme is built around five distinct formats — each designed to develop a different dimension of vinous understanding.

Together, they form a complete education in fine wine: analytical, historical, geographical, and above all, pleasurable.

I
Blind Tasting
Weekly

Our weekly blind tasting circles are the engine of vinous education at Clos Velours. Covered bottles, tasting grids, and honest discussion — the palate learns to identify variety, vintage, and provenance without the crutch of the label.

Circles are limited to eight members to ensure genuine participation. A written sheet is distributed after each session with full tasting notes and context.

II
Vertical Tasting
Monthly

Monthly vertical tastings take a single estate through multiple vintages — sometimes spanning 30 years or more. There is no better way to understand how time transforms wine, or what the signature of a great estate really means.

Verticals are selected by the cellar committee and draw on the club's Bordeaux cave stock wherever possible.

III
Horizontal Tasting
Quarterly

Quarterly horizontal tastings compare multiple estates from a single vintage — revealing the character of the appellation, the influence of soil and winemaking, and the relative performance of different producers in a given year.

IV
Bordeaux Visit
Annual

Every year, a delegation of members visits Bordeaux during the harvest or primeur season. Three days of vineyard walks, barrel tastings, winemaker dinners, and purchases for the cellar. The closest any club member gets to making the wine themselves.

V
Grand Dégustation
Annual Gala

The highlight of the Clos Velours calendar. Forty extraordinary labels in a single evening — full glassware, professional service, and guest sommeliers from three-star restaurants. Black tie. Unforgettable.

The Grand Dégustation is the event that defines the year's character, and the benchmark against which all other tastings are measured.
