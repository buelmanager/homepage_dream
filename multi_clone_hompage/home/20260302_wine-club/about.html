Our Provenance

Forty-Six
Years of Taste

Clos Velours was founded in 1978 by eight London wine merchants and collectors who believed that fine wine deserved a more serious, more curious, more generous community.

From a first-floor room in St James's to a Bordeaux cave with 1,200 labels, our journey has been guided by a single question: what makes a great wine great?

The Beginning

A Room in St James's, 1978

It was the autumn of 1978. Eight friends gathered in a first-floor dining room above a wine merchant's shop in St James's. They opened three bottles of Bordeaux — a 1959, a 1961, and a 1966 — and argued happily about them for four hours. Clos Velours was born that evening.

The founding insight was straightforward: that wine is best understood in company, through comparison, over time. That you cannot appreciate a great vintage by drinking it once, but only by returning to it, measuring it against others, and watching it change.

Wine is a conversation between the vineyard and the glass. Clos Velours exists to make sure that conversation is never one-sided.

Today the club has 160 members, a cellar in Bordeaux, a full tasting programme, and a tradition of hospitality that has not changed since that first evening in St James's.
