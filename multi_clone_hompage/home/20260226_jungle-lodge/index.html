The Experiences

01

Canopy Walk at Dawn

Suspended bridges connect ancient emergent trees at 30 meters. Watch scarlet macaws emerge as the forest wakes. Guided by resident naturalists.

02

River Expedition

Navigate blackwater tributaries by silent electric canoe. Spot river dolphins, giant otters, and pink boto on your private waterway.

03

Nocturnal Forest Immersion

The jungle transforms at night. Guided barefoot walks through bioluminescent fungi paths with infrared observation of nocturnal species.

04

Forest Bathing & Ceremony

Shinrin-yoku sessions with indigenous knowledge holders. Medicinal plant immersion, breathing rituals, sensory silence.

05

Zero-Impact Gastronomy

Chef-led dinners on the forest platform using ingredients foraged within 2km. No outside food, no waste, no compromise on luxury.

Our Zero-Impact Promise

  • ◆ 100% solar energy — zero grid connection, zero emissions from operations
  • ◆ All construction used elevated post-and-beam — no roots disturbed, no land cleared
  • ◆ 1% of every booking funds Amazon conservation trust permanently
  • ◆ Certified with IUCN Green List and Rainforest Alliance Gold Standard
  • ◆ Indigenous community employment — 80% of staff from local Ribeirinhos families
  • ◆ Zero single-use plastics. All materials biodegradable or reclaimed timber.
