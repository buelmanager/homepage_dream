Getting Here

The Journey Is Part of the Experience

Reaching SELVA requires intention. We take this as a given — those who arrive have already begun the process of leaving their ordinary world behind.

All transfers are arranged by SELVA in coordination with each guest's travel schedule. No logistics are left to chance. Our team meets you at every stage of the journey.

◆ SELVA arranges all internal Brazil transfers as part of your stay package. Cost is included from Belém onward.
◆ Luggage is limited to soft bags only — 15kg per person — due to the river and forest transport nature of the final leg.
◆ Yellow fever vaccination is required for entry to the Amazon region. SELVA's medical advisory team can assist with preparation recommendations.

From International

International Flight → Belém (BEL)

Direct connections from Miami (8h), Lisbon (9h), London via São Paulo (14h). SELVA can advise on optimal routing and trusted aviation partners.

Belém → Lodge

Private Floatplane Transfer

45-minute floatplane flight over primary rainforest canopy. The most extraordinary pre-arrival experience imaginable. Operated by SELVA exclusively.

Floatplane → Villa

Silent Electric River Canoe

20-minute canoe transfer from the river landing to your villa. Your naturalist guide meets you here and your journey properly begins.

Common Questions

What Guests Ask Us

May through November is the dry season — lower water levels, more accessible trails, and optimal wildlife visibility. December through April is the wet (or "flooded forest") season, which transforms the Amazon into something utterly otherworldly. Both seasons offer extraordinary experiences. We are happy to advise based on your specific interests.

SELVA welcomes families with children aged 10 and above. The forest environment requires physical confidence and genuine curiosity. We design age-appropriate programmes for younger guests that are rich and genuine — not simplified. Children who have visited SELVA often describe it as the most important experience of their lives.

No specific fitness level is required for most SELVA experiences. Canopy walks involve staircases to 30m and moderate walking on suspended bridges. Forest walks are gentle to moderate. River expeditions are fully accessible. We ask that guests disclose any physical considerations during the booking consultation so we can design appropriately.

Yellow fever vaccination is required at least 10 days before arrival, and is a legal requirement for the region. Hepatitis A and B, typhoid, and rabies vaccinations are recommended by our medical advisory team. Full pre-travel health guidance is provided upon confirmation. Malaria prophylaxis is recommended and SELVA provides guidance on the most appropriate protocol.

There is no WiFi in the forest or villas — intentionally. Satellite connectivity is available in the main pavilion for essential communications. We find that guests who embrace the digital silence describe it as the single most transformative aspect of their stay. Emergency communication channels are always available.

Partnerships

Conservation & Group Partnerships

SELVA collaborates with conservation organizations, scientific institutions, media partners, and carefully selected luxury travel agencies who share our values. We believe that the most powerful advocates for the Amazon are those who have experienced it.
