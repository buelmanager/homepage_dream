Our Story & Mission

Born from the Belief That Luxury and Nature are One

The Founding Story

A Decade in the Making

"We did not come to the Amazon to conquer it. We came to listen. And what we heard changed everything about what we believed luxury could be."

In 2012, conservation biologist Dr. Ana Ferreiro and architect Tomás Vidal spent eighteen months living alongside the Ribeirinhos communities deep in the Pará basin. What began as a research expedition became a calling: to create a space where the world's most discerning travelers could encounter the raw magnificence of the living forest — without costing the forest anything at all.

Five years of design, engineering, and consultation with indigenous knowledge holders followed. SELVA opened its first two villas in 2018, expanded to eight by 2021, and has since welcomed guests from sixty-two countries — each one changed by the encounter.

Every decision at SELVA returns to a single question: Does this serve the forest? The guest experience is designed within that constraint, and in doing so, transcends it entirely.

What We Believe

The SELVA Philosophy

◈

Radical Invisibility

Architecture that disappears into the forest. Elevated structures leave the ground untouched. Materials sourced from reclaimed or certified sustainable timber only. No concrete poured. No land cleared. Every villa is woven into the canopy, not imposed upon it.

◉

Deep Presence

Luxury as we define it is not distraction — it is immersion. At SELVA, there is no WiFi in the forest, no curated noise. There is only the extraordinary theater of the living world, available to anyone willing to be fully present within it.

◐

Reciprocal Stewardship

The forest gives everything. We give back proportionally. One percent of every booking funds permanent conservation trust. Local employment at living wages. Knowledge exchange between guests and indigenous naturalists flows in both directions — always.

Conservation Mission

Protecting the Infinite Green

SELVA operates in partnership with the Amazônia Conservation Fund, the Ribeirinhos Foundation, and the IUCN Green List programme. Our lodge sits within a 40,000-hectare private reserve — one of the largest private conservation zones in the Amazon basin.

Our team of resident biologists conducts continuous ecological monitoring, supporting both scientific research and indigenous land stewardship. Guests are invited to participate in observation sessions, data collection, and morning biodiversity walks.
