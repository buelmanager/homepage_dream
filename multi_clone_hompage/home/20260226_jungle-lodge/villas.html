Eight Private Sanctuaries

Suspended Between the Forest and the Sky

The Architecture of Reverence

Where Structure Meets Living Wood

Every SELVA villa is a feat of invisible architecture. Elevated on hand-fitted post-and-beam frames, no foundations penetrate the root systems. The forest floor is left entirely untouched.

Interiors are composed of reclaimed hardwood, hand-loomed natural textiles, and open-air bathing spaces that vanish the boundary between inside and out. No glass separates you from the canopy — only intention does.
