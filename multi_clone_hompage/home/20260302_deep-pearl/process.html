The Craft

Seven Years
in Making

01

Nucleus Seeding

A master nucleator implants a spherical bead nucleus into the gonad of a mature oyster. This operation takes less than 30 seconds — yet requires years of training. The oyster then begins secreting nacre layer by layer around the nucleus, a process as slow and precise as the accumulation of wisdom.

02

Ocean Cultivation

Seeded oysters are suspended in mesh panels at precise depths — between 15 and 25 metres — in the warm, nutrient-rich waters of the Timor Sea. Our divers check each panel monthly, adjusting depth to follow seasonal thermoclines. Full maturation takes 5 to 7 years.

03

Deep Harvest

Mira Okafor leads each harvest dive personally. At 60 metres, time slows. She selects only oysters that have matured fully — leaving the others to continue their growth. Each pearl is opened by hand, on the boat, as it always has been. No conveyor belt. No machine opening.

04

Grading & Selection

Pearl grader Yuki Tanaka assesses each pearl across 47 criteria — lustre, nacre thickness (minimum 2mm for ABYSSÉ standard), surface perfection, shape, and orient. Only the top 12% of the harvest advances. The remainder are returned to the sea.

05

Atelier Setting

Master Jeweller Thomas Adeyemi studies each pearl before designing its setting. The gold is 18-karat, recycled and certified. Diamonds, when present, are conflict-free and GIA certified. Every piece takes between 40 and 120 hours to complete. No two are alike.
