The Sanctuary

A Forest That Has
Never Been Logged

Our forest is not managed woodland. It has never been logged, never been cleared, never had its understory removed. What you enter is a continuous succession of life that has been accumulating for over four hundred years.

The oldest trees — ancient hemlock and western red cedar — create a canopy so dense that the forest floor exists in its own microclimate. Cooler by an average of 6°C in summer. Humidity 40% higher than open land. Phytoncide concentrations up to twelve times those of urban parks.

This is not a backdrop for wellness activities. The forest itself is the practitioner. Our guides are simply translators between its ancient language and your nervous system.

The History

Shinrin-yoku:
A Living Lineage

Forest bathing is not new. It is a return. SHINRIN carries a lineage that stretches from ancient Japanese forest culture to contemporary clinical research.

Ancient

Forest Veneration in Shinto

Japanese Shinto tradition has revered forests as sacred dwelling places of kami (spirits) for thousands of years. The practice of entering forests for spiritual restoration predates recorded history.

1982

Shinrin-yoku Founded

Japan's Forestry Agency coins the term "Shinrin-yoku" (forest bathing) and begins promoting structured forest immersion as a public health initiative in response to rising urban stress.

2004

Clinical Research Begins

Dr. Qing Li and colleagues publish the first controlled clinical studies on phytoncide effects, demonstrating measurable immunological benefits from forest exposure.

2012

ANFT Founded

The Association of Nature and Forest Therapy is established in the United States, creating standardized guide training, evidence-based protocols, and an international practitioner network.

2015

SHINRIN Opens

Kenji Nakamura establishes SHINRIN in Oregon's old-growth forest after returning from Japan. The first guided walks draw guests from Portland, Seattle, and the Bay Area.

Today

A Living Practice

SHINRIN has guided over 2,400 guests across private and corporate programs. The forest grows more ancient with each year. Our practice deepens with it.
