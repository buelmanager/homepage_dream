Personal Confirmation

Every inquiry is reviewed personally by Kenji or one of our certified guides. You will receive a personal response — not an automated email — within 24 hours.

◈

Small Groups, Always

Our maximum group size for standard experiences is six guests. If your party is larger, we'll discuss custom options. We will never overbook to maximize revenue — only to maximize presence.

◈

Flexible Scheduling

We offer morning and afternoon walks year-round. Moonlit walks are scheduled around the full moon calendar. We'll find a date that works for your group.

◈

Accessible for All

Please share any physical, accessibility, dietary, or emotional needs in your message. We will adapt the experience to serve you fully. Nothing is too much to ask.
