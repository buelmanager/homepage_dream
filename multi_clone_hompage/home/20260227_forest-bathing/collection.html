All experiences are guided by ANFT-certified forest therapy guides. Groups are intentionally small — never more than six guests in our standard programs — to ensure depth of presence for each participant.

Signature
01

Half-Day Forest Bath

Duration 3 Hours
Time Morning or Afternoon
Max Guests 6 Persons
Fitness Level All Abilities

The ideal introduction to Shinrin-yoku for those new to forest therapy. We walk slowly — often covering less than a mile in three hours — through the most phytoncide-rich corridors of our old-growth sanctuary. Your guide offers a series of sensory invitations, creating space for genuine encounter with the living forest.

We close with a hand-gathered forest tea ceremony beside a centuries-old hemlock. Guests consistently report sleeping more deeply the night following this walk.

What's Included

  • ANFT-certified guide facilitation
  • Forest tea ceremony with locally gathered botanicals
  • Guided sensory invitations and sit spot practice
  • Pre-walk grounding meditation
  • Post-walk reflection circle
  • Seasonal field notes card from your guide
From $180 per person

02

Full Immersion Day

Duration 7 Hours
Time Sunrise to Dusk
Max Guests 4 Persons
Fitness Level Moderate

A complete day of forest immersion for those ready to go deeper. We enter at sunrise when the forest is most alive — birds in full voice, light filtering through mist, the day beginning. We spend the morning in phytoncide-rich old-growth stands, pause for a forest lunch prepared with foraged ingredients, and spend the afternoon in deeper contemplative practice.

The day closes with an extended tea ceremony at sunset. Clinical studies show that three or more hours of forest immersion produces the most significant immunological and neurological benefits. A full day multiplies this exponentially.

What's Included

  • Full-day ANFT-certified guide facilitation
  • Forest-foraged lunch prepared by our guide
  • Morning and afternoon tea ceremonies
  • Extended sit spot practice (45 minutes)
  • Sensory nature journaling with materials provided
  • Personalized forest prescription card
From $380 per person

03

Moonlit Forest Walk

Duration 2.5 Hours
Schedule Full Moon Nights Only
Max Guests 4 Persons
Frequency Monthly

Available only on full moon nights, the Moonlit Walk is the rarest and most transformative of our offerings. The forest at night is a different world — the senses recalibrate, sight yields to hearing and smell, and a profound stillness emerges. Moving through centuries-old trees in moonlight is an experience that stays in the body long after you leave.

No artificial light is used during the walk. Your guide navigates by memory and moonlight, having walked these paths through every season for over a decade. Spaces are extremely limited — four guests per moon.

What's Included

  • Expert nighttime forest navigation by your guide
  • Night-adapted sensory invitations
  • Warm herbal tea ceremony by firelight
  • Astronomy brief — reading the canopy at night
  • Soundscape meditation (20 minutes)
  • Moon calendar card for the coming year
From $260 per person

Corporate Restoration

Duration Full Day · Custom
Guests 8–20 Persons
Format Private & Exclusive

Designed for leadership teams, creative departments, and organizations navigating burnout, conflict, or transition. Each corporate program is co-designed with an organizational context interview. We do not run off-the-shelf team-building. We design a day specific to your group's needs, size, and current challenges. Multiple guides are assigned for groups over twelve.

Before You Arrive

How to
Prepare

Forest therapy requires nothing special. But a little preparation ensures you arrive ready to receive rather than manage.

◈

What to Wear

  • Sturdy closed-toe shoes or boots
  • Comfortable, layered clothing
  • Waterproof outer layer (all seasons)
  • Hat for sun or warmth depending on season
  • Nothing precious — the forest will touch you
◈

What to Bring

  • Water bottle (we provide forest tea)
  • Small snack if you have dietary needs
  • A quiet, open mind
  • Leave your phone in airplane mode or in your vehicle
  • Nothing to achieve — no goals, no fitness targets
◈

What to Leave Behind

  • Podcasts, audiobooks, music
  • Your schedule and the voice that runs it
  • Expectations of what "healing" looks like
  • Performance — there is nothing to do correctly
  • Urgency of any kind

Every Experience Is Limited
