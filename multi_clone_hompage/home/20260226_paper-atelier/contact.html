Getting Here

Visiting the
Atelier

Echizen is a 30-minute drive from Sabae Station on the JR Hokuriku Line. We recommend visiting alongside the Echizen Washi no Sato museum complex nearby.

1
From Tokyo: Shinkansen to Maibara → JR Hokuriku Line to Fukui → Local bus or taxi to Echizen (approx. 3.5 hours total)
2
From Osaka: JR Limited Express Thunderbird direct to Fukui Station → Local transport to Echizen (approx. 2 hours total)
3
By car: Echizen IC exit from Hokuriku Expressway. FOLIO Atelier is 8 minutes from the motorway exit. Parking available on-site.
4
Visitors are warmly welcomed during atelier hours. We recommend contacting us in advance for guided studio tours.
