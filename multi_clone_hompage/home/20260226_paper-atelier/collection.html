Featured Selection

Gampi Silk —
The Rarest Washi

Gampi (Wikstroemia sikokiana) is Japan's most prized washi fiber — it cannot be cultivated, only gathered from wild plants in mountain forests. The result is a sheet of extraordinary refinement: naturally lustrous, silk-like in texture, insect-resistant, and dimensionally stable over centuries.

FOLIO produces Gampi Silk in limited seasonal batches following each autumn harvest. Production is capped at 200 sheets per year.
