Commission Options

Types of Commission

Tier I

Functional Commission

Bespoke vessels, bowls, cups, platters, and tableware made to your specification. Perfect for domestic use, gifting, or restaurant commissions.

  • Fully negotiated form, size, and glaze
  • Single pieces or sets
  • Functional food-safe glazes available
  • Gift packaging available
  • Care guide included

Lead time: 10–16 weeks

Tier II

Sculptural Commission

One-of-a-kind sculptural objects for collectors, galleries, and private spaces. Includes detailed design development with studio visit or call.

  • Design consultation (1–2 sessions)
  • Material and glaze development
  • Studio photography of completed work
  • Signed certificate of provenance
  • Shipping arranged globally

Lead time: 12–20 weeks

Tier III

Architectural Installation

Large-scale ceramic works for hospitality, residential, and cultural spaces. Full collaboration from concept to delivery and installation.

  • Full design consultation with architect/designer
  • Material testing and sampling
  • Multiple glazes and clay bodies
  • On-site installation available
  • Maintenance guidance provided

Lead time: 16–40 weeks
