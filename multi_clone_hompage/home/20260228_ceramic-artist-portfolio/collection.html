Bespoke Work

Commission a Unique Piece

Not seeing what you're looking for? We take bespoke commissions for vessels, sculptures, and installation work. Every commission begins with a conversation about form, function, and material.

Lead time for commissions is typically 8–16 weeks depending on complexity. We fire in batches four times a year — your piece will be made in the next available firing.
