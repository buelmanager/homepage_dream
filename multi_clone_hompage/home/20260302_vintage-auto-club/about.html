FROM EIGHT

The Grand Touring Vintage Club was established in the autumn of 1962 by eight motoring enthusiasts who shared a conviction: that the great automobiles of the interwar and post-war eras were engineering masterworks worthy of preservation. Their combined fleet included a 1938 Bentley Derby, two 1956 Alfa Romeo Giuliettas, a 1961 Ferrari 250 GT, and four further vehicles of comparable distinction.

In the early years, the club operated from a member's farmyard workshop, pooling mechanical expertise and sourcing period-correct components through painstaking correspondence with specialist dealers across Europe. The ethos was always practical: a classic car must be driven to be truly honoured.

The inaugural Coastal Grand Tour in 1975 was a turning point. Twenty-eight vehicles completed a three-day route along the Western seaboard, attracting press coverage and applications from enthusiasts who had heard of the club by word of mouth alone. Membership doubled within the year.

THE MEN AND

STARTED IT ALL

ARTHUR PEMBERTON
1938 Bentley 4.25 Derby

Retired engineer and the club's first Chairman. Served in that role for fourteen years.

ELEANOR FROST
1956 Alfa Romeo Giulietta

The club's first female member and its most accomplished rally navigator of the early era.

GIANCARLO MORI
1961 Ferrari 250 GT

Italian expatriate who brought continental motoring culture and supplier contacts to the club.

HENRY VANCE
1959 Aston Martin DB2

Mechanical engineer who established the club workshop standards still in use today.
