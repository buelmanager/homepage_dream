Our Philosophy

Conservation Through Wonder

We believe the surest path to protecting nature is helping people fall in love with it. Every visit is a quiet revolution in perception.

01

Living Conservation

Every butterfly in our care is part of an active breeding and release program. We partner with tropical forest communities to protect habitats where our species naturally thrive.

02

Slow Encounter

We deliberately limit visitor numbers. Butterfly watching rewards patience — a quality rare in modern life. Our guided journeys take you to the edge of stillness, where wings land uninvited.

03

Scientific Artistry

Our resident lepidopterists are both scientists and storytellers. The stories of migration, metamorphosis, and mimicry they share transform observation into awe.

The Journey

From Egg to Wing

Watch the full arc of metamorphosis unfold before you. Our lifecycle displays let you witness one of nature's most profound transformations in real time.

Stage I

The Egg Chamber

Observe butterfly eggs smaller than a pinhead as they're laid on host plants. Each species selects its plant with extraordinary precision.

Stage II

Larval Gallery

Watch caterpillars in their many instars — feeding, molting, and growing — each species wearing its own remarkable camouflage.

Stage III

The Chrysalis

The hanging chrysalides glow under light, some gold-tipped, others jade-green. Inside, a complete cellular reorganization unfolds over two weeks.

Stage IV

Emergence & Release

The daily emergence ceremony is the heart of LEPIDOPTERA. Guests are invited to witness wings unfolding for the very first time.

Our Heritage

Three Decades of
Living Wonder

Founded in 1987 by lepidopterist Dr. Elena Maris and botanist Alejandro Vega, LEPIDOPTERA began as a single greenhouse on the edge of a tropical reserve. What started as a personal obsession became a world-renowned institution.

Today our collection has grown to include species rescued from habitat destruction, bred under careful guidance, and released back into protected wild corridors. Conservation, science, and beauty are inseparable here.
