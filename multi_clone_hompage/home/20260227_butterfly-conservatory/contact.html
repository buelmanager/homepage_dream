Common Questions

Before You Arrive

Can butterflies land on me during the visit?
+
Yes — this is one of the great pleasures of LEPIDOPTERA. Butterflies land freely on visitors and are attracted to bright colors and warm skin. We ask that visitors do not attempt to pick them up, but being landed on is entirely welcome and happens frequently in all five zones.
What should I wear to the conservatory?
+
Light, breathable clothing is recommended — our tropical zones are maintained at 22–35°C with high humidity. Bright colors, particularly red, yellow, and orange, attract butterflies. We recommend comfortable closed-toe footwear for the forest path between zones. Avoid wearing heavy fragrances as they can disorient the butterflies.
Is the conservatory suitable for young children?
+
LEPIDOPTERA is especially wonderful for children. The Larval Gallery is designed to be accessible at eye level for small visitors, and the species identification booklets we provide have a children's edition with simpler language and illustration-focused design. We ask that all children under 8 remain with an adult at all times within the zones.
Can I photograph inside the conservatory?
+
Personal photography is welcome with standard cameras and mobile phones throughout all zones. Flash photography is not permitted as it can disorient the butterflies. Tripods and professional camera equipment are permitted only during the Photography Dusk Session. Commercial photography or filming requires prior written permission.
What happens if it rains on the day of my visit?
+
Our five biome zones are entirely enclosed and climate-controlled, so rainfall outside does not affect the conservatory experience at all. In fact, post-rainfall periods often bring increased butterfly activity in the open-air botanical garden areas. We operate in all weather conditions and do not cancel or reschedule for rain.
Is the facility accessible for visitors with mobility needs?
+
Yes. All five biome zones are accessible via wide, level pathways suitable for wheelchairs and strollers. Accessible parking spaces are available immediately adjacent to the main entrance. If you have specific accessibility requirements, we encourage you to contact us in advance so we can prepare the most comfortable experience for your visit.
