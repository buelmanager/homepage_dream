Commission Process

How your piece comes to life.

All works are bespoke commissions. We do not maintain stock. Every piece shown is an example of what we make — your commission will be unique to you.

01 — Enquiry
Tell us your vision
Complete our commission form with your piece type, dimensions, and preferred wood species.
02 — Consultation
We visit your space
Thomas will visit your home, take measurements, and understand how you live with your furniture.
03 — Proposal
Design & quote
Detailed drawings, material samples, and a full price proposal within 10 working days.
04 — Making
The craft begins
16–24 weeks of focused making. You may visit the workshop at any stage to see progress.
