# Clone Plan — ARBORIS STUDIO

## Concept Origin

This template is an original creation inspired by the aesthetic of luxury British craft studios such as:
- Benchmark Furniture (Newbury, UK)
- Ptolemy Mann's textile studio aesthetic
- Pinch Design London
- The New Craftsmen collective

No direct cloning of any existing template. All copy, structure, and visual design are original.

## Design Language

**Category:** Furniture maker / Artisan studio portfolio
**Aesthetic:** Dark luxury meets natural warmth — the "elevated craft" aesthetic
**Target client:** Independent furniture makers, small-batch woodworking studios, bespoke joinery ateliers

## Structural Decisions

### Page Architecture (5 pages)
1. **index.html** — Full portfolio showcase with 6-section narrative arc
2. **about.html** — Human story and credentials (trust-building)
3. **collection.html** — Commercial catalogue with filtering
4. **process.html** — Process transparency (premium positioning signal)
5. **contact.html** — Commission funnel with friction-reducing information

### Hero Type C Rationale
The diagonal split hero was chosen because:
- It creates visual dynamism without requiring animation at load
- The geometry mirrors the precision of joinery (mitre cuts, chamfers)
- It allows brand text to breathe on a clean dark background while the image speaks at full bleed
- Reference implementation: `hat-atelier`, `leather-studio`, `lacquer-studio` templates

### Color Palette P10 Rationale
Dark olive (#141810) references:
- Aged patinated timber
- Forest floor
- Traditional Japanese urushi lacquerware
- Provides strong contrast for ivory text without harsh pure black

Accent green (#8AB56A) references:
- Young oak leaves
- Living wood
- Sustainable/ecological values

## Differentiation from Existing Templates
- No other template targets the bespoke furniture/woodworking niche
- The commission form with lead times table is unique to this template
- Wood species guide with color swatches is a novel section
- Press quotes section positions the studio as editorially recognised
