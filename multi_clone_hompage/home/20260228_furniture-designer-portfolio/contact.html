Care & Maintenance

How to live well
with your piece.

Daily Care
  • Wipe with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth
  • Avoid leaving wet glasses or hot dishes directly on the surface
  • Use felt pads on all items placed on the piece
  • Keep away from direct heat sources and radiators
  • Rotate objects periodically to allow even patination
Seasonal Maintenance
  • Apply a thin coat of natural oil or wax annually
  • Use a natural beeswax polish for oiled pieces
  • Buff with a clean lint-free cloth after application
  • Inspect joints annually — report any movement to us immediately
  • Minor scratches can be sanded lightly and re-oiled at home
Long-term Stewardship
  • Arboris furniture is designed to be repaired, not replaced
  • We offer a full repair and refinishing service for all our pieces
  • Bring damaged pieces back to us — we will restore them
  • Pass care instructions to the next owner if you move the piece
  • Contact us before any significant restoration work by others
