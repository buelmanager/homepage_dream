Our Founding Story

Born from a single chair.

Arboris Studio was founded in 2008 by master craftsman Thomas Mercer, who left a career in architecture to return to working with his hands. He built his first chair in a converted barn in Shropshire — and has never stopped.

"The satisfaction of architecture was always in the detail. Wood taught me the detail could be everything."

What began as a solo practice has grown into a studio of four dedicated craftspeople, each with a specialism — joinery, finishing, upholstery, and timber sourcing. Together, they produce between 30 and 40 bespoke pieces each year.

The name Arboris — from the Latin arbor, meaning tree — reflects our fundamental belief: every piece of furniture begins its life as a living thing. We honour that.

The Craftsman

Thomas Mercer,
Founder & Lead Maker

Thomas trained as an architect at the Bartlett, UCL, before spending three years as an apprentice to Norwegian furniture maker Erik Haugen in Oslo. He returned to England with a determination to build furniture that bridged the formal precision of architecture with the warmth of Nordic craft.

Today, Thomas leads every commission — from the initial client consultation through to final delivery and placement in the home. He believes the maker should never be separated from the client.

Our Principles

Three things we believe.

01
Slowness is a virtue.
We refuse to rush. The curing of timber, the fitting of a joint, the hand-sanding of a surface — each stage takes the time it needs. Furniture that is hurried is furniture that fails. We do not accept that compromise.
02
The maker must know the owner.
Every piece we create is for a specific person in a specific room for a specific life. We visit your home. We ask about your routines. We learn how you will live with what we make. A piece made without this knowledge is not a commission — it is a product.
03
Beautiful things should be durable.
We are allergic to the aesthetic of disposability. Everything we make is designed to outlive its owner and be passed to the next generation. We use no veneers, no MDF, no hidden fixings that cannot be repaired by a future craftsperson.
Sustainability

We give back
more than we take.

Every board we use is sourced from certified sustainable forests or reclaimed from estate demolitions and flood-cleared woodland. We work exclusively with sawyers and foresters we know personally.

For every commission completed, we plant twelve trees through our partnership with the Woodland Trust — the approximate timber yield of a mature hardwood tree. By our calculation, we have now returned more timber to English woodland than we have taken.
