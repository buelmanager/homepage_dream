The Materials

Where the Gown Begins

We source exclusively from European heritage mills with whom we have maintained relationships for over two decades.

Lyon, France

Duchess Satin

Woven by Jacquard Français since 1887. 280g/m² with a luminous, structured hand. Our primary fabric for Classic Eternal and formal ball-gown commissions.

Calais, France

Calais Lace

The world's most celebrated bridal lace, produced on original Leavers looms. We visit the Calais mills twice yearly to select exclusive patterns unavailable to ready-to-wear.

Como, Italy

Silk Crepe de Chine

Produced by Taroni, Como's most prestigious silk house since 1921. Our bias-cut crepe has a matte, body-following weight that no synthetic can replicate.

Paris, France

French Tulle

Marescot tulle, produced in the Vosges and finished in Paris. Sixty metres per Princess Grace gown, chosen for its ability to hold volume without stiffness.

Each Gown Is a Beginning, Not a Selection

Our lines describe an aesthetic — not a fixed design. Every ALBA gown is created from scratch for a specific woman, her body, her story, and her day.
