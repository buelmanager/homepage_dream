Important Notes

We accept commissions from brides worldwide. International brides typically require a minimum of three visits to Paris over the course of eighteen months.

All enquiries are treated with absolute discretion. We do not discuss commissions publicly without explicit permission from the bride.

What We Offer

Services at ALBA

✦

Bespoke Commission

A fully bespoke gown designed and built entirely from scratch for a single bride. Twelve fittings, eighteen months, no two alike. Our primary and most celebrated offering.

✦

Private Fabric Viewing

Brides who have begun the commission process are invited to private fabric presentations at the atelier. We present silks, laces, and embellishments selected specifically for their gown.

✦

Veil & Accessories

Bespoke veils, gloves, sashes, and headpieces created as complements to each gown commission. We do not sell accessories as standalone items.

✦

Preservation

Museum-grade gown preservation for ALBA commissions and select heirloom pieces. Climate-controlled storage available for up to five years post-wedding.

✦

Archive Consultation

Daughters of past ALBA brides may request a private viewing of their mother's archived patterns, fabric swatches, and original sketches. By appointment only.

✦

Press & Editorial

ALBA works selectively with print and digital editorial partners. Product loans, atelier visits, and interviews may be arranged through our press enquiry form above.
