How We Work

Six Acts of Creation,
Eighteen Months of Devotion

Each ALBA gown follows a deliberate, unhurried sequence. There is no shortcut between listening and perfection.

The Process

From First Word to Final Gown

A bespoke ALBA commission is a collaboration that unfolds over the course of a year and a half. Below is the path we walk together.

I
Month 1 — 2 hours

The Consultation

We begin not with catalogues but with conversation. In our private salon at Rue de Passy, Camille Morel meets you for two uninterrupted hours. She asks forty-two questions — about how you move, how you want to feel, what your grandmother wore, which memory of a dress has stayed with you longest. No sketches are produced at this meeting. We listen only.

  • Private salon at the atelier — by appointment only
  • Champagne and light refreshments served
  • Mood references welcome but never required
  • No design decisions made at this stage

The salon — Rue de Passy, Paris

II
Months 1–2 — 3 weeks

The Atelier Sketch

Following the consultation, Camille retires to the studio and produces three original watercolour sketches — each a distinct interpretation of what she heard. They may agree or diverge wildly. You choose a direction, and we refine through two further rounds of drawing before any fabric is touched.

  • Three initial sketches, A3 watercolour on rag paper
  • Two rounds of refinement included
  • Final sketch countersigned by both parties
  • Sketches remain property of the bride

Camille Morel's studio — original watercolour sketches

III
Months 2–4 — 6 weeks

The Toile Trial

Before a single centimetre of luxury fabric is cut, we construct a full test garment in unbleached muslin — the toile. You try it on twice. We mark, pin, remark. The toile is where silhouette lives and is perfected. We often make three or four toiles before we are satisfied. This is not a delay. It is the foundation of everything that follows.

  • Muslin toile constructed to your exact measurements
  • Two toile fittings included, further rounds as needed
  • Structural boning and underpinning confirmed at this stage
  • Train length and volume finalised

Toile fitting — the architecture of the gown

IV
Months 4–5 — 4 weeks

Fabric Selection

We invite you to the atelier for a private fabric presentation. All materials have been pre-selected from our mill partners in Lyon, Calais, and Como — we present only what we believe is right for your gown. You touch, you drape, you hold them to the light. Some brides select in an hour. Others return twice. There is no pressure in this room.

  • Primary fabric, lining, and trim selected together
  • Sample swatches sent to your home for reference
  • Fabric ordered exclusively — no shared lots
  • Deadstock and archive fabric available on request

Fabric room — Lyon silks, Calais lace, Como crepe

V
Months 5–16 — 12 appointments

Twelve Fittings

The twelve fittings are the heart of the ALBA experience. Each one is a private appointment, two to three hours in the fitting suite, with Camille and the lead petite mains present. Champagne is always served. There is no rushing. Between fittings, the gown lives in the archive — breathed upon by the artisans, each working on their singular discipline.

  • Twelve appointments, approximately one per month
  • Fitting suite reserved exclusively for your appointment
  • Partner or one guest welcome at fittings six and twelve
  • Photography permitted from fitting nine onward

The fitting suite — Rue de Passy

VI
Final Month — Delivery ceremony

The Final Gown

The last fitting is called the remise — the handover. You try the completed gown for the final time in the fitting suite. Isabelle Morel is always present. She adjusts a single button, or nothing. The gown is then packed in a hand-stitched silk preservation bag, boxed in archival tissue, and accompanied by a written certificate of provenance signed by both Isabelle and Camille.

  • Hand-stitched ivory silk preservation bag
  • Certificate of provenance — fabric origins, artisan names
  • Archival pattern preserved at the atelier in perpetuity
  • Care and preservation guide included

The remise — final handover ceremony

The Fittings

What Happens in Each Appointment

The twelve fittings are not redundant. Each serves a specific purpose in the construction sequence. We do not hold fittings to check boxes — we hold them because the body is a living thing and the gown must learn it over time.

Below is a simplified schedule. Every commission varies, and we adapt as needed.

1–2
Foundation & StructureBodice construction confirmed, boning positioned, primary seams established.
3–4
Skirt & VolumeSkirt layers assembled, train length confirmed at movement, underpinning adjusted.
5–6
Lace & Detail PlacementLace appliqué positioned by eye on the body. Partner invited to fitting six.
7–9
Embellishment & BeadingHand-beading begun. Embroidery placement confirmed. Photography permitted from nine.
10–11
Finishing DetailsButton closing, hem, veil coordination, shoe heel height incorporated into length.
12
The RemiseFinal completed gown. Isabelle present. Certificate signed. Gown delivered.
Our Mill Partners

Where We Source Our Fabrics

We have worked with the same mill partners for twenty years. These relationships allow us access to exclusive fabrics unavailable in ready-to-wear.

Lyon, France — Since 1887

Jacquard Français

Our primary source for duchess satin, taffeta, and structured silk. Jacquard Français weaves on original Jacquard looms and produces limited yardage annually. We visit the mill each October to select our allocation for the following year's commissions.

Calais, France — Since 1817

Marescot Lace

The world's finest bridal lace, produced on original Leavers looms built in the early 19th century. We work with Marescot to commission exclusive patterns — lace designs created only for ALBA, never reproduced for any other house or ready-to-wear brand.

Como, Italy — Since 1921

Taroni Silks

Como's most prestigious silk house. Taroni silk crepe de chine has a matte weight and body-following quality that no synthetic fabric can replicate. Our Modern Romance and Pure Minimalist lines depend entirely on Taroni's unique production process.

✦
Certificate of Provenance Signed by Isabelle and Camille Morel. Lists fabric origins, mill names, and the names of each artisan who worked on the gown.
✦

✦
Original Watercolour Sketches All three original consultation sketches, matted and in an archival sleeve, are included with the final delivery.
✦
Lifetime Restoration Access ALBA commissions carry a lifetime restoration guarantee. Should the gown require preservation treatment, we will undertake it.
