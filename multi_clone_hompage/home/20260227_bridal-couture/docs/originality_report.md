## Brand Originality

### Name & Identity
- **ALBA** — original name, derived from the Latin for "dawn" and "white." Not a direct reference to any existing bridal house. The name was chosen for its elegance, cross-cultural resonance, and bridal connotation (white, dawn, new beginning).
- **Tagline** "Your Most Beautiful Day, Begins Here" — original copywriting. Not sourced from or derived from any existing bridal brand.
- **Founder** Isabelle Morel — fictional character, no real person referenced or implied.
- **Atelier address** 12 Rue de Passy, 75016 — a real Parisian street used as a plausible location for a luxury atelier; not the address of any real bridal house.

### Narrative Content
- All copy is original, written specifically for this template.
- The "forty-two questions" consultation detail, the "remise ceremony," the "seven months / sixteen fittings" origin story — all fictional brand lore, not referencing any real atelier's documented practice.
- Mill partner names (Jacquard Français, Marescot Lace, Taroni) are partially based on real French and Italian textile heritage (Marescot is a real Calais lace manufacturer; Taroni is a real Como silk house), used in a fictional commercial relationship context. Their names are used as aspirational reference, not as endorsement.

## Design Originality

### Layout
- The asymmetric collection grid (`1.6fr 1fr 1fr` with large card spanning two rows) is original to this template.
- The Type E canvas hero with diamond/lace dot pattern is a novel visual interpretation of bridal lace in a digital medium. The specific implementation (diamond offset rows, accent dot with diamond outline stroke, upward drift) is original.
- The six-act process as a left-border vertical timeline with three-column rows (numeral / content / image) is an original layout not used in any other template in this series.

### Color Palette
- P4 Deep Burgundy was designed as an original palette for this template. The specific combination of `#1A0A0E` base, `#C96A8A` accent, and `#F0DCE4` ivory creates a distinctive bridal-luxury mood distinct from both traditional bridal white and obvious luxury dark.

### Typography
- Bodoni Moda at optical size 96 for the hero title is an intentional design decision — the extreme hairline serifs at large sizes create a fashion editorial aesthetic specific to this brand.
- The pairing of Bodoni Moda (classical couture) with Work Sans (contemporary atelier) is an original pairing for this series.

## Technical Originality

### Canvas Animation
- The hero canvas dot pattern with diamond alignment, proximity-based alpha, accent intersection dots with outline diamonds, and upward drift is a novel animation not present in any previous template in this series.
- The dual-ring preloader (two rings counter-rotating at different speeds) is original to this template.

### SplitText Polyfill
- The inline SplitText polyfill used in `index.html` is a shared project utility (documented in project memory), not externally sourced.
