How I Work

The Creative
Process

Five Phases

From Brief to
Final Master

01
Brief
Discovery
Week 1

Every project begins with listening — not to music, but to the story. An in-depth conversation to understand the project's emotional landscape, target audience, cultural references, and the feelings you want to evoke.

I review reference materials, study the visual direction, and ask the questions most people forget: What should the music make people feel? What should they do differently after hearing it?

Discovery Call Moodboard Review Reference Analysis Creative Brief
02
Concept
Development
Week 1–2

A sonic concept is defined: the instrumentation palette, tonal approach, harmonic language, and rhythmic character. Three distinct sonic worlds are proposed, each as a written description and short audio swatch.

This phase ensures we're aligned on direction before a single note of the final score is written — saving time and preserving creative energy for what matters.

Sonic Mood Boards Audio Swatches Instrumentation Brief Direction Approval
03
Sketch
Composition
Week 2–4

Thematic material is developed, cues are spotted and sketched, and key musical moments are shaped to picture (or to narrative beat). A rough mix is shared for feedback after each major cue.

The sketching phase is collaborative and iterative — I share work early and often, so no major revision surprises arise at the finish line.

Logic Pro X Ableton Live Spitfire Audio Native Instruments
04
Record
Production
Week 3–5

Live elements are recorded: solo instruments, ensembles, field recordings, or Foley depending on the project. The studio's Neve console and high-end microphone collection ensure warmth and presence.

Remote sessions with musicians worldwide are facilitated through Source Connect Pro. Live performances in Brooklyn can be arranged for local artists and small ensembles.

Pro Tools HDX Neve 8078 Neumann U87 Source Connect
05
Master
Delivery
Week 5–6

Final mix and mastering for the intended format — theatrical surround, stereo streaming, game engine stems, or broadcast spec. All deliverables are provided in the required formats with metadata and licensing documentation.

A detailed stem package is included with all scores, allowing for future re-edits, trailer versions, or regional variations without returning to session.

Prism ADA-8XR Dolby Atmos iZotope Ozone Stem Delivery
