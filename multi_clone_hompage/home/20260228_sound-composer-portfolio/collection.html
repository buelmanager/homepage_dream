Film Score · 2025

Liminal Spaces — Scoring the Unknowable

Director Mira Valdez approached me with a challenge: score a film where sound itself is the primary language. The narrative is told almost entirely through sonic texture — no dialogue for the first 22 minutes.

The score combines live string quartet with processed field recordings from liminal spaces: airports at 3am, hospital waiting rooms, underground car parks. The goal was to make silence feel inhabited.
