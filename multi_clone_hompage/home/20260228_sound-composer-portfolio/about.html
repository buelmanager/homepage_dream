Biography

Sound as a
Way of Knowing

Trained at the Berklee College of Music and the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland, my practice spans two decades of sonic exploration — from orchestral composition to modular synthesis, from film scoring to immersive installation.

"The most powerful sounds are the ones that bypass the mind and speak directly to the body."

I approach each project as a form of deep listening — to the story, the space, the client's vision, and the invisible possibilities that emerge when you truly pay attention. My compositions have been performed in concert halls from Carnegie to the Barbican, and heard in cinemas, galleries, and living rooms around the world.

Based in Brooklyn with a fully equipped studio, I collaborate remotely and in-person with directors, brands, artists, and game studios who share a belief in the transformative power of sound.
