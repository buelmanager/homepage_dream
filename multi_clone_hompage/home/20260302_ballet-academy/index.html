Our Philosophy

The Pointe Royale Method

Three pillars form the foundation of a Pointe Royale education — technical mastery, artistic depth, and the performer's spirit.

𝅘𝅥𝅮

Technical Foundation

The Vaganova curriculum forms the technical backbone of every student's training. Seven years of progressive study build the placement, strength, and precision required for professional classical ballet.

✦

Artistic Interpretation

Ballet is not athletics — it is storytelling. Every class, every combination, every rehearsal is guided by the question: what does this movement communicate? We cultivate dancers with genuine artistic presence.

◈

Performance Character

The transition from student to professional requires resilience, adaptability, and stage intelligence. Our performance program places students on stage 40 times per year — building unshakeable confidence.

Training Programs

Your Training Path

Four programs spanning junior intensive to pre-professional training, each delivered by former principal dancers from the world's great companies.

Junior Intensive

Foundation Programme

For students aged 8–12. Three weekly classes develop correct alignment, musicality, and the joy of movement within the Vaganova system's earliest grades.

Ages 8–123 classes/week
Intermediate

Académie Programme

For students aged 12–15. Daily training in classical ballet technique, character dance, pas de deux foundations, and Cecchetti exams toward Royal Academy of Dance grades.

Ages 12–15Daily training

Conservatoire Programme

The Studio

Studios Built
for Brilliance

Our Marais district atelier in Paris houses eight professional-grade studios, all with sprung Harlequin floors, floor-to-ceiling mirrors, and natural light. The historic building has been the training ground of three generations of Pointe Royale graduates.

"The studio is where we learn to listen — to the music, to our bodies, and to the character within each role."

London students train in our newly renovated Covent Garden studio, three minutes from the Royal Opera House. Summer intensives at our Paris campus are open to international students by audition.

Admissions

The Audition Process

01

Enquiry & Application

Submit an online enquiry including the student's age, current training background, and any recent examinations or performance experience. Our admissions team will confirm eligibility within five days.

02

Video Audition

Submit a 10-minute video showing barre work, centre exercises, and a short solo variation. Video auditions are reviewed by our faculty panel within two weeks of receipt.

03

Live Audition Class

Shortlisted candidates attend a live audition class at our Paris or London studio. The class is taken by a senior faculty member and observed by the artistic director.

04

Assessment & Placement

The artistic committee assesses each candidate's technical foundation, proportions, musicality, and potential. Accepted students receive a programme offer and scholarship assessment within one week.

05

Enrolment & First Class

Complete enrolment formalities and receive your class timetable and pointe shoe fitting appointment. New students are welcomed at an orientation evening before the first week of term.

Our Legacy

Seventy-Eight Years of Grace

1948

Founded in Paris

Maître de ballet Isabelle Fontaine establishes Pointe Royale in the Marais, offering girls of exceptional talent tuition in the classical French and Russian traditions.

1967

London Studio Opens

A Covent Garden studio brings the Pointe Royale tradition to London, establishing the first partnership with the Royal Ballet for student exchanges.

1989

Vaganova Accreditation

Pointe Royale receives formal accreditation from the Vaganova Ballet Academy, certifying the curriculum and ensuring graduates meet the standard expected by the world's top companies.

2012

Pre-Professional Track

The Company Preparation Programme launches, with its first cohort placing students in the Paris Opéra Ballet, English National Ballet, and the Bolshoi within three years.
