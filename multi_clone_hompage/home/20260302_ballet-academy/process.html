Pointe Royale Ballet

The Art of Ballet

Pointe Royale Ballet School has trained professional ballet dancers for the world's greatest companies since its founding in Paris in 1948. Our faculty of former principal dancers brings the living tradition of classical ballet to each new generation of students.

With studios in Paris and London, we offer programs from Foundation level through to our Company Preparation track for pre-professional dancers aged 18–21.

Our Faculty

All teaching is conducted by former principal or soloist dancers from companies including the Paris Opéra Ballet, the Royal Ballet, the Bolshoi, and the Royal Danish Ballet. Our artistic director brings over thirty years of professional performance and teaching experience.
