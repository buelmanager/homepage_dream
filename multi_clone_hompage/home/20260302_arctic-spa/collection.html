Each BOREAS treatment is a precisely structured journey through thermal contrast — designed to unlock the body's deepest adaptive responses and leave you changed.

Glacial Immersion Ritual

Our most transformative offering. A three-phase cold therapy experience beginning with extended time in the geothermal steam grotto, followed by guided full-body arctic immersion in glacial meltwater at 3°C, and concluding with a recovery soak in heated mineral pools.

Your therapist guides breathing technique throughout the cold phase — making the experience not an endurance challenge but a meditation in radical presence.

Arctic Stone Ritual

Volcanic basalt stones from Iceland's lava fields — heated to 55°C — are placed at precise meridian points along the body, then alternated with ice-cold glacial stones drawn from the spring. This thermal oscillation drives deep muscle release and triggers powerful lymphatic movement.

The treatment concludes with a full-body warm compress of arctic birch leaf and mineral salts drawn directly from the spring.
