Our Philosophy

The Healing Power of Ice and Stone

Nestled at the edge of the Arctic Circle, BOREAS was founded on the ancient Nordic belief that cold purifies — that submersion in glacial waters awakens the body's deepest healing mechanisms.

Our mineral-rich springs rise from beneath the glacial bedrock, filtered over centuries through layers of crystalline stone, emerging perfectly balanced in mineral composition and temperature.

Each ritual at BOREAS is a journey through contrasts: the searing warmth of geothermal steam, the electric shock of arctic immersion, and the profound stillness that follows.

Core Principles

The Arctic Method

❄

Glacial Cold Therapy

Controlled immersion in near-freezing glacial waters triggers the body's adaptive response — reducing inflammation, accelerating recovery, and elevating mood through endorphin release.

◈

Mineral Immersion

Our geothermal pools contain 82 naturally occurring minerals — magnesium, silica, sulphur — each absorbed through the skin during extended soaking at precise thermal gradients.

✦

Thermal Contrast

The ancient Norse practice of alternating extreme heat and cold — expanding and contracting blood vessels — creates a powerful cardiovascular and lymphatic flush unlike any other wellness modality.

The Arctic Method

Your Ritual Journey

01

Arrival & Orientation

Upon arrival your dedicated guide introduces you to the BOREAS philosophy, assesses your wellness goals, and tailors your arctic journey accordingly.

02

Thermal Preparation

Begin in the geothermal steam grotto — 48°C — where volcanic minerals open pores and prepare the vascular system for cold exposure.

03

Arctic Immersion

Guided submersion in glacial waters at 3°C. Our therapists coach breathing techniques that make the cold an ally rather than an adversary.

04

Mineral Recovery

Extended restoration in heated mineral pools with herbal infusions, followed by a private resting room draped in arctic wool blankets.
