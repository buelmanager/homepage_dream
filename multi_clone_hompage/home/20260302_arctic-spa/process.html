Our Approach

The Arctic Method

Developed over 37 years of practice and informed by Scandinavian cold physiology research, the BOREAS Arctic Method is a precise sequence of thermal contrasts, breathing protocols, and mineral absorption — each phase designed to build on the last.

Four Phases of Transformation

01

Thermal Preparation (20 min)

You begin in the geothermal steam grotto — a natural cave formation where volcanic steam rises through basalt fissures at 48°C. This extended heat exposure dilates peripheral blood vessels, opens skin pores, and brings the cardiovascular system into a state of heightened circulation — preparing the body for cold's profound effect.

What's Happening in the Body
  • Vasodilation of peripheral capillaries
  • Core temperature rises 0.5–1°C
  • Sweat glands flush stored toxins
  • Muscles soften and lengthen
02

Cold Immersion (3–5 min)

Guided full-body entry into glacial meltwater maintained at 2–4°C. Your therapist introduces you to the BOREAS breathing pattern — long exhales, controlled inhale rhythm — that transforms the cold from shock into something closer to euphoria. Most guests report the moment the cold becomes welcome arriving at roughly 90 seconds.

What's Happening in the Body
  • Massive norepinephrine release (300–500% increase)
  • Brown adipose tissue activation
  • Endorphin flood — the "cold euphoria" state
  • Inflammation markers decrease sharply
03

Mineral Absorption (30 min)

Following cold immersion, the body's expanded vascular system and open pores are uniquely receptive to mineral absorption. Our deep geothermal pool at 38°C — rich with 82 dissolved minerals — allows silica, magnesium, and trace elements to penetrate deeply into the skin and enter systemic circulation through dermal absorption.

What's Happening in the Body
  • Transdermal magnesium absorption peaks
  • Silica reinforces collagen matrix
  • Blood pressure normalises
  • Heart rate variability improves measurably
04

Integrative Rest (45 min)

The final phase is one most spas omit — and the one BOREAS guards most carefully. A private heated rest room draped in arctic wool, warmed birchwood tea, and complete silence. The body requires deep stillness to integrate the hormonal and vascular changes of the preceding phases. This is where the lasting change is encoded.

What's Happening in the Body
  • Parasympathetic nervous system activates fully
  • Growth hormone release continues for 4–6 hours
  • Deep lymphatic drainage continues passively
  • Mood elevation persists 24–48 hours

The Science Beneath the Ice

Cold Shock Proteins

Acute cold exposure triggers the production of RNA-binding proteins that protect cells from damage. Regular cold therapy practitioners show significantly elevated baseline levels of these protective compounds.

Brown Adipose Activation

Unlike white fat, brown adipose tissue burns calories to generate heat. Cold immersion activates brown fat deposits around the neck and upper spine, improving metabolic rate and thermal resilience long-term.

Mineral Dermal Absorption

Peer-reviewed studies confirm that skin absorbs meaningful quantities of magnesium, silica, and trace minerals during extended thermal bathing — particularly following cold exposure, which opens dermal channels.
