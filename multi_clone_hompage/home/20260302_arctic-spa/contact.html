Find BOREAS

Location

Lofoten Islands, Arctic Norway

BOREAS is located on the western coast of Vestvågøy island, accessible by boat from Svolvær (28 min) or private helicopter from Tromsø Airport (45 min). Coordinates available upon confirmed reservation.

Season

Open June — February

The arctic winter — October through February — is our peak season. The Northern Lights are frequently visible from the outdoor pools during this period. Summer guests experience the Midnight Sun phenomenon.

Response Time

Within 48 Hours

Our reservations team reviews each enquiry personally. Availability is confirmed within 48 hours of receiving your request. We do not take same-day bookings.
