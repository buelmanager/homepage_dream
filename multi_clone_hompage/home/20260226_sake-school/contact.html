Getting Here
By Train (recommended) Kintetsu Kyoto Line to Momoyama-Minamiguchi Station (8 min walk) or Kintetsu to Fushimi-Momoyama Station (10 min walk). From central Kyoto: 20 minutes.
By Taxi Approximately 25 minutes from central Kyoto. Show the driver: 中村蔵人酒造 (Nakamura Kurabito Shuzo). ¥2,500–3,500 from Kyoto Station.
Hours Tasting Room: Wed–Sun 11:00–18:00
By appointment: Mon–Tue and evenings available
Parking We recommend using public transport. Limited parking is available for 4 vehicles — please call ahead to confirm availability.
Accessibility The tasting room and courtyard are step-free accessible. Some kura areas involve uneven historic floors — please enquire when booking.
Exclusive Use

Private Events at the Kura

The Kurabito kura is available for exclusive private hire for groups of up to 40 guests. Events can be tailored to include tasting programmes, sake ceremonies, live brewing demonstrations, and kaiseki dining.

酒
Corporate & Incentive

Immersive sake experiences for corporate groups. Bespoke tasting programmes, team-building brewery sessions, and private dining with sake pairings designed in collaboration with our toji.

花
Celebrations

Weddings, anniversaries, and milestone occasions held in the cedar-beamed tasting room or the stone courtyard beneath the stars. The kura has hosted celebrations for a century and a half.

撮
Media & Creative

Photography, video production, editorial shoots, and media tastings. The brewery offers extraordinary visual character — weathered cedar, ancient stone, copper, and the morning light through the kura windows.
