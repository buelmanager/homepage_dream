Foundation
Sake Passport
酒パスポート
Duration 2-day Weekend
About This Course

The Sake Passport is our entry-level weekend programme, designed for curious minds who want to understand what they are drinking. Over two immersive days inside the kura, you will discover the geography, history, and science of sake — and taste twelve exceptional examples alongside Toji Nakamura.

Curriculum
  • The history of nihonshu from ancient Japan to the present
  • Rice varieties, polishing ratios, and their effect on flavour
  • The role of koji, water, and yeast in fermentation
  • Sake classification: Junmai, Ginjo, Daiginjo, Nigori, and beyond
  • 12 guided tastings with full tasting notes
  • Food and sake pairing fundamentals
  • Sake service: temperatures, vessels, and etiquette
  • Certificate of completion ceremony

Sake Expert
日本酒エキスパート
Duration 5-day Intensive
About This Course

A rigorous professional course for sommeliers, restaurateurs, and serious enthusiasts. Five days of structured study, blind tasting, and hands-on brewery work, culminating in the Japan Sake Sommelier Association-endorsed qualification. This is the benchmark credential for sake service in top Japanese restaurants worldwide.

Curriculum
  • Advanced sake chemistry and fermentation microbiology
  • Blind tasting technique: identifying grade, origin, and style
  • 40+ structured comparative tastings
  • Full day in the brewery during active fermentation
  • Sake cellar management and inventory
  • Advanced food pairing — kaiseki, French, and fusion
  • Sake ceremony and tableside service
  • JSSA written examination preparation and sitting

Master
Toji Fellowship
杜氏フェローシップ
Duration 10-day Residential
About This Course

The Toji Fellowship is a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Ten days in residence at the kura, working beside Toji Takeshi Nakamura in the live brewing season. Each Fellow participates in every stage of sake production — milling, koji making, mashing, fermentation, and pressing. The programme culminates in each Fellow's personal blend, bottled under the Kurabito label.

Curriculum
  • Daily morning briefing with Toji Nakamura in the kura
  • Hands-on koji room work (48-hour shifts with the toji)
  • Full fermentation monitoring — temperature, Brix, pH
  • Traditional fune pressing — press your own sake
  • Blending session: create your personal cuvée
  • Private label design and 12-bottle take-home allocation
  • Kurabito Master certificate — numbered and signed by the toji
  • Lifetime alumni access to toji consultations

Why Kurabito

Learning at the Source

Most sake education happens in classrooms. Kurabito happens in a working brewery, beside a fifth-generation toji.

01
Active Kura

You are not visiting a museum. You are learning inside a brewery that has been producing sake continuously since 1872 — with live fermentation tanks, working koji rooms, and a toji who brews every single batch.

02
Small Groups

We limit every course to eight students maximum — and the Toji Fellowship to two. This is not a school where you are one of forty students. The toji knows your name before the first morning is over.

03
JSSA Endorsement

Our Sake Expert certification is recognised by the Japan Sake Sommelier Association. Graduates have gone on to lead sake programmes at three-Michelin-star restaurants in Tokyo, London, and New York.

04
The Toji Teaches

Toji Nakamura teaches every session personally. Not a certified teacher reading from notes — the actual master brewer, sharing knowledge accumulated over five generations, in the very space where it was born.

05
Exceptional Sake

Every tasting uses Kurabito's own production, including rare seasonal releases and vertical library wines unavailable for commercial sale. You will taste sake that the public cannot access.

06
Kyoto Setting

Fushimi is the heart of Kyoto sake culture — home to the Fushimi Inari shrine, ancient sake streets, and the underground springs that define this style. Context makes knowledge deep.
