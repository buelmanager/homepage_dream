Living Collections

Seven Worlds Under Glass

Each of our seven biome pavilions recreates an entire ecosystem, housing plants collected over 177 years of botanical exploration.

Biome Pavilions

Explore the Collections

01
Tropical Biome
The Rainforest Pavilion

A 12-metre canopy height simulates the layered complexity of Amazonian and South-East Asian rainforests. Home to 2,400 species including the largest collection of Arecaceae palms in Europe.

02
Alpine Biome
The Mountain House

Carefully maintained at sub-zero temperatures, this pavilion houses cold-adapted species from the Himalayas, Andes, Alps and Arctic tundra.

03
Arid Biome
The Desert Garden

Cacti, succulents and drought-adapted species from the Sonoran, Namib and Atacama deserts, displayed in a recreation of arid rocky terrain.

04
Epiphyte Collection
The Orchid Sanctuary

Over 1,800 orchid varieties across every genus, from Phalaenopsis to rare Dracula species. Considered one of the finest orchid displays in the world.

05
Mediterranean Biome
The Olive Court

Aromatic herbs, ancient olive trees and sclerophyllous shrubs from the Mediterranean basin, California chaparral and the Cape Floristic Region.

Priority Species

Endangered & Critically Threatened

Critically Endangered
Jade Vine
Strongylodon macrobotrys

With fewer than 500 individuals remaining in the wild, our propagation programme has produced over 200 specimens for reintroduction.

Endangered
Franklin Tree
Franklinia alatamaha

Extinct in the wild since 1803, this species survives solely in cultivation. Our specimen is descended from original colonial collections.

Vulnerable
Ghost Orchid
Dendrophylax lindenii

Among the rarest orchids, successfully cultivated in our specialist epiphyte house under precisely monitored humidity conditions.
