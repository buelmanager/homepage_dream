Our Institution

The Story Behind the Collection

Founded in 1847, The Royal Botanical Conservatory stands as one of the world's premier institutions for plant conservation, scientific research and horticultural education.

A Mission Rooted in Science and Stewardship

We were established under Royal Charter at a moment of unprecedented botanical discovery. Victorian explorers were returning from every corner of the globe with specimens never before seen in Europe, and the Conservatory was created to house, study and protect this astonishing influx of plant life.

Today, our mandate is as urgent as ever. With up to one in five plant species threatened with extinction, the Conservatory serves as both a refuge and a research frontier — combining traditional horticultural skill with the latest advances in genomic science.

Our 68 specialist horticulturalists tend to over 12,000 species across seven climate-controlled biome pavilions, supported by a research team that collaborates with 60 universities worldwide.

Core Values

What Guides Us

◈
Scientific Integrity

All our conservation and research work is grounded in peer-reviewed science, transparent methodology and collaborative international standards.

◈
Accessible Wonder

We believe botanical science belongs to everyone. Our public programmes, free school days and digital resources reflect this commitment.

◈
Ecological Responsibility

The Conservatory is carbon-neutral since 2020. Geothermal heating, rainwater harvesting and solar panels power our pavilions sustainably.

◈
Global Partnership

Conservation cannot be local. We partner with communities, governments and institutions across 40 countries to return plants to healthy habitats.
