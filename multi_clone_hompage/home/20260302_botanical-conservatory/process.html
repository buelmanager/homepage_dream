Scientific Research

Science at the Service of Nature

Our research spans genomics, ecology and ethnobotany — producing knowledge that shapes conservation policy worldwide.

Core Disciplines

Research Pillars

01
Conservation Genetics

We analyse the genetic diversity of threatened species to identify distinct populations requiring targeted protection. Our genomic database covers 3,400 species globally.

02
Horticultural Science

Developing propagation techniques for species that resist conventional cultivation — including mycorrhizal inoculation protocols and in-vitro germination methods.

03
Ethnobotany

Working with indigenous communities to document traditional plant knowledge, ensuring this wisdom is preserved alongside the physical specimens.

04
Climate Adaptation

Studying how plant species respond to shifting climate envelopes, informing predictions for which populations face the greatest extinction risk by 2050.

Field Work

Active Conservation Programmes

Amazon Epiphyte Recovery

Restoration of canopy-dwelling bromeliads and orchids in degraded Amazonian forest fragments.

Brazil & Peru

Cape Fynbos Rescue

Protecting 180 endemic fynbos species threatened by invasive pines and urban encroachment.

South Africa

Himalayan Medicinal Plants

Documenting and propagating medicinal plants facing over-harvesting pressure in Nepal and Bhutan.

Nepal & Bhutan

Island Flora Revival

Reintroducing extinct-in-wild species to restored island habitats in the Indian Ocean.

Mauritius & Reunion
