## Brand

The Royal Botanical Conservatory houses over 12,000 rare plant species across seven biome pavilions. Founded under royal charter in 1847, it serves as a living archive of botanical heritage, combining world-class conservation science with public education.

**Tagline:** Where Nature's Diversity Flourishes
