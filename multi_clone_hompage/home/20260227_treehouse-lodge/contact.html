Response Times

All enquiries are responded to within 24 hours, seven days a week. For urgent matters, please call directly. During the forest's busy season (April–October), our hosts are often out in the estate until early evening — messages received after 6pm may receive a morning response.

Booking Process

After your initial enquiry, we will confirm availability and send a detailed proposal. A 30% deposit secures your dates. The balance is due 14 days before arrival. We accept bank transfer and major cards. No booking fees are charged.

Gift Experiences

CANOPY stays make extraordinary gifts. We issue hand-printed gift certificates in bespoke botanical packaging, valid for 24 months from date of issue. To arrange a gift stay, simply mention this in your message and we will guide you through the process.
