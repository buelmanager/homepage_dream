Signature Suite

Oak Canopy · 18 Metres Elevation · For Two

The Canopy Suite

Our most iconic nest rests in the arms of a 300-year-old oak, 18 metres above the woodland floor. Three walls of floor-to-ceiling glass open the room entirely to the canopy. A curved deck follows the trunk. A copper bath sits on the open deck — warm water, cold air, forest silence.

Inside: hand-joined fallen-oak flooring, a single king bed positioned to face the dawn, a fireplace of reclaimed estate stone, and a small library curated entirely from the estate's natural history archive.
