The Experience · From Enquiry to Memory

A Stay That
Changes You

We do not offer a checklist of activities. We offer an unfolding. From the moment you reach out to the moment you leave in silence, everything is considered.

I

Step One

Enquire with Intention

We begin not with a booking form but with a conversation. Tell us what brings you to the forest — whether it is rest, restoration, a celebration, or simply the need to be somewhere entirely different from where you are. We listen carefully before we respond.


Our forest hosts are available seven days a week, and they will return every enquiry within 24 hours with genuine attention — not a template.

What We'll Ask You

  • Your preferred dates and flexibility
  • How many guests are joining you
  • What you hope to feel during your stay
  • Any dietary requirements or health considerations
  • Whether you have a specific treehouse in mind
  • Special occasions we should know about
II

Step Two

Prepare for the Forest

Once your stay is confirmed, you will receive a private guest pack — not a generic PDF but a document prepared specifically for your treehouse, your season, and what the forest is likely to be doing when you arrive.


This includes your private arrival route, a personal packing guide, and a letter from Clara about what to expect in the woodland at that particular time of year.

Your Guest Pack Includes

  • Private canopy path arrival map
  • Seasonal packing guide (what to wear, what not to bring)
  • Your treehouse key and access code
  • Clara's seasonal forest letter
  • Optional activity pre-booking sheet
  • Forest emergency and host contact details
III

Step Three

Arrive Alone

There is no reception desk. No check-in process. No waiting. You follow the canopy path to your treehouse, which is already lit, already warm, already waiting. Your foraged welcome board is laid. A fire is set. Your first breath inside the treehouse is entirely your own.


We leave a short handwritten note — never more than three sentences — welcoming you to the specific tree you are now living within.

On Arrival You'll Find

  • Foraged welcome board and estate honey
  • Lit fire in the stove or fireplace
  • Chilled bottle of estate-foraged elderflower
  • Handwritten welcome note
  • Beeswax candles on every surface
  • Your Field Notes journal, ready and blank
IV

Step Four

Let the Forest Lead

There is no agenda. Wake when you wake. Eat when you're hungry. Walk when the light calls you out. Stay inside when the rain comes — there is no better sound. Your forest host is reachable but invisible. The forest will show you what it has for you.


Optional experiences can be arranged — foraging walks, fire ceremonies, private forest bathing sessions, guided nighttime moth-watching — but none are expected.

Available Experiences

  • Guided foraging walk (1.5 hours, included)
  • Dawn forest bathing session
  • Evening fire ceremony on the long deck
  • Nighttime moth-watching and star orientation
  • Private botanical drawing workshop
  • Foraged dinner cooked in your treehouse
V

Step Five

Leave as You Came

Departure at CANOPY is not a checkout. It is a leave-taking. We ask guests to spend their final morning slowly — a last walk, a last cup of coffee on the deck, a moment to write something in the Field Notes journal before you take it home.


You leave the treehouse as the forest left it for you: ready for the next breath, the next season, the next guest who needs it.

Departure Notes

  • Checkout by 11am — we extend to noon where possible
  • No checkout form — simply lock and leave
  • Your Field Notes journal is yours to keep
  • A bottle of estate botanical oil for the journey home
  • Follow-up letter from Clara within one week
  • Complimentary return stay credit after three visits

Good to Know

Before You Ascend

◈

Getting Here

CANOPY is located in the Wye Valley, Herefordshire. We are 2.5 hours from London by train to Hereford station, from which a private estate vehicle collects guests. Car directions are provided privately — we do not publish our location publicly.

◉

Connectivity

There is no WiFi on the estate. Mobile signal is faint and unreliable. This is intentional. We have a single satellite phone for emergencies, and a landline in the welcome lodge. We encourage guests to inform contacts they will be unreachable.

◎

Children & Pets

We welcome children aged 12 and above in the Beech Pavilion and Willow Bower. We are not able to accommodate pets, as they disturb the estate's wildlife habitats. We apologise and appreciate your understanding.

◇

Cancellation

Full refund up to 30 days before arrival. 50% refund between 30 and 14 days. Within 14 days, a credit is held for a future stay valid for 18 months. We transfer dates willingly — the forest will be here.

◈

Accessibility

The Willow Bower is our most accessible treehouse, with a gradual approach path and lower elevation. We are transparent about the physical demands of each treehouse and ask guests to communicate any requirements during enquiry.

◉

Dietary Needs

All dietary requirements are accommodated. Our kitchen is plant-forward by nature — the forest rarely offers meat. We source any animal products from within five miles of the estate. Please share requirements on enquiry.

Questions

What Guests Ask Us

How long in advance should I book?+
The Canopy Suite and Ash Observatory are typically reserved 4–6 months in advance, especially for summer and autumn. Other treehouses often have availability within 6–8 weeks. We keep a small number of dates in reserve for last-minute enquiries — it is always worth asking.
Is there heating in the treehouses?+
Yes. Each treehouse has underfloor radiant heating powered by our on-site micro-hydro system, supplemented by a wood stove or stone fireplace. We provide firewood and instructions. In summer, natural ventilation and the canopy shade make the treehouses naturally cool.
What happens if it rains heavily?+
Rain is part of the experience and the treehouses are designed for it. The sound of rain on living wood is something guests frequently cite as a highlight. All structures are fully weatherproof. Decks have covered sections. We provide waxed waterproof ponchos for walking.
Are the treehouses physically demanding to access?+
Access varies by treehouse. The Willow Bower is the most accessible, with a gentle path and only a short stair system. The Canopy Suite and Ash Observatory involve ladder-and-stair systems at elevation and require basic physical confidence. We discuss this during enquiry for all guests.
Can I have a completely private stay with no contact?+
Yes, and many guests specifically request this. Your breakfast basket will be left without any interaction. Foraging walks and dinners can be cancelled. Your host will be completely invisible unless you reach out. The emergency landline is always available. Complete solitude is an option we fully support.
What is the minimum stay?+
Two nights is the minimum across all treehouses. We recommend three nights or more — guests consistently tell us that it takes one full day to genuinely decompress, and that the magic of the forest only becomes clear on day two. The forest rewards patience.
