# TENSHO — Summit Tea House

## Concept
High-altitude tea ceremony house at 3,800m in the Himalayan foothills. Brand fictional: TENSHO. The tea house practices traditional ceremony where altitude physics (87°C boiling point) are incorporated into the ritual, not fought against.

## Color Palette — P7 Deep Teal
- Background: #0A1818 (very dark teal)
- Surface: #0F2020
- Accent: #4DCFB0 (jade/celadon green)
- Ivory: #DCEFEC

## Typography — F4 Cinzel + Crimson Pro
- Cinzel: Roman-classical capitals for gravitas and ceremony
- Crimson Pro: Elegant serif body text for readability

## Hero — Type B Parallax
Dual-layer parallax background with mousemove interaction. Mountain mist / cold water photography. Whisper animation for hero entrance.

## Animation — A2 Whisper
Slow, contemplative animations matching tea ceremony philosophy: 1.5s duration, 14px y, stagger 0.06.

## Chinese Characters in Philosophy
Using kanji numerals (一二三) for the three philosophy cards — cultural reference to Japanese tea ceremony without being appropriative. Adds visual texture unique to this template.

## Pages
- index.html: Full landing page
- about.html, collection.html, process.html, contact.html: Supporting pages

## Unique Elements
- Stats counter for 87°C water temperature — unusual stat that conveys the altitude physics concept
- Kanji step markers (一二三四五) in ceremony timeline
- Deep teal palette evokes mountain mist and green tea
