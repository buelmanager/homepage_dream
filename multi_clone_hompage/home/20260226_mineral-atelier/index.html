Provenance Promise

From Earth to Your Hands

Every LITHIC specimen passes through a rigorous four-stage verification and documentation process before it reaches our gallery.

☲ 1

Field Sourcing

Our geological partners operate in over 28 countries, sourcing ethically from licensed quarries with documented chain of custody from extraction site.

◆ 2

Gemological Review

Each specimen is assessed by GIA-certified gemologists who issue full mineralogical reports covering composition, crystal system, and formation geology.

✏ 3

Documentation

Blockchain-anchored provenance certificates are issued for each piece, ensuring permanent, tamper-proof ownership records and acquisition history.

★ 4

White Glove Delivery

Museum-standard crating, climate-controlled transport, and on-site installation by our specialist team — anywhere in the world.
