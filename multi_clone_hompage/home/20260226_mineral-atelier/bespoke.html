The Vision

Earth as
Interior Design

LITHIC's bespoke atelier creates custom mineral installations for private residences, luxury hotels, corporate headquarters, and cultural institutions. We believe that geological specimens — properly selected, displayed, and narrated — are among the most profound interior design elements available to a collector.

Each project begins with a consultation: understanding the architecture, the collector's sensibility, and the narrative they wish their space to communicate. From that foundation, our team selects or sources the specimens that best realise that vision.

How It Works

The Bespoke
Process

Every LITHIC installation project follows a structured methodology refined over eight years and dozens of landmark commissions worldwide.

Start Your Project
01

Initial Consultation

We begin with a detailed brief covering the project space, architectural context, collector's personal narrative and budget parameters. This is conducted in person or via secure video conference, and forms the foundation of all subsequent decisions.

02

Geological Curation

Our gemological team draws on our existing collection and global sourcing network to identify candidate specimens. We present a curated shortlist with detailed geological reports, origin documentation, and 3D spatial renderings of proposed installation configurations.

03

Design Integration

Working alongside the client's architect and interior designer, we develop the structural, lighting, and climate specifications for the installation. All weight-bearing, vibration, humidity, and UV considerations are addressed at this stage.

04

White-Glove Installation

LITHIC's specialist installation team handles every aspect of physical placement, mounting, and commissioning. We use museum-standard crating and climate-controlled transport, with GPS tracking throughout transit. Installation teams are available in London, New York, Tokyo, and Dubai.

05

Documentation & Care

Every completed installation receives a full provenance dossier, insurance valuation, care protocols, and access to our ongoing maintenance programme. We offer annual inspection visits for all major commissions.
