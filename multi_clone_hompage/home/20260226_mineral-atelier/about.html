A Gallery Born
from the Deep Earth

LITHIC was founded by geologist and collector Dr. Elara Voss after two decades of field work across six continents. What began as a private collection of extraordinary mineral specimens evolved into a curatorial vision: to present the earth's most extraordinary geological creations as the fine art they truly are.

We operate at the intersection of geological science, artisanal curation, and interior connoisseurship. Every specimen in our gallery is selected not merely for beauty, but for narrative — the story of its formation, its journey from quarry to gallery, and its meaning within the greater tapestry of Earth's 4.5-billion-year history.

Today, LITHIC maintains four gallery spaces — in London, New York, Tokyo, and Dubai — and partners with over 60 licensed quarry operations across 28 countries to bring only the most exceptional specimens to our clients.

Our Philosophy
Every mineral is a time capsule. Our work is to ensure those capsules are opened by hands worthy of holding them.
— Dr. Elara Voss, Founder & Chief Curator
☀

Ethical Provenance

We work exclusively with licensed extraction operations. Every specimen is sourced with full traceability — from GPS-recorded extraction coordinates to gemological certification — ensuring zero conflict sourcing and full legal compliance in every jurisdiction.

◆

Scientific Integrity

Our in-house team of four GIA-certified gemologists and two consulting paleontologists conduct rigorous mineralogical analysis on every acquisition. We publish full technical reports for each specimen, available to collectors on request.

★

Curatorial Vision

Less than 3% of specimens presented to LITHIC are accepted into the collection. Our curation standard prizes geological rarity, aesthetic excellence, and narrative significance in equal measure — ensuring every piece belongs to the canon of extraordinary Earth art.
