Katanga Malachite:
Congo's Green Fire

This exceptional botryoidal malachite from the Katanga Copperbelt represents one of the finest examples of copper carbonate mineralization ever documented. Its velvety concentric banding — each layer deposited over thousands of years — creates a visual rhythm that no human artisan could replicate.

Accompanied by full provenance documentation, GIA mineralogical report, and ethically sourced certification from a licensed COMIDA-registered operation.

The Cosmos Collection

Meteorites

LITHIC maintains one of the world's most distinguished private meteorite collections. Each piece is classified by internationally recognised meteoriticists and carries full Meteoritical Society nomenclature documentation. These are not decorative objects — they are tangible pieces of the early solar system.

MET-001
Gibeon IIIAB
Namibia · Found 1836

Fine octahedrite with spectacular Widmanstätten figures revealed by Nital etching. Bandwidth of 1.2 mm indicates slow cooling of 1°C per million years within the core of a destroyed asteroid.

MET-007
Chelyabinsk LL5
Russia · 2013

A witnessed fall from the famous 2013 bolide event. Chondrite material with characteristic shock veins and partial fusion crust. One of the most scientifically significant meteorites of the 21st century.
