How We Work Together

Monthly

Print Critique Evening

Every third Thursday, 7PM

Bring up to three prints or a digital portfolio for review. Work is discussed by the full group with a guest critic. Honest, generous critique — this is the heart of the Society.

Monthly Workshop

Technical Masterclass

Second Saturday of each month

Hands-on technical sessions covering film development, darkroom printing, digital post-processing, studio lighting, and more. Guest practitioners invited quarterly.

Annual

Public Exhibition

November, 2 weeks · Partner Gallery

Our annual curated exhibition open to all members who have attended at least four critique sessions. Selection by a panel. The most significant thing we do together each year.
