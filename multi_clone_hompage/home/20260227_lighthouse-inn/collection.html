The Keeper's Suite

The lighthouse's premier accommodation, occupying the entire fifth floor of the tower with 360-degree views of the Atlantic and Cape Meridian coastline. Named for Head Keeper Samuel Aldrich, this suite preserves the original brass navigation instruments and the keeper's logbook desk where decades of Atlantic weather were recorded.

Tower Room
Tower · Floor 4

The Lantern Room

Adjacent to the working lighthouse lantern, this tower room glows with the ambient light of the Fresnel lens mechanism. Glass walls on three sides frame the Atlantic horizon. William Aldrich Jr. was born in this room in 1899, and a commemorative brass plaque marks the precise location of his crib.

  • Three-sided Atlantic glass views
  • Working Fresnel lens visible
  • Antique ship's compass headboard
  • Storm-watching window seat
