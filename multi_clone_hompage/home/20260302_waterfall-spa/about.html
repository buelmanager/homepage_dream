The Sanctuary

A Valley Shaped

VELLUM was built around what nature placed here — twelve waterfalls, three thermal springs, and an ancient forest that has been filtering air and water for centuries.

The Location

Costa Verde Valley

Found by Following Water

In 2006, Dr. Mara Fontaine was conducting a botanical survey of the Costa Verde mountains when she followed a sound she couldn't identify. The sound was twelve waterfalls converging in a natural basin — a place no road reached and very few people knew existed.

She spent three days there, swimming in the cold cascade pools and sitting in the warm thermal springs, and documented measurable changes in her own cortisol and serotonin levels using portable test equipment she carried for fieldwork.

Two years later, with the land protected by an environmental trust she established, VELLUM opened to its first guests. The single access road was designed by a landscape architect who spent months ensuring it caused minimal disturbance to the valley's hydrology.

The Science

Why Water Works

Negative Ionization Research

Studies published in the International Journal of Biometeorology confirm that negative ion concentrations near waterfalls are 1,000–2,000 per cubic centimeter, compared to 50–100 in urban environments. At VELLUM's main cascade, we measure above 5,000.

Hydrostatic Pressure Effects

Standing in water reduces blood pressure, decreases heart rate variability, and lowers inflammation markers. The specific pressure gradient created by waterfall immersion activates the parasympathetic nervous system within 8–12 minutes.

Acoustic Ecology

The frequency spectrum of falling water (roughly 20–2,000 Hz) has been shown to mask intrusive sound, reduce cognitive load, and promote the alpha brainwave state associated with creative thinking and emotional processing.

Thermal Contrast Therapy

The alternation of cold and warm immersion triggers the production of norepinephrine, reduces systemic inflammation, and has been documented to improve mood and sleep quality for up to two weeks after a single session.
