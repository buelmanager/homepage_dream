Our Approach

The Science of Water Healing

Every treatment at VELLUM harnesses the documented therapeutic properties of moving water — negative ionization, hydrostatic pressure, and thermal variation.

~

Negative Ionization

Waterfalls generate a field of negative ions proven to elevate serotonin levels, reduce cortisol, and promote deep sleep. Our cascade pools maximize this effect through engineered waterfall height and basin design.

◎

Thermal Contrast

The ancient practice of alternating between cold waterfall plunge and warm thermal spring creates a cardiovascular response that has been refined by indigenous cultures worldwide for thousands of years.

∿

Sound Therapy

The frequency of falling water has measurable effects on the autonomic nervous system. Our acoustic architects have mapped the sanctuary's soundscape to create zones of varying intensity and rest.

The Location

A Hidden Valley Sanctuary

Where the Valley Narrows

VELLUM occupies a private valley in the Costa Verde mountains, accessible only by a single forest road. The property encompasses twelve natural waterfalls, three thermal springs, and eleven hectares of pristine primary forest.

The main sanctuary was designed by architect Lucia Benvenuto, who spent two years studying the valley's water patterns before placing the first foundation. Every structure follows the water's existing paths — nothing was diverted or redirected.

Accommodation consists of three private villas and eight individual suites, each positioned to ensure guests wake to the sound of falling water. The valley's natural contours ensure complete acoustic privacy between structures.

The Experience

Your Retreat Journey

A typical VELLUM stay unfolds over three days, each with a different water element as its focus.

Arrival & Orientation

Guests arrive in the late afternoon to allow the nervous system to decompress before any treatments begin. A long immersion in the warm arrival pool, followed by a consultation with your personal wellness curator, sets the tone for your stay.

Day One — Cascade

The first full day is devoted to the waterfalls themselves: standing under the main cascade for thirty minutes of negative ion immersion, followed by guided breathwork at the middle pool. Afternoon free time for forest walking on unmarked trails.

Day Two — Thermal

The thermal circuit begins at dawn with a cold plunge under the upper cascade, followed by the hot spring, repeated in cycles across three hours. Afternoon treatment: the signature River Stone Ritual. Evening sound bath under stars.

Day Three — Integration

The final day focuses on stillness: a guided meditation at the lowest pool, where water barely moves. A closing consultation with your wellness curator documents the physical and emotional markers before departure, with follow-up protocol provided.

Our Story

Founded on Water

2006

The Discovery

Wellness researcher Dr. Mara Fontaine discovers the Costa Verde valley on a botanical survey. The twelve waterfalls and three thermal springs convince her this land holds exceptional therapeutic potential.

2008

VELLUM Opens

After two years of sympathetic development, VELLUM welcomes its first twelve guests. The three-villa sanctuary is immediately booked 18 months in advance.

2013

Clinical Recognition

A peer-reviewed study published in the Journal of Integrative Medicine documents the measurable health outcomes of VELLUM's water immersion protocols.

2019

Acoustic Program

Partnership with acoustic ecology pioneer Dr. Gordon Hempton leads to the development of VELLUM's unique soundscape therapy program — the first of its kind in the world.

2024

The Springs

Geological survey confirms three additional thermal springs beneath the lower valley. Expansion plans for two new treatment pools developed in consultation with the valley's ecosystems.
