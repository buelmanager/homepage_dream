The Bespoke Process

Every bespoke commission begins with a pearl consultation — either in person at our Geneva salon or by arrangement at our Finnish studio. You select from specimens held in the private reserve, including baroque, round, and Biwa pearls not available in the standard collection.

A goldsmith then works with you over 2–3 consultations to develop a setting. Delivery is typically 6–12 weeks from brief confirmation.
