How We Invest

INVESTMENT
PROCESS

We have refined our investment process over 16 years and 127 companies. It is deliberate, rigorous, and deeply founder-centric. From first contact to IPO, we are the most operationally engaged partner you will ever have.

Our Methodology

FROM DEAL TO
DECADE-LONG PARTNER

01
Phase One

DISCOVERY & SOURCING

Great companies rarely walk through our door — we find them. APEX maintains active sourcing channels across academic research institutions, government labs, technical conferences, and our portfolio founder networks across 28 countries. We also monitor 40+ technology domains using proprietary signal tools to identify companies before they formally fundraise.

  • Active outreach to researchers publishing breakthrough papers in priority domains
  • Referrals from existing portfolio founders (our highest quality source)
  • Technical conference presence at NeurIPS, ICLR, SynBioBeta, and CERAWeek
  • Deep relationships with leading PhD programs at MIT, Stanford, Caltech, and Oxford
Ongoing — 200+ companies evaluated per year
02
Phase Two

INITIAL EVALUATION

We respond to every inbound submission within 5 business days with a decision or a request for more information. Our initial evaluation focuses on three core questions: Is the technology real? Is the market large and durable? Is the team uniquely qualified to win? We deliberately avoid slide-deck theater — we want to understand the underlying science.

  • One-hour technical call with a relevant domain expert from our partner team
  • Review of core IP, patents pending, and any published research
  • Preliminary market sizing and competitive landscape assessment
  • Reference checks with technical advisors who know the domain
1–3 weeks
03
Phase Three

DEEP DILIGENCE

This is where APEX is different. We invest 60–90 days in a rigorous technical and commercial assessment before any investment decision. Our diligence process involves our full partnership, plus 4–6 external technical advisors specific to the technology domain. We are not trying to catch you out — we are trying to understand your company at the same depth you do.

  • Full technical review with domain-specific scientific advisory board
  • Customer discovery with 15–20 potential buyers or pilot partners
  • Regulatory pathway analysis (especially for biotech, energy, and defense)
  • Detailed financial model build, co-constructed with founder team
  • IP freedom-to-operate assessment by specialized IP counsel
60–90 days
04
Phase Four

INVESTMENT & PARTNERSHIP

Once the partnership committee approves an investment, we move fast. Term sheet within 48 hours of decision. Legal close typically within 21 days. We take a board seat on every investment and assign a dedicated APEX platform resource to each portfolio company — a senior operator embedded to support specific functional needs in their first 12 months.

  • Board seat with active governance and strategic counsel
  • Dedicated platform resource for talent, go-to-market, and regulatory
  • Immediate introductions to relevant customers in our 200+ enterprise network
  • Co-investment syndication with our LP co-invest network if needed
21-day close, then ongoing
05
Phase Five

SCALE & EXIT

Our job is not complete at the investment. We stay deeply engaged through every growth stage — Series A through IPO. We have helped 23 companies navigate public markets and 18 achieve unicorn status. Our exit advisory team, led by former Morgan Stanley and Goldman bankers, begins exit optionality planning from Day 1 of our investment.

  • IPO readiness preparation beginning 24–36 months before target listing
  • Strategic M&A advisory and buyer relationship management
  • Follow-on investment at every round where we have conviction
  • Post-exit alumni network — we remain founders' partners for life
Multi-year, through IPO or acquisition
Investment Criteria

WHAT WE
LOOK FOR

We have evaluated over 3,000 companies. These are the six attributes that the best ones — the ones that became unicorns — all had in common.

01

TECHNICAL MOAT

Proprietary science, IP, or process that cannot be easily replicated. Must be defensible for 7+ years. We look for first-principles breakthroughs, not incremental improvements.

02

MASSIVE MARKET

We target markets that are at least $10B and growing — ideally being created by the company itself. We are not interested in better mousetraps; we want entire new industries.

03

EXCEPTIONAL TEAM

Domain expertise so deep that the founders are the world's leading authorities on their technology. We back scientists who learned to be builders, not builders who learned science.

04

TIMING SIGNAL

Why is this possible now? We look for specific enabling factors — cost curves crossing thresholds, regulatory windows opening, adjacent technology maturation — that create the right moment.

05

ETHICAL FRAMEWORK

We do not invest in technologies that cause systemic harm, even if they are technically brilliant. Every investment goes through our internal ethics review before term sheet.

06

CAPITAL EFFICIENCY

We look for founders who have demonstrated the ability to do more with less — who have used constraint as an accelerant rather than a barrier to progress.
