Our Story

16 YEARS AT
THE FRONTIER

FROM A WHITEBOARD
TO $2.4B AUM

APEX was founded in 2008 by three scientists-turned-investors who believed that the most consequential companies of the next century would emerge from laboratories, not spreadsheets. They were right. The firm's first fund invested in seven companies — four of which became unicorns.

Today, APEX manages $2.4B across four funds, with a portfolio spanning 127 companies in deep technology, climate solutions, and artificial intelligence. We have backed 23 companies to IPO and witnessed 18 unicorn exits — with more in the pipeline.

2008
Fund I — The Founding $40M raised. First seven investments made. Three co-founders leave top-tier research institutions to back founders full-time.
2014
Fund II — Scaling Up $180M raised. Portfolio reaches 40 companies. First two unicorn exits recorded. Climate tech vertical established.
2019
Fund III — AI Focus $620M raised. Artificial intelligence becomes a primary vertical. AI specialist partners recruited from OpenAI and DeepMind.
2024
Fund IV — Global Expansion $1.56B raised. Offices in San Francisco, London, and Singapore. 127 active portfolio companies across 28 countries.
What We Believe

OUR CORE
VALUES

These are not platitudes on a wall — they are the operational principles that govern every decision we make, every board meeting we attend, and every founder relationship we build.

INTELLECTUAL HONESTY

We challenge our own assumptions as vigorously as we challenge the founders we evaluate. Groupthink is the enemy of exceptional returns.

FOUNDER ALIGNMENT

We take concentrated positions and high conviction bets. When we invest, we are genuinely in it with you — not just on your cap table.

LONG-TERM THINKING

We optimize for generational value creation, not quarterly metrics. Our fund structures are designed to support companies through multiple market cycles.

SCIENTIFIC RIGOR

Our diligence process is built on evidence, not narrative. We engage world-class technical advisors and do the work that most investors skip.

ETHICAL LEADERSHIP

We only back technologies and business models that pass our internal ethics review. Profit and impact are not in opposition — they are the same goal.

RELENTLESS GROWTH

We learn continuously — from our portfolio companies, from our failures, and from the founders who trust us with their most important work.
