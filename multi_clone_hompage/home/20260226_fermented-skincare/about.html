The Founder

Dr. Seo Yeon Park

"Every great ferment requires a relationship between time, temperature, and living matter. I gave skin the same patience my grandmother gave her kimchi."

Dr. Seo Yeon Park grew up in Jeonju, South Korea — a city legendary for its food culture and fermentation heritage. Watching her grandmother tend to rows of onggi jars on the kitchen floor instilled in her a deep respect for time as an ingredient.

After completing her doctorate in microbiology at Seoul National University, Seo Yeon relocated to Zürich to pursue postdoctoral research in bioactive skin delivery systems at ETH Zürich. It was there, in 2017, that she first combined her two worlds: fermenting Korean botanicals using Swiss-precision biotechnology.

FLORA was founded in 2018 in a converted laboratory space in Seoul's Seongbuk district. Today, the lab operates across two sites — the fermentation atelier in Jeonju and the biotech formulation lab in Zürich — producing every batch by hand, exactly as she envisioned.

Our Philosophy

Three Principles
of Living Skincare

We believe skincare should be as alive as the skin it serves. Every FLORA formula is guided by three non-negotiable principles.

Time is the Active Ingredient

No synthetic shortcuts. Every formula requires exactly 90 days of monitored fermentation. The time invested becomes measurable in the molecular complexity of the final serum — a richness no laboratory synthesis can replicate.

Botanical Integrity First

Every raw ingredient is hand-harvested at peak bioactivity — documented by our sourcing partners with GPS coordinates and harvest date. We refuse to work with spray-dried, solvent-extracted, or irradiated botanicals.

The Microbiome is Sacred

Conventional skincare disrupts. FLORA cultivates. Every formula is pH-matched to the skin's acid mantle and clinically tested for microbiome compatibility. We do not strip; we supplement what the skin already knows how to do.

Company History

A Decade
of Cultivation

2017
The First Experiment
Dr. Park ferments her first batch of Korean wild artemisia in a single onggi jar in her Zürich apartment. The result — a 78-day lacto-ferment — becomes the precursor to the Deep Culture Serum.
2018
FLORA Lab Founded
The first FLORA atelier opens in Seoul's Seongbuk district. Three fermentation rooms, twelve onggi vessels, and a two-person team. First product: First Ferment Essence, sold exclusively to 40 clients by referral.
2020
Swiss Biotech Partnership
Partnership established with Zürich-based biotechnology firm BioActiv AG for nano-encapsulation technology. The collaboration enables sub-50nm delivery of fermented actives — a breakthrough in penetration depth.
2022
Jeonju Fermentation Atelier
A purpose-built fermentation facility opens in Jeonju, South Korea — the birthplace of Korean food culture. Traditional onggi-making masters collaborate with the FLORA team to produce bespoke ceramic vessels for each formula.
2024
Global Expansion
FLORA enters retail in London, New York, and Tokyo through exclusive spa and apothecary partnerships. The Living Barrier Cream achieves a 14-month waitlist at Selfridges London.
2026
The Complete Ritual Launch
FLORA launches the complete five-step ritual, including the new Nocturne Ferment Mask and an expanded online consultation service connecting clients with our in-house fermentation scientists.
