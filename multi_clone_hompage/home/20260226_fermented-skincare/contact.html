Complimentary Service

Your Personal
Skin Consultation

Every FLORA client receives access to a complimentary 30-minute consultation with one of our in-house fermentation scientists. This is not a sales call — it is a diagnostic conversation.

We will discuss your skin history, current concerns, microbiome indicators, and lifestyle factors. Our scientist will then design a personalised ritual sequence and product selection based on the science of your skin, not a standard skin-type categorisation.

Consultations are available via video call, phone, or — for clients in Seoul, Zürich, London, New York, and Tokyo — in-person at our partner locations.

Trade Partnerships

Wholesale &
Retail Partners

FLORA selects wholesale partners with the same intentionality applied to ingredient sourcing. We partner exclusively with luxury apothecaries, destination spas, and five-star hotel retail programmes that share our commitment to ingredient integrity.

We do not supply to mass-market retailers. Our distribution is deliberately limited to protect product integrity and the exclusivity of the client experience.

Apothecary Partner
MOQ: 6 units per SKU / Season

Spa & Wellness Partner
MOQ: Treatment programme minimum
Spa-exclusive formulations available. Our fermentation scientists will design a bespoke in-treatment ritual using FLORA actives, trained on-site.

Min. 3 SKUs in guest amenity format
