The Ingredient Philosophy

What Fermentation
Unlocks

Fermentation does not add ingredients. It transforms them — breaking molecular bonds to reveal bioactive compounds invisible in raw form.

米

Gyeonggi Rice

Korea — Fermented 90 days

Triple-fermented using traditional nuruk culture. Releases ferulic acid, inositol, and natural kojic acid — a triad that brightens, plumps, and refines in one.

草

Korean Mugwort

Jirisan, Korea — Wild Harvest

Fermentation breaks down chlorophyll into artemisinin derivatives with potent anti-inflammatory and barrier-repair activity unavailable from raw extraction.

參

White Ginseng

Geumsan, Korea — 6-year root

Six-year-old roots, fermented to convert ginsenosides into more bioavailable compound-K — the form clinically proven to stimulate collagen synthesis.

藻

Swiss Alpine Edelweiss

Valais, Switzerland — 2,200m

Grown at extreme altitude, fermented with koji to unlock leontopodic acid — a rare phenolic compound with exceptional UV-stress protection.

Fermented bioactive skincare, cultivated in ceramic vessels over 90 days. Where ancient Korean wisdom meets Swiss biotech precision.
