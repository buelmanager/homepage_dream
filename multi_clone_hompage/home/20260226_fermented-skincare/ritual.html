The Philosophy

Why the Order
Matters

The FLORA ritual is designed around one central principle: each step activates the next. The essence does not simply hydrate — it creates an acidic, probiotic environment in which the serum's fermented actives can penetrate and function at maximum efficacy.

This is not layering for the sake of ritual theatre. It is biochemistry applied sequentially. Skip a step and you do not merely miss a benefit — you reduce the effectiveness of every step that follows.

We recommend a minimum of 30 seconds between each application, and that you approach the ritual with the same patience that our ceramics vessels bring to your formulas: slowly, deliberately, presently.

Morning & Evening

The Five Steps

Both morning and evening rituals share the same foundation. The evening adds the Nocturne Mask as a final, transformative step.

01

Step One

Cleanse

Microflora Balance Gel Wash

Dispense one pump. Activate with water on fingertips. Massage in slow circular movements for 45 seconds. Rinse with lukewarm water — never hot. Pat dry with a clean linen cloth.

Duration: 60–90 seconds

02

Step Two

Tone

Microflora Balance Toner

Saturate a cotton pad or pour into palms. Press — never wipe — across face and neck. This step re-establishes the correct pH environment for the fermented actives to follow.

Wait: 30 seconds before next step

03

Step Three

Essence

First Ferment Essence

6–8 drops into both palms. Warm between hands until you feel gentle heat. Press simultaneously onto cheeks, forehead, and chin. Repeat pressing until fully absorbed — approximately 60 seconds.

Wait: 45–60 seconds

04

Step Four

Serum

Deep Culture Serum

Two drops only. These are highly concentrated fermented actives — more is not more. Warm between fingertips, then press into the mid-face and sweep upward toward the temples. Avoid the immediate eye area.

Wait: 60 seconds

05

Step Five

Seal & Protect

Living Barrier Cream / Nocturne Mask (PM)

AM: A pea-sized amount of Living Barrier Cream. Warm in palms, press gently into skin, finish with upward neck sweeps. PM (3×/week): Replace with Nocturne Ferment Mask as the final, leave-on step.

PM add-on: SPF 50 after cream (AM only)

Application Science

The Art of
Layering

Every FLORA formula is formulated for sequential layering. The molecular weight decreases from step 1 to step 4, ensuring each active reaches its optimal skin depth without interference from thicker formulas applied first.

01
Lightest to Heaviest
Always apply from the thinnest consistency (toner/essence) to the thickest (cream/mask). This allows each formula to reach its target skin layer.
02
Press, Never Rub
Rubbing disrupts the temporary microbiome film created by the essence. Pressing with warm palms gently encourages absorption without friction damage.
03
Damp Skin is Optimal
Apply each step on skin that is still slightly damp from the previous step. This increases hyaluronic acid's ability to attract and lock in moisture from all subsequent layers.
04
Temperature Matters
Warming formulas between hands before application is not merely ritual — it briefly opens surface pores and activates certain fermented enzymes in the formula.

Expert Guidance

Tips from the Lab

Skin Sensitivity

Starting the Ritual with Sensitive Skin

If you have reactive skin, begin with the essence only for the first week. The fermented actives will begin recalibrating your microbiome. Introduce the serum in week two, the cream in week three. Patience mirrors the fermentation process.

Frequency

How Often Should I Use the Nocturne Mask?

Three times per week is optimal for most skin types. Using it nightly can over-saturate the microbiome environment. Think of it as an intensive treatment, not a daily moisturiser — your skin needs rest cycles between applications.

Travel & Storage

Caring for Living Formulas on the Move

All FLORA formulas are shelf-stable at room temperature but avoid prolonged exposure above 30°C. The live probiotic cultures in our essences are heat-sensitive. Store away from direct sunlight and never refrigerate below 10°C.

Combination with SPF

Layering FLORA with Sun Protection

After your AM barrier cream, wait 2 minutes before applying SPF 50. Choose a mineral sunscreen over chemical filters — they sit on top of the skin rather than absorbing through it, preserving the integrity of the fermented barrier layer.

The 90-Day Commitment

Trusting the Fermentation Timeline

Our formulas are fermented for 90 days precisely because profound biological transformation takes time. Your skin's response mirrors this. Texture improvements are typically visible at day 14. Full transformation is assessed at day 90.
