The Science of Fermentation

Ancient Wisdom.
Modern Precision.

Every FLORA formula begins in a hand-thrown ceramic vessel, where carefully curated botanical extracts undergo a 90-day lacto-fermentation process guided by Swiss biotech protocols. The result is a bioactive concentrate teeming with microbiome-supportive actives, at a molecular weight small enough to reach the dermis.

01
Wild Botanical Harvest
Seasonal ingredients sourced from Korean highlands and Swiss alpine meadows, hand-harvested at peak bioactivity.
02
Ceramic Vessel Inoculation
Each batch is placed in hand-thrown onggi vessels with heirloom lacto-cultures dating back three generations.
03
90-Day Controlled Fermentation
Temperature-regulated rooms maintain optimal microbial activity. Daily hand-stirring by our lab artisans.
04
Swiss Biotech Concentration
Cold-process extraction and nano-encapsulation technology ensures maximum bioavailability and skin penetration.

The Five-Step Ritual

A Living
Practice

The FLORA ritual is not a routine — it is a daily ceremony of intention. Each step activates the next, building a living ecosystem on your skin.

Step 01
Cleanse & Prepare
Low-pH gel cleanser removes impurities without disrupting your microbiome. Pat dry — never rub.
Step 02
First Ferment Essence
Press 6–8 drops into warm skin with both palms. Allow 60 seconds for full absorption.
Step 03
Deep Culture Serum
Two drops warmed between fingertips. Press into cheeks, forehead, and neck in upward sweeps.
