By Train
From Paris Gare de l'Est

TGV to Épernay (1h15). From Épernay station, take the D10 south toward Oger — 12km. Taxi available from the station. We can arrange private transfer upon request.

By Car
From Paris (A4)

A4 motorway direction Reims. Exit 22 toward Épernay. Follow N51 south to Épernay, then D10 south toward Oger. Total journey approximately 1h30 from central Paris. GPS: 48.9534°N, 3.9781°E.

By Air
From Charles de Gaulle (CDG)

CDG to Épernay: 1h15 by car. Private transfer can be arranged through our concierge service. Reims airport also available (LFSR) — 45min to Oger by car. Contact us for transfer arrangements.
