Nuit Étoilée

Vintage 2016 · Extra Brut · Blanc de Noirs
97
Wine Advocate
James Whitmore, 2023

Eight years in our deepest caves — the 2016 Nuit Étoilée is the culmination of an exceptional harvest and the extraordinary patience that defines Maison Éclat. Pinot Noir from our oldest parcels in the Montagne de Reims, aged on fine lees at 11°C until each molecule of complexity was fully extracted.

On the nose: charred brioche, smoked stone, blood orange, and wild strawberry compote. The palate reveals extraordinary density and precision — toasted almond, rose hip, dark cherry, and a mineral drive that extends the finish to over ninety seconds. Hauntingly long. Profoundly still.

Grape Varieties 100% Pinot Noir
Dosage 3 g/L Extra Brut
Ageing 8 years on lees
Production 3,200 bottles

The Art of Pairing

Each Éclat cuvée is designed not in isolation, but in conversation with the finest ingredients of French gastronomy.

◎

Beluga Caviar

Cuvée Lumière · Blanc de Blancs

The mineral precision of grand cru Chardonnay is the only match for the salinity and silk of Beluga. Two expressions of the same earth — one from the sea, one from chalk.

◎

Truffe Noire du Périgord

Les Craies · Perpétuel

The truffle's earthy depth meets the autolytic complexity of a champagne carrying decades of cave memory. An accord of extraordinary resonance and persistence.

◎

Homard Bleu Rôti

Nuit Étoilée · Blanc de Noirs

Blue lobster roasted in butter, tarragon, and sea salt. The richness demands the structured intensity of a premier blanc de noirs. Each amplifies the other exponentially.

◎

Fraises des Bois & Cream

Rêve Rosé · Saignée Brut

The wild strawberry and rose of our vintage rosé transforms the dessert course. A pairing of pure sensory romance — the culmination of a long and leisurely table.
