## Overview

A luxury multi-page portfolio template for a fine art and documentary photographer. Designed with a dark, contemplative aesthetic inspired by gallery spaces and analogue film photography.

## Pages

- **index.html** — Home: Parallax hero, Vision/About, Signature Series, Featured Work masonry grid, Awards & Press, Client List, Commission CTA
- **about.html** — Artist Statement, Philosophy (3-column grid), Exhibition History, Publications
- **collection.html** — Portfolio grid with series filter tabs, Fine Art Print CTA
- **process.html** — 4-phase creative process, Equipment guide, Location Scouting, Post-Production
- **contact.html** — Commission form, Studio info, Print Orders pricing, FAQ accordion

## Design System

- **Background**: #181818 (avg RGB 24 — above threshold)
