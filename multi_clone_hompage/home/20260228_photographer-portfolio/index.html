The Artist

Chasing light
through shadow

LUMIÈRE STUDIO is the practice of photographer Élise Marchand — a two-decade exploration of light, texture, and the fragile moments that define human experience. Working primarily with large-format film and analogue techniques, every image is a deliberate act of preservation.

From remote landscapes to intimate portraiture, the work occupies the space between documentation and fine art — gallery-exhibited internationally and collected by private institutions.
