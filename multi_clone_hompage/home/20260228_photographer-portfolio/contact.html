The Studio

Studio Information

I.
Print Consultations

Studio visits by appointment. See existing editions in their physical form before acquisition. We welcome private collectors, curators, and institutions. Paris studio, Tuesday through Friday.

II.
Commission Projects

New commissions are accepted selectively — typically four to six projects per year. Initial consultation is always free and without obligation. We prefer to meet in person when geography allows.

III.
Institutional Sales

LUMIÈRE works directly with museums, galleries, and corporate art collections. Institutional pricing and acquisition terms are available on request. Works can be loaned for exhibition.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a commission project typically take? +
Most landscape commissions require a minimum of three to six months from initial brief to final prints. This allows adequate time for location scouting, waiting for optimal conditions, developing the film, and producing the final edition. Rush commissions are not something we accept.
Are prints available to view before purchase? +
Yes. We strongly encourage viewing prints in person at the Paris studio before any purchase decision. Many editions are also held at our gallery representation — Galerie Karsten Greve — where they can be viewed during normal gallery hours.
What does the archival rating of 200 years mean? +
This is the Wilhelm Imaging Research projected lifespan under standard museum conditions (55% relative humidity, 65°F, away from UV light). The actual lifespan under ideal archival storage conditions may be considerably longer. The rating applies to selenium-toned fibre-base prints.
Do you offer payment plans for acquisitions? +
For acquisitions over €5,000, we are able to arrange a payment plan of up to six months with no additional charge. The print is delivered upon receipt of the final payment. Please mention this preference in your initial enquiry.
Can prints be loaned for exhibition? +
Selected works from the artist's personal archive are available for institutional loan. Enquiries from museums, galleries, and cultural institutions are welcome. A loan fee and full insurance arrangement are required. Contact us directly to discuss.
