Master Gilder — MOF 1994
Édouard Merillon
Founder & Master Gilder

Édouard Merillon was born in Lyon in 1960 into a family of decorative arts craftsmen. His grandfather gilded frames for the Lyonnais silk trade; his father restored painted furniture for Parisian auction houses. Gold was the material of his childhood.

In 1978 he entered the workshop of Maître Henri Dupont at the Ateliers Dupont, then the principal supplier of frame restoration services to the collections of the Musée du Louvre. He remained there twelve years, learning water gilding, oil gilding, conservation chemistry, and the preparation of traditional gessoes from raw chalk and rabbit-skin glue.

In 1989 he established AURUM in Paris's seventh arrondissement — the same quarter where the great dealers and restorers of the nineteenth century had maintained their workshops. The atelier has never moved.

In 1994 he was awarded the title Meilleur Ouvrier de France — Best Craftsman of France — in the gilding category, the highest distinction available to a French artisan. He remains one of eleven living gilders to hold the title.

He has taught gilding at the École Boulle since 1997 and has trained twelve apprentices, six of whom now lead their own ateliers. He divides his working week between the bench and the museums and private clients who rely on his judgement.

History

Milestones of the Atelier

1978
Apprenticeship
Édouard Merillon enters the Ateliers Dupont under Maître Henri Dupont, beginning twelve years of rigorous classical training.
1985
Louvre Commission
First independent contribution: water-gilding sixty-four frames from the reserve collection of the Musée du Louvre under Dupont's supervision.
1989
AURUM Founded
AURUM Atelier established at 12 Rue du Bac, Paris. The first commission: a suite of twenty gilded mirrors for a private residence in Geneva.
1994
MOF — Best Craftsman of France
Édouard Merillon awarded Meilleur Ouvrier de France in the gilding discipline. One of eleven living gilders to hold this distinction.

Appointed conservation partner for the Musée d'Orsay's gilded frame reserve — a mandate renewed continuously for over two decades.

Completed the gilded plasterwork of the main halls of two Baltic state opera houses — a combined 1,200 square metres of 23k water-gilded surface.
2015
Teaching at École Boulle
Appointed maître de stage at the École Boulle, Paris's foremost applied arts school. Annual intensive in traditional water gilding methodology.

35 Years — AURUM Anniversary
The atelier marks thirty-five years of operation with an exhibition of restored works at the Grand Palais Éphémère, Paris. Over 200 commissions completed.

Transmission

Teaching as Obligation

A craft that is not transmitted dies with its master. AURUM has trained twelve gilders since its founding. Apprenticeships are structured identically to the model Merillon received — three years of full immersion before independent work is permitted.

  • Three-year full-time apprenticeship, bench work from day one
  • First year: surface preparation only — gesso, bole, and polishing
  • Second year: oil gilding on prepared surfaces, then on raw wood
  • Third year: water gilding and burnishing, conservation practice
  • Annual week of teaching at the École Boulle, open application
  • Six apprentices now lead their own ateliers across Europe
