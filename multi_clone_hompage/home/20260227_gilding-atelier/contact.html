What Happens Next

The Commission Process

1
Enquiry

Submit this form or contact us directly. Include as much detail about your object as possible — photographs are especially helpful.

2
Assessment

We respond within five working days with a preliminary assessment — and, where required, arrange to examine the object in person at the atelier or your location.

3
Proposal

A detailed written proposal is provided: method, materials, timeline, and pricing. No work begins without your written approval of the proposal.

4
Commission

The work is carried out at the atelier, documented photographically at each stage. Completion is accompanied by a full treatment report and care instructions.

Discretion

Confidentiality as Standard

Many of our clients are institutions, collectors, or private individuals for whom discretion about the provenance and condition of their collections is of the highest importance. We operate under complete confidentiality.

We do not discuss our client list, individual commissions, or the condition of objects entrusted to us — except where the client has specifically authorised us to do so for publication or reference purposes. The testimonials on this site were provided with the express consent of the individuals named.

Commission enquiries, assessments, and all correspondence are treated as confidential and are retained only as long as necessary to fulfil the commission and comply with our obligations as a registered conservation facility.

Shipping & Insurance

Safe Transit for Precious Objects

Objects entrusted to AURUM for gilding or restoration are covered by our specialist fine art insurance from the moment of collection to the moment of return delivery. We work with a small number of trusted fine art carriers — not general couriers — who understand how to handle fragile and high-value objects.

For international shipments requiring customs documentation, we have extensive experience preparing the certificates, valuations, and technical descriptions required for the temporary import of cultural objects into France and for their re-export. We can advise on ATA Carnet procedures for objects from non-EU countries.

For objects of exceptional value or fragility, we recommend hand-carrying by one of our staff, accompanied by the appropriate courier insurance. This service is available to clients within Western Europe by arrangement.
