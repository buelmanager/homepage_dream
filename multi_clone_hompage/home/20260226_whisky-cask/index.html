The Journey

From Distillery Floor
to Your Private Label

I

Acquisition

We match you with a newly filled cask that aligns with your palate and investment horizon. Your name is legally registered on the cask certificate.

Year 0

II

Maturation

Your cask rests in a bonded Scottish warehouse under HMRC supervision. Annual tasting samples are dispatched to your door each spring.

Years 1 – 20

III

Bottling

When the time is right, you choose your bottling: private label design, cask strength or cut, personalised batch number, wax seal colour.

Your Choice

IV

Legacy

Gift bottles to family, sell at auction, trade within our private exchange, or simply open the finest whisky you have ever tasted — your own.
