Cask Terminology

Understanding Your Cask

Cask Type

The previous contents of the oak vessel. First-fill bourbon barrels, Oloroso sherry butts, port pipes, Madeira drums — each imparts distinct flavour characteristics.

ABV (Cask Strength)

Alcohol by volume at the time of sampling or bottling, before dilution. Cask strength whiskies are valued at a premium, typically bottled between 52–68%.

Outturn

The estimated number of bottles produceable from a given cask, calculated from volume, density, and ABV. Outturn decreases as whisky evaporates — the "angel's share."

Dunnage Warehouse

The traditional earth-floored, low-ceilinged stone warehouse considered optimal for gentle, consistent maturation. Contrasted with modern racked or palletised warehouses.
