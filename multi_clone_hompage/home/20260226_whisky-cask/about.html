Our Story

About Cask Société

Founded 1987

Thirty Years of
Patient Excellence

CASK SOCIÉTÉ was born in a draughty Edinburgh study in 1987, when former diplomat and whisky obsessive Sir Alastair Drummond-Fettes acquired his first personally named cask from a Speyside distillery whose Master Distiller happened to be his cousin.

What began as a private gentleman's arrangement evolved — slowly, deliberately, in the manner of good whisky — into a structured programme by 2012, when Sir Alastair formalised partnerships with twelve distilleries and opened membership to a carefully vetted circle of investors and collectors.

Today, CASK SOCIÉTÉ manages over £38 million in cask assets across 47 distilleries, with 280 active members holding personally named casks ranging from three-year fillings to 25-year veterans.

  • 1987 Sir Alastair's first personally named Glennach sherry butt — the seed of what would become CASK SOCIÉTÉ.
  • 1999 First private tasting event for twelve invited guests. Three casks auctioned at Christie's for 4× acquisition price.
  • 2012 Formal programme established. Twelve distillery partnerships signed. Forty founding members accepted.
  • 2018 200th member milestone. £12M in member cask assets under management. HMRC wasting-asset ruling confirmed.
  • 2024 47 distillery partners. 280 members. £38M in cask assets. Expansion into Irish and Japanese expression advisory.

Our Philosophy

Four Principles
of the Cask

We are neither a fund, nor a wine merchant, nor a broker. We are a programme — a long-term partnership between those who make whisky and those who wish to own it, guided by four principles that have shaped every decision since 1987.

I

Provenance First

Every cask we source carries a named distillery, a dated spirit safe record, and an HMRC-bonded warehouse receipt. No blended stocks. No speculative parcels. Only traceable, verifiable whisky.

II

Patient Capital

We do not encourage short-term flipping. The finest whisky requires time. Our members enter with a minimum 10-year horizon and are counselled to hold for 15–25 years where possible.

III

Annual Transparency

Every December, members receive a full cask audit: current weight gauge, estimated outturn, an independent valuation, and a 50ml sample drawn directly from their own cask.

IV

Exit Freedom

Three exit routes are always open: private labelled bottling, listing on our member auction platform, or trade sale at independent valuation. We never hold members to a single path.

Our Partners

Three of Our
Founding Distilleries

From forty-seven partners, three stand as founding pillars of the programme — each representing a distinct Scottish whisky tradition.

Glennach Distillery
Speyside — Est. 1832

The original CASK SOCIÉTÉ partner. Glennach's five-decade relationship with González Byass yields Oloroso butts of extraordinary quality. A distillery without a modern still in sight — entirely direct-fired copper pot stills, worm tub condensers.

Caol Rùm
Islay — Est. 1843

The most heavily peated of our partners, producing spirit at 45–55 PPM phenol. Caol Rùm's casks develop a unique maritime peat character that has attracted the highest auction premiums of any CASK SOCIÉTÉ holding.

Lochaber Malt
West Highland — Est. 1872

Port Pipe specialists since 1951. Lochaber's relationship with Portuguese wine merchants produces finishing casks of unmatched provenance. Their 25-year port-finished expressions consistently achieve 6× acquisition value at auction.
