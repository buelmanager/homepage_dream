Distillery Access

Visits & Expeditions

Available to Investor and Connoisseur members. Warehouse visits and full distillery expedition programmes — all arranged by CASK SOCIÉTÉ at no additional cost.

Investor & Connoisseur
Warehouse Visit

Visit your cask in its bonded warehouse. Stand beside it. Hear it breathe. Receive a 50ml sample drawn directly from your specific cask at the time of your visit. Guided by our warehousing partner.

Duration: Half Day   Frequency: Up to 2× per year

Connoisseur
Distillery Expedition

An immersive two-day distillery experience — production floor access, mash tun, washbacks, still room, cask filling, and a private dinner with the Master Distiller. Limited to four guests per year.

Duration: 2 Days   Availability: 4 guests annually

All Members
Annual Member Tasting

Our annual gathering in Edinburgh — an evening of tasting from member casks, a keynote address from a renowned Master Blender, and informal introductions across our membership circle.

Location: Edinburgh   Frequency: Annual — October

Corporate
Private Corporate Tasting

A curated whisky tasting experience for up to 20 guests — hosted in Edinburgh or at your premises. Guided by one of our WSET-qualified advisors with four to six CASK SOCIÉTÉ member cask expressions.

Duration: 2.5 Hours   Guests: Up to 20
