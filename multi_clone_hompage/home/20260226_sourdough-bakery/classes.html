From First Loaf
to Fine Artisan

Every class takes place in our stone-floored atelier on Rue du Faubourg Saint-Antoine. Students work with heritage grain, our living starter, and the same tools our bakers use each morning. You leave with everything you baked and a culture of your own.

Beginner
Introduction to Sourdough
Your first encounter with wild fermentation. You will create and feed your own starter, understand the chemistry of levain, mix and fold your first dough, and bake a country loaf to take home. No experience required — only patience and curiosity.

Heritage Grains Workshop
A deep exploration of ancient wheat varieties. You will mill your own flour on our stone mill, develop hydration intuition for low-gluten grains, and bake two heritage loaves — an einkorn bâtard and a spelt levain.
