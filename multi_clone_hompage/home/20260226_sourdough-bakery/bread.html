How We Bake

The Five Rituals

Every loaf follows the same unhurried sequence. There are no shortcuts, no accelerants, and no compromise at any stage.

01
Mill
Heritage grain arrives whole. We mill it on-site each morning, releasing the germ oils that commodity flour long since lost.
02
Feed Henri
Our 30-year-old starter is fed at 6am with fresh-milled flour and spring water. He doubles by 10am and is ready to leaven.
03
Mix & Fold
Dough is mixed by hand and folded every 30 minutes for 4 hours to build structure — no mechanical kneading, ever.
04
Shape & Rest
Loaves are shaped by hand and placed in linen-lined baskets for 48 hours of cold fermentation at 4°C.
05
Score & Bake
Cold dough is scored with a razor lame and baked at 260°C on volcanic stone with steam. The ear blooms. The crust sings.
Ingredients & Nutrition

What Goes In

Our country sourdough contains four ingredients: heritage wheat flour, water, salt, and living starter. That is all. Long fermentation pre-digests the gluten and lowers the glycaemic impact.
