How It Works

Simple,
Reliable Collection

Order Thursday Night

Orders placed by 23:59 Thursday are confirmed for Saturday morning collection. You will receive an email confirmation within 24 hours.

Saturday Collection

Collect between 07:00 and 10:00. Your loaf is reserved under your name. Come early — the bakery fills with the smell of fresh crust from 07:00 and the atmosphere is worth lingering in.

Payment on Collection

We accept card and cash at the counter. We do not process payment online — the relationship between baker and customer is still something we want to happen in person.

Delivery (Subscription Only)

Home delivery is available exclusively to Maison subscription members within the 11th and 12th arrondissements. Delivered between 07:30 and 09:30 every Saturday.
