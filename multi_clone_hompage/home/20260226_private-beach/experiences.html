01 — The Beach

3km of Undisturbed
White Sand

The beach at AZURA is never shared with strangers. Every inch of sand, every glint of the Mediterranean horizon belongs exclusively to our members and their guests.

Private Cabana Service

Your dedicated cabana host arrives before you do. Fresh chilled towels, a curated menu for the day, and your preferred ice bucket are set before you step onto the sand.

From sunscreen application rituals to handcrafted cocktails at the shore break, nothing is left to chance.

  • Personal beach butler from 8:00am
  • Complimentary water and cold press juices
  • Chilled towels and seasonal fruit service
  • Private outdoor shower at each cabana

The Water

The cove's natural formation creates one of the Mediterranean's most sheltered and transparent bodies of water. A snorkel reveals a posidonia meadow carpeting the sea floor in vivid green.

Water quality is independently tested weekly and publicly published on the members portal. Transparency, literally.

  • Average clarity: 30m visibility
  • Water temp 22–28°C (May to October)
  • Protected posidonia marine ecosystem
  • Zero motorised craft in the swimming zone

Command the Sea

03 — Spa & Wellness

Rituals at the
Edge of the Sea

The AZURA Wellness Pavilion is built into the limestone cliff above the main cove. Treatment rooms open to the sea. All therapies use locally sourced botanicals and marine minerals.

90 min

Mediterranean Thalasso Ritual

Warm sea water immersion followed by a seaweed exfoliation and warm island-stone massage. The signature treatment of the AZURA spa, performed only at this location.

60 min

Sunrise Yoga — Sea Platform

Dawn practice on AZURA's cantilevered sea platform, led by resident yogi Marta Soto. Limited to 8 guests per session. Reserve on arrival or via the Members Portal.

75 min

Cliff-Edge Hot Stone Massage

Basalt stones heated in sea water and applied with long Mediterranean oil strokes while the sound of the cove provides the score. Treatment rooms fully open to the horizon.

45 min

Salt Cave Halotherapy

Micrograded Mediterranean salt crystal session in the purpose-built salt cave adjacent to the spa. Exceptional for respiratory clarity and skin rejuvenation.

04 — Fine Dining

Marea — Two Stars,
One Horizon

Chef Alain Dumas earned his second Michelin star at Marea in 2022 — the first beach-side restaurant in the Balearic region to achieve the honour. Lunch and dinner service, plus cabana delivery for all day-parts.

Marea

The principal dining experience — open for lunch and dinner, seven days

The Tasting Menu

Nine courses. Nightly service from 7pm.

Cabana Service

Full Marea menu delivered to your shore.

Provenance

Every ingredient is sourced within 200km of the island. Fish is landed daily from AZURA's own longline fishing vessel. Vegetables and herbs grow in the cliff-side kitchen garden.

The Wine Cellar

Over 800 labels curated by Sommelier Clara Vidal, with a focus on organic Iberian and natural Mediterranean producers. A private cellar reserve is available to Estate members.

Private Events

Marea can be reserved exclusively for private dinners of 12 to 40 guests. Chef Dumas offers bespoke tasting menu design for milestone celebrations and corporate gatherings.
