Our Origin

Founded on the Belief That Privacy is the Ultimate Luxury

"The sea does not require an audience — it simply is. So too, should be your time within it."

AZURA was conceived in 2009 when shipping magnate and conservationist Henri-Louis Marchetti acquired a small, uninhabited limestone island off the Mediterranean coast. His vision was radical in its simplicity: preserve the island entirely, and invite only those who would cherish it as he did.

By 2012, the club's founding 22 members had been invited by personal letter. There was no website, no advertisement, no application process. Today, that same philosophy governs every element of AZURA — one does not find the club. The club finds you.

The island remains 94% in its natural state. The 47 cabanas and two dining venues occupy a carefully considered 6%, designed by architect Rosario Vilà with materials quarried from the island itself.

Location

The island is situated 28 nautical miles south-southeast of the Balearic archipelago — accessible exclusively by private vessel or helicopter from our departure points in Ibiza and Palma de Mallorca.

The Bay

A natural limestone amphitheatre shields the main cove from prevailing winds, creating an exceptionally calm body of water with visibility exceeding 30 metres. Water temperature averages 26°C from June to October.

Flora & Fauna

The island is home to 47 endemic plant species, nesting Eleonora's falcons, and a thriving posidonia meadow — a protected marine ecosystem visible from the shore.

Conservation

AZURA operates under a strict Environmental Management Charter. Solar energy provides 100% of club power. All water is desalinated on-site. Plastic is prohibited island-wide.

Our Philosophy

The Three Principles of AZURA

I
Absolute Privacy

The island cannot be photographed from sea or air without permission. No social media is published by the club. What happens within AZURA stays within AZURA — forever.

II
Zero Compromise

Every staff member trains for a minimum of six months before serving a guest. Every ingredient is sourced within 200km. Every detail is considered before it reaches you.

III
Living Conservation

Membership includes a personal stake in the island's conservation endowment. Members vote on annual environmental initiatives. The sea you enjoy today will exist for your grandchildren.
