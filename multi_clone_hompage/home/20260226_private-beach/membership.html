The Circle

An Invitation, Not a Transaction

AZURA does not sell memberships in the conventional sense. The club exists because a small group of like-minded individuals chose to share something they held precious. That spirit has never changed.

Each year, we open a limited number of new positions across our three membership tiers. Applications are reviewed by a standing committee of existing members. What we look for is not a balance sheet — it is a shared conviction that some places deserve to be kept extraordinary.

We invite you to apply. We will be in touch.

Application Process

How to Join AZURA

The application process is deliberately unhurried. We are not building a club — we are preserving one.

01
Submit Enquiry

Complete the application form below or write to our membership director directly. Tell us how you came to hear of AZURA and what draws you to the island.

02
Initial Review

Our membership committee reviews all applications within 14 business days. We may reach out for a brief conversation to learn more about you.

03
Member Endorsement

Successful applicants are invited to the island for an introduction visit, hosted by an existing member who has volunteered to serve as sponsor.

04
Welcome to AZURA

Upon acceptance, you will receive your member's welcome package and be granted access to the Members Portal. The sea is now yours.

Application

Begin Your Journey

All applications are treated with the utmost discretion. Your information is shared only with the AZURA membership committee and will never be disclosed to third parties.

Questions Before You Apply?

Our membership team is available to answer questions by email or telephone. We are happy to arrange a confidential call to discuss the club, the island, and what membership at AZURA involves.

We respond to all enquiries within two business days.
