Membership Enquiries

For membership applications and tier questions, please contact our Membership Director Inés Ramos Vilà directly at membership@azura-island.com or complete the full application at our Membership page.

Day Visit Requests

Day visits to the island are available exclusively for guests of current members. All day visit requests must be sponsored by an active member. Contact us with your sponsor's name and we will coordinate directly.

Press & Media

AZURA does not engage with media requests for photography or editorial visits. We do not accept sponsored content arrangements. For factual club information, write to press@azura-island.com.

Discretion Policy

All enquiries are treated as confidential. Your information is not shared with third parties, is not used for marketing, and is held only for the duration required to address your enquiry.

Getting Here

Island Access

The island is accessible only by private vessel or helicopter. We do not publish its precise coordinates. Upon confirmation of your visit, our team provides detailed transfer instructions through the Members Portal.

By Private Vessel

Our dedicated island tender service operates from two departure points. AZURA provides mooring coordination and assistance with berthing for privately owned vessels.

  • Ibiza Town Marina — 38nm south, approximately 2h under power
  • Palma de Mallorca — 48nm, approximately 2.5h
  • AZURA tender service: 7 days, 08:00 – 20:00
  • Private mooring coordination available

By Helicopter

Azura and Estate members have access to AZURA's helicopter transfer service operated in partnership with a licensed Balearic charter operator. Transfers are subject to 48-hour advance booking via the Members Portal.

  • Ibiza Airport — Transfer time: 22 minutes
  • Palma Airport — Transfer time: 35 minutes
  • Maximum 6 passengers per flight
  • Baggage limit: 20kg per passenger

Arrival on the Island

Upon arrival at the island jetty, your personal concierge will greet you and escort you directly to your cabana. Check-in formalities are completed before arrival using the Members Portal.

  • Cabana ready from 09:00 daily
  • No queues, no lobbies, no formality
  • Luggage taken directly to cabana
  • Departure transfers pre-arranged

What to Bring

Very little. AZURA provides everything from premium suncare to snorkelling equipment, watersports gear, dining wardrobe guidance, and all day-use amenities.

  • Smart-casual dress for evening dining
  • All beach equipment provided
  • Electronic devices: use at your discretion
  • Photography: personal use only, no shared media
Day Visits

Experience AZURA for a Day

Day visits are reserved for prospective members and guests of existing members. They provide an opportunity to experience the island, the cove, and the cabana service before committing to a membership application.

Day visit availability is limited to six new guests per week. Priority is given to those already in the application process. Your sponsoring member should make introductions directly.

To register your interest in a day visit, please submit an enquiry using the form above, selecting "Day Visit Request" and including the name of your AZURA member contact.
