Heritage & Philosophy

Ancient Practice,
Uncompromised

GOLDEN HIVE traces its lineage to 1887, when founder Edmund Ashwell established the first treatment-free apiary in the Bay of Plenty. Four generations later, we manage fewer than 300 hives across our global partner apiaries — each one chosen for ecological remoteness and botanical singularity.

We refuse supplemental feeding, synthetic treatments, and queen replacement schedules. Our bees follow the season; our harvests follow the bees. The hive dictates the yield — never the market.

Treatment-Free
Zero synthetic inputs — no oxalic acid, no antibiotics, no miticides. Natural colony strength only.
Single-Source
Each product originates from one apiary, one botanical species, one harvest season.
Cold-Pressed
Never heated above 35°C — the hive's natural temperature — preserving full enzyme activity.
Numbered Jars
Every jar bears its harvest number, hive identifier, extraction date, and beekeeper initials.

Standards & Certification

Verified at
Every Step

UMF Certified
Independently tested by the UMF Honey Association. Every Mānuka batch verified for leptosperin, DHA, and methylglyoxal content.
Certified Organic
All products certified to EU 834/2007 and NZ Ministry of Primary Industries organic standards. Annual third-party audits of every apiary site.
Raw & Unfiltered
Cold-pressed below 35°C. No ultrafiltration, no pasteurisation. Pollen, propolis, and enzyme content preserved exactly as nature intended.
Numbered & Traced
Blockchain-verified provenance for every jar. Scan your label to access the full harvest record — hive, beekeeper, date, and independent lab results.
