How We Work

5-PHASE
WORKFLOW

Every project follows a rigorous five-phase process designed for clarity, collaboration, and creative precision. No surprises — just great motion.

01
BRIEF & DISCOVERY

Deep-dive sessions to understand your brand, audience, goals, and existing visual language. We ask the hard questions early so nothing is left to chance in production. This phase produces a creative brief document that aligns all stakeholders before a single frame is animated.

2–3 days
Deliverables
Creative Brief Document
Brand Motion Audit
Competitor Analysis
Project Scope & Timeline
Kickoff Meeting
02
CONCEPTING

Moodboards, reference reels, and styleframe explorations to establish visual direction. Typically 2–3 distinct creative directions are presented for review. Motion tests and timing studies help clients feel the energy and rhythm before full production begins.

3–5 days
Deliverables
2–3 Creative Directions
Moodboards
Styleframes (5–10 key frames)
Motion Test Clips (5–15s)
Direction Approval
03
STORYBOARD

Once direction is approved, we map out the complete animation sequence frame by frame. Storyboards define every transition, timing, and motion beat — creating a visual roadmap that eliminates ambiguity and keeps production on track.

2–4 days
Deliverables
Full Storyboard Panels
Timing Annotations
Motion Notes per Scene
Audio Sync Points (if applicable)
Storyboard Approval
04
ANIMATION

Full production begins. Working iteratively with milestone reviews at 25%, 50%, and 75% completion. Two rounds of revisions are included — additional rounds available at a day rate. Sound design and music clearances handled in this phase if required.

1–3 weeks
Deliverables
Milestone Reviews (3x)
2 Revision Rounds
Draft Exports (H.264)
Sound Design (optional)
Final Approval
05
DELIVERY

Final export in all required formats with complete project file handover. Motion guidelines document included for ongoing brand use. 30-day post-delivery support window for technical questions and minor tweaks.

1–2 days
Deliverables
All Final Format Exports
Source Project Files
Lottie/JSON (UI projects)
Motion Guidelines PDF
30-Day Support Window
Technical Philosophy

HOW WE
BUILD MOTION

△
EASING CURVES

Custom bezier curves for every animation type. We never use default linear or ease-in/out — every motion is calibrated to feel physically plausible and emotionally appropriate.

■
TIMING SYSTEMS

All animations derived from a base tempo unit (typically 12 or 24 frames). This creates rhythmic consistency across a project — animations feel orchestrated, not arbitrary.

◆
RENDER PIPELINES

GPU-accelerated rendering with Octane or Redshift for 3D. Multi-pass compositing in After Effects ensures maximum quality and flexibility in post-production.

◯
MODULAR SYSTEMS

All deliverables are built modularly — every element can be recombined, resized, or extended by your internal team without needing custom code or specialized software knowledge.

■
LOTTIE PIPELINE

After Effects animations exported via Bodymovin for web and mobile integration. Tested across browsers and iOS/Android before delivery — not just "it looks right in AE".

△
COLOR SCIENCE

All projects worked in ACEScg or Rec.709 depending on delivery target. Final color graded to match your brand guidelines with Delta-E tolerances documented.
