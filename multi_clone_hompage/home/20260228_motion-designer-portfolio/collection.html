Featured Case Study

APEX BRAND
IDENTITY SYSTEM

APEX came to KINETIC needing a complete motion identity system for their rebrand — a tech consultancy positioning themselves as the future of enterprise AI. The challenge: create a motion language that felt simultaneously cutting-edge and trustworthy.

We developed a modular animation system built around three core principles: precision (tight timing, mathematical easing curves), momentum (kinetic energy that suggests forward motion), and clarity (no gratuitous complexity — every frame serves the message).

The deliverables included a 3D logo animation suite, transition library with 40+ variants, social media templates with auto-animation, and a 60-page motion guidelines document for the internal team.
