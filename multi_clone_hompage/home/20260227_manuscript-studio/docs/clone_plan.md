# Clone Plan — SCRIPTORIUM Manuscript Studio

**Template:** 20260227_manuscript-studio
**Date:** 2026-02-27
**Category:** Luxury Fine Art / Medieval Craft

## Concept

A fictional luxury medieval manuscript illumination studio based in rural Oxfordshire, England. The studio specialises in hand-produced manuscripts on calf vellum using authentic historical methods: iron-gall ink, quill pens, mineral pigments, and raised gold leaf.

The brand identity is grounded in monastic gravitas and literary luxury — the kind of unhurried, permanent artistry that defined the great scriptoria of medieval Europe.

## Reference Sources

The site does not clone any specific real-world studio. Conceptual inspiration was drawn from:
- The Society of Scribes and Illuminators (SSI)
- The Lindisfarne Scriptorium tradition
- Academic literature on medieval manuscript production (de Hamel, Backhouse, Brown)
- General luxury atelier site conventions

## Hero Strategy (Type G)

The hero occupies 220vh with a sticky inner panel. As the user scrolls:
1. Words "SCRIPTO" and "RIUM" scale outward and spread apart
2. Letter spacing expands from -0.01em to 0.34em
3. Tagline and CTA fade out in the first 20% of scroll
4. Background image parallaxes upward

This creates a dramatic, text-first entrance that pays homage to the importance of lettering in medieval culture.

## Differentiation

- Highly specific medieval vocabulary throughout (vellum, gesso, lunellum, quire, quill, gum ammoniac, agate burnisher)
- Process section describes actual historical illumination techniques with authentic detail
- Master illuminators have plausible training institutions (Sint-Andries, Hochschule Leipzig, NCAD Dublin, Scuola del Libro di Urbino)
- Testimonials use European patrons with appropriate registers of speech
- No anachronistic references to digital tools in brand voice
