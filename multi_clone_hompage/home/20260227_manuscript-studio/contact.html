Before You Write

You may find it helpful to review our commission types and process pages before enquiring. The more specific your vision, the richer our initial conversation will be.

"A letter sent to the Scriptorium is the beginning of a conversation that may last two years and produce an object that lasts two centuries."

We do not regard commission enquiries as transactions. They are the opening of a working relationship between patron and illuminator that the medieval tradition regarded as one of the most sacred forms of human collaboration. Write to us as you would write to someone whose time and craft you respect. We shall respond in kind.

◈
Confidentiality

All commission details are held in strict confidence. We do not photograph or publish work without explicit written permission from the commissioning patron.

◈
International Patrons

We ship worldwide in bespoke archival packaging and have successfully delivered commissions to patrons in the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, and East Asia. Customs documentation is handled by the studio.

◈
Institutional Commissions

We welcome enquiries from libraries, museums, universities, ecclesiastical institutions, and private foundations. Institutional commissions are priced and structured individually.
