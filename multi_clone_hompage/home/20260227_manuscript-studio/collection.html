Our Work

Commissions &
Illuminations

Each Scriptorium commission is unique. We work with you to create a manuscript artefact that will be meaningful across generations — not a reproduction, but an original work in the medieval tradition.

"Not the painter's art, nor the sculptor's, nor the jeweller's — it is the illuminator alone who binds word and image, text and gold, meaning and beauty into one inseparable whole."

Below you will find the four principal forms of commission we accept. Each can be adapted substantially: scripts, languages, dimensions, binding styles, and iconographic programmes are all tailored to your requirements. We also accept commissions for works that do not fit these categories; contact us to discuss.

Signature Commission — I

Book of Hours

The Book of Hours was the most personal and visually elaborate manuscript of the Middle Ages — a devotional book structured around the canonical hours of prayer, adorned with calendar illuminations, full-page miniatures, and border sequences of astonishing intricacy. Each Scriptorium Book of Hours is a complete work: it contains the Hours of the Virgin, calendar miniatures for all twelve months, litany, suffrages, and penitential psalms, all on calf vellum with raised gold and mineral pigments.

  • Vellum Calf skin, scraped and prepared in-house
  • Script Gothic Textura Quadrata or Carolingian Minuscule
  • Gold Raised gesso gilding, 23¾ carat gold leaf, burnished
  • Pigments Lapis lazuli, vermilion, malachite, lead white
  • Binding Oak boards, leather covered, clasp binding
  • Timeline 18–30 months from commission to delivery

Heirloom Commission — II

Wedding Manuscript

Your vows, your poem, your chosen scripture — rendered in iron-gall ink on single-sheet calf vellum and illuminated with a historiated initial incorporating portrait medallions of the betrothed. The borders are drawn from the Flemish and English traditions of the fifteenth century: inhabited by grotesques, flowers, and heraldic devices. A wedding manuscript from Scriptorium is not a keepsake — it is an heirloom object intended to survive multiple generations.

  • Format Single folio or bound booklet of up to 8 pages
  • Script Client's choice from our full range of 23 scripts
  • Gold Flat or raised gilding on the opening initial
  • Border Flemish acanthus, English bar border, or Insular interlace
  • Language Any Latin-script language; Latin and Greek welcome
  • Timeline 8–16 months from commission to delivery

Heraldic Commission — III

Family Crest &
Genealogy

Armorial bearings researched, drawn, and blazoned according to the correct laws of heraldry, painted in traditional tinctures with gold-raised quarterings. We trace your lineage from contemporary records back as far as documentation allows, then set the genealogical text in a formal hand alongside the arms. Where heraldic entitlement does not exist, we can create a new grant of arms in consultation with an appropriate heraldic authority.

  • Research Genealogical research included up to 6 generations
  • Format Single large folio, rolled or framed presentation
  • Arms Painted in heraldic tinctures, gold-leafed
  • Text Genealogical record in formal heraldic hand
  • Extras Achievement of arms with helm, crest, mantling, motto
  • Timeline 10–18 months from commission to delivery

Literary Commission — IV

Illuminated
Poem Book

A beloved poem, sonnet sequence, scripture passage, or personal text — transcribed on vellum or high-quality cotton rag paper, opened by an illuminated initial of your chosen style, and adorned throughout with hand-painted foliate borders. This is the most accessible of our commissions and the most intimate: many patrons choose a poem that marks a particular friendship, loss, or joy. The result is a small bound volume that repays daily handling.

  • Format Small bound booklet, 4–20 pages on vellum or rag
  • Script Italic, Uncial, Gothic, or Carolingian
  • Illumination Historiated or decorated initial, foliate borders
  • Gold Flat shell gold or raised leaf on the initial
  • Binding Limp leather or flexible vellum cover
  • Timeline 6–10 months from commission to delivery
Enquire — Poem Book
