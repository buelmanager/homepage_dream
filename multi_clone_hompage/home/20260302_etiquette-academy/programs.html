The Art of Graceful Living

The Graces Academy has been teaching social etiquette and protocol to distinguished students from across the world since 1971. Our programmes are held in authentic luxury environments in London, Geneva, and Singapore.

Whether you are preparing for a diplomatic posting, senior corporate role, or simply wish to move with greater ease through formal environments, The Graces provides the knowledge, practice, and personal coaching to build genuine confidence.
