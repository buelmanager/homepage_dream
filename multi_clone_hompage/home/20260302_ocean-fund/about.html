Our Manifesto
"The ocean does not care for borders, politics, or quarterly reports. It operates on geological time, responds to chemical balances, and sustains half the oxygen we breathe. Our obligation to it is unconditional."
Leadership

Scientists Who
Go to Sea

Pelagic Ocean Fund was founded by marine biologists who had spent decades watching coral reefs bleach, seagrass meadows disappear, and plastic accumulate in the deepest ocean trenches. They founded Pelagic not as an advocacy organisation, but as an action fund: a body designed to fund, conduct, and scale direct restoration.

Our board includes oceanographers, environmental lawyers, coastal economists, and indigenous sea-rights advocates. Governance is transparent, science is peer-reviewed, and every financial decision is published annually.

Dr. Mara Seabright
Executive Director, Pelagic Ocean Fund
Core Values

What Drives Us

Evidence-Based Action
We fund only what the science supports. Every initiative is preceded by marine health assessment and followed by rigorous outcome monitoring.
Open Science
All data collected by Pelagic researchers is published in open-access journals. The ocean's condition is everyone's problem and everyone deserves the data.
Coastal Equity
Communities whose livelihoods depend on the sea have the most at stake. We design programmes with them, not for them.
History

Eighteen Years
of Ocean Work

2008
Pelagic Founded
Established in Lisbon by a team of marine scientists with seed funding from three ocean-focused philanthropic foundations.
2011
First Field Operations
Launch of Ocean Sweep pilot programme in the North Atlantic, removing 47 tonnes of plastic in the first operational season.
2013
Azaran Marine Sanctuary
Successful designation of Pelagic's first marine protected area — the Azaran Sanctuary — covering 240,000 km² of ocean.
2018
1,000 Tonnes Milestone
Cumulative plastic removal reached 1,000 verified tonnes. Blue Sanctuary Network expanded to 20 protected areas.
2024
47 Sanctuaries, 22 Nations
Global expansion of the Blue Sanctuary Network, with 3,200 cumulative tonnes of plastic removed and 680 reef zones actively restored.
