Strategic Programmes

Initiatives Shaping the Future

Beyond our grants programmes, the Council leads strategic initiatives that address structural challenges facing the arts sector — from workforce diversity to digital transformation.

Current Programmes

Strategic Initiatives

01
Let's Create

Our 10-year strategy for the arts, culture and creativity — committing to a country where the creativity of each person is valued, connected and supported. Focus on access, education and cultural infrastructure outside major cities.

02
Freelance Futures

A programme dedicated to supporting the 190,000 self-employed arts professionals in the country — providing professional development, legal support and mental health resources.

03
Creative Case for Diversity

A long-term commitment to changing who makes art, who presents it and who attends. All funded organisations must demonstrate a credible plan for diversity and inclusion.

04
Digital Arts Fund

£30 million dedicated to supporting artists and organisations working with digital technologies — from interactive installation to AI-generated performance and online commissioning.

International Work

Global Partnerships

British Council

Joint international touring fund supporting British artists presenting work in 80+ countries annually.

European Cultural Foundation

Collaborative grant programmes with 14 European funding bodies for transnational artistic exchange.

Arts Council Australia

Bilateral residency exchange programme for mid-career artists between the UK and Australia.

UNESCO Creative Cities Network

Supporting UK cities in the UNESCO network to leverage cultural designation for community development.

Global Fund for Women in Arts

Co-funding for women artists from the Global South, enabling 60 residencies and commissions per year.

IFACCA

Membership of the International Federation of Arts Councils, contributing to global policy frameworks.
