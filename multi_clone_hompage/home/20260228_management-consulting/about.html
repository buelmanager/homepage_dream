About Meridiem

Founded in 1995, Meridiem Strategy Partners has grown from a boutique New York advisory to one of the world's most respected management consulting firms — trusted by Fortune 500 leaders and ambitious growth companies alike.

Our Story

Built on conviction

Meridiem was founded on a single conviction: that the best consulting isn't about delivering reports, it's about delivering results. Three decades later, that conviction has shaped everything we do.

1995 Founded in New York by Eleanor Hartwell and David Meridiem. First client: a Fortune 100 industrial conglomerate undergoing strategic restructuring.
2003 Opened London and Singapore offices. Expanded into financial services and healthcare sectors. Grew to 200 consultants.
2011 Launched Digital & Technology practice. Pioneered data-analytics-driven strategy consulting. 500 consultants across 20 countries.
2018 Reached $1B in annual revenue. Named Top 10 Global Management Consulting Firm by Vault. Opened 35th global office in Nairobi.
2026 1,200 consultants across 40 countries. Launching AI-strategy practice. 98% client retention rate. 30 years of transforming organisations.

What We Stand For

Our core values

Four principles guide every engagement, every hire, every decision we make as a firm.

I

Intellectual Courage

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. Honest, evidence-based counsel — even when it challenges prevailing wisdom — is the foundation of true partnership.

R

Relentless Rigour

We are obsessive about analytical quality. Every insight is stress-tested. Every recommendation is grounded in evidence. We never sacrifice rigour for speed.

E

Enduring Impact

We measure success not by the thickness of our deliverables but by the lasting performance improvement we create. Sustainable value is our north star.

I

Inclusive Partnership

The best solutions emerge from genuine collaboration. We bring diverse perspectives — global and local, sector-specific and cross-industry — to every engagement.
