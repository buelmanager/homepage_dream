Within 24 hours for all business enquiries, Monday through Friday. Our global switchboard is staffed 24/7 for urgent matters. For non-urgent enquiries submitted on weekends, expect a response first thing Monday morning.
A 60-minute conversation — no PowerPoint, no pitch. We listen to your context, ask probing questions to understand the real challenge, and share honest perspectives on whether and how Meridiem can help. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you.
Primarily Fortune 500 and FTSE 100 corporations, private equity portfolio companies, and ambitious growth businesses seeking institutional-quality strategic counsel. Minimum engagement size is typically $500K, though this varies by scope and geography.
Diagnostic and strategy phases typically run 6–12 weeks. Full transformation programmes including implementation support range from 12 to 36 months. We are also engaged for shorter, highly-focused advisory mandates of 4–8 weeks where scope is clearly defined.
We offer fixed-fee, time-and-materials, and increasingly, outcome-linked fee structures. We are transparent about pricing from our first conversation and never introduce surprise costs. For transformation engagements, we actively seek to tie a portion of our fees to delivered outcomes.
