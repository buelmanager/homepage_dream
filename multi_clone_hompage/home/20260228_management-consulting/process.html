Our Methodology

Four phases.
Lasting results.

The Meridiem Method is not a rigid framework — it is a discipline. We adapt the pace, depth, and sequence to your context while maintaining unwavering analytical standards throughout.

01
Phase One

Diagnose

We begin every engagement with an uncompromising assessment of reality. Using 200+ proprietary data sources, competitive benchmarks, and structured stakeholder dialogue, we rapidly identify root causes — not symptoms — and expose the most valuable opportunities and critical risks.

  • Rapid organisational and market assessment (weeks 1–3)
  • Proprietary data collection across 200+ benchmarks
  • Structured leadership interviews and team diagnostics
  • Identification of value-creation and risk priorities
  • Hypothesis-driven problem framing
Weeks 1–4
Primary Output
"A clear-eyed picture of where you are, why it matters, and where the greatest value lies."
02
Phase Two

Design

We co-create the transformation blueprint with your leadership team — not for them. Working in intensive design sprints, we pressure-test strategic options against real-world constraints, financial models, and implementation realities before committing to a path.

  • Strategic option generation and structured evaluation
  • Financial modelling and scenario analysis
  • Implementation feasibility and risk assessment
  • Leadership alignment workshops
  • Board-ready strategy presentation and narrative
Weeks 4–10
Primary Output
"A board-approved transformation blueprint with full financial modelling and implementation plan."
03
Phase Three

Deliver

Unlike firms that hand over a report and leave, Meridiem stays through implementation. Our consultants embed within your teams, lead agile work streams, manage programme governance, and adapt the plan as real-world conditions evolve — keeping a relentless focus on measurable outcomes.

  • Embedded consultant programme management
  • Agile work stream execution and weekly cadences
  • Performance tracking against agreed KPIs
  • Change management and stakeholder communication
  • Risk escalation and adaptive replanning
Months 3–18
Primary Output
"Measurable business results — cost savings, revenue growth, or operational improvements — delivered on time."
04
Phase Four

Embed

Results that endure require capabilities that outlast our engagement. In the final phase, we systematically transfer knowledge, build internal expertise, redesign governance structures, and establish the performance management systems your organisation needs to sustain improvement independently.

  • Capability transfer and internal team coaching
  • Knowledge management systems and playbooks
  • Governance and decision-making framework design
  • Performance management system implementation
  • 90-day post-engagement review and support plan
Final 2–3 months
Primary Output
"An organisation fully equipped to sustain and accelerate the transformation independently."

Guiding Principles

How we work,
every day

Six non-negotiable principles that govern how we engage — regardless of industry, scope, or team.

I

Hypothesis-Driven

We form clear hypotheses early and test them ruthlessly with data. We never "boil the ocean" — we focus on the questions that matter most.

II

Facts, Not Opinions

Every recommendation rests on evidence. We do not recommend what feels right — we recommend what the data, benchmarks, and analysis support.

III

Speed with Rigour

Slow consulting is bad consulting. We move fast — but we never sacrifice analytical quality for speed. Rigour and velocity are not mutually exclusive.

IV

Client Co-Creation

The best strategies are built with clients, not delivered to them. We create alignment through co-creation, not persuasion after the fact.

V

Outcome Accountability

We agree upfront on specific, measurable outcomes and report against them transparently throughout the engagement. No hiding behind ambiguity.

VI

Intellectual Humility

We listen more than we talk. We update our views when evidence demands it. The smartest people in the room are often our clients.
