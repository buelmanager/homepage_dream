Find Us

ul. Mariacka 24, Gdańsk

Our atelier occupies the ground floor of a restored 17th-century merchants' house on ul. Mariacka — Gdańsk's legendary amber street, stretching from St. Mary's Church to the Green Gate and the Motława River.

By rail: 10 minutes from Gdańsk Główny (Central) station. By air: 25 minutes from Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport. Private car transfers can be arranged for international clients.
