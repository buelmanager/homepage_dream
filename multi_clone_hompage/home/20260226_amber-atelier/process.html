The Journey

Our Process

Creating a piece for INCLUSIONS begins long before a goldsmith touches the metal. The amber must be sourced, scientifically assessed, and its prehistoric passenger documented before a single setting concept is drawn.

"The amber speaks. Our task is to listen."

Zofia Kowalska, Master Goldsmith
1
Amber Sourcing
Week 1–2

Raw specimens are acquired from certified Baltic miners and independent collectors. Each stone is assessed for resin quality, optical clarity, and inclusion potential.

2
Scientific Assessment
Week 2–4

Dr. Wiśniewska examines each stone under polarized and UV light. FTIR spectroscopy confirms Baltic succinite origin. Inclusions are microscopically documented.

3
Design & Consultation
Week 3–5

For commissions, client meets with Zofia to select their specimen and discuss setting concepts. The design is drawn by hand, refined iteratively until perfect.

4
18k Gold Fabrication
Week 5–10

Piotr hand-forges the setting in 18k gold. Each component is individually made, fitted, polished, and tested against the amber before final assembly.

5
Setting & Completion
Week 10–12

The amber is hand-set under magnification. Final polish, stone security checks, and photography for the provenance certificate.

6
Certification & Delivery
Week 12–14

Complete documentation package prepared. Piece delivered in hand-crafted amber-wood case with certificate portfolio and care instructions.

100% Baltic Origin Certified

The Material

Baltic Amber Sourcing

We source exclusively from the Baltic Sea region — the Sambian coast, Gdańsk Bay, and licensed inland mines. No synthetic, pressed, or non-Baltic material is ever used.

Certified Miners Only
All Baltic amber sourced from three certified mining partners operating under Polish Geological Survey permits.
FTIR Verification
Every stone tested by Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy before acceptance. The Baltic succinite "fingerprint" is non-replicable.
Chain of Custody Documentation
From mine to atelier to client — every transfer of ownership documented and included in provenance certificate.

The Craft

Hand-Forged in Gdańsk

Every metal component is fabricated by hand in our ul. Mariacka atelier. We use no casting, no prefabricated findings. Only forged and shaped 18k gold.

01
Drawing & Annealing
18k sheet and wire drawn to specification. Annealed to working hardness.
02
Forming & Shaping
Bezel and structural elements formed by hand over steel mandrels.
03
Finishing & Polish
Progressive polishing from 400 to 8000 grit. Surface mirror-finished by hand.
04
Amber Setting
Specimen hand-set under 10x magnification. Bezel rolled to secure without pressure on amber.

Complete Documentation

Certification & Provenance

Every INCLUSIONS piece comes with a comprehensive documentation portfolio — scientific, gemological, and artisanal provenance verified to the highest international standards.

Infrared analysis confirming Baltic succinite. Comparison to reference spectra included.

Provenance Chain
