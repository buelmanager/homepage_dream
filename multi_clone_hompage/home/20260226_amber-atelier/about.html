Our Heritage &
The Atelier

Founded 1987, Gdańsk

A Lineage of Light

INCLUSIONS was founded on the shores of the Baltic Sea by master goldsmith Aleksander Kowalski, whose obsession with prehistoric amber led him to establish what is now Europe's most respected amber authentication and jewelry atelier.

What began as a single workshop on ul. Mariacka — Gdańsk's legendary amber street — has grown into a three-generation family atelier. Today, his granddaughter Zofia Kowalska leads the craft alongside resident paleontologist Dr. Marta Wiśniewska and head goldsmith Piotr Adamczyk.

We believe that prehistoric inclusions are not curiosities — they are irreplaceable windows into an ancient world. Our role is to honor them with settings worthy of their forty million years of formation.

"Amber does not merely preserve — it illuminates. Our task is to let forty million years speak."

Aleksander Kowalski, Founder

Baltic Amber Heritage

Gdańsk — Amber Capital of the World

For over 3,000 years, Gdańsk has stood at the centre of the amber trade. The Baltic Sea and its shores hold the world's largest deposits of succinite — formed when the ancient Hyperborea conifer forests released their resin some 40–60 million years ago.

~50M BCE
Resin Formation Ancient Pinus succinifera trees along the Eocene coast produce resin, trapping insects, pollen, and air.
1000 BCE
The Amber Road Phoenician and Roman traders establish the Amber Road from Baltic shores to the Mediterranean.
1308
Gdańsk Amber Guild Formation of the amber craftsmen's guild in Gdańsk, codifying standards for the craft.
1987
INCLUSIONS Founded Aleksander Kowalski establishes the atelier on ul. Mariacka, specializing in inclusion-bearing specimens.
