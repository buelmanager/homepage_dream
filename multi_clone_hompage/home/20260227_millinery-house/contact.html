Common Questions

Before You Write

How long does a bespoke commission take?
+

Lead times vary according to the complexity of the commission and our current workshop capacity. A simple fascinator may take 3–4 weeks. A fully bespoke bridal hat or wide-brim with elaborate trimming typically requires 6–10 weeks. We always provide an accurate lead time at consultation, and we recommend commissioning at least 12 weeks before your event where possible.

What is the minimum budget for a bespoke commission?
+

Our bespoke commissions begin at £500 for a simple hand-blocked fascinator and rise in accordance with material, complexity of trimming, and the number of fittings required. Bridal work typically begins at £1,400. We are transparent about pricing at every stage and will never exceed an agreed budget without your explicit approval.

Can I commission a hat from outside the UK or France?
+

Absolutely. Many of our clients commission from abroad and conduct their consultation via video call. We ship worldwide using insured specialist art and couture couriers, and we have extensive experience with customs documentation for shipments to the United States, the Gulf, Japan, and Australia. The final delivery is always fully insured to replacement value.

Do you offer alterations to existing Chapeau Noir hats?
+

Yes. We offer a full restoration and alteration service for all Chapeau Noir hats. Hats may be re-blocked to a new size, re-trimmed in a different colourway, or refreshed with new feathers and ribbon. We also offer a conservation service for historic hats. Please contact your nearest atelier with photographs to obtain an assessment.

Is the consultation private and confidential?
+

Entirely. We schedule one client at a time in our consultation rooms, and client records are kept in handwritten ledgers rather than digital databases. We do not discuss client commissions publicly, and we never share client information with third parties or press contacts without explicit written permission. This has been house policy since 1987.
