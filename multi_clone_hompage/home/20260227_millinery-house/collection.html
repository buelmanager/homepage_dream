The Fedora Noir

Perhaps our most celebrated creation, the Fedora Noir is the house's answer to a timeless question: can utility be transformed into art? Blocked from the finest fur felt sourced from Italian mills in Biella, steamed over our signature last and trimmed with a raw-edge petersham ribbon, it is the hat that wears as well on the Left Bank as at the Ritz. Available in black, chocolate, and smoke — or in any hue from our custom colour programme.
