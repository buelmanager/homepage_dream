About STRATUM

Founded on the banks of the Dordogne in 1987, STRATUM is the world's most respected river stone sculpture studio. Our story is one of geological patience and artistic devotion.

The Founding

From Geology to Art

Edouard Masse & The First River Stone

In the summer of 1987, geologist Edouard Masse was conducting a river sediment survey along the upper Dordogne when he picked up a piece of basalt worn to an extraordinary smoothness by perhaps ten thousand years of current. He spent three days in his field tent polishing it further, and when he returned to Paris, a gallery owner offered him a commission on the spot.

He never returned to geology. Instead, he went back to the river, and STRATUM was born — not as a business plan or a brand, but as an inevitability between a man who understood stone and a river that had been shaping it for millennia.

Today, the atelier employs three master sculptors and four apprentices. Edouard still selects every stone personally. The process has not changed: walk the river, listen, choose. Then let the stone tell you what it wants to become.

Our Values

What We Stand For

I

Geological Honesty

We never falsify a stone's origin or artificially age a surface. The geological documentation that accompanies every work is scientifically accurate and permanently archived. What is old is old; what is crafted is crafted.

II

Non-Reproduction

Because every river stone is unique, no STRATUM work can ever be reproduced. We do not create editions, prints, or casts. Every work leaves the atelier knowing it is singular in the world.

III

Ecological Restraint

We take no more than ten percent of what we find in any single river section. Stones are selected from naturally occurring accumulations, never extracted from living riverbed. The river's health comes before our art.

IV

Apprenticeship

Every sculptor at STRATUM began as an apprentice. We invest three years in foundational training before a sculptor touches a commission. The knowledge accumulated over 37 years must be transmitted precisely, or it will be lost.
