TIDECRAFT Glass Studio

Studio &
Collection

TIDECRAFT Glass Studio occupies a converted fishing store at Mawgan Porth, Cornwall. Founded in 2001 by Luca Ferraro, who trained for eleven years on the island of Murano. Three furnaces, eight master glass blowers, and the tides of the North Cornish coast as our design brief.

Every piece in the TIDECRAFT collection is hand-blown using Murano techniques — the same methods mastered over seven centuries of Venetian glass tradition. Applied to the light and tide rhythms of the Atlantic, they produce work unlike anything made anywhere else.

The studio accepts commissions for residential, commercial, and public installation projects. Lead time is typically 12 to 18 months. Please visit the index page for our full commission form and collection overview.
