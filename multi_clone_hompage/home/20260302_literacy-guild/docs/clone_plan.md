# Clone Plan — The Literacy Guild

## Design Direction
A sophisticated adult literacy nonprofit landing page evoking the gravity of books, learning, and community transformation. The cool blue-steel palette (#A0C4D8) references ink, water, and clarity of thought. Libre Baskerville provides scholarly weight; Source Sans 3 gives accessible readability.

## Hero Approach
Type G scroll-driven text transform. The words "WORDS" and "OPEN" split apart as the user scrolls, revealing the tagline "Every Door" and a call-to-action. A dark library/book photograph at 30% brightness anchors the mood.

## Content Strategy
- Stats emphasize human impact (4,800 adults, 96% completion, 140 volunteers, 17 years)
- Philosophy section: Access for All / Community-Centered / Lifelong Empowerment
- Programs presented as "Learning Pathways" with track level and duration
- Process framed as a journey — from first contact to graduation
- Heritage timeline from 2009 founding to 2025 Literacy Leadership Academy
- Press quotes from award bodies and academic journals (fictional but credible)
- Form positions as both learner enrollment and volunteer/donor intake

## Animation (A3)
- x-slide: -20px (horizontal slide from left)
- Duration: 0.7–0.8s
- Stagger: 0.04
- Ease: power3.out
