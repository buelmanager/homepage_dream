About The Literacy Guild

Our Mission

The Literacy Guild exists to ensure that every adult in our region has access to the foundational reading skills needed to live, work, and participate fully in community life.

We believe literacy is not merely a skill but a gateway to dignity, independence, and civic voice. Founded in 2009 by three former teachers and a librarian, our organization has grown from a single reading room to a network spanning eleven library branches, four community health centers, and two workforce training campuses.

Our work is guided by evidence-based adult learning theory, a commitment to cultural humility, and the understanding that each learner brings a lifetime of knowledge and experience to their reading journey.

Founder's Story

Built on Belief

Eleanor Hartley spent eighteen years teaching elementary school before she encountered her neighbor — a retired factory worker named Harold — who confided he had never learned to read. The shame Harold carried, and the joy on his face when he read his first full paragraph at age 63, became the founding story of the Guild.

Eleanor recruited colleagues and friends, wrote grants on her kitchen table, and convinced the Millhaven Public Library to donate space on Tuesday evenings. By the end of the first year, eleven adults had learned to read independently.

Today Eleanor serves as the organization's Executive Director, leading a staff of seven full-time employees and a volunteer corps of over 140 trained tutors.

Core Values

What We Stand For

01

Dignity in Learning

Every adult deserves to learn without shame. Our spaces and our language are designed to honor the courage it takes to seek literacy as an adult.

02

Radical Welcome

We serve anyone, regardless of age, immigration status, language background, or prior education. No application is rejected; no story is too complicated.

03

Community Accountability

We are governed by the communities we serve. Our board includes current and former learners, and program decisions are made with, not for, the people we support.
