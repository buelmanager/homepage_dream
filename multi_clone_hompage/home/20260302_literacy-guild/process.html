Our Method

How It Works

The path from first contact to independent reading is different for every person. Our process is designed to be flexible, private, and supportive — built around your schedule, your interests, and your goals.

Your Journey, Step by Step

01

Reach Out Confidentially

Contact us by phone, email, or in person at any of our partner library sites. There are no forms to fill out, no documentation required. A coordinator will follow up within 48 hours in your preferred language.

02

Confidential Intake Assessment

A private conversation — never a test — helps us understand where you are and where you want to go. We also ask about your schedule, preferred learning style, and any language preferences.

03

Tutor Matching

We match you with a trained volunteer tutor based on your availability, goals, and background. Most matches take one to two weeks. You can request a new match at any time.

04

Personalized Learning Plan

With your tutor, you set milestones and choose reading materials that interest you — cookbooks, news, novels, work documents. The plan is yours. We provide the materials at no cost.

05

Regular Sessions

Two to three sessions per week at a library, community center, or by video call. Monthly check-ins with staff ensure progress and address any challenges early before they become barriers.

06

Graduation and Next Steps

Completing a track is a community celebration. Graduates can advance to the next program, become peer mentors, enter the Literacy Leadership Academy, or transition to further education and career support.

Learner Resources

Free Materials

All reading materials, workbooks, and practice sets are provided free of charge. We never ask learners to purchase anything.

Multilingual Support

We offer initial consultations in Spanish, Portuguese, Somali, and Haitian Creole, with interpreter services for other languages on request.

Childcare Partners

Several of our sites offer on-site childcare during sessions in partnership with local family resource centers. Ask your coordinator for details.

Take the First Step

Starting is the hardest part. We are here to make it as easy and welcoming as possible.
