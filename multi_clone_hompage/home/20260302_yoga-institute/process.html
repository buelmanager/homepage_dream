How We Teach

The Prana
Method

Our approach to yoga education is built on progressive depth, honest enquiry and the understanding that meaningful change unfolds slowly, over time, through sustained and attentive practice.

"Yoga is not a technique to be acquired but a relationship to be cultivated — between the practitioner and their body, between the individual and the present moment, between the student and the tradition." — Arjun Mehta, Founder
01
Weeks 1–3

Foundation: Body Awareness

We begin with the body as teacher. Students learn to observe sensation without judgement, developing proprioceptive intelligence and a stable relationship with the physical form through foundational asana sequences.

  • Standing postures and spinal awareness
  • Fundamentals of weight distribution and joint integrity
  • Introduction to Savasana and Yoga Nidra
  • The concept of Sthira Sukham — steadiness and ease
02
Weeks 4–6

Breath: The Bridge Within

Pranayama is introduced as the science of vital energy. Students learn classical techniques that transform concentration and bring stillness to the agitated mind. Breath becomes the direct link between body and consciousness.

  • Ujjayi — the victorious breath
  • Nadi Shodhana — alternate nostril purification
  • Kapalabhati — skull-brightening breath
  • The Kumbhakas: retention and its practice
03
Weeks 7–10

Integration: Sequence & Flow

Postures and breath unite in sequenced practice. Students build intelligent sequences drawing from Hatha and Ashtanga traditions, learning the internal logic of pose progression and the principle of vinyasa — conscious movement with breath.

  • Primary Series foundations (Ashtanga)
  • Intelligent sequencing and counter-postures
  • Backbend, forward-bend and twist integration
  • Anatomy of the practice: fascia, breath and nervous system
04
Weeks 11–14

Philosophy: The Inner Architecture

Patanjali's eight-limbed system provides a complete map of consciousness. Lectures in Sanskrit, the Yoga Sutras and the Bhagavad Gita illuminate the deeper context of practice and reveal yoga as a complete philosophy of living.

  • The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali — selected study
  • Yama, Niyama and the ethics of practice
  • Samkhya cosmology and the nature of mind
  • Introduction to Sanskrit for practitioners
05
Weeks 15–18

Mastery: Teaching the Teachable

The final stage prepares students to transmit practice with clarity and compassion. Methodology, observation, demonstration, hands-on adjustment and the ethics of the teacher-student relationship are studied in depth through practice-teaching and peer review.

  • Teaching methodology and sequencing for students
  • Observation, demonstration and verbal cueing
  • Safe hands-on adjustment and therapeutic applications
  • Professional ethics and the responsibilities of the yoga teacher
