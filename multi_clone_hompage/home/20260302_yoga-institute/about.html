Our Story

About Prana
Yoga Institute

For nearly five decades, Prana Yoga Institute has held a steadfast commitment to classical yoga — transmitted with honesty, depth and an unbroken respect for the lineages from which this art flows.

A Sanctuary for Sincere Practice

Prana Yoga Institute was never conceived as a business. It began as an offering — a place where those who had been touched by yoga could come to study it properly, rigorously, and with the guidance of teachers who had themselves spent years in dedicated practice.

Our mission is simple: to preserve and transmit classical yoga in all its dimensions — physical, energetic, psychological and philosophical — to students who arrive with an open heart and a willingness to be changed by the practice.

We are not a studio. We are a school. The distinction matters. Students who come to Prana leave as practitioners — grounded, clear and equipped to carry the tradition forward in their lives.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

I

Lineage & Integrity

Every method we teach traces an unbroken lineage to verified masters. We do not innovate for novelty's sake — we preserve, transmit and honour the tradition as received.

II

The Individual Student

No two bodies, minds or life histories are the same. Our teaching responds to the person before us, not an abstract ideal. Small groups and personal guidance define our model.

III

Patience as Practice

Depth cannot be rushed. We resist the culture of overnight transformation and invite students to remain with what is difficult long enough to discover what it has to teach.

IV

Ethics of the Teacher

The teacher-student relationship is sacred and carries ethical weight. All faculty at Prana adhere to a rigorous code of conduct, boundaries and professional responsibility.
