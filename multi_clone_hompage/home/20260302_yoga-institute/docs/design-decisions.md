# Design Decisions — Prana Yoga Institute

## Color Strategy
Deep teal (#0A1818) background evokes the pre-dawn stillness of a Mysore shala — dark, calm and purposeful. The sage-mint accent (#4DCFB0) references both the freshness of prana (breath) and the lush Himalayan foothills setting.

## Typography
Fraunces (optical-size serif) is elegant yet organic — its variable weight range allows both the delicate italic tagline and strong 600-weight headings. Inter ensures clear, minimal body reading at all sizes.

## Hero Choice (Type B Parallax)
Parallax layers suggest the layered nature of yoga practice: multiple dimensions of experience moving at different rates beneath a single surface. The mousemove interaction rewards attention, mirroring the practice of awareness itself.

## Layout Logic
- Stats strip establishes credibility immediately after hero
- Philosophy grid (3-col) creates visual balance — three equals three pillars
- Program cards use real imagery to ground the abstract in tangible experience
- Curriculum numbered steps mirror the 5-stage Patanjali structure

## Animation
A2 Whisper (y:14, 1.5s, power1.out) creates a calm, meditative page feel — never rushed, never dramatic. Appropriate for a classical yoga school that values patience as a core virtue.
