# Copy Guide — Prana Yoga Institute

## Voice & Tone
- Calm, authoritative, unhurried
- Rooted in lineage and tradition — never trendy
- Invites rather than sells
- Sanskrit terms used sparingly and explained in context
- Avoids wellness clichés ("journey", "transform", "vibrate")

## Key Messages
1. Classical tradition, not contemporary fitness yoga
2. Lineage-verified teaching from identified masters
3. Small cohorts, personal guidance
4. Patient, progressive approach to depth
5. The institute as a sanctuary, not a studio

## Brand Voice Examples
- "We do not innovate for novelty's sake — we preserve, transmit and honour the tradition."
- "Yoga is not a technique to be acquired but a relationship to be cultivated."
- "Students who come to Prana leave as practitioners — grounded, clear."

## Avoid
- Words: transform, journey, manifest, vibrate, awaken, wellness, selfcare
- Imagery: Lululemon aesthetics, fitness models, Instagram yoga
- Claims: "life-changing", "revolutionary", guaranteed results
