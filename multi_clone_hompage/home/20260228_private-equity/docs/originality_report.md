## Design Uniqueness

### vs. Common PE Website Patterns
- **Color:** Most PE firms use navy/black/white. Warm amber distinguishes this template significantly.
- **Hero:** Standard PE sites use split text/stats layouts but not Type D asymmetric grid format.
- **Typography:** Playfair Display is editorial and unusual for finance — most use Gotham, GT America, or similar geometric sans.
- **Personality:** Most PE sites are austere and information-dense. This template blends institutional credibility with luxury brand aesthetics.

### vs. Luxury Brand Templates in Library
- Clear PE-sector content (investment criteria tabs, track record, fund history, LP resources)
- Portfolio grid with investment metrics (MOIC, IRR, EV) rather than product features
- Regulatory disclaimer in footer
- Inquiry form with investor relations routing
