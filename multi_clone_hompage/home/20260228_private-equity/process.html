Our investment strategy is the product of 26 years of focused specialization. We do not attempt to be all things — we aspire to be the best possible partner for the precise category of business we know deeply.

Investment Parameters
  • Enterprise Value: $80M – $1.5B at entry
  • Strategy: Buyout (majority) and Growth Equity (minority)
  • Sectors: Luxury Consumer, Premium Hospitality, Wellness & Lifestyle, Fine Food & Beverage
  • Geography: North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific
  • Hold Period: 4–7 years (patient capital)
  • Check Size: $50M – $600M equity (larger with co-investment)
  • EBITDA at Entry: $10M+ preferred; earlier-stage for growth equity

What We Look For

Our Investment Criteria

We are selective by design. Our team reviews ~200 opportunities per year and closes 3–4 investments. This selectivity is the foundation of our performance.

Brand Heritage Authentic origin story with genuine craft, provenance, or cultural meaning. Not a manufactured premium — a real one.
Pricing Power Demonstrated ability to command a price premium of 3× or more vs. mass-market alternatives without volume erosion.
Brand Equity High unaided awareness within target consumer segments. NPS of 60+ preferred. Brand that customers actively recommend.
Whitespace Credible path to brand extension — new geographies, adjacent categories, or new distribution channels — without diluting brand positioning.
Revenue $20M+ trailing revenue for buyout; earlier stage acceptable for growth equity. Preference for recurring or subscription revenues.
EBITDA Margins 15%+ EBITDA margins (or clear path to 20%+ post-investment). Gross margins of 55%+ preferred across product-based businesses.
Growth Rate Top-line growth of 10–25% p.a. with evidence that growth is brand-driven and repeatable, not promotional or channel-dependent.
Capital Intensity Low to moderate. We prefer asset-light or hybrid models with strong FCF conversion — 70%+ EBITDA-to-FCF preferred.
Founder Vision Authentic connection to the brand and credibility with consumers, employees, and trade partners. The best luxury brands are built by believers.
Coachability Willingness to build institutional infrastructure and supplement with professional management while protecting the brand's creative core.
Incentive Alignment Management must participate in meaningful equity ownership. Rollover of 25–40% of proceeds from founding shareholders is standard in our structures.
Market Size $2B+ addressable market with secular growth drivers. Premium and luxury spending globally has grown at 2–3× GDP rate over two decades.
Competitive Moat Sustainable advantages — brand heritage, proprietary supply chain, exclusive relationships, regulatory barriers, or network effects — that are difficult to replicate.
Geography North America, Europe, and select Asia-Pacific markets. Cross-border expansion potential — specifically entry into Chinese and Middle Eastern luxury consumers — is often a key value lever.

Responsible Investing

ESG & Impact

We believe environmental and social responsibility is not a constraint on returns — it is a driver of them. Brands that embed sustainability and purpose create stronger consumer loyalty and long-term competitive advantage.

Environmental Standards

Mandatory carbon footprint baseline within 6 months of investment. Annual reduction targets embedded in management KPIs and incentive compensation.

Human Capital

Living wage commitment across all portfolio companies globally. Annual employee engagement surveys with results reviewed at board level. Diversity and inclusion programs in every portfolio company by year 2.

Governance

Independent board members in all portfolio companies. Annual governance review against institutional best practices. Whistleblower programs and ethics hotlines in place from day 100.

Supply Chain Integrity

Deep supply chain auditing for luxury goods companies. No tolerance for forced labor or environmental violations at any tier. Preference for suppliers with certified sustainable sourcing programs.
