Philosophy

The Art of
Flamenco

"Duende is the spirit that cannot be taught — only summoned."

Rooted in the soul of Andalusia, flamenco is not merely dance — it is a living language of the body, born from centuries of joy, suffering, and transcendence. At DUENDE, we preserve the purity of this tradition while welcoming students from every corner of the world.

Our curriculum draws from the Escuela Bolera, the Gypsy traditions of Triana, and the grand theatrical stages of Madrid. Every movement carries history. Every stamp echoes the earth.

Faculty

Our Maestros

Each master teacher brings decades of performance and pedagogy from the highest stages of flamenco.

Rafael Morales

Artistic Director & Baile

A former principal dancer of the Ballet Flamenco de Andalucía, Rafael has performed in 43 countries and brings four decades of deep tradition to DUENDE's curriculum.

Lucía Fuentes

Cante & Musicality

Born into a gitano family in Triana, Lucía is one of the most respected cantaoras of her generation. She teaches the living soul of flamenco song — from siguiriyas to bulerías.

Antonio Herrera

Toque & Guitar

A concert guitarist trained under Paco de Lucía's school, Antonio demystifies the complex rhythmic world of flamenco guitar for dancers and musicians alike.
