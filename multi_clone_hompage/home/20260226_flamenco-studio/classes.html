Common Questions

FAQ

Do I need previous dance experience?
Not at all. Our Foundations program is designed for complete beginners. Many of our most accomplished graduates arrived never having danced a single step.
What language are classes taught in?
Foundations classes are taught in both English and Spanish. Intermediate and higher classes are primarily in Spanish, with English support available from all faculty.
Can I visit the academy before enrolling?
Absolutely. We offer complimentary observation visits on any Tuesday or Thursday morning. Contact us to arrange your visit.
Is accommodation available nearby?
We maintain partnerships with several guesthouses and apartments in Triana within walking distance of the academy. We are happy to make recommendations upon enrollment.
Are men welcome at DUENDE?
Absolutely and warmly. Flamenco has a rich tradition of male dance (baile masculino) with its own aesthetic and technique. All programs are open to all genders.
What shoes do I need?
For Foundations classes, regular dance shoes are sufficient. We recommend investing in proper flamenco shoes (zapatos de flamenco) from your second month. We can guide you to Seville's best zapatería.
