Monthly Series

Tablao Nights

Every second Friday, DUENDE presents its monthly Tablao Night — an intimate evening of live flamenco performed by faculty and advanced students in partnership with Seville's most storied tablaos.

Unlike tourist flamenco shows, our Tablao Nights are curated performance events where each palo is chosen and prepared with artistic intention. Guests sit in close proximity to the performers — within touching distance of the duende.

Tickets include a pre-show sherry reception and a printed programme with notes on each artist and palo performed.

Student Events

Student Showcases

Each semester, DUENDE presents formal student showcases where our dancers perform developed works before a live audience. These events are open to the public and offer a window into the depth of our curriculum.
