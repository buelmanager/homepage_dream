Find Us

Visit the Academy

DUENDE is situated in the Triana barrio — the historic gypsy quarter of Seville, birthplace of some of flamenco's most important artists. Our studio is on Calle Betis, the riverside street looking across to the old city.

We welcome prospective students to visit the academy for a complimentary observation session. You will be able to watch a class in progress and speak with one of our faculty or administration staff.

◆
By Metro
Metro Line 1 to Puerta Jerez, then 12 min walk across Triana Bridge
◆
By Bus
Lines C1, 40, 41 — Puente de Triana stop. 2 min walk south along Calle Betis.
◆
By Taxi / Rideshare
Tell the driver "Calle Betis catorce, Triana." The red door, across from the river.
◆
Observation Visits
Available Tuesdays and Thursdays, 09:30–12:00. Please email ahead to confirm.

Private & Group Events

DUENDE offers a range of private and group experiences for corporate events, cultural programs, and special occasions.

◆

Private Classes

One-on-one instruction with a senior faculty member. Available for all levels. A deeply personal and immersive introduction to flamenco. From €180 per session.

◆

Group Workshop

A curated flamenco experience for groups of 6–20. Perfect for cultural tourism, hen parties, corporate team events, and school programs. From €45 per person.

◆

Private Performance

Commission a private tablao performance for your event — corporate dinners, weddings, cultural celebrations. Our faculty perform for audiences of 10–200. From €1,200.
