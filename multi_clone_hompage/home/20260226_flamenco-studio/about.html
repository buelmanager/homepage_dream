The DUENDE Story

DUENDE was founded by Rafael Morales Sr. in the Triana barrio of Seville — the cradle of flamenco. What began as a small studio above a ceramics workshop became, within a decade, one of the most respected academies in Andalusia.

The name comes from the ineffable spirit that Federico García Lorca described as the dark, trembling power that gives authentic art its force. We believe that spirit can be cultivated — through discipline, immersion, and love for the tradition.

Today DUENDE welcomes over 400 students annually, offering programs from complete beginners to professional-level intensives, attracting dancers from over 60 countries who come to Seville seeking the source.

What We Believe

Our Philosophy

Flamenco is not performance — it is conversation. Between the dancer and the musician, between the past and the present, between the body and the soul. At DUENDE we teach this conversation from the ground up, beginning with the feet and ending with the spirit.

We reject shortcuts. Every student — whether beginner or professional — begins with the same fundamentals: posture, weight, breath, and silence. Only when these are inhabited completely do we move forward. This is not rigidity; it is respect.

◆
The Body
Técnica

Precise, demanding, and deeply rooted. We teach footwork, arm styling, and coordination with an attention to detail that reveals the architecture inside every movement.

◆
The Voice
Cante

All dancers at DUENDE study cante. Understanding the song — its pain, its joy, its silences — is inseparable from dancing it truly.

◆
The Spirit
Duende

The inexplicable force. We cannot teach it — but we create the conditions in which it can arrive. Through repetition, release, and total presence.

Faculty

Master Teachers

Rafael Morales Jr.

Artistic Director & Baile

Son of the founder, Rafael inherited his father's passion and extended it through formal study at the Real Conservatorio de Arte Dramático. A former principal of Ballet Flamenco de Andalucía.

  • 40+ years performing experience
  • Performed in 43 countries
  • Choreographer — Bienal de Flamenco 2018

Lucía Fuentes

Cante & Musicality

A gitana from Triana whose voice has been called "the purest in Seville" by El País. Lucía specialises in palos from siguiriya to bulerías por soleá, teaching their emotional architecture.

  • Grammy Latino nominee 2019
  • Resident artist — Tablao El Arenal
  • Published — Cante Jondo: A Living Grammar

Antonio Herrera

Toque & Guitar

Trained under Paco de Lucía's school in Algeciras and at the Conservatorio Superior de Música de Sevilla, Antonio brings an intimate knowledge of compás that transforms how dancers hear rhythm.

  • Concert guitarist — 25 years
  • Accompanied Mayte Martín world tour
  • Teacher — Escuela Superior de Arte Flamenco
