The Pod

Engineered for
Absolute Nothing

Our custom-manufactured float chambers are precision instruments. Each pod maintains a salt solution density 1.5 times that of the Dead Sea, achieving effortless buoyancy without any conscious effort. The walls are acoustically isolated to a threshold of 0 decibels.

The lid creates total darkness. The air inside is filtered and temperature-matched to your skin — 34.5°C — the precise point at which thermoreceptors become indistinguishable from the surrounding environment.

Verified by Science,
Known by the Soul

REST (Restricted Environmental Stimulation Therapy) has been studied in clinical settings since the 1950s. The evidence for its benefits is both deep and broad, spanning neuroscience, sports medicine, and psychology.
