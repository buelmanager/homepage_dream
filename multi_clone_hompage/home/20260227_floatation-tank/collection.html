Common Questions

Before You Float

Can I float if I can't swim?
Absolutely. The Epsom salt solution is so dense that you float effortlessly regardless of your body type, weight, or swimming ability. You could not sink in a float pod even if you tried. The water is only 30cm deep at most — and you lie on your back with complete buoyancy support.
Is it safe if I'm claustrophobic?
Many of our regular clients began floating specifically to address claustrophobia. Our pods are spacious — 2.4 metres long, 1.5 metres wide. The lid is never locked. You are always in complete control and can open the pod or turn on the interior light at any time. Many claustrophobic floaters keep the lid slightly open for their first sessions.
How clean is the water?
Our filtration standard exceeds requirements significantly. Between every session, the entire 1000L solution is filtered through UV sterilization, ozone treatment, and hydrogen peroxide. The high salinity of the solution (1.5x the Dead Sea) creates a naturally inhospitable environment for bacteria. Our water is tested and certified weekly.
What should I do to prepare?
Avoid caffeine 2–3 hours before floating. Eat a light meal, not an empty stomach. Avoid shaving for 24 hours (the salt will sting). Remove contact lenses. If you have any open wounds or skin conditions, please contact us first. Beyond that — arrive, breathe, and let go.
What if I feel anxious inside the pod?
This is very common, especially in your first few floats. The internal light switch is always within reach. You can turn the lights on, open the pod, or signal for your guide at any moment. Many people experience an initial 10–15 minutes of restlessness before the mind quiets — this is completely normal and precisely the threshold we help you through.
