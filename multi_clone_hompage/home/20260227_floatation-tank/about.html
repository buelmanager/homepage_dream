Our Origin

Founded in the Pursuit of Nothing

ZERO was founded in 2018 by Dr. Elara Voss and her partner Thomas Kael, a neuroscientist and a former competitive freediver. The studio emerged from a shared obsession: what happens to human consciousness when every sensory input is removed?

What began as a personal research project — Elara floating in a converted tank in their basement, Thomas measuring her brainwaves — has grown into London's most respected float therapy studio. But the mission has never changed: to offer the most scientifically rigorous and experientially beautiful float environment in existence.

ZERO is not a spa, though it is indulgent. It is not a clinic, though it heals. It is a place of pure encounter — between you, your mind, and the silence that has always been beneath the noise.

History of Floating

Seven Decades of Research

1954
John C. Lilly Invents the Isolation Tank

Neuroscientist John C. Lilly creates the world's first sensory deprivation tank at the National Institute of Mental Health, beginning the scientific investigation into consciousness and sensory withdrawal.

1972
Epsom Salt Solution Developed

Researchers pioneer the use of magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt) solutions to achieve effortless buoyancy without submersion tanks, making floating accessible and comfortable for longer durations.

1983
REST Therapy Enters Clinical Practice

Dr. Peter Suedfeld coins the term "Restricted Environmental Stimulation Therapy" and publishes landmark studies demonstrating its efficacy for stress, anxiety, chronic pain, and creative performance.

2001
Modern Pod Technology Emerges

Commercial float pod manufacturers develop the first climate-controlled, acoustically isolated modern float chambers, bringing the experience out of research labs and into the wellness world.

2018
ZERO Opens Its Doors in London

Dr. Elara Voss and Thomas Kael open ZERO Float Therapy Studio, combining clinical rigor with luxury design. Within six months, every session is fully booked. A waitlist begins.

What We Stand For

Our Four Commitments

◈

Scientific Integrity

Every claim we make about floating is backed by peer-reviewed research. We partner with universities and clinical researchers to continue advancing our understanding of how REST affects human health and consciousness.

◈

Obsessive Hygiene

Our pods use a triple-filtration system combining UV sterilization, ozone treatment, and hydrogen peroxide. The entire 1000L solution passes through the system multiple times between each session. We are meticulous beyond requirement.

◈

Radical Privacy

Your float data, health information, and experience notes are kept entirely private. We never share, sell, or analyze client data beyond what is necessary to improve your personal float protocol. Silence extends to our data practices.

◈

Accessible Transcendence

We offer first-float grants, community rates, and partnerships with NHS mental health referral programs to ensure that access to this transformative practice is not limited by financial circumstance.

The Evidence Base

What Research Shows

Over 300 peer-reviewed studies have examined the effects of REST. The findings are consistent and compelling. This is not alternative therapy — it is an evidence-based intervention with a growing body of support.

  • 01
    Cortisol Reduction. A single 90-minute session reduces salivary cortisol by an average of 21.6% — comparable to four weeks of daily meditation practice.
  • 02
    Theta State Induction. EEG studies show floaters reliably enter theta brainwave states (4–8 Hz) — the frequency associated with creativity, problem-solving, and deep insight.
  • 03
    Pain Relief. Studies in fibromyalgia patients show 33% reduction in pain intensity. Floaters report effects lasting 72–96 hours post-session.
  • 04
    Athletic Recovery. Lactic acid dispersal is accelerated by magnesium absorption through skin. Sports science studies confirm 18% faster muscle recovery in float groups.
The Founders

Built on Curiosity
and Salt Water

"The question that haunts me is not what happens in the tank. It is what happens when everything else is removed — who are you then?"

Dr. Elara Voss holds a PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience from University College London. After a decade of research into altered states of consciousness, she became convinced that sensory deprivation was the most direct, accessible, and underutilized tool for exploring the mind. She directs our research partnerships and oversees the scientific integrity of our float protocols.

Thomas Kael spent twelve years as a professional freediver, training himself to hold his breath for over six minutes through deep meditative practice. His understanding of the body in extreme stillness informs every aspect of ZERO's physical environment — from pod acoustics to water temperature calibration to the scent of our post-float lounge. He is, as he puts it, the one who makes the science feel like nothing at all.
