Cancellation Policy

Cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before your session for a full credit. Same-day cancellations are credited at 50%. No-shows are charged in full. We understand that life is unpredictable — please call us and we will always try to find a solution.

◈

First-Time Consultation

Every first-time floater at ZERO is paired with a float guide for a complimentary 15-minute pre-float consultation. We answer your questions, walk you through the process, and ensure you enter the pod with full confidence and zero anxiety. This is included in all first-float bookings.

◈

Gift Certificates

ZERO gift certificates are available for any session type or monetary value. They make extraordinary gifts — for someone dealing with stress, recovering from injury, exploring meditation, or simply open to something transformative. Purchase in-studio or contact us by email.

◈

Corporate Wellness

We work with organisations to offer float programmes as part of executive wellness and high-performance initiatives. Bulk session packages, private studio hire, and on-site float tank installations are available. Contact us at corporate@zerofloat.com to discuss your organisation's needs.
