HOW WE ENGAGE SCIENCE

Our evidence-based approach to science communication draws on learning science, exhibit design research and longitudinal impact studies.

Research Pillars

OUR METHODOLOGY

01
LEARNING SCIENCE

We base all exhibit design on peer-reviewed learning science research, testing for curiosity activation, comprehension and retention in collaboration with university education departments.

02
EXHIBIT EVALUATION

Every exhibit undergoes a three-phase evaluation cycle: formative (design), summative (post-launch) and remediation (ongoing improvement) — ensuring effectiveness over time.

03
EDUCATOR RESEARCH

Our education research team conducts annual studies on STEM career aspiration, tracking whether Nexus visits correlate with students' long-term science engagement.

04
PUBLIC ENGAGEMENT THEORY

We contribute to national and international literature on science communication, publishing findings on participatory exhibit design and informal learning environments.
