Our Institution

WHERE SCIENCE MEETS EVERYONE

Nexus Science Pavilion is the country's largest interactive science centre, dedicated to making frontier research accessible, exciting and relevant to all audiences.

BUILT FOR CURIOSITY

When Nexus opened its doors in 2002, it represented a new kind of science institution — one that put participation ahead of observation. Our founders believed that science literacy was a civic right, not a privilege reserved for those with a university degree.

Over two decades, that founding philosophy has shaped everything: how we design exhibits, train our communicators, partner with schools and measure our impact. Today, 450,000 visitors per year experience science as something they do — not something done to them.

Our 14,000 m² facility is home to eight permanent exhibition halls, a live demonstration laboratory, a digital learning studio and a 240-seat lecture theatre hosting the country's leading science communication events.

What Drives Us

OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

RADICAL ACCESSIBILITY

Free school visits for state-funded schools. British Sign Language tours. Sensory-friendly hours. Science belongs to everyone, without exception.

LIVE SCIENCE

Our resident researchers don't work in hidden laboratories — they work in glass-fronted labs where visitors can watch, ask questions and sometimes participate.

CRITICAL THINKING

We don't just teach facts. We teach method, uncertainty and how to question evidence — skills as important as the science itself.

CLIMATE ACTION

Nexus runs entirely on renewable energy and has been carbon-neutral since 2019. Our Climate Hall dedicates 1,800 m² to the science of climate change and solutions.
