Bespoke Commissions

Something Made
Only for You

A Terra Forma commission begins not with clay, but with conversation. We want to understand your space, your story, and what you hope the piece will hold — not just materially, but emotionally.

01

Initial Inquiry

Complete the form and we will arrange a consultation call or studio visit within 5 working days.

02

Design Consultation

A 90-minute conversation — in person or by video — to understand your vision, your space, and your brief.

03

Proposal & Agreement

We present a detailed proposal: form, clay body, glaze treatment, dimensions, timeline, and price. A 30% deposit confirms the commission.

04

Making & Delivery

Timelines vary by complexity — typically 4 to 18 months. We provide regular updates, including photographs from the studio. Worldwide shipping available.

Thank you — your inquiry has been received. A member of our studio team will be in touch within 5 working days to arrange your initial consultation.

Studio Visits

Come and See the Work

Our studio in Shoreditch is open to visitors by appointment. Whether you are a collector, a prospective commission client, or simply someone who loves beautiful objects, we welcome you to experience the studio and the gallery in person.

Gallery Visit

Browse the current collection at your own pace. Our team is on hand to share the story of each piece, its materials, and its making process.

Complimentary — Tuesday to Saturday, 10am–4pm, by appointment

Studio Tour

A guided 90-minute tour of the working studio — see the wheels, the clay store, the kilns, and pieces in various stages of making. Includes private gallery viewing.

£45 per person · Min. 2 / Max. 6 people

Commission Consultation Visit

For those considering a bespoke commission, this dedicated 90-minute visit includes a full studio tour, private collection viewing, and an initial design conversation with the team.

Complimentary for commission inquiries
