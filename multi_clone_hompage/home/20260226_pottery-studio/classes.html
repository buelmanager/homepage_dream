Your Experience

What to Expect

Every Terra Forma workshop is designed around the complete experience — from the moment you arrive to the moment your pieces are fired and ready to collect.

  • 01

    Premium Clay & Materials

    All clay, tools, glazes, and aprons are provided. We use the same clay bodies and materials as our commission pieces.

  • 02

    Small Group Instruction

    A maximum of six students per class ensures genuine individual attention from our instructors throughout the session.

  • 03

    Studio Lunch

    Full-day workshops include a studio lunch — seasonal and locally sourced, served in the gallery space among finished pieces.

  • 04

    Kiln Firing Included

    All pieces are bisque and glaze fired after the workshop. Finished ceramics are ready to collect or ship within four weeks.

  • 05

    Post-Workshop Support

    All students receive access to our private studio resource library and one follow-up consultation with their instructor.

Meet the Teachers

Your Instructors

Eleanor Whitmore
Master Potter & Co-founder

With forty years at the wheel and international recognition for her singular contribution to British ceramics, Eleanor brings an extraordinary depth of knowledge and a patient, generative teaching philosophy. Her Private Masterclass programme is one of the most sought-after one-to-one teaching experiences in British craft.

Private Masterclass Intermediate Throwing
Thomas Okoro
Senior Instructor & Kiln Master

Thomas trained at the Royal College of Art before joining Terra Forma in 2009. He is internationally recognised for his expertise in alternative firing techniques — particularly anagama, salt, and raku firing. His workshops are known for their rigorous technical grounding and unexpected warmth.
