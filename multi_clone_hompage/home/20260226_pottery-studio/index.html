Our Method

From Earth
to Heirloom

01

The Clay

We source our clay from three distinct quarries in Devon, Cornwall and the Scottish Highlands — each offering a unique mineral fingerprint that shapes the final character of every piece.

02

The Wheel

Thrown by hand on traditional kick-wheels, each form emerges through an intimate dialogue between maker and material. No two pieces are ever identical — nor should they be.

03

The Fire

Our anagama kiln reaches 1300°C over 72 hours of wood-firing. The flame itself becomes the final artist, leaving its mark in the glaze, the ash deposits, and the quiet perfection of imperfection.
