Our Institution

132 YEARS OF MARITIME LEADERSHIP

Meridian Port Authority was established by Act of Parliament in 1892 as a trust port — operating in the public interest with no shareholders and all surpluses reinvested in infrastructure. Today we manage the nation's second-largest commercial port, handling 42 million tonnes of cargo annually.

  • 78 operational berths across 4 specialist terminals
  • 14 km of deep-water quayside
  • 7,800 direct employees in the port estate
  • £2.8 billion annual contribution to the regional economy
  • ISO 14001 Environmental Management certified since 2006
Operations

THE PORT LOGISTICS CYCLE

Stage 01
VESSEL SCHEDULING

All vessel arrivals are coordinated 72 hours in advance through our Port Operations Centre, managing tide windows, berth allocation and service crew deployment.

Stage 02
PILOTAGE & BERTHING

Meridian's 42 licensed pilots guide vessels from the approaches through our 18-metre deep-water channel to their assigned berth, 24 hours a day.

Stage 03
CARGO HANDLING

Cranes, automated straddle carriers and conveyor systems work simultaneously to achieve the fastest possible cargo discharge and loading rates in the sector.

Stage 04
CUSTOMS & COMPLIANCE

Our integrated customs and border coordination ensures seamless inspection and clearance — reducing dwell time through pre-arrival documentation processing.

Stage 05
ONWARD DISTRIBUTION

Intermodal connections to rail, road and inland waterway ensure cargo moves efficiently from the port estate to final destinations across the country and Europe.

Our Legacy

132 YEARS OF COMMERCE

1892
Royal Charter

Established by Act of Parliament as a trust port, serving the region's growing industrial export economy.

1968
Container Revolution

First port in the country to convert a terminal to dedicated container handling, pioneering modern logistics.

2009
LNG Terminal Opens

Commissioning of the national LNG importation terminal, securing energy supply for 4 million homes.

2023
Green Port 2040

Launch of the industry-leading sustainability roadmap committing to net-zero port operations by 2040.
