Terminal Services

FOUR SPECIALIST TERMINALS

From containerised freight to liquefied natural gas, Meridian's four specialist terminals handle every cargo type with precision, speed and safety.

Terminal Operations

OUR SERVICES

A
Container Handling
CONTAINER TERMINAL

Our flagship terminal with 30 million TEU annual capacity. 12 ship-to-shore cranes, automated stacking yards and direct rail connection for seamless intermodal transfer.

B
Dry Bulk
BULK CARGO TERMINAL

Handling grain, coal, steel, ore and agricultural products. Four mobile harbour cranes, a 480,000-tonne grain silo and covered storage for weather-sensitive cargoes.

C
Roll-on / Roll-off
RO-RO & VEHICLE TERMINAL

Europe's second-largest vehicle logistics facility. 85,000 car storage spaces, pre-delivery inspection facilities and direct access to the national motorway network.

D
LNG & Offshore
ENERGY TERMINAL

National LNG importation terminal supplying gas for 4 million homes. Also providing offshore energy vessel support, crew transfer and subsea cable operations.
