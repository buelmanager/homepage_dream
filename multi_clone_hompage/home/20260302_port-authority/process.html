Port Infrastructure

BUILT FOR THE FUTURE OF TRADE

Meridian's infrastructure programme invests £400 million over the next decade in deeper channels, automated terminals and zero-emission quayside equipment.

Physical Infrastructure

PORT ESTATE & FACILITIES

01
DEEP WATER CHANNEL

An 18-metre navigable channel capable of accommodating ULCV vessels of up to 24,000 TEU. Maintained by our own dredging fleet to ensure year-round access.

02
QUAYSIDE EQUIPMENT

12 Post-Panamax ship-to-shore cranes, 48 rubber-tyred gantry cranes and a fleet of automated guided vehicles for the container terminal, all moving to electric power by 2030.

03
LOGISTICS ESTATE

280 hectares of port estate including 420,000 m² of covered warehousing, a temperature-controlled distribution centre and a 240-acre free zone for value-added processing.

04
INTERMODAL CONNECTIONS

Direct rail connection to the national network with 16 daily freight services. Dual-carriageway access to the motorway network and a new inland container depot 40 miles north.

Green Port 2040

NET-ZERO ROADMAP

2026

SHORE POWER ROLLOUT

All 78 berths connected to shore power, enabling vessels to shut down auxiliary engines while in port — eliminating 40,000 tonnes of CO2 annually.

2028

ELECTRIFIED QUAYSIDE

Full replacement of diesel rubber-tyred gantries with electric equivalents across all four terminals, reducing port-based emissions by 65%.

2032

GREEN HYDROGEN BUNKERING

Opening of the first green hydrogen bunkering facility in the UK, enabling zero-emission vessel fuel supply for early-adopter shipping lines.

2040

NET-ZERO PORT OPERATIONS

Complete elimination of Scope 1 and 2 emissions from all port operations — Meridian becomes the UK's first net-zero major commercial port.
