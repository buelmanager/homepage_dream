Our Institution

132 YEARS OF MARITIME AUTHORITY

Meridian Port Authority is a statutory trust port — operating in the public interest, with all surpluses reinvested in the infrastructure that powers our national trade.

BUILT ON PUBLIC TRUST

As a statutory trust port, Meridian is constitutionally obligated to serve the public interest. There are no shareholders extracting dividends — every surplus generated is reinvested in berths, equipment, sustainability programmes and community initiatives.

Our mandate, set by Parliament, is to ensure safe, efficient and sustainable port operations for the benefit of trade, the regional economy and the communities that depend on us. That mandate has guided 132 years of continuous operation through industrial revolution, two world wars and a digital age transformation.

Today, Meridian employs 7,800 people directly and sustains a further 24,000 jobs across the logistics supply chain, making us the largest single employer in the region.

What Drives Us

OUR OPERATING PRINCIPLES

SAFETY FIRST

Zero tolerance for unsafe practice. Our safety management system is certified to ISO 45001 and we have maintained zero fatalities for 14 consecutive years.

OPERATIONAL RELIABILITY

Shipping lines depend on Meridian's 99.2% berth availability rate. We maintain standby equipment and redundant systems to guarantee uptime.

ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP

Our Green Port 2040 roadmap commits to net-zero operations. Shore power, electrified equipment and an end to diesel quayside machinery by 2032.

COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP

£12 million invested annually in the community fund — supporting schools, social housing initiatives and apprenticeship programmes for local young people.
