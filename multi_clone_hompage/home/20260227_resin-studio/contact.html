Our Commitments

Why Commission ARDENT

True Originals

Every ARDENT piece is guaranteed unique. We produce no editions, no series, no replications. The chemistry of resin ensures it — but we also commit to it in writing.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, no surprise invoices. After your consultation we provide a comprehensive, fixed-price quote. What you agree to is exactly what you pay.

Lifetime Support

Your relationship with ARDENT doesn't end at delivery. Our artisans are available for care advice, restoration consultations, and questions — for as long as you own your piece.
