Creation Process

Six Steps.
No Shortcuts.

Every ARDENT commission follows a meticulous process developed over five years of experimentation, failure, and mastery. This is how fire becomes form.

How We Create

The ARDENT Method

01
Consultation & Concept

Every great piece begins with an honest conversation. We explore your space, your sensibility, your emotional brief. What should this piece make you feel? What story should it hold? We listen before we touch a single tool.

01
02
Palette & Material Selection

We develop a custom pigment formula for your commission — blending metallic powders, mica flakes, alcohol inks, and our proprietary earth pigments to create a palette that exists nowhere else. Your colors, mixed once, for you alone.

02
03
Surface Preparation

If the commission involves wood — and many do — the live edges are treated, sealed, and leveled to perfection. Molds are built. Surfaces primed. Everything must be exactly right before a single drop of resin is poured, because there are no corrections once chemistry begins.

03
04
The Pour

This is where craft meets controlled chaos. Resin is poured in timed, layered sequences. Heat guns introduce movement. Trowels guide rivers of pigment. We work in real time with a medium that waits for no one — creating patterns that can never be exactly replicated.

04
05
Cure & Refinement

Every piece cures in our climate-controlled environment for a minimum of 72 hours — temperature and humidity monitored constantly. Afterward, a 12-stage sanding process brings the surface to an optically clear, mirror-level finish. This alone takes two full days.

05
06
Delivery & Installation

White-glove delivery and installation is included with every commission. We build custom crating for each piece, personally oversee transport, and install on-site if required. You receive a certificate of authenticity, care instructions, and a direct line to Elena for any questions — forever.
