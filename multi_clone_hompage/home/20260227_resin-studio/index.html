The Atelier

A Studio
Born of Flame

"Resin is a living medium. Every pour is a negotiation between intention and entropy — and beauty emerges in that tension."

Founded in 2019 in the hills of Portland, Oregon, ARDENT began as a single artist's obsession with the molten language of epoxy resin. Today our atelier houses three master artisans, a climate-controlled pour room, and a growing archive of pigment blends developed exclusively for our studio.

How We Work

From Vision
to Vessel

01
Consultation

We begin every commission with a deep conversation — exploring your space, your aesthetic sensibility, and the emotional resonance you seek in the finished piece.

02
Palette Design

Our artisans develop a custom pigment formula specific to your commission, blending metallic powders, alcohol inks, and proprietary colorants to achieve your exact vision.

03
The Pour

The heart of our craft. Timed with precision, the resin is poured in controlled layers, each cured before the next is added. Heat guns and motion create the organic patterns that define ARDENT's aesthetic.

04
Curing & Finishing

Each piece cures in our temperature-controlled environment for 72 hours minimum. Final sanding through 12 grits and UV-resistant topcoat application ensures museum-quality durability.

05
Delivery

White-glove delivery and installation is included with every commission. Each piece ships in custom-fabricated crating with a certificate of authenticity and care instructions.
