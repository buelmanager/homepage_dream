Our Story

Born of
Ember & Obsession

ARDENT began not with a business plan, but with a compulsion — an artist's need to capture the living, breathing beauty of fire in permanent form. This is the story of how craft becomes art.

The Founder

Elena Marsh

Elena's background in chemistry gave her an unusual lens through which to understand resin — not as a craft material, but as a responsive, living medium governed by physics and temperature and time. After a decade working as a materials scientist, she walked away in 2019 to pursue what she calls "the most honest work I've ever done."

"I don't make the art. I create the conditions. The resin, the pigment, the heat — they make the art. My job is to know when to guide and when to let go."
Elena Marsh
Founder & Lead Artisan, ARDENT Studio
How We Believe

Four Principles
That Guide Our Craft

Material Truth

We never disguise what our materials are. Resin is resin — extraordinary in its own right. We celebrate its translucency, its depth, its chemical personality rather than making it imitate something else.

Controlled Chaos

The most breathtaking patterns emerge from a precise calibration of order and disorder. We understand the chemistry deeply enough to know exactly which variables to release to chance — and that knowledge is our art.

Singular Objects

Every ARDENT piece is unique. We produce no editions, no series, no replications. When we say your commission is one of a kind, we mean it in the most absolute sense — the chemistry ensures it.

Relentless Refinement

Our process has been tested, failed, and rebuilt hundreds of times. Each commission teaches us something new about the medium. We apply that knowledge to every piece that follows, making our craft perpetually sharper.
