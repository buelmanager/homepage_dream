Responsibility

Built to Give
Back to the Earth

Every booking funds direct ecological restoration. We are not guests in nature — we are its stewards.

01

Carbon-Neutral Materials

All structural timber is FSC-certified reclaimed wood. Glass panels use low-embodied-carbon production. Zero poured concrete on site.

02

Rewilding Programme

4,800 m² of previously degraded land is actively rewilded per annum through native planting and wildlife corridor restoration.

03

Solar & Rainwater Systems

100% off-grid energy. Roof-integrated solar panels and grey water recycling. Heating via biomass from on-site coppicing.
