Our Story

The Art of
The Mountain

Since 2009

Born From
Obsession

SUMMITS was founded in Verbier in 2009 by former Swiss national freeride champion Lucien Marchand. After a decade of guiding the world's elite to the finest hidden terrain, he realised that truly exceptional alpine experiences required something no luxury travel agency could provide — the intimate knowledge of a mountain life lived completely.

"We do not simply book ski trips. We compose them — note by note, run by run, moment by moment."
— Lucien Marchand, Founder

Today, SUMMITS operates across four of the world's most demanding and rewarding mountain environments. Our 84-strong guide collective holds every major international certification and collectively represents over 1,400 seasons of alpine expertise. We have never lost a guest, and we have never once compromised on what the mountain demands of us.

We take on fewer than 200 private clients per year. This is a deliberate choice. The mountain rewards attention.

Our Values

The SUMMITS
Philosophy

01
Absolute Safety

Every SUMMITS programme begins with a detailed snowpack briefing, real-time avalanche assessment, and a private weather consultation. No run is ever taken unless conditions are verified by both our guide and our in-house meteorologist. The mountain does not forgive impatience, and neither do we.

02
Radical Exclusivity

We hold exclusive landing rights on 40+ private summits across the Alps and Rockies. We limit annual membership to 200 clients — not because we cannot grow, but because we refuse to. Exclusivity is not a marketing word at SUMMITS. It is the entire model.

03
Deep Memory

Every preference, every turn you favour, every run that made you catch your breath — we record it and recall it. Your second season with SUMMITS is built entirely on the intelligence of your first. The mountain forgets nothing. Neither do we.
