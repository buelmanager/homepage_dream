What's Included

Every Detail
Considered

Private Butler

Your dedicated SUMMITS butler is available 18 hours a day — from dawn coffee to après arrangements. They know your preferences before you arrive.

Chef Service

A private chef trained at three-Michelin-star kitchens prepares every meal — from sunrise fuel to post-ski dinners worthy of a mountain setting.

Equipment Tuning

Your skis are tuned nightly to the following day's conditions. Edge sharpness, wax selection, and boot heat are all calibrated before dawn.

24/7 Concierge Line

A direct number answered by a SUMMITS alpine expert at any hour. Weather questions, reservation changes, medical contacts — we handle it all.

Spa & Recovery

In-chalet treatment rooms with sports physiotherapy, infrared sauna, and hydrotherapy pool. Recovery is as important as the descent.

Cellar Selection

Each chalet is stocked with a curated wine and spirits selection personally chosen by our in-house sommelier. Burgundy for après, Champagne for summits.

Private Transfer

Arrival by private helicopter or chauffeured Mercedes. Transfers between chalets, restaurants and helipads are handled without exception.

Children's Programme

Certified ski instructors for children from age 3. Mountain art workshops, snowshoeing, and supervised après activities are available on request.

Booking Process

How It Works

01
Private Consultation

A 60-minute call with your dedicated SUMMITS concierge. We learn your ability, your preferences, your ideal party size, and your dream of what a ski week looks like.

02
Curated Proposal

Within 48 hours we send a bespoke proposal: chalet, guide, helicopter itinerary, and restaurant reservations — all aligned with your consultation notes.

03
Confirmation & Preparation

Upon confirmation, we begin the mountain preparation: snowpack monitoring, equipment profiling, provisional weather briefings, and pre-arrival arrangements.

04
Arrive. Ski. Remember.

Your guide meets you at the chalet door. The mountain is ready. The only thing you need to decide is which run to take first.
