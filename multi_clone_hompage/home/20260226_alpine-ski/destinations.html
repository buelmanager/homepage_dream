Verbier

The Freeride Capital of the World

Verbier commands 400km of ski terrain across four connected valleys. Its off-piste terrain is the most technical and rewarding in the Alps — the Vallon d'Arby, the Stairway to Heaven, and the infamous Mont Fort couloirs are known by name to every serious freeride skier on earth. SUMMITS holds exclusive access to powder bowls that never appear on any piste map.

Chamonix

Where Alpinism Was Born

Beneath the shadow of Mont Blanc, Chamonix offers the most dramatic skiing on the planet. The Vallée Blanche — 20km of untracked glacial descent from the Aiguille du Midi at 3,842m — is an experience unlike any other in skiing. SUMMITS guides hold exclusive early-morning access permits, allowing you to descend before the first public cable car arrives.

Zermatt

Under the Eye of the Matterhorn

Zermatt skiing is unique on earth. At 3,883m, Klein Matterhorn offers Europe's highest-altitude lift-accessed terrain — and the Matterhorn itself casts its unmistakable shadow across every run. The glacier is open year-round, and in January the powder below the Rothorn is as pristine as anything in the Alps. We ski here where no public piste map will ever lead you.
