FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For standard shoots, we recommend 5–10 business days. Events and productions requiring permits in controlled airspace need a minimum of 14 days. For international work, allow 4–6 weeks for all permits and logistics. Emergency same-day deployment is available through our Cinema tier with prior arrangement.

What happens if weather conditions prevent flying?

We monitor weather windows carefully during pre-production. If conditions are unsafe on the scheduled day, we reschedule at no additional cost. Our DJI Matrice 350 operates in wind and light rain conditions that would ground other operators, so we can often fly in marginal conditions others cannot. We'll always brief you honestly on what's achievable.

Do you handle airspace permissions and insurance?

Yes — completely. We manage all NOTAM submissions, CAA permissions, local authority notifications, and ATC coordination as part of every project. We carry £5M public liability insurance and can provide certificates on request. You simply provide us the location and date, and we handle everything.

What format will I receive the final files in?

Standard delivery is high-resolution JPEG for stills (retouched, colour-graded), and H.264/H.265 MP4 for video in your specified resolution. RAW files (DNG for stills, BRAW/R3D for video) are available on request. We deliver via secure WeTransfer Pro or can upload directly to your server or cloud storage.

Can we use the imagery for commercial purposes?

All deliverables come with a commercial usage licence covering digital and print use for the commissioned project. Broadcast licensing, editorial syndication, and expanded usage rights are available — just discuss your requirements at the enquiry stage and we'll include appropriate licensing in your proposal.
