Core Services

Four Disciplines.
One Studio.

Each service is delivered end-to-end: pre-production, flight operations, post-production, and delivery.

01
Architecture & Construction

Comprehensive aerial documentation for architects, developers, and construction firms. From concept renders to completion photography, we create imagery that communicates scale, context, and design intent.

What's Included
  • Pre-flight location survey and NOTAM filing
  • Multi-altitude capture in RAW format
  • Photogrammetry survey (on request)
  • Full-res retouched stills delivery
  • 4K or 6K cinematic grade video
  • 24-hour rapid stills turnaround available
02
Real Estate & Property

Transformative aerial perspective for residential and commercial property marketing. We capture not just the property, but the lifestyle — surroundings, connectivity, landscape, and views that ground-level photography simply cannot convey.

What's Included
  • Exterior aerials from multiple altitudes
  • Context shots showing local amenities
  • Drone flythrough video for listings
  • Virtual tour integration support
  • Same-week delivery standard
  • Licensing for all major portals included
03
Events & Live Coverage

Real-time aerial coverage of festivals, sporting events, corporate gatherings, and public ceremonies. With live downlink capability, we deliver broadcast-ready feeds directly to production trucks or streaming infrastructure.

What's Included
  • Pre-event flight planning and permits
  • Live HD downlink to production gallery
  • Multi-drone deployment for large events
  • Rapid same-day edited highlights
  • Full event archival in RAW format
  • Contingency plans for all weather conditions
04
Cinematic & Film Production

RED camera operator partnerships for feature films, commercials, and documentary productions. We operate gyro-stabilised cinema-grade platforms capable of carrying ARRI, RED, and Sony cinema cameras for the most demanding productions.

What's Included
  • Full pre-production creative collaboration
  • ARRI / RED / Sony cinema payload options
  • On-set director of photography support
  • Colour-managed RAW deliverables
  • Offline rough-cut available same day
  • EASA / FAA international permits managed

Airspace & Permits

We Handle
All Permissions

Operating drones commercially in the UK requires navigating complex airspace regulations. Every flight we conduct is planned and operated in full compliance with CAA regulations, NOTAM restrictions, and local authority requirements.

We manage all permit applications, airspace notifications, and coordination with airports and NATS (National Air Traffic Services) as part of every project. You simply brief us — we handle the rest.
