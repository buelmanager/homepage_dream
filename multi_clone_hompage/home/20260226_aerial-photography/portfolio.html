Featured Case Study

Canary Wharf
Tower Complex

Commissioned by a leading architecture firm to capture a complex of four interconnected high-rises during the final construction phase. The brief called for a comprehensive documentation suite — structural aerials for the engineers, marketing assets for the developers, and time-lapse sequences for the press launch.

Over three separate flight sessions at sunrise, golden hour, and dusk, we captured 4,200+ frames across 6K RAW and 8K stills. The final deliverable included a 4-minute brand film, 40 hero stills for print, and a photogrammetry point-cloud survey model.
