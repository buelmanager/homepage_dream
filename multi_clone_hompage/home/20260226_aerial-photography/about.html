The Story Behind
the Lens

ALTITUDE was born from a simple conviction: that the most transformative perspective is the one nobody has seen yet. Founded in 2012 by James Holbrook and a team of aviation and filmmaking veterans, our London studio has grown into one of the UK's most respected aerial cinematography houses.

We began as a two-person operation, flying early-generation drones for architectural surveys. Today, we operate a fleet of cinema-grade aerial platforms, collaborate with RED Digital Cinema, and have captured imagery for some of the world's most recognised brands, broadcasters, and property developers.

What hasn't changed is our relentless pursuit of the perfect frame. Every mission begins with meticulous pre-flight planning, every shot is composed with the same care as ground-level cinematography, and every delivery is colour-graded to the highest broadcast standard.

What We Believe

Our Principles

◆
Safety First, Always

Every mission begins with a comprehensive risk assessment. We hold full CAA PfCO and FAA Part 107 certifications with £5M public liability insurance. No creative brief outweighs operational safety.

◆
Cinema-Grade Imagery

We refuse to compromise on image quality. Every platform is matched to the specific requirements of each project — from 20MP RAW stills to 6K RAW cinema files with 17+ stops of dynamic range.

◆
Meticulous Planning

Great aerial work is 80% preparation. We conduct thorough location surveys, NOTAMs, weather analysis, and shot-list development before a single rotor turns.

◆
Creative Partnership

We don't just execute briefs — we develop them. Our creative input has transformed countless projects from good to extraordinary, from unexpected angles to impossible lighting windows.

◆
On-Time Delivery

Broadcast deadlines, property listing dates, event schedules — we understand the commercial imperative of timely delivery and have a 98.7% on-schedule record across all projects.

◆
Global Reach

Based in London but licensed to operate across Europe and internationally. We manage international flight permissions, customs carnets, and local partnership requirements on your behalf.

Credentials

Licensed, Certified
& Insured

CAA PfCO

UK Civil Aviation Authority Permission for Commercial Operations. All pilots hold current PfCO with annual renewal and operational manual compliance.

FAA Part 107

Federal Aviation Administration Remote Pilot Certificate for US operations. Enables legal commercial drone work across all 50 US states.

GVC Certified

General Visual Line of Sight Certificate under the new EASA/UK UAS regulation framework. Required for specific category operations.

£5M Public Liability

Comprehensive public liability insurance to £5,000,000. Covers all commercial operations, equipment, and third-party property. Certificates available on request.
