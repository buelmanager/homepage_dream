Geothermal
Mineral Pools

Our six outdoor pools are fed continuously by the hydrothermal vent beneath IGNIS, each with a distinct mineral profile and temperature. The water carries silica at 350mg/L — the highest concentration of any publicly accessible geothermal pool in Iceland — producing the characteristic opalescent shimmer and silky texture guests describe as "water unlike any other."

A guided mineral circuit walks guests through each pool in a scientifically designed sequence, beginning with the cooler Insight Pool at 28°C and ascending through the Deep Heat Pool at 43°C, ending in the Transition Cold Spring to seal the circulatory cycle.

Volcanic Ash
Ritual

Practiced by Icelandic healers since the medieval period, volcanic ash treatments draw on the extraordinary mineral density of basaltic tephra. Our ash is harvested from the 2010 Eyjafjallajökull eruption deposits — aged a minimum of 10 years in flowing glacial water to remove soluble salts and concentrate therapeutic silicates.

Applied as a heated body mask and left to draw for 45 minutes in an obsidian-heated chamber, the ritual draws impurities from the skin while depositing trace minerals. The session concludes with a cold volcanic spring rinse and mineral infusion balm, unique to IGNIS.

Obsidian
Steam Chambers

Carved directly into a natural volcanic rock formation 14 meters below the surface, our three obsidian steam chambers receive volcanic steam directly from geological fissures. No machinery, no boilers — raw geothermal steam enters through channels we cleared, not created.

The naturally formed obsidian walls hold heat with extraordinary efficiency and radiate far-infrared energy that penetrates deeply into muscle tissue. Each chamber seats four guests on carved basalt benches. Sessions are private, 45 minutes, with a guided breathing protocol taught by our resident breathwork practitioners.

Aurora
Glass Pods

Set on the volcanic plain 800m from the main lodge, our twelve aurora pods are the most intimate way to experience Iceland's greatest spectacle. Each pod features a 180° curved glass ceiling engineered by Norwegian specialists who design arctic observation facilities, with anti-condensation heating that keeps the glass at 2°C above ambient dew point at all times.

Inside, hand-laid basalt walls and obsidian heated floors create a deeply warm cocoon even as temperatures outside fall below −20°C. The IGNIS aurora alert system monitors geomagnetic KP indices in real time and wakes guests with a gentle amber glow only when aurora activity reaches KP4 or above — ensuring you sleep through ordinary nights but wake for the extraordinary.
