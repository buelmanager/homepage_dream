The Geology

Powered by
Iceland's Heart

IGNIS sits directly above a hydrothermal system fed by one of Iceland's most active volcanic zones. Our waters travel through 3km of volcanic rock before reaching you.

3km

Water Journey

Every liter of mineral water travels three kilometers through volcanic basalt, absorbing silica, magnesium, and trace minerals along the way.

82%

Renewable Energy

Our entire facility runs on geothermal energy drawn from the same volcanic system that heats our pools — zero carbon impact.

4,000

Years of Flow

The hydrothermal vent beneath IGNIS has been flowing continuously for over four millennia, witnessed by Norse settlers.
