Getting Here

Arrival
Logistics

IGNIS is located in Iceland's southern highlands, 3.5 hours from Reykjavik by private vehicle. We handle all ground logistics from your international arrival point.

1

Fly to Reykjavik

Keflavik International Airport receives direct flights from most major European and North American cities. We recommend flying via Icelandair for the widest route network.

2

Airport Meet & Greet

Your dedicated IGNIS concierge meets you at arrivals with a name card. No navigation, no taxis — just warmth and a volcanic mineral water welcome pack for the journey.

3

Private Highland Transfer

3.5 hours through some of Iceland's most extraordinary landscapes in a heated 4x4 vehicle. Your concierge narrates the geology of every volcanic formation you pass.

4

Arrive at IGNIS

Your pod or lodge room is prepared. First pool access begins 30 minutes after arrival. There is no check-in desk — just a warm towel and your first mineral pool.

Frequently Asked

Common
Questions

How far in advance should I book?

+

For aurora season (October–March), we recommend booking 8–12 months in advance, particularly for aurora pods. The Lava Flow and Magma packages in peak aurora season often sell out within 2 weeks of availability being released. For spring and summer stays, 3–4 months advance is generally sufficient.

Is IGNIS suitable for solo travellers?

+

Absolutely. Approximately 30% of our guests arrive solo. All packages are priced per person and our rooms and pods accommodate single occupancy with no supplement. Solo guests frequently describe IGNIS as one of the most comfortable solo travel experiences they have had — the spa environment naturally facilitates connection without pressure.

What is the cancellation policy?

+

Reservations cancelled more than 90 days before arrival receive a full refund minus a €150 administration fee. Cancellations 60–90 days prior receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 60 days are non-refundable but transferable to another guest or converted to a gift voucher valid for 24 months. We strongly recommend travel insurance.

Are there age restrictions?

+

IGNIS is designed for adult guests aged 18 and above. Guests aged 16–17 may access the mineral pools when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Children under 16 are not permitted on property. We believe this creates the atmosphere of deep quiet and restoration that defines the IGNIS experience.

What should I pack?

+

We provide high-quality robes, slippers, towels, and a mineral water kit for all guests. For pool use, bring a swimsuit (or purchase one from our boutique). For outdoor aurora viewing, we provide thermal outerwear — Iceland's weather is unpredictable and we ensure you are always equipped. We recommend bringing personal medications and any specialist skincare products your skin requires.

Is there mobile phone signal at IGNIS?

+

There is intermittent 4G signal at IGNIS — we sit in a highland valley where coverage is partial. We provide WiFi in the lodge, but deliberately leave the aurora pods free of WiFi to encourage full disconnection. Many guests tell us the partial phone signal was one of the most unexpected gifts of their stay.
