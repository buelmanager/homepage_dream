Origin Story

A Geologist's
Discovery

In the summer of 2011, geologist Dr. Sigrid Magnusdóttir was mapping a previously uncharted hydrothermal system in Iceland's southern highlands when she encountered something extraordinary — a cluster of volcanic vents producing water of exceptional mineral purity at precisely the temperatures optimal for therapeutic bathing.

The site sat above a magma intrusion at unusual shallowness, just 2.8km below the surface, creating natural heating far more intense and consistent than Iceland's famous Blue Lagoon. The water chemistry was unlike anything she had documented in three decades of field research.

"I knew in that moment this place was not meant to be studied. It was meant to be experienced."

— Dr. Sigrid Magnusdóttir, Founder

Eight years of geological surveys, regulatory consultation, and architectural planning followed. IGNIS opened in the winter of 2019, during the peak of the aurora season, to its first twelve guests — each sworn to secrecy until the official opening.

Geothermal Science

Why Our Waters
Are Different

Most geothermal spas use water that has been diluted, treated, or artificially heated. At IGNIS, every pool is fed by a single-source hydrothermal vent with water chemistry unchanged from its volcanic origin.

01

Silica Suspension

Our water carries dissolved silica at 350mg/L — significantly higher than most geothermal sources. Silica is clinically linked to improved skin elasticity, reduced inflammation, and accelerated cell renewal. It is also responsible for the distinctive opalescent appearance of our pools.

02

Mineral Stratification

Distinct pools are fed from different depths of the hydrothermal system, producing naturally varying mineral profiles. The Obsidian Pool draws from 2.1km depth, rich in magnesium and boron. The Solar Pool draws from 800m, with higher sulfur content and antimicrobial properties.

03

Thermotherapeutic Range

We maintain six distinct temperature zones from 28°C to 43°C across our pool circuit, designed to progressively dilate blood vessels, reduce cortisol levels, and promote deep muscular relaxation. The sequence follows protocols developed in collaboration with the University of Reykjavik Wellness Research Institute.

Sustainability

Born from the Earth,
Returned to It

IGNIS was designed from its first sketch with a fundamental principle: the volcanic system that gives us our purpose must be protected absolutely. Every operational decision is governed by this commitment.

◉

100% Geothermal Energy

Every kilowatt powering IGNIS — from pool heating to aurora pod climate systems — derives from the geothermal vent beneath us. We draw no energy from Iceland's national grid and produce zero carbon emissions from operations.

0 kg CO₂
◈

Closed-Loop Water System

Used pool water is cooled, filtered through volcanic basalt, and reinjected 1.4km underground — returning to the hydrothermal cycle naturally. We extract no more than the vent naturally produces and have never disrupted flow rates.

100% Return
◐

Icelandic Highland Protection

IGNIS operates on a 40-year environmental stewardship covenant with the Icelandic Environment Agency. We fund annual highland moss restoration, maintain a 500m wildlife buffer zone, and report quarterly to an independent ecological review board.

500m Buffer
◎

Zero-Waste Food Programme

Our kitchen sources exclusively from within 80km — Icelandic lamb, arctic char, foraged highland herbs, and skyr from a cooperative farm 12km away. Food waste is composted and returned to our kitchen garden. We have achieved Zero Waste certification since 2022.
