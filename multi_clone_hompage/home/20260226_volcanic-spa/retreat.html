Every IGNIS
Stay Includes

Regardless of package, every IGNIS guest receives the same foundational commitment to luxury, privacy, and volcanic excellence.

◉

Unlimited Pool Access

All six geothermal mineral pools, open from 06:00 to 23:00 daily. Maximum 24 guests on property means true solitude is always possible.

◈

Nordic Breakfast

Fresh skyr, smoked arctic char, highland-foraged berries, sourdough from our woodfire oven, and single-origin Icelandic coffee. Served until 11:00.

◐

Welcome Volcanic Kit

Each guest receives the IGNIS mineral water collection — four formulations from different vent depths — plus volcanic ash body soap and an obsidian smooth stone.

◎

Geothermal Orientation

A private 60-minute orientation with our geological team explains the volcanic system beneath your feet, the mineral science of each pool, and how to use the circuit for maximum benefit.

◉

24-Hour Wellness Concierge

One dedicated team member is assigned to each guest at check-in. They handle all scheduling, dietary requests, and any special arrangement — available around the clock.

◈

Return Airport Transfer

Private vehicle transfer from Reykjavik Keflavik International Airport. Helicopter transfer available on The Magma package for an additional charge.

Exclusive Hire

Private
IGNIS

For groups of 12 or more, or for extraordinary occasions — weddings, private corporate retreats, significant celebrations — IGNIS is available for exclusive hire. During private hire, the entire facility operates solely for your group: all 12 aurora pods, all 6 thermal pools, all treatment facilities, and the complete kitchen team.

Private hire enquiries are handled personally by our Founder. We accept a maximum of 10 exclusive hire bookings per year, and availability is extremely limited.
