135 Years
Under Sail

The Blue Water Sailing Society was founded in 1889 by twelve Solent sailors with a shared obsession: to sail beyond the horizon and discover what lies beyond.

One hundred and thirty-five years later, we have completed 62 ocean passages and produced some of the finest offshore sailors in the British tradition.

Our Values

What We Sail For

01
Excellence in Seamanship

We hold seamanship to the highest standard. Our training programme, mentoring culture, and race results speak to a society that takes the craft of sailing seriously.

02
Safety at Sea

No race is worth a life. Our safety protocols are rigorous and our safety culture uncompromising. Every skipper who sails under the Blue Water burgee is responsible for their crew.

03
Ocean Fellowship

The bonds formed on an ocean passage — through storms and calms, sleepless nights and brilliant dawns — are unlike any others. We cherish and cultivate them.

04
Open to All

We welcome sailors of all backgrounds and abilities. The sea cares nothing for your background — only your commitment and your seamanship.
