Restoration Programme

Saving What
the Centuries
Have Kept

INTONACO's restoration programme is directed by Maestro Chiara Moretti, whose training at the Opificio delle Pietre Dure and the Hermitage Museum gives her an unmatched command of consolidation, inpainting, and preventative conservation.

We undertake restoration commissions on fresco cycles from the 13th through 19th centuries, working in close partnership with local heritage authorities and private owners. All restoration work is reversible and fully documented to the highest conservation standards.
