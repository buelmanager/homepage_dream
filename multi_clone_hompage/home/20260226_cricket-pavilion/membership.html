The Brotherhood

Membership Cannot Be Purchased. Only Earned.

The Crease admits no more than twelve new members in any calendar year. Every candidate must be nominated by two existing members in good standing and endorsed by the Committee.

This is not a subscription. It is a covenant — with the game, with the grounds, and with the brotherhood of those who have played and watched here since 1891.

If you believe you share our values, we invite you to enquire. The Secretary will correspond with you privately.

How to Join

The Application Process

The process reflects the seriousness with which we approach membership. We do not advertise. We do not accept unsolicited applications. We trust the judgment of our members.

01
Nomination
Two existing members of The Crease in good standing must nominate you in writing. Nominations open in January each year.
02
Correspondence
The Secretary will correspond with you privately to arrange an introduction. This is a conversation, not an interview.
03
Visit
You will be invited to visit the pavilion and grounds as a guest of your nominators — ideally during a match day.
04
Committee Review
The Committee meets in September. Candidates are reviewed, and the annual intake of no more than twelve is confirmed in October.

Important Notice

If you do not yet know two existing members, we encourage you to attend one of our public Heritage Open Days (held twice yearly) where introductions can be made under no obligation.
