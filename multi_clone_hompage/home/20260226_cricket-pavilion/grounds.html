Ground One — Est. 1891

The Pavilion Ground

The Pavilion Ground is the jewel of The Crease. Laid in 1891 and maintained by our head groundsman and two assistants year-round, it hosts all championship fixtures, invitational matches, and the Annual Gala.

The Pavilion Ground pitch is prepared exclusively on a North-South axis to avoid afternoon sun glare affecting the batsman. The Berkshire loam provides pace and carry through a full season, making for first-class conditions throughout the summer.

The famous covered pavilion veranda affords members unobstructed views from long-on to mid-off — regarded by many as the finest match-day viewing in England outside of Lord's.

Ground Two — Est. 1923

The Meadow Ground

Opened in 1923 on land donated by founding member Archibald Rowlandson, The Meadow Ground serves members' matches, coaching sessions, and the Academy's development programme. Intimate, pastoral, and utterly English.

The Meadow Ground offers a more intimate cricketing experience — surrounded by mature English oaks and a dry-stone boundary wall that has stood since the ground's opening. It is where junior members learn the game and where senior members enjoy afternoon fixtures without ceremony.

Members' Access

Fielder members may use The Meadow Ground. Pavilion members have access to both grounds. Contact the Secretary to book a slot.

The Code

Ground Regulations

01
Dress
Whites for playing members. Smart-casual attire minimum for spectating. No denim, no trainers, no exceptions. The standard is the standard.
02
Guests
Each member may bring up to two guests per fixture. Guests must be signed in at the secretary's office and are the member's responsibility throughout.
03
The Square
No member, guest, or visitor may enter the square without express permission from the head groundsman. This is non-negotiable.
04
Mobile Devices
Telephone calls are not permitted within 50 metres of the playing area during play. Silence is required when a bowler is at the crease.
05
Photography
Photography of other members requires their consent. Photography of the grounds for private use is warmly encouraged.
06
The Spirit
The spirit of cricket is our highest law. Dissent, poor sportsmanship, or conduct unbecoming a member of The Crease will result in formal review.
