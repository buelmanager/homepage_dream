The Pavilion

Where Cricket Meets Civilisation

Constructed in 1891 and sympathetically restored in 2008, the Grade II listed pavilion houses our long room, members' library, Michelin-recognised dining room, and a private bar stocked exclusively with single-estate bottles.

The Long Room overlooks the Pavilion Ground directly — so every luncheon is accompanied by the sound of leather on willow.

The Academy

Developing the Next Generation of Cricketers

Since 1968, The Crease Academy has produced three England Test cricketers and over forty county professionals. Our coaching faculty comprises former Test players committed to the classical traditions of the game.
