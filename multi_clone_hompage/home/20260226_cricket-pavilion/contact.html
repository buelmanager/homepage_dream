Find Us

Plan a Visit

The Crease is located in the village of Upper Compton, Berkshire — approximately 45 minutes from London Paddington by rail, and 90 minutes from central London by road.

Visits are by appointment only. Non-members are welcome as guests of existing members, or during our bi-annual Heritage Open Days.

By Rail
Train from London Paddington to Reading (30 min), then local taxi service to Upper Compton. Members can arrange collection on request.
By Road
M4 Junction 12, follow A4 westbound, then B4009 through Compton village. The pavilion gates are signed from the village green.
Parking
Member parking within the pavilion grounds. 45 spaces available. Overflow parking on Rowlandson Lane on match days.
Heritage Open Days
Held in April and September each year. Open to non-members for a day of cricket, pavilion tours, and Long Room luncheon.
