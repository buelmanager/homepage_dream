The Borealis Experience

Four Days at the
Edge of the World

I
Day 1 — Arrival

Welcome to Silence

Your private transfer arrives at dusk. Our lodge steward greets you with a warm cloudberry elixir and a briefing on the week's aurora forecast. The expedition begins at nightfall.

II
Day 2 — The Hunt

Aurora Vigil

Led by our aurora meteorologist to a pre-selected clearing with unobstructed sky. Camp chairs, a crackling fire, and thermoses of Nordic herbal tea. Then — the sky fractures open.

III
Day 3 — Depth

The Ice Cathedral

A full day inside a private glacier system. Lunch in a natural ice chamber. An afternoon of photography. A Saami ceremonial fire in the evening, guided by an indigenous elder.

IV
Day 4 — Farewell

Dawn Departure

A final breakfast as the first light turns the horizon amber. Each guest receives a bespoke print of their most extraordinary aurora photograph. Some return every season.
