Getting to Mandala

Travel Information

Lhasa Gonggar Airport

IATA: LXA

Direct connections from Chengdu (1h 45m), Chongqing, Beijing, Shanghai, and Kathmandu. All international guests transit through Chengdu or Beijing. Our team arranges private airport transfers.

The Monastery

Upper Lhasa Valley
3,650 metres altitude

90 minutes by private vehicle from the airport along the Yarlung Tsangpo valley road. We recommend arriving in daylight for the most extraordinary introduction to the landscape.

Tibet Travel Permits

Required for all foreign visitors in addition to a Chinese visa. Our concierge team manages the entire application process. Allow 3–4 weeks for permit processing. We handle this for every confirmed retreat guest at no extra charge.

Altitude Advisory

Lhasa sits at 3,650 metres. Most guests adapt within 24–48 hours. We include a mandatory rest day on arrival. Guests with cardiovascular or pulmonary conditions should consult their physician before booking.
