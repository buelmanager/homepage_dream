Sample Daily Rhythm

06:00

Dawn Meditation

Optional sunrise practice in the original meditation hall, led by Lama Karma Tsering. The altitude light at this hour is extraordinary.

07:30

Healing Breakfast

Traditional Tibetan tsampa porridge, butter tea, seasonal herbs, and Himalayan honey sourced from monastery hives.

09:30

Morning Treatment

Your primary treatment of the day — singing bowl session, hot stone ritual, or herbal steam bath, as scheduled by your healer.

13:00

Healing Lunch

19:00

Evening & Dinner

Shared dinner around the monastery hearth, followed by optional fire ceremony or private star-gazing from the rooftop terrace.

Getting Here

Travel Logistics

By Air

Lhasa Gonggar Airport (LXA) receives direct flights from Chengdu, Chongqing, Beijing, and Kathmandu. Our team arranges all ground transfers from the airport to the monastery in private vehicles — approximately 90 minutes along the Yarlung Tsangpo valley.

Altitude Acclimatisation

All guests are asked to arrive one day before their retreat begins. We include a complimentary acclimatisation day with gentle walks, altitude tea, and a soft orientation session. This is not optional — it is medical protocol at 3,650 metres.

Permits & Documentation

Travel to Tibet requires a Tibet Travel Permit in addition to a Chinese visa. Our concierge team manages the full permit application process on behalf of all guests — simply send your passport details and we handle the rest.

Health Requirements

We recommend guests consult their physician about altitude before travel. We advise against visiting if you have uncontrolled heart conditions or severe pulmonary disease. A health questionnaire is completed before booking is confirmed.
