The Healing Arts

Four Paths to Wholeness

Each treatment draws from centuries of Tibetan healing wisdom, administered by masters trained within our monastery's lineage.

Singing Bowl Therapy

Himalayan bronze bowls placed on and around the body, their resonant frequencies dissolving tension at a cellular level.

Hot Stone Ritual

Sacred river stones from the Yarlung Tsangpo, heated to precise temperatures, unlock deep muscular release and restore vital energy flows.

Herbal Steam Bath

Ancient formulas of juniper, artemisia, and alpine herbs combine in steam chambers carved from monastery stone.

Meditation Immersion

Guided stillness practices in chambers aligned with Tibetan astronomical wisdom, under the care of resident lama instructors.
