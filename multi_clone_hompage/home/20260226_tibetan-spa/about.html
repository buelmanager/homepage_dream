Our Story

The Monastery

Four centuries of unbroken healing tradition

Heritage

Born of Sacred Ground

Perched above the Lhasa Valley at 3,650 metres, our monastery was founded in 1623 by the Third Rinpoche of the Kengye lineage — a physician-monk renowned throughout Central Tibet for healing ailments that had defeated all other treatments.

After centuries of continuous operation, the monastery was painstakingly restored over twelve years using original construction methods, traditional pigments, and timber sourced from the same forests that supplied the original builders. Today it stands as both a living heritage site and the world's most authentic Tibetan healing destination.

1623

Foundation

The Third Kengye Rinpoche establishes a healing monastery combining Tibetan medicine, sound therapy, and contemplative practice.

1887

Golden Era

Under the Seventh Rinpoche, the monastery receives royal patronage and codifies its healing practices into a comprehensive medical text.

1954

Preservation

Master healer Tashi Wangdu hides the monastery's sacred texts and healing formulae, preserving the lineage through turbulent decades.

2014

Restoration

A twelve-year restoration project begins, led by the descendants of the original master healers working alongside conservation architects.

2026

Mandala Opens

The monastery reopens as a luxury healing sanctuary, welcoming guests to experience authentic Tibetan healing for the first time.

Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Tibetan Healing

Tibetan medicine does not treat symptoms — it restores harmony. Every treatment at Mandala is guided by the Gyushi, the foundational four-tantra text of Tibetan medical science, which articulates health as the dynamic balance of body, mind, and environment.

Lung

The wind element governing movement, breath, and nervous system function — restored through sound and gentle touch.

Tripa

The bile element controlling metabolism, vision, and intelligence — balanced through cooling herbs and stone therapy.

Beken

The phlegm element regulating sleep, joints, and structural integrity — addressed through steam baths and warm compress treatments.

Integration

All three elements in dynamic equilibrium, achieved through the totality of the Mandala experience over consecutive days.

The Practitioners

Master Healers

Each practitioner at Mandala has trained for a minimum of twelve years within the Kengye lineage. Their knowledge is not taught from textbooks but transmitted through direct practice and lived experience alongside senior healers.

Tenzin Dorje

Head Healer & Founder

Trained under the late Master Karma Rinzin from age nine, Tenzin Dorje spent twenty-two years in deep practice before founding Mandala. He holds the complete Kengye healing transmission and is one of only three living masters certified in the full Gyushi diagnostic system.

38 Years of Practice

Pema Wangchuk

Master Sound Therapist

Pema studied traditional bowl-making under the last practising smith of the Shigatse tradition, and spent eight years in a sound therapy lineage in Bhutan. His bowl programmes combine precise acoustic science with meditative presence.

24 Years of Practice

Sangye Lhamo

Herbal Alchemist

Descended from seven generations of Amchi (Tibetan physicians), Sangye Lhamo collects, processes, and formulates all herbal preparations used at Mandala from plants grown within the monastery's sacred garden and wild-harvested at altitude.

19 Years of Practice

Karma Tsering

Lama & Meditation Guide

A fully ordained lama who completed a three-year silent retreat before joining Mandala, Karma Tsering guides all meditation and mindfulness programmes. His teachings draw from both Vajrayana Buddhism and the Bon healing tradition.

31 Years of Practice
