Our Model

How We Work

Most water projects fail because they are designed for donor cycles, not community timelines. Our model is built around a single principle: we do not leave until the community is genuinely equipped to own what we built together.

From Assessment to Handover and Beyond

01

Community-Led Assessment

We begin with listening. A hydrological assessment is conducted alongside a community-driven consultation, mapping existing water governance, seasonal patterns, social dynamics, and the community's own priorities. No solution is proposed at this stage.

02

System Design and Community Approval

Based on the assessment, our engineers propose a system design. This is presented to community representatives in illustrated plain-language formats. All modifications requested by the community are incorporated before final approval. No ground is broken without community endorsement.

03

Construction with Local Labor

At least 70% of our construction workforce is hired locally. Construction creates economic benefit and ensures that local workers understand the system they are building — a critical knowledge foundation for future maintenance.

04

Training and Knowledge Transfer

Before commissioning, two or more local technicians complete a full maintenance training program to a recognized qualification standard. Operations and maintenance manuals are produced in the local language, in illustrated form, and stored in a waterproof community document kit on-site.

05

Commissioning and Community Handover

At official commissioning, legal ownership is transferred to the community water committee. The spare parts fund is established and the water committee is trained in financial management, pricing, and record-keeping.

06

Five-Year Monitoring and Support

AquaVerde provides remote technical support, quarterly field visits, and a rapid-response repair fund for the first twenty-four months. Annual assessments continue for five years. Our 97% operational rate is the product of this commitment — not of better engineering alone.

Our Field Resources

Multilingual Materials

Operations manuals are produced in the local language in illustrated format. Currently available in 18 languages across all program regions.

Spare Parts Supply Chain

Every project includes a pre-positioned spare parts fund and a vetted local supply chain for components — ending the pattern of defunct pumps with no available parts.

24-Hour Remote Support

A 24/7 technical support line staffed by regional engineers provides troubleshooting assistance to trained local technicians in all active monitoring projects.

Partner With Us

We welcome engineers, hydrologists, funders, and institutional partners who share our commitment to durable, community-owned water infrastructure.
