Our Story

A Legacy of
Exploration

Founded in 1968 under a national mandate to advance space science and exploration, Stellaris Space Centre has grown from a single ground tracking station into one of the hemisphere's most comprehensive space institutions.

Across five decades, we have launched 247 missions, placed 56 satellites into orbit, and partnered with 24 international space agencies. Our work spans the full spectrum — from planetary science and deep space probes, to climate-monitoring constellations and youth education programs that reach over 12,000 educators annually.

We operate with a belief that the cosmos belongs to everyone. Every discovery we make is shared openly with the global scientific community, and our public observatory and outreach programs bring the wonders of the universe to citizens of all ages and backgrounds.

Leadership

The Minds
Behind the Mission

Director General
Dr. Nadia Voss-Kepler

A former mission commander with two orbital deployments to her credit, Dr. Voss-Kepler leads Stellaris with a philosophy of radical transparency and international scientific cooperation. She holds a PhD in Astrophysics from the Institute of Advanced Sciences.

Chief Science Officer
Prof. Alaric Mwangi

Professor Mwangi oversees Stellaris's planetary science and deep-space research divisions. His work on magnetospheric physics has been cited in over 400 peer-reviewed publications, and he serves on the editorial board of Nature Astronomy.

Head of Operations
Commander Selin Demirci

A veteran aerospace engineer who designed the orbital insertion manoeuvres for the Selene Program's first lunar lander. Commander Demirci oversees all mission control operations, launch infrastructure, and flight readiness assessments.

What We Stand For

Our Core
Values

→

Scientific Rigour

Every mission is built on peer-reviewed objectives, verified methodologies, and transparent data sharing with the international community.

→

Public Accountability

As a national institution, we are answerable to the citizens who fund our work. All mission data is publicly archived and accessible.

→

Global Cooperation

Space is a shared frontier. We actively seek partnerships across borders to pool expertise, resources, and the wonder of discovery.

→

Inspiring the Next Generation

Our education programs, observatory nights, and junior researcher initiatives ensure the future of space exploration is bright and inclusive.
