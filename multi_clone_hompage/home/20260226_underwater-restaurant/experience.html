The Experience

From Surface
to the Depths

The Descent

Five Stages of Immersion

Arriving at ABYSSAL is unlike arriving anywhere else. The experience begins the moment you step onto the pier and ends only when you resurface, transformed, into the world above.

01
~
Surface Arrival

The Pavilion Welcome

Guests arrive at the bespoke surface pavilion, a low-slung structure of weathered Norwegian oak and raw copper positioned directly above the tunnel. A host greets you by name. Champagne is offered. The ocean is visible in every direction. The pavilion is the last moment of the world you know.

02
↓
The Descent

The Submersion Elevator

A glass-sided elevator descends silently through 5 metres of Norwegian seawater. The journey takes forty seconds. As the ocean closes above you, the light changes — deepening from surface blue to abyssal teal. The world above becomes a rippling memory. By the time the elevator doors open, you are already somewhere else entirely.

03
*
The Arrival

Entry into the Tunnel

The tunnel stretches before you — 38 metres of glass corridor, each wall alive with the movement of fish, of light, of the slow undulation of kelp forests. Your table awaits at your designated position within the tunnel. Candles cast warm light against the glass. Beyond the glass, the Norwegian sea moves in absolute silence.

04
+
The Dining

Seven Courses Beneath the Sea

Each of the seven courses is presented by a member of our kitchen brigade who describes not only the dish, but the specific marine species or natural phenomenon inspiring it. Service is unhurried. A single sitting lasts three to four hours. The ocean, in every moment, provides a different scene through the glass.

05
^
The Ascent

Return to the Surface

As the final coffee is served, the elevator is prepared. Guests ascend through the same 5 metres — now in reverse. The surface light returns gradually. The pavilion awaits with petit fours, a final nightcap, and an undisturbed view of the Norwegian night sky above the water you just dined beneath.

Practical Guide

What to Expect

  • ~

    Arrival Time

    Guests are requested to arrive at the surface pavilion 30 minutes before their sitting time. This allows for a welcome drink and briefing without rushing the descent experience.

  • *

    Dress Code

    Smart elegant attire is required. Formal evening wear is welcomed and encouraged. Jeans, trainers, and casual garments are not permitted. Temperatures underwater are consistent at 21°C year-round.

  • +

    Duration

    The full experience — from pavilion welcome to final nightcap — typically lasts 4 to 4.5 hours. Guests may linger at the pavilion bar after service for as long as they wish.

  • ^

    Photography

    Tasteful photography is permitted throughout the experience. We ask that flash photography not be used during the tunnel service to protect the marine environment. A complimentary photography package is available.

  • -

    Accessibility

    The glass elevator is fully accessible. We accommodate all mobility requirements with advance notice. Please contact us directly to discuss your needs and we will ensure your experience is without compromise.
