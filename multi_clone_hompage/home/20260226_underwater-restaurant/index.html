The Experience

Where the ocean
becomes your theatre

ABYSSAL redefines the boundary between dining and immersion. Enclosed within a hand-crafted glass tunnel five metres beneath the Norwegian sea, every course arrives as marine life drifts silently past the crystal walls. The ocean is not a backdrop — it is the dining room.


From the moment the descent begins, time slows. Sound softens. And the world above becomes a memory.

The Architecture

Glass, Steel &
the Abyss

Three years of engineering mastery produced a structure that blurs the border between engineering and art. ABYSSAL's glass tunnel spans 38 metres of the Norwegian seabed, its walls composed of triple-laminated optical-grade glass capable of withstanding the full ocean above.

  • Depth5 metres below sea level
  • Tunnel Length38 metres
  • Glass Thickness21 cm triple-laminated
  • Construction3 years, 240 craftsmen
  • Capacity36 guests per sitting
