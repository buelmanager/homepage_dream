The Origin

A Norwegian
Vision

ABYSSAL was conceived on a winter's night in 2019, when Norwegian restaurateur Erik Halvorsen stood on a Bergen pier watching the fjord's surface and imagined inverting the relationship between diner and ocean. The question was simple: what if the ocean were not the view, but the room?

Three years of collaboration between marine engineers, award-winning architects, and one of Scandinavia's most celebrated kitchen brigades produced a structure and a culinary programme that has no precedent.

ABYSSAL opened to a waiting list of 24,000 on its first day of reservations. It has remained among the world's most sought-after dining experiences ever since.

  • 2019Concept developed by Erik Halvorsen on Bergen Pier
  • 2020Marine engineering commission granted; seabed surveys begin
  • 2021Glass tunnel structure installed over 14 months by 240 craftsmen
  • 2022ABYSSAL opens; waiting list reaches 24,000 on day one
  • 2024Named the world's most innovative restaurant by three international publications

The Architecture

Engineered for Wonder

The ABYSSAL tunnel is a singular achievement in marine architecture. Its 38-metre span follows the natural contour of the Bergen fjord floor, allowing the structure to integrate seamlessly with the seabed rather than imposing upon it.

Every surface visible to diners is optical-grade glass — a 21-centimetre triple-laminated composite that distorts nothing, amplifies everything, and will outlast any building above the waterline by centuries.

  • Depth5 m below mean sea level
  • Tunnel Length38 m spanning the seabed
  • Glass Specification21 cm triple-laminated optical
  • Pressure Rating4× engineering safety margin
  • Build Duration14 months on-site construction
  • Craftsmen Involved240 across 7 specialisations
  • Structural Warranty200 years minimum design life

Our Ethos

In Service of the Ocean

~

Zero Seabed Disturbance

The tunnel's foundation uses a reversible anchor system. Not a single centimetre of the seabed was permanently altered. Marine biology audits are conducted quarterly.

*

Marine Noise Protocol

All mechanical systems operate within WHO marine noise thresholds. Pump and ventilation schedules are adjusted seasonally to avoid breeding cycles of resident species.

+

Light Pollution Control

Interior lighting is tuned to wavelengths that do not disrupt nocturnal marine behaviour. No external illumination is used. The tunnel is invisible from above at night.

The People

Behind the Depths

Lars Eriksson
Head Chef & Culinary Director

Formerly of Noma and Geranium, Lars was appointed ABYSSAL's founding Head Chef in 2021. His philosophy is one of radical restraint: allowing the finest Nordic ingredients — sourced from the same waters the restaurant inhabits — to speak without interruption.

Dr Astrid Moen
Marine Biologist & Environmental Consultant

Dr Moen has studied the Bergen fjord ecosystem for 18 years. She serves as ABYSSAL's permanent environmental guardian, ensuring that every operational decision — from menu sourcing to maintenance scheduling — causes no harm to the marine habitat surrounding the tunnel.

Our Pledge

A Promise to the Sea

ABYSSAL exists because of the ocean. We are committed to ensuring that the ocean exists, in its fullest splendour, long after we are gone. Our sustainability programme is not a marketing commitment — it is our operating licence.

  • 100% of seafood sourced from certified sustainable fisheries within 200 km
  • Zero single-use plastics across all operations since opening day
  • Carbon-neutral operation certified since 2023; net-negative target by 2027
  • 1% of annual revenue committed to Norwegian marine conservation programmes
  • Seasonal menu rotation that follows, rather than strains, natural availability cycles
  • Quarterly independent marine impact audits, published publicly
