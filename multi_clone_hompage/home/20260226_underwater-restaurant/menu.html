The Calendar

Following the Seasons

ABYSSAL's menu is never static. Head Chef Lars Eriksson revises each course when the season turns, sourcing ingredients that reflect what the Norwegian sea and surrounding lands produce at their finest moment.

Spring brings langoustine at peak delicacy. Summer offers wild coastal herbs and fresh sea vegetables. Autumn yields game and the deepest shellfish harvests. Winter returns us to the ocean floor — cured, aged, and reduced to pure depth.

Guests who dine across all four seasons will experience four entirely distinct menus — the same seven courses, four entirely different journeys.

Accommodations

Every Guest, Considered

All dietary requirements must be communicated at time of booking. Our kitchen is fully equipped to accommodate the following without compromise to the experience.

Pescatarian

The full seven-course menu is naturally seafood-focused. Course V (duck) is replaced with a second fish course of equal standing, sourced from the same waters.

Vegan

A complete vegan tasting menu is available. Course V is served without any animal products. Note: the full vegan menu must be requested no fewer than 7 days in advance.

Gluten Free

All courses can be prepared gluten-free. Please note this at the time of reservation. We cannot guarantee the absence of trace allergens in shared kitchen environments.

Shellfish Allergy

Courses I, II, III, and IV feature shellfish prominently. Full shellfish-free menus are available on request, subject to 72-hour advance notice.

Non-Alcoholic Pairing

Our award-winning non-alcoholic pairing menu — crafted by our sommelier using fermented botanicals, seawater reductions, and Norwegian herbal infusions — is available at NOK 980 per person.

Children

ABYSSAL welcomes guests aged 12 and above. A dedicated five-course junior tasting menu is available at NOK 1,900 per young guest. Prior arrangement required.

The Ultimate Seat

The Chef's Table

Positioned at the apex of the glass dome — the deepest point of the tunnel, where the water above is thickest and the marine life most dense — the Chef's Table seats up to four guests directly adjacent to the open kitchen pass.

Lars Eriksson personally visits each Chef's Table service to describe the day's sourcing, demonstrate a technique, and present the final dessert course himself. The Chef's Table is the single most sought-after seat in Norwegian dining.

NOK 6,500 per person · Maximum 4 guests per sitting · 24-month advance booking required
