The Descent

How to Reach
5 Metres Below

ABYSSAL's underwater access is a choreographed experience in itself. From the moment guests arrive at the surface pavilion to the moment the glass lift descends, every step is attended by our staff. Below is the sequence every guest follows.

  1. 1
    Arrive at the Marine Pavilion

    Guests are received at the ABYSSAL Marine Pavilion on Bryggen Pier. The pavilion entrance is clearly marked with a single cyan-lit ABYSSAL sign. No signage visible from the main road — by design. GPS coordinates: 60.3913° N, 5.3221° E.

  2. 2
    Welcome & Briefing

    Your host greets you in the upper pavilion — a 38-metre glass and timber structure positioned over the fjord. Welcome Champagne is served. You are given a brief orientation: the descent, the tunnel, what to expect from the evening ahead.

  3. 3
    The Glass Lift Descent

    A floor-to-ceiling glass elevator descends from the pavilion directly into the tunnel, 5 metres below the fjord surface. The descent takes 45 seconds. The ocean rises around you. Marine life is visible from the moment the lift enters the water.

  4. 4
    Entry into the Tunnel

    The lift doors open directly into the ABYSSAL dining tunnel. Guests are guided to their table by their dedicated host. The ocean is on three sides and above. Service begins immediately. The surface is 5 metres above and already feels very far away.

  5. 5
    The Ascent

    Following the final course and petits fours, guests ascend via the glass lift. The upper pavilion is available for post-dinner cognac and conversation, with views across the Bergen fjord to the lights of the city. No time limit is placed on the evening above.

Getting Here

Transport to the Pier

Bergen is served by Bergen Airport Flesland (BGO), 20 km from the city centre. We recommend all guests make transport arrangements in advance, particularly for evening returns after a 3–4 hour sitting.

Private Transfer

ABYSSAL operates a partnership with Bergen Executive Cars. Vehicles collect guests from Bergen Airport, hotels, or any specified address. Pre-booking required.

Book via reservations@abyssal.no

By Taxi

Licensed Bergen taxis are available from the airport and all central hotels. Journey time from Bergen Sentrum is approximately 8 minutes. Inform the driver: Bryggen Pier ABYSSAL entrance.

8 min from city centre

Light Rail

Bergen's Bybanen light rail connects the airport to Bergen Sentrum (45 min). From Sentrum, a short 12-minute walk along the waterfront reaches the pavilion entrance on Bryggen.

12 min walk from Sentrum

By Water Taxi

For a suitably cinematic arrival, we recommend the Bergen water taxi service, which docks directly at the pier adjacent to the pavilion. Subject to weather and availability — arrange in advance.

Direct pier arrival

When We Are Open

Hours of Service

ABYSSAL operates one sitting per evening, five evenings per week. We do not offer lunch service. The tunnel requires full operational readiness from our marine systems team, which necessitates a minimum three-hour preparation window before each sitting.

Each sitting runs approximately 3.5 to 4 hours. Guests are welcome to remain in the upper pavilion after the tunnel portion of the evening concludes. ABYSSAL closes its upper pavilion at 01:00 on Fridays and Saturdays, and 23:30 on all other evenings. The restaurant closes for a full two-week maintenance period every January and reopens for the spring season.
