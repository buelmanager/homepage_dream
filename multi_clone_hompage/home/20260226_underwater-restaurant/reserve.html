Cancellation Policy

Our Policy

We understand that plans change. Our policy is designed to be fair to both our guests and to the kitchen brigade, whose preparation for each sitting begins many days in advance.

More than 30 days Full refund of holding deposit, no cancellation fee. Your place on the waiting list is maintained for re-booking.
14–30 days 50% of holding deposit retained. No additional charge. We will make every effort to rebook your table to another guest.
7–14 days Full holding deposit retained. No additional charge beyond the deposit. We recommend guests arrange appropriate travel insurance.
Under 7 days Full holding deposit retained plus 25% of the total booking value. This reflects the cost of ingredients already procured and preparations begun.
No show Full booking value charged. ABYSSAL will not make exceptions to this policy. Chef's Table no-shows are charged at the full rate and may affect future booking eligibility.
Force Majeure In the event of severe weather, emergency, or circumstances beyond a guest's control, we will work with you on a case-by-case basis. Please contact us as soon as possible.
