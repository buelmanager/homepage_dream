What to Wear

Dress for the Occasion

ABYSSAL maintains a formal dress code. We believe the standard of dress reflects the respect guests have for the experience — and for the other diners sharing the tunnel with them.

Gentlemen

A suit or tailored jacket with shirt and trousers is required for all gentlemen. Ties are not mandatory but warmly encouraged. Smart loafers, Oxford shoes, or similar formal footwear. Trainers, sportswear, denim, and shorts are not permitted at any sitting.

Ladies

An evening dress, cocktail dress, or formal trouser suit is appropriate and welcomed. Formal heels or elegant flat footwear are both acceptable. As ABYSSAL involves a slight descent by elevator, we recommend secure footwear for comfort.

Temperature Below

The tunnel maintains a constant ambient temperature of 18°C year-round, which is cooler than standard indoor dining rooms due to the ocean environment. We recommend a layer that can be removed as the evening progresses and body warmth from the experience takes hold.

Photography

Discreet personal photography is permitted during the first course and the final course only. During courses II through VI, we ask guests to place devices away so that the experience — and the quiet of the deep — is fully honoured. Our dedicated photographer is available by arrangement.

Our Policy

Cancellation & Changes

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both our guests and our brigade, whose preparations for each sitting begin many days in advance of service.

More than 30 days Full refund of holding deposit. No cancellation fee. Your place on the waiting list is retained for re-booking at your preferred future date.
14–30 days 50% of holding deposit retained. No additional charge. ABYSSAL will make every effort to fill the table and may issue a partial credit upon successful rebooking.
7–14 days Full holding deposit retained. No additional charge beyond the deposit. We recommend all guests arrange comprehensive travel insurance prior to their visit to Norway.
Under 7 days Full holding deposit retained plus 25% of total booking value. This reflects the cost of perishable ingredients already procured and preparations already underway.
No Show Full booking value is charged. No exceptions are made to this policy. Repeated no-shows may affect future booking eligibility. The Chef's Table no-show rate is charged in full.
Force Majeure In circumstances of severe weather, medical emergency, or events entirely beyond a guest's control, ABYSSAL will review on a case-by-case basis. Contact us immediately at reservations@abyssal.no.
